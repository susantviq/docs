Vortex IQ is an AI Operating System for ecommerce. The phrase "operating system" is deliberate - it is not a dashboard you log into to read numbers, not a chatbot bolted onto your store, and not a suite of point tools sharing a logo. It is a connective layer that reads every signal across your stack, reasons across that signal with AI, and ships fixes back into the platforms you already run on. The value is in the **connector graph**: the thing that knows that this Stripe payment relates to this Shopify order, which relates to this Google Ads click, which relates to this customer's lifetime value, which relates to this Klaviyo email send.

## How it works: the connector graph

The connector graph is the architectural reason Vortex IQ is an OS rather than a stack of tools. Every feature - KPI cards, root-cause diagnostics, conversational queries, audits, fixes - is a query against the same graph.

Here is how a typical signal flows through the system:

1. **The Nerve Centre** detects an anomaly: cart abandonment is up 22% in the last four hours.
2. **Vortex Mind** runs the Checkout Conversion Failure report automatically, identifies that a recent theme push slowed Largest Contentful Paint on the cart page from 1.8s to 4.6s.
3. **Vortex Mind** generates an Action: _Roll back the cart-page theme change_.
4. **Actions** surfaces the new card on the Kanban board.
5. **Vortex Apps** executes the rollback when you approve it.
6. **Ask Viq** can answer "what just happened to my cart?" from any of these surfaces, citing the report from **Vortex Memory**.

That is one signal becoming a diagnosis becoming a fix in three clicks across four modules, without a developer, without a CSV export, and without a Slack thread.

## What Vortex IQ is not

The clearest way to understand what Vortex IQ is, is to be precise about what it is not.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="It is not a dashboard">
    Dashboards are passive. They display numbers you have to interpret. They do not run diagnostics, generate work, or act on your store.

    Vortex IQ has a dashboard layer (the Nerve Centre), but the dashboard is the entry point, not the product. The product runs work behind the dashboard: anomaly detection, root-cause diagnosis, action generation, and execution through Vortex Apps.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It is not a chatbot">
    Chatbots are language-only. They sit on top of an LLM and answer general-purpose questions. They do not know your store data, they do not have a connector graph behind them, and they do not produce structured findings or generate Actions.

    Ask Viq is the conversational surface inside Vortex IQ, but it queries the live connector graph - the same Nerve Centre signals, Vortex Mind diagnoses, and Vortex Memory archive that every other module uses. The chat is a UI choice, not the value.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It is not a SaaS suite">
    A SaaS suite is a marketing label for several tools sharing a parent brand. The data does not flow between them at runtime; you copy-paste between tabs. Each tool has its own admin, its own user model, its own pricing.

    Vortex IQ has six modules, but they are not six tools. A finding generated in Vortex Mind appears as a card on the Actions Kanban board because they read the same backing store, not because there is an integration between two products. The connections are pre-wired and run continuously.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## The agentic loop

Vortex IQ is an AI agentic OS. The agentic part means three things:

- **Reasoning over signal.** When the Nerve Centre fires an anomaly, Vortex Mind reasons across the full connector graph - not just one source - to identify likely causes, weighted by impact.
- **Action proposal.** Findings turn into Actions automatically. You do not have to translate a diagnostic insight into a Jira ticket; the Action card is created and surfaces with the recommended fix.
- **Execution.** Approved Actions execute through Vortex Apps - a theme rollback, a bulk content rewrite, a backup restore, a marketplace listing update - in a controlled, auditable way through the relevant app.

The agentic loop is: **signal → diagnosis → action proposal → human approval → execution**. You are in the loop at the approval step for any high-impact action; the OS does the rest.
