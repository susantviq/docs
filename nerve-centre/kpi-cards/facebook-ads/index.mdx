The Meta Ads connector covers Facebook + Instagram paid surfaces - typically the largest social-paid spend bucket in most DTC stacks. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 65 KPI pulses for Meta Ads accounts in the Vortex IQ **AI OS** catalogue including the standard ad hero cards (*ROAS*, *Wasted Spend*, *Zero-Conversion Spend*, *Spend vs Budget*) plus campaign / device / geography / dayparting cuts. **Vortex Mind** investigates when ROAS drops or waste concentrates; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
