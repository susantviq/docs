Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **18** KPI pulses for Opsgenie stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Opsgenie connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to on-call teams using Atlassian Opsgenie for alert routing, on-call schedules, and incident response across DevOps, IT, and SRE practices. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 18 KPI pulses covering *Alerts Firing*, *Alerts Acknowledged*, *MTTA / MTTR*, *Incidents Open*, *Incidents Resolved (24h)*, *Services Healthy / Degraded / Down*, *Top Alerting Services*, and *SLA Compliance* - so on-call load and commerce-side alerts sit in the same view. **Vortex Mind** investigates when one team's alert volume spikes; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the alert.
