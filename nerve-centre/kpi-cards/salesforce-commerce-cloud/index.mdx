The Salesforce Commerce Cloud connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to enterprise SFCC merchants. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 12 KPI pulses for SFCC stores at the time of writing - Total Revenue, Conversion Rate, Refund Rate, Out-of-Stock Products, plus customer-health and inventory-health cards (deeper SFCC-specific coverage is in development). **Vortex Mind** investigates when a number moves; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask anything in plain English with citations back to the source card.
