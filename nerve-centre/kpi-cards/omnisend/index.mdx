Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **29** KPI pulses for Omnisend stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Omnisend connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to merchants on Omnisend's email + SMS + push platform purpose-built for ecommerce - popular with mid-market Shopify and BigCommerce brands needing automation depth without enterprise-tier price. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 29 KPI pulses including subscriber, deliverability, engagement, and revenue-attribution metrics. **Vortex Mind** investigates when a number moves; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
