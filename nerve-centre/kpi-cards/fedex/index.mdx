Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **25** KPI pulses for FedEx stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The FedEx connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to merchants shipping with FedEx Express, Ground, Freight, and International services across the US and global lanes. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 25 KPI pulses with hero alerts on *Avg Shipping Cost*, *On-Time Delivery Rate*, *Priority Overnight Service Promise*, *Late Shipments*, *Exception Rate*, *Open Claims*, *Days to Token Expiry*, plus cross-channel *FedEx OTD by Sales Channel* and *International Customs-Hold Rate by Lane* so customs delays surface before customers chase them. **Vortex Mind** investigates when hazmat acceptance dips or a specific zone goes off-pace; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the tracking number.
