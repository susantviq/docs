Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **32** KPI pulses for Jira stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Jira connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to engineering and operations teams on Atlassian Jira — the dominant project + issue tracker for software-led organisations. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 32 KPI pulses focused on **how Jira handles findings the AI OS pushes back to it**: hero alerts on *VortexIQ Findings Open*, *Finding Resolution Rate (90d)*, *Abandoned Findings (>14d no movement)*, *Atlassian Token Expiry Imminent*, plus cross-channel cards (*Critical Findings Without a Jira Ticket*, *Finding-to-Ticket Dispatch Lag*) that surface coverage gaps no Jira-only dashboard would catch. Throughput and backlog-health metrics round out the catalogue. **Vortex Mind** investigates when findings stall or coverage gaps open; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
