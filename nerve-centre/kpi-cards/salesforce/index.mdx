The **Nerve Centre** tracks 29 KPI pulses for Salesforce accounts focused on the marketing-and-engagement layer (subscribers, deliverability, campaign status, conversion, revenue attribution). **Vortex Mind** investigates when a number moves; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
