Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **18** KPI pulses for Bugsnag stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Bugsnag connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to engineering teams using Bugsnag (now SmartBear Insight Hub) for application stability monitoring across web, mobile, and backend services with a focus on release-by-release error trends. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 18 KPI pulses covering *Error Rate*, *p95 / p99 Latency*, *Apdex Score*, *MTTA / MTTR*, *Incidents Open*, *Services Healthy / Degraded / Down*, *SLA Compliance*, and *Top Error Types*. **Vortex Mind** investigates when error spikes follow a deploy; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the issue.
