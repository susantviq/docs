Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **69** KPI pulses for Mailchimp stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Mailchimp connector covers the most widely-used SMB + mid-market email marketing platform. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 69 KPI pulses for Mailchimp accounts in the Vortex IQ **AI OS** catalogue — including hero alerts (*Email-Attributed Revenue*, *Audience Size*, *Email Open Rate*, *Bounce Rate*, *Bounce Spike*, *Spam Complaint Rate*, *Abandoned-Cart Automation Status*, *Top Campaigns by Revenue*) plus cross-channel cards (*Audience Coverage of Customer Base*, *Email Share of Total Store Revenue*, *Abandoned-Cart Recovery Value*). **Vortex Mind** investigates when email revenue dips or audience health degrades; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
