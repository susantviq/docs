Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **76** KPI pulses for CyberSource stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The CyberSource connector covers Visa's enterprise payment platform, used by larger merchants needing fine-grained 3DS, BIN intelligence, and decline-recovery analytics. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 76 KPI pulses in the Vortex IQ **AI OS** catalogue - including hero alerts (*Authorisation Success Rate*, *3DS Challenge Abandon Rate*, *Top Declining Issuers*, *Open Chargebacks*, *Payment Health Score*) plus cross-channel cards (*Decline Spike vs Checkout Funnel Drop*, *Recoverable Revenue (decline-driven)*, *Disputes vs Returns Correlation*, *3DS Friction Revenue Loss*). **Vortex Mind** investigates when an issuer starts blocking transactions or 3DS friction climbs; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
