## At a glance

- **Category:** Capacity
- **Type:** Supporting Metrics
- **Chart:** gauge
- **Format:** percent
- **Time window:** RT
- **Alert trigger:** >85%


## Calculation

Total PGA allocated / PGA aggregate target from V$PGASTAT. Over target = work areas spilling to temp tablespace.
