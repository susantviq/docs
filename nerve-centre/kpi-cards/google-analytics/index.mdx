**[Nerve Centre KPIs](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/google-analytics) · [Audit Profile](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/google-analytics/audit) · [Sentiment Settings](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/google-analytics/sentiment)**

Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **100** KPI pulses for Google Analytics 4 stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Google Analytics 4 connector is the analytics layer most stores already have, surfaced into the Vortex IQ **AI OS** catalogue with the cross-channel context the GA4 UI alone doesn't show. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 100 KPI pulses for GA4 properties - including the *AI Traffic* cluster (AI Conversion Rate, AI Engagement, AI Revenue, AI vs Search Traffic) for stores tracking citations from ChatGPT, Perplexity, and other AI shopping agents, plus the *Ad Attribution Consistency (GA4 vs Google Ads)* and *LCP-to-Conversion Correlation* cross-channel cards. **Vortex Mind** explains the why when conversion drops or AI traffic shifts; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask anything in plain English with citations back to the GA4 source.
