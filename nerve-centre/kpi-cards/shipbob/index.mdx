Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **87** KPI pulses for ShipBob stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The ShipBob connector is the deepest fulfilment integration in the Vortex IQ **AI OS** catalogue — 87 KPI pulses spanning warehouse throughput, inventory health, and last-mile delivery for brands using ShipBob's distributed 3PL network across the US, Canada, EU, UK, and Australia. The **Nerve Centre** runs hero alerts on *Fulfilment Rate*, *On-Time Delivery Rate*, *Pending Fulfilment Backlog*, *Out-of-Stock Products*, *SLA Compliance by Warehouse*, and *Shipping Cost per Order* — the cards that decide whether you're shipping to promise or burning margin. **Vortex Mind** investigates when a warehouse falls behind, a SKU stocks out with active demand, or shipping costs spike at one fulfilment centre; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English — "which warehouse is hurting margin this week?" — with citations back to the source order.
