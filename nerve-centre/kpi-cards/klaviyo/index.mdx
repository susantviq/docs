**[Nerve Centre KPIs](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/klaviyo) · [Audit Profile](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/klaviyo/audit) · [Sentiment Settings](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/klaviyo/sentiment)**

Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **50** KPI pulses for Klaviyo stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Klaviyo connector is the most-used email + SMS lifecycle platform in DTC. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 50 KPI pulses for Klaviyo accounts in the Vortex IQ **AI OS** catalogue - including hero alerts (*Email-Attributed Revenue*, *Flow vs Campaign Revenue Mix*, *Bounce Rate*, *Bounce Spike*, *Spam Complaint Rate*, *Abandoned-Cart Flow Status*) plus cross-platform cards (*Abandoned-Cart Recovery Value*, *Email Share of Total Store Revenue*, *Top Sends → Purchase Lag*, *Unsubs Within 7d of Purchase*). **Vortex Mind** investigates when email revenue dips or deliverability degrades; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English (*"why did flow revenue drop last week?"*) with citations back to the source card.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:data_availability */}
> **A note on data availability.** Cards populate from your connected Klaviyo account, so what you see depends on your data and key permissions:
> - **Revenue & engagement metrics** (open/click/conversion rates, revenue) need the **Reports** scope enabled on the connected API key. If a card reads "no data yet", the dashboard tells you which scope to turn on.
> - **Cross-platform cards** (email share of revenue, abandoned-cart recovery) light up once a store (BigCommerce/Shopify/etc.) is connected on the same workspace.
> - **SMS and predictive/RFM segment cards** appear once those Klaviyo features are in use; until then they're kept off the default dashboard and listed here as roadmap.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:data_availability */}
