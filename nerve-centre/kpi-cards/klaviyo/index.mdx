Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **53** KPI pulses for Klaviyo stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Klaviyo connector is the most-used email + SMS lifecycle platform in DTC. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 53 KPI pulses for Klaviyo accounts in the Vortex IQ **AI OS** catalogue - including hero alerts (*Email-Attributed Revenue*, *Flow vs Campaign Revenue Mix*, *Bounce Rate*, *Bounce Spike*, *Spam Complaint Rate*, *Abandoned-Cart Flow Status*) plus cross-channel cards (*Abandoned-Cart Recovery Value*, *Email Share of Total Store Revenue*, *Top Sends → Purchase Lag*, *Unsubs Within 7d of Purchase*). **Vortex Mind** investigates when email revenue dips or deliverability degrades; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English (*"why did flow revenue drop last week?"*) with citations back to the source card.
