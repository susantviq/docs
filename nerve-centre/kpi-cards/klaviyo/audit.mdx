Audits flows, lists, segmentation health, and revenue attribution.

## What this audit checks

### Flows
- Inactive flows (draft / manual / disabled)
- Welcome series present
- Abandoned-cart series present
- Browse-abandonment series present

### Lists & segmentation
- List growth rate
- Suppressed contact %
- Active vs unengaged ratio

### Deliverability
- Bounce rate
- Spam complaint rate
- Reported sender domain authentication

### Revenue attribution
- Klaviyo-attributed revenue trend
- Top-performing flows by revenue

## Data sources

- `GET https://a.klaviyo.com/api/flows/` - Flow inventory + status
- `GET https://a.klaviyo.com/api/lists/` - List metadata + counts
- `GET https://a.klaviyo.com/api/segments/` - Segment definitions + counts
- `GET https://a.klaviyo.com/api/metrics/` - Metric aggregates incl. attributed revenue
