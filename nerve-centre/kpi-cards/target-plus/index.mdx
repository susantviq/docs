Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **17** KPI pulses for Target Plus stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Target Plus connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to invited brands selling on Target Plus - Target's curated US marketplace for select third-party sellers, complementary to its first-party retail business. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 17 KPI pulses including listings, sales, reputation, returns, and marketplace economics. **Vortex Mind** investigates when a number moves; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
