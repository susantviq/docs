## Calculation

Vortex IQ blends three underlying Target Plus signals into one score: SLA compliance (led by on-time dispatch), Mirakl feed-import success, and brand-presentation compliance. Each contributes to a normalised 0-100 figure, presented as a real-time reading with a 7-day view so you can see both the current state and the recent trajectory. Because the inputs mirror what Target weighs in its own partner-status reviews, the score moves when any one pillar weakens, even if the others hold. The alert fires when the composite drops below 75, signalling that at least one signal needs urgent attention. The worked example below shows how a single weak pillar pulls the whole score down.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
*A representative reading of **Target Plus Partner Health Score** for a typical Target Plus partner.*

On 09 Jun 26 an apparel partner sat at a healthy 88, with strong dispatch, clean feeds, and no presentation flags. Two weeks later, on 23 Jun 26, the score had slipped to 73 and tripped the alert. Dispatch and feeds were still fine, but a batch of brand-presentation flags from a recent listing refresh had dragged the compliance pillar down enough to pull the composite under 75. The owner could see at a glance that the risk was presentation, not fulfilment, and focus the team accordingly. ## Reconciling against Target Plus Partners

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}

**Where to look in the Target Plus Partners portal:** Open the partner scorecard, which presents Target's own view of your SLA, feed, and presentation standing. There may not be a single matching headline number, so compare each pillar against the corresponding Vortex IQ sibling card rather than expecting an exact figure match.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Period boundary (RT/7D vs portal review window) | Either | Compare the recent trend rather than a single instant; Target's review window may be longer. |
| Time zone applied to underlying events | Either | Confirm which zone borderline SLA and feed events are evaluated in before reconciling. |
| Filter scope (pillar weighting) | Vortex IQ may differ | The composite weighting is a Vortex IQ blend, so read the individual pillars to understand any gap. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** Where weak feed or presentation signals originate from your BigCommerce source of truth, confirm the upstream data in the BigCommerce admin so the score recovers from the root, not just the symptom.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}

**Q: How often does this number update?**
It refreshes on the standard Vortex IQ data refresh and is shown as a real-time reading with a 7-day trend view.

**Q: Why does the Partners portal show a different figure?**
Target's scorecard may not publish a single composite number and can use a longer review window. Compare the individual pillars (SLA, feed, presentation) rather than expecting an exact match.

**Q: How does this relate to the sibling cards?**
It is a weighted blend of `on-time-dispatch-rate-partner-sla`, `mirakl-feed-import-success-rate`, and `brand-presentation-compliance-flags`, and it triggers the `partner-health-composite-crossed-threshold` event.

**Q: Can I change the alert threshold?**
Yes. The default fires below 75, but you can raise or lower that floor for this card on the Alert Rules tab.
