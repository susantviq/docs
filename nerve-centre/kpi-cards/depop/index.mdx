The Depop connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to merchants selling on Depop - the Etsy-owned Gen Z and millennial resale fashion marketplace, strong on streetwear, vintage, and Y2K aesthetics. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 17 KPI pulses including listings, sales, reputation, returns, and marketplace economics. **Vortex Mind** investigates when a number moves; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
