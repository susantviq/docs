## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Listings with <5 Hashtags** is the share of your active catalogue tagged with fewer than five hashtags. Depop discovery is hashtag-driven: hashtags are how buyers find one-off items in search and Explore, so a listing with thin tags is invisible on most of the surfaces a shopper actually browses. This card shows how much of your shop is hiding from search for want of a few more relevant tags.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it counts** | The percentage (and count) of active listings carrying fewer than five hashtags. |
| **Sample type** | Backend API data from Depop, refreshed on the standard data refresh. |
| **Why it matters** | Hashtags are Depop's main organic discovery lever. Each relevant tag is another search surface a buyer can find you on. Under-tagged listings miss most of that traffic, which suppresses views, then saves, then sales. |
| **Reading the value** | A low share means most listings are well tagged. When more than roughly one in five is under five hashtags, a meaningful slice of your catalogue is missing free discovery. |
| **Currency** | percent |
| **Time window** | `RT` (real-time) |
| **Alert trigger** | `> 20% of active listings` |
| **Sentiment key** | `dep_hashtag_health` |
| **Roles** | owner, marketing |
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

## Calculation

The card divides the count of active listings with fewer than five hashtags by total active listings, expressed as a percentage. Vortex IQ reads the tag count per listing from the Depop integration; the hashtag boundary and alert percentage are configurable in the Sensitivity tab.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
*A representative reading of **Listings with <5 Hashtags** for a typical Y2K accessories shop on Depop.* Of 210 active listings, 58 had three or fewer tags, putting the share near 28 percent, above the 20 percent guide. Vortex Mind showed the under-tagged group drew far fewer search impressions than well-tagged peers. The seller worked through them adding brand, era, style and colour tags to round each out to the full set. Over the next month search-driven views on the reworked listings roughly doubled and several long-stale items finally sold. ## Reconciling against Depop

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Depop's own dashboard:**

Depop does not publish a shop-wide hashtag-completeness figure. Reconcile by opening a sample of flagged listings and counting the tags on each. The per-listing tag count is what Vortex IQ aggregates into the share.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Active scope.** Only active listings count toward the share. | Variable | Confirm which listings are live. |
| **Boundary setting.** The under-five threshold is configurable. | Variable | Check your Sensitivity tab value. |
| **Refresh timing.** Newly added tags register on the next refresh. | Marginal | Force a manual refresh after retagging. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** read this with photo health and save rate to confirm discovery, not pricing, is the bottleneck. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Listings with <5 Hashtags update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). Force a manual refresh after a retagging session.

**Q: Does adding any five hashtags help?**
No. Relevance matters more than count. Five accurate brand, era, style and colour tags beat five generic ones. The card measures completeness; pair it with save rate to confirm the tags you add are the ones buyers search.

**Q: Why five and not more?**
Five is an illustrative floor that captures the main descriptive dimensions. Depop allows more, and most strong listings use them. Set your own boundary in the Sensitivity tab.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, both the hashtag boundary and the alert percentage are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab.
