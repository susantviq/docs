## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Same Item Priced Differently vs Mercari / Vinted** is the price-parity card for sellers who list the same stock across Depop and rival resale marketplaces like Mercari and Vinted. It lists items where the Depop price has drifted meaningfully from the price you set elsewhere. A wide spread is money left on the table when Depop is cheaper, or lost sales when Depop is dearer, and it usually means a price change on one channel never reached the others.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it counts** | Items listed on Depop and at least one rival marketplace whose price spread exceeds your tolerance, listed row by row with the gap. |
| **Sample type** | Cross-channel comparison of Depop integration data against rival marketplace listings, refreshed on the standard data refresh. |
| **Why it matters** | Inconsistent pricing across marketplaces erodes margin or kills conversion depending on which way it leans. Buyers comparison-shop the same resale platforms, so a visible gap can cost a sale or undercut your own price. The fix is usually one missed sync. |
| **Reading the value** | Each row is an item to reprice. A few small spreads are normal; a count above roughly five items with more than a 15 percent spread signals a pricing-sync gap worth fixing systematically. |
| **Currency** | count (items over the spread threshold) |
| **Time window** | `30D` |
| **Alert trigger** | `> 5 items with > 15% spread` |
| **Sentiment key** | `dep_xc_price_parity` |
| **Roles** | owner, finance, marketing |

## Calculation

The card matches items listed on Depop and on rival marketplaces, computes the price spread for each, and lists those exceeding the spread tolerance. Vortex IQ reads prices from the Depop integration and the comparison channels and matches on the shared item identifier. Both the spread tolerance and the item-count threshold are configurable in the Sensitivity tab.

## Reconciling against Depop

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Depop's own dashboard:**

Depop shows only its own price, with no view of rival marketplaces, so the spread is invisible there. Reconcile by opening a flagged item on Depop and comparing its price directly against the same listing on Mercari or Vinted.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Match key.** Comparison relies on matching the same item across marketplaces. | Variable | Confirm item mapping is reliable. |
| **Spread tolerance.** The percentage that counts as a meaningful gap is configurable. | Variable | Check your Sensitivity setting. |
| **Fee and currency basis.** Marketplaces differ on fees and currency, which can justify some spread. | Variable | Decide whether to compare list price or net. |

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does this card update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations), recomparing prices across marketplaces each cycle.

**Q: Some spread is intentional. Does the card account for that?**
It flags spreads above your tolerance, so set the threshold to reflect deliberate channel pricing. Items within tolerance do not appear; only gaps wide enough to matter are listed.

**Q: Should Depop always be the cheapest channel?**
Not necessarily. The point is intent, not uniformity. Decide your channel pricing strategy, then use the card to catch unintended drift away from it rather than enforcing identical prices.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, both the spread tolerance and the item-count threshold are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab.
