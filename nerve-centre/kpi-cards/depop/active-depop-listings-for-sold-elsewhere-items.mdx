## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Active Depop Listings for Sold-Elsewhere Items** counts listings still live on Depop for items that have already sold on another channel. For resellers running one-of-a-kind stock across Depop, BigCommerce and other marketplaces, this is the most expensive sync failure there is: a buyer purchases something you cannot ship, forcing a cancellation, a refund and almost always a poor rating. Every item on this list is an oversell waiting to happen.

## Calculation

The card matches active Depop listings against item availability on your other channels and counts those whose matched item is already sold or out of stock elsewhere. Vortex IQ reads both sides through their integrations and matches on the shared item identifier. The set of channels checked is configurable in the Alert Rules tab.

## Reconciling against Depop

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Depop's own dashboard:**

Depop shows the listing as live and has no knowledge of the other channel's sale, so the conflict is invisible there. Reconcile by opening a flagged listing in Depop and confirming the matched item shows sold or out of stock on the other channel.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does this card update?**
It evaluates in real time and refreshes on the standard data refresh, so a sale on another channel surfaces here quickly. Act on any count without waiting.

**Q: Why is any count an alert?**
Because the items are typically one-of-a-kind. A single live listing for a sold item is one buyer away from a forced cancellation, a refund and a damaging rating. There is no safe non-zero level.

**Q: What is the fastest fix?**
Mark the Depop listing as sold or remove it. If an order has already come in for an unavailable item, refund immediately and message the buyer to limit the rating impact.

**Q: Can I customise this card?**
Yes, the channels checked and the matching logic are configurable per profile in the Alert Rules tab.
