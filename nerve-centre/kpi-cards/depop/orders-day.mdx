## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Orders / Day** is your daily Depop order count plotted over time. It is the pulse of the shop: the line shows the rhythm of sales, the spikes from a viral listing or a relist push, and the dips from a quiet stretch. For a solo seller, it is also the workload forecast, because every order on the line becomes a parcel to pack and a dispatch deadline to hit.

## Calculation

The card counts orders per calendar day across the window and plots them as a time series. Vortex IQ reads order timestamps from the Depop integration and aggregates them by day in the merchant reporting time zone.

## Reconciling against Depop

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Depop's own dashboard:**

Your order history in the Depop app lists every order with its date. Reconcile by counting orders for a sample day and comparing to the point on the Vortex IQ line.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Time zone.** Day boundaries depend on the reporting time zone. | Marginal | Confirm time zone alignment. |
| **Cancelled orders.** These may be excluded from the daily count. | Lower in Vortex IQ | Confirm order scope. |
| **Refresh timing.** Today's count fills in across refreshes. | Marginal | Read completed days for a stable figure. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** read the daily line with pending shipment and the revenue trend to plan capacity and spot demand shifts. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Orders / Day update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). Today's bar continues to fill through the day.

**Q: Today looks low. Is something wrong?**
The current day is incomplete until it closes. Compare completed days for a fair read, and check the revenue trend before reading a single low day as a problem.

**Q: How do I tell a real dip from noise?**
Look for several days below your weekday baseline, not one. Tie sustained dips to listing cadence and seasonality before assuming demand has changed.

**Q: Can I customise this card?**
There is no alert threshold by default, but the reporting period and time zone are configurable per profile.
