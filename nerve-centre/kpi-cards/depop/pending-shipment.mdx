## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Pending Shipment** is the live count of paid Depop orders waiting to be dispatched. It is the most actionable fulfilment number in the shop: every order on this list is a clock running toward its dispatch deadline. A backlog that swells well past your normal level is the earliest sign that on-time shipping, ratings and disputes are all about to come under pressure.

## Calculation

Pending Shipment is the live count of paid, undispatched orders from the Depop integration, compared against the trailing 30-day average to set the alert. Vortex IQ derives the baseline from your own order history so the threshold reflects your normal volume rather than a fixed number.

## Reconciling against Depop

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Depop's own dashboard:**

Your unshipped orders are listed in the Selling or orders view of the Depop app. The Vortex IQ count should match the orders awaiting dispatch there; gaps are usually refresh timing or differences in how cancelled orders are treated.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Refresh timing.** New orders and dispatch marks register on the next refresh. | Marginal | Force a manual refresh to confirm. |
| **Cancelled or refunded orders.** These may leave the queue at different moments. | Variable | Confirm the live order set. |
| **Baseline window.** The 2x trigger compares to your trailing average, which shifts over time. | Variable | Check the current baseline in the card. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** read backlog with orders per day and on-time rate to tell a demand spike from a capacity problem. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Pending Shipment update?**
The count tracks close to real time and refreshes on the standard data refresh. Force a manual refresh after a packing session to watch it fall.

**Q: My backlog is high but it is normal for me. Is that a problem?**
Not necessarily. The alert is relative to your own 30-day average, not an absolute number. If your baseline is genuinely high, tune the multiplier in the Sensitivity tab so the alert fires only on real surges.

**Q: Why is the trigger relative rather than a fixed count?**
A fixed count would be wrong for most shops. Two pending orders is a crisis for a low-volume seller and a quiet day for a high-volume one. The relative trigger adapts to your scale.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, the backlog multiplier is configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab.
