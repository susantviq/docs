The Customer.io connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to product-led brands on Customer.io's behavioural messaging platform — strong on event-driven email, SMS, push, and in-app messaging triggered by product events. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 29 KPI pulses including subscriber, deliverability, engagement, and revenue-attribution metrics. **Vortex Mind** investigates when a number moves; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
