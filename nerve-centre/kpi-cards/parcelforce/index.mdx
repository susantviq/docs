The Parcelforce Worldwide connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to UK merchants shipping with Royal Mail Group's premium parcel arm across Express24, Express48, Saturday, and international services. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 24 KPI pulses with hero alerts on *Avg Shipping Cost*, *On-Time Delivery Rate*, *Express24 Service Day Promise*, *Late Shipments*, *Exception Rate*, *Open Claims*, *Days to Token Expiry*, plus a cross-channel *Parcelforce OTD by Sales Channel* card and a *Saturday Delivery Uplift ROI* metric - so paying for premium services only when they pay back becomes evidence-based. **Vortex Mind** investigates when service-class OTD dips; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the tracking number.
