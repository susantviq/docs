Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **21** KPI pulses for Bring stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Bring connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to merchants shipping across the Nordics via Posten Norge's logistics arm for parcels, letters, freight, and pickup-point delivery in Norway, Sweden, Denmark, and Finland. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 21 KPI pulses with hero alerts on *Avg Shipping Cost*, *On-Time Delivery Rate*, *Late Shipments*, *Exception Rate*, *Open Claims*, and *Days to Token Expiry*. **Vortex Mind** investigates when first-attempt delivery dips or specific routes drift; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the source shipment.
