Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **21** KPI pulses for Sendle stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Sendle connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to small businesses in the US, Canada, and Australia using Sendle's carbon-neutral door-to-door parcel network — designed as an Australia Post and USPS alternative for SMB shippers. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 21 KPI pulses with hero alerts on *Avg Shipping Cost*, *On-Time Delivery Rate*, *Late Shipments*, *Exception Rate*, *Open Claims*, and *Days to Token Expiry*. **Vortex Mind** investigates when failed-delivery counts trend up or a route's OTD drifts off pace; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the source shipment.
