## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Mixpanel Tracking Health Score** is a single 0-100 read that rolls up the health of your event pipeline into one number. It blends whether event volume is holding against its baseline, whether your named core events are still firing, and whether ingestion is arriving promptly. The idea is simple: every funnel, retention curve, and DAU count in Mixpanel is only as trustworthy as the events feeding it, so this score tells you at a glance whether the data underneath your reports can be relied on today. A high score means tracking is healthy; a falling score is your cue to look before you trust the numbers.

## Calculation

Vortex IQ derives the health score by combining several tracking signals from Mixpanel into one normalised 0-100 figure. The inputs are the current event volume measured against its recent baseline, the firing status of your designated core events, and the freshness of ingestion. A real-time read is blended with a trailing 7-day view so a brief blip does not crater the score while a sustained problem clearly does. The exact weighting is tuned to surface genuine tracking faults rather than noise, and the alert latches when the composite falls below the configured threshold. Because it is a roll-up, the score tells you that something is wrong; the sibling cards tell you which input drove it.

## Reconciling against Mixpanel

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Mixpanel's own dashboard:**

Mixpanel does not publish a single health score, so reconcile against its parts. Build an Insights report on total event volume for the last 7 days to confirm the volume input, check your core events in the Events view to confirm they are still firing, and look at the data-management or ingestion view for arrival timing. When all three look healthy, the score should sit high; when one degrades, the score should reflect it.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Composite weighting.** The score blends three inputs, so no single Mixpanel report equals it. | Score moves before any one report looks bad | Read all three sibling inputs to see which one moved the score. |
| **Real-time versus 7-day blend.** A brief blip is softened by the trailing view. | Score steadier than a live raw metric | Allow for the smoothing when comparing to a real-time chart. |
| **Late-arriving events.** Batched SDK data lowers the live volume and lag inputs temporarily. | Score reads lower momentarily | Re-check after the SDK flush interval before treating it as a fault. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** if the health score falls but your ecommerce platform still shows normal orders and sessions, the problem is almost certainly Mixpanel-side tracking rather than a real demand change. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Mixpanel Tracking Health Score update?**
It blends a real-time read with a trailing 7-day view, so it reacts quickly to genuine breaks while ignoring momentary blips. Allow for SDK batching, which can briefly soften the live inputs.

**Q: The score dropped but all my reports still look fine. Why?**
The score is an early warning that leads individual reports. A degraded input such as rising ingestion lag can pull the score down before any single funnel or retention chart looks wrong, which is the point of having it.

**Q: What is a healthy score?**
Stores in good shape typically sit well into the 90s. The default alert fires below 70, but the right floor depends on how spiky your volume is, so treat the threshold as a starting point to tune.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, the score's alert floor is configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Stores with steady volume can raise the floor for an earlier warning, while spiky or low-volume stores may lower it to cut noise.
