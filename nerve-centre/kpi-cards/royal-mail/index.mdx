Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **25** KPI pulses for Royal Mail stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Royal Mail connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to UK merchants shipping with the country's largest letter and small-parcel carrier across Tracked24, Tracked48, Special Delivery, and Signed services. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 25 KPI pulses with hero alerts on *Avg Shipping Cost*, *On-Time Delivery Rate*, *Tracked24 Service Day Promise*, *Late Shipments*, *Exception Rate*, *Open Claims*, *Days to Token Expiry*, plus cross-channel *Royal Mail OTD by Sales Channel* and *Collection vs Post-Office Handover Failure Rate* - so you know whether OTD slips happen on the carrier side or before handover. **Vortex Mind** investigates when P58 claims age past resolution targets or specific service classes drift; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the consignment.
