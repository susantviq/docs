[KPI Cards](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards) / **Microsoft Teams** · [Sentiment](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/microsoft-teams/sentiment)

Vortex IQ tracks how actively the team uses Microsoft Teams: active users, messages sent, meetings and calls, and their trends over time. Cards derive from the Microsoft Graph getTeamsUserActivity usage reports. All cards require the Reports.Read.All application permission with admin consent on the Entra app registration.
