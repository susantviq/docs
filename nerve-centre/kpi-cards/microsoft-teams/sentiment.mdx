[KPI Cards](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards) / [Microsoft Teams](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/microsoft-teams) / **Sentiment**

Cards carrying a sensitivity band flip to Action Needed when the live value crosses the bad threshold. Bands are shared across connectors on the same archetype and tunable per store in Alert Rules.

| Card | Direction | Good | Bad |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Active Users (30d)](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/microsoft-teams/active-users-30d) | Trend (drop is the alarm) | 0 | -30 |
| [Total Messages (30d)](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/microsoft-teams/total-messages-30d) | Trend (drop is the alarm) | 0 | -40 |
| [Collaboration Health Score](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/microsoft-teams/collaboration-health-score) | Higher is better | 80 | 55 |
| [Message Volume Change (vsP)](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/microsoft-teams/message-volume-change-vsp) | Trend (drop is the alarm) | 0 | -40 |
| [Active Users Change (vsP)](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/microsoft-teams/active-users-change-vsp) | Trend (drop is the alarm) | 0 | -30 |
| [Active User Drop Alert](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/microsoft-teams/active-user-drop-alert) | Trend (drop is the alarm) | 0 | -30 |
| [Message Volume Drop Alert](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/microsoft-teams/message-volume-drop-alert) | Trend (drop is the alarm) | 0 | -40 |
