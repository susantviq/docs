## Calculation

```
Account ID from STS GetCallerIdentity (caller ARN shown as the card detail). Renders with any valid key pair, no Cost Explorer needed.
```

Computed by the Amazon Web Services engine from daily cost buckets over a 60-day window (current 30 days plus the previous 30 for trend comparisons), refreshed on the standard sync. The engine degrades to no data rather than estimating.

### Tracked live in Vortex IQ Nerve Centre

Connect Amazon Web Services in [Settings, Connectors](https://app.vortexiq.ai/v2/settings?tab=sources) and this card renders live alongside the rest of the connector's metrics.
