- **Category:** Alerts & Anomaly Detection
- **Type:** Supporting Metrics
- **Chart:** kpi
- **Format:** percent
- **Time window:** 30D vsP
- **Alert trigger:** up >50% vsP


## Calculation

Spend change vs the prior 30d window ((current - previous) / previous x 100); fires on a spike per the ai_spend_trend band.
