The Braintree connector covers the PayPal-owned payment processor used by many subscription-led and marketplace-led merchants for card processing alongside PayPal. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 16 KPI pulses for Braintree accounts in the Vortex IQ **AI OS** catalogue (Success Rate, Decline Rate, Refund Rate, Chargeback Rate, Dispute Rate, settlement metrics, Top Decline Reasons, Top Payment Methods, Volume Trend). **Vortex Mind** investigates when a number moves; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
