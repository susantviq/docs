## At a glance

## Calculation

This is a cross-platform card: it joins your Alibaba B2B listings against the matching items on AliExpress by their shared identifier, then compares the Alibaba wholesale price against the AliExpress retail price. An item counts when the wholesale price exceeds the retail price, an inversion of the normal relationship. The card reports how many items are currently inverted over the time window. Because both channels share a parent company, the comparison is the one a buyer is most likely to make. See the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Reconciling against Alibaba Seller dashboard

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Alibaba's own dashboard:**

Alibaba shows only its own side of this comparison. In your supplier workbench, open Product Management to read each listing's wholesale and MOQ tier prices. Alibaba has no view of AliExpress retail prices, so to reconcile you compare each flagged Alibaba item against the same item's price on AliExpress directly. The card does that join; the workbench is where you adjust the Alibaba B2B price.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Period boundary.** Vortex IQ evaluates over a 30-day window; a short AliExpress promotion may move retail prices in and out of inversion. | Variable | Match the comparison dates and account for promo windows. |
| **Time zone.** Price-change timestamps differ between the account time zone and your merchant reporting time zone. | Marginal | Confirm the time zone match when checking recent changes. |
| **Filter scope.** Profile-level filters (which item set or MOQ tier is compared) may narrow the Vortex IQ view. | Variable | Match the item set and tier filters on both sides. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** because this card spans Alibaba and AliExpress, reconcile flagged items against live AliExpress retail prices rather than Alibaba alone. For divergence investigations across the sister channels, use Vortex Mind.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does B2B vs Retail Pricing Coherence vs AliExpress update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). Because retail promotions can move AliExpress prices quickly, allow for a refresh cycle before treating a brief inversion as settled.

**Q: Why does my Alibaba dashboard show a different number?**
Alibaba's workbench shows your wholesale prices but cannot see AliExpress retail prices, so it has no equivalent count. Differences usually come from promotion timing on AliExpress, time-zone alignment of price changes, and which item set or MOQ tier is in scope. Reconcile against live AliExpress prices.

**Q: How does this relate to the other cross-platform cards?**
Price inversion is the pricing-coherence member of the cross-platform set; read it next to the catalogue-drift and feed-validation cards for the full revenue-at-risk picture. Within Alibaba, Listings with MOQ Tier Inversion catches a related pricing fault.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, the alert fires whenever an Alibaba B2B price exceeds the matching AliExpress retail price, and the sensitivity around that comparison is configurable per profile in the Alert Rules tab. Adjust any tolerance to suit your pricing strategy rather than relying on the generic default.
