## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

Alibaba weighs this rate heavily when awarding and maintaining the Verified and Gold Supplier badges, so falling below the badge threshold directly risks your standing and inbox visibility. ## Calculation

Calculated automatically from your Alibaba data as the count of RFQs and buyer inquiries answered within 24 hours divided by the total received over the trailing 30 days, expressed as a percentage with a comparison against the prior period. Inputs are the RFQ and message records the Alibaba integration exposes. The card renders as a gauge so the distance from the badge threshold is visible at a glance. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
*A representative reading of **RFQ Response Rate (30d)** for a typical Alibaba supplier.* Suppose the gauge reads 64% on 12 Mar 26, down from 78% the prior period, and the alert has fired because it slipped below the 70% badge threshold. Out of roughly 50 RFQs and inquiries in the window, 18 went unanswered inside 24 hours, most of them arriving overnight and on weekends. Left unaddressed, this rate is the kind that triggers a badge review. The fix is to ensure every inquiry gets at least a holding reply within the day, using saved templates and broader coverage hours. ## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does RFQ Response Rate (30d) update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). For real-time signals, force a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Alibaba dashboard show a different number?**
The most common reasons are differences in the assessment window Alibaba uses for badges versus the 30-day rolling window here, plus time-zone alignment and filter scope. Match these settings before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: How does RFQ Response Rate (30d) relate to other Listing Health metrics?**
Read it alongside Median RFQ Response Time and Buyer Messages Unread for the full responsiveness picture, and alongside the badge status cards since this rate is a direct input. Single-metric reads can mislead; the diagnostic value is in the cross-reference.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, alert thresholds are configurable per profile in the Alert Rules tab. The default trigger fires below 70%, aligned to the badge threshold. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
