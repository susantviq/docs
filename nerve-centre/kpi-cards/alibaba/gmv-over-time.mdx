## Calculation

Calculated automatically from your Alibaba data by summing the gross merchandise value of orders across each interval in the trailing window and plotting the series. Inputs are the order values the Alibaba integration exposes over the period. The card is an area trend over 90 days rather than a single live value. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
*A representative reading of **GMV Over Time** for a typical Alibaba supplier.* Suppose the 90-day curve sits flat near 120,000 USD per month through January and February, then climbs steadily from early March, reaching roughly 165,000 USD by 12 Mar 26. Reading the shape, the lift lines up with a new wholesale tier launch and a run of RFQs converting to repeat orders. The next question is whether the gain came from more orders or larger ones, which is exactly where Total Transactions and Average Order Value come in. ## Reconciling against Alibaba Seller dashboard

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Alibaba's own dashboard:**

Open My Alibaba > Data Insights / Business Analysis and view the sales or transaction trend over a comparable date range. Confirm period boundaries and filter settings match the Vortex IQ profile to reconcile cleanly.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Period boundary.** Vortex IQ uses a 90-day rolling window by default; Alibaba's analytics may use calendar months or a different range. | Variable | Match the period range. |

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does GMV Over Time update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). For real-time signals, force a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Alibaba dashboard show a different number?**
The most common reasons are period-boundary differences (Vortex IQ uses a 90-day rolling window vs Alibaba's calendar months), time-zone alignment, and filter scope (profile-level vs Alibaba's all-account view). Match these settings before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: How does GMV Over Time relate to other Revenue & Sales metrics?**
Read it alongside Total Revenue, Average Order Value, and Total Transactions to tell whether a move in the curve came from more orders or larger ones. Single-metric reads can mislead; the diagnostic value is in the cross-reference.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, alert thresholds are configurable per profile in the Alert Rules tab. This card ships without a default alert since it is a trend view, but you can add one. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
