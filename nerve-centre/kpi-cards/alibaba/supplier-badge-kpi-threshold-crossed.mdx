## Calculation

Vortex IQ reads your current RFQ response rate, on-time delivery rate, and Trade Assurance dispute rate from the Alibaba integration and compares each against its badge threshold. When response rate falls below 70%, on-time delivery falls below 95%, or dispute rate rises above 3%, that KPI is counted as crossed and added to the alert list. The card reports how many of the three are currently breached and names each one. See the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
*A representative reading of **Supplier Badge KPI Threshold Crossed** for a typical Alibaba supplier.* Suppose a Gold Supplier sees RFQ response rate slip to 64% during a busy week while on-time delivery holds at 97% and dispute rate sits at 1.2%. The interpretation is clear: two KPIs are healthy, but the response-rate miss alone is enough to risk a downgrade at the next review, so the priority is clearing the inquiry backlog and lifting reply speed back over the threshold. ## Reconciling against Alibaba Seller dashboard

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Alibaba's own dashboard:**

The three underlying KPIs live in My Alibaba under the Account Health or Star Rating view, with response metrics also visible in the Message Centre and delivery and dispute metrics in Trade Assurance and Orders. Confirm period boundaries and filter settings match the Vortex IQ profile to reconcile cleanly.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Period boundary.** Alibaba's badge KPIs roll over a fixed assessment window; Vortex IQ evaluates the live current reading. | Variable | Match the assessment window before comparing. |
| **Time zone.** Alibaba uses the account time zone for KPI cut-offs; Vortex IQ aligns to your merchant reporting time zone. | Marginal | Confirm time zone match. |
| **Filter scope.** Profile-level filters (storefront, product line, test orders) may narrow the Vortex IQ view of inquiries and orders. | Variable | Match filter settings. |

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Supplier Badge KPI Threshold Crossed update?**
This is a real-time alert card, so it re-evaluates on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). For an immediate check, force a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Alibaba dashboard show a different number?**
The most common reasons are assessment-window differences (Alibaba scores over a fixed badge window while Vortex IQ reports the live current reading), time-zone alignment of KPI cut-offs, and filter scope (profile-level vs Alibaba's all-account view). Match these settings before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: How does Supplier Badge KPI Threshold Crossed relate to other Nerve Centre metrics?**
It rolls up the response, delivery, and dispute KPIs that the sibling cards track individually, and feeds the Alibaba Supplier Health Score. Read it alongside those cards to see which single KPI is driving the alert; single-metric reads can mislead.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, the response, on-time, and dispute thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust them to match your own badge tier and business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
