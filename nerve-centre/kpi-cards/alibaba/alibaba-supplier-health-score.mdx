## Calculation

Vortex IQ reads your badge level, RFQ response rate, on-time delivery rate, and Trade Assurance dispute rate from the Alibaba integration and combines them into a weighted composite, inverting the dispute rate so that fewer disputes lift the score. The result is a single health number reported live and trended over a 7-day window. Because it is a composite, a strong score can still hide one weak input, which is why the sibling cards matter. See the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
*A representative reading of **Alibaba Supplier Health Score** for a typical Alibaba supplier.* Suppose a Gold Supplier carries strong response and delivery KPIs but a recent run of disputes pushes dispute rate up, and the composite slips from 82 to 66 over the week of 15 Jun 26, below the 70 threshold. The interpretation is that the headline score is being dragged down almost entirely by the dispute input while the other three hold, so the priority is resolving the open Trade Assurance cases and addressing whatever quality or fulfilment issue is causing them. For deeper investigation into which input moved the score, use Vortex Mind to trace upstream causes; to ask how the score has trended by week in plain English, use Ask Viq.

## Reconciling against Alibaba Seller dashboard

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Alibaba's own dashboard:**

The contributing signals live in My Alibaba under the Account Health or Star Rating view, with response data in the Message Centre and delivery and dispute data in Trade Assurance and Orders. Alibaba does not publish this exact composite, so reconcile by checking each input. Confirm period boundaries and filter settings match the Vortex IQ profile to reconcile cleanly.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Period boundary.** Vortex IQ blends a live reading with a 7-day trend; Alibaba scores each KPI over its own assessment window. | Variable | Compare each input over a matched window rather than the composite directly. |
| **Time zone.** KPI cut-offs are evaluated in the account time zone; Vortex IQ aligns to your merchant reporting time zone. | Marginal | Confirm time zone match. |
| **Filter scope.** Profile-level filters (storefront, product line, test orders) may narrow the inquiries and orders behind each input. | Variable | Match filter settings. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** complement with the sibling badge and operational cards in the same category for the full diagnostic picture. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Alibaba Supplier Health Score update?**
It reports a live composite with a 7-day trend and refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). For an immediate read, force a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Alibaba dashboard show a different number?**
Alibaba does not publish this exact composite, so differences come from how the inputs are combined and from assessment-window, time-zone, and filter-scope differences on the underlying KPIs. Reconcile by comparing each input KPI rather than expecting the composite to match a single Alibaba figure.

**Q: How does Alibaba Supplier Health Score relate to other metrics?**
It is the executive roll-up of the same response, delivery, dispute, and badge KPIs that the sibling cards track individually and that the Supplier Badge KPI Threshold Crossed alert watches. When the score drops, open those siblings to find which input moved.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, the score threshold (default below 70) and the weighting of each input are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust them to match your badge tier and business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
