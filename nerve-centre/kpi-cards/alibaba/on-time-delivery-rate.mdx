## Calculation

Vortex IQ divides the number of orders delivered on or before their promised date by the total number of orders due for delivery in the trailing 30-day window, expressed as a percentage. The card also reports the change against the prior comparable period. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
*A representative reading of **On-Time Delivery Rate** for a typical Alibaba supplier.* Suppose 420 B2B orders were due for delivery in the 30 days ending 12 Mar 26 and 393 of them arrived on or before the promised date. That is an on-time rate of about 93.6 percent, which sits below the 95 percent alert floor and turns the gauge amber. The prior window ran at 96.2 percent, so the operations owner sees a real slide rather than noise. They check the pending-shipment backlog and dispatch cadence to find the held orders before the badge KPI is affected. For deeper investigation of which lanes or order types caused the misses, use Vortex Mind to trace upstream causes; for natural-language exploration such as "which orders shipped late last week", ask Ask Viq.

## Reconciling against Alibaba Seller dashboard

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Alibaba's own dashboard:**

Open the Alibaba Supplier workbench and go to the service or performance section, where on-time delivery and other supplier performance KPIs are reported alongside badge standing. The workbench typically shows a rolling performance figure tied to badge eligibility. Confirm period boundaries and filter settings match the Vortex IQ profile to reconcile cleanly.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does On-Time Delivery Rate update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). Delivery confirmations can post with a lag, so a borderline rate may settle over a few refreshes.

**Q: Why does my Alibaba dashboard show a different number?**
The most common reasons are period-boundary differences (Vortex IQ uses 30-day rolling versus the workbench's own performance cycle), time-zone alignment on borderline deliveries, and filter scope (profile-level versus all-account view). Match these settings before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: How does On-Time Delivery Rate relate to other fulfilment metrics?**
Read it next to the pending-shipment backlog and late-shipment cards. A rising backlog or late-shipment count usually shows up here within days, and all three feed the supplier badge KPI, so the diagnostic value is in the cross-reference.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, alert thresholds are configurable per profile in the Alert Rules tab. The default fires below 95 percent; adjust it to match your business baseline or your category's badge requirement rather than relying on the generic default.
