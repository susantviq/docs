{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Alibaba connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to brands selling on Alibaba's B2B wholesale marketplace - the largest cross-border B2B platform globally, used for sourcing and selling at trade-volume scale. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 17 KPI pulses including listings, sales, reputation, returns, and marketplace economics. **Vortex Mind** investigates when a number moves; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
