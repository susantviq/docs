## Calculation

Vortex IQ counts the orders currently flagged in the WAITING_SELLER_SEND state in real time and compares that live count against the rolling 30-day average for the same state. The alert fires when the current backlog exceeds twice the 30-day average. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
*A representative reading of **Pending Shipment (WAITING_SELLER_SEND)** for a typical Alibaba supplier.* Suppose the 30-day average backlog sits around 6 orders awaiting dispatch at any given time. ## Reconciling against Alibaba Seller dashboard

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Alibaba's own dashboard:**

Open the Alibaba Supplier workbench, go to Trade then Order Management, and filter orders by the awaiting-shipment or pending-dispatch status. The workbench shows the same queue of paid orders not yet marked as shipped. Confirm period boundaries and filter settings match the Vortex IQ profile to reconcile cleanly.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Pending Shipment (WAITING_SELLER_SEND) update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations) and the time window is real time. After you mark orders shipped, force a manual refresh to see the backlog drop immediately.

**Q: Why does my Alibaba dashboard show a different number?**
The most common reasons are timing (Vortex IQ counts live while the workbench view may be cached or paged), time-zone alignment on overdue calculations, and filter scope (profile-level versus all-account view). Compare the same awaiting-shipment status filter before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: How does Pending Shipment (WAITING_SELLER_SEND) relate to other fulfilment metrics?**
It is the leading indicator. A spike here precedes drops in on-time delivery rate and rises in late shipments, all of which feed the supplier badge KPI, so clearing this queue protects the whole fulfilment chain.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. The default fires when the live backlog exceeds twice the 30-day average; adjust it to match your dispatch capacity rather than relying on the generic default.
