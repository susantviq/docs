## Calculation

This is a cross-channel card: it joins your active Alibaba B2B listings against the live stock position of the same SKUs on the BigCommerce store. A listing counts when it is active on Alibaba but its matching SKU shows zero available stock on BigCommerce. The card reports the current count in real time, because the cost of one of these listings is a fresh inquiry that lands the moment a buyer reaches out. BigCommerce is treated as the stock source of truth. See the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Reconciling against Alibaba Seller dashboard

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Alibaba's own dashboard:**

Alibaba shows only its own side of this comparison. In your supplier workbench, open Product Management to see which listings are active, and check the RFQ Market / Message Centre for inquiries arriving on them. Alibaba has no view of your BigCommerce stock, so to reconcile you compare each active Alibaba listing against the SKU's stock in your BigCommerce admin. The card does that join; the workbench is where you pause the offending listings.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Period boundary.** The card is real time, but a very recent BigCommerce stock change may not yet have synced to the Alibaba view. | Variable | Allow for sync lag before treating a SKU as a false positive. |
| **Time zone.** Stock-change and listing timestamps differ between the account time zone and your merchant reporting time zone. | Marginal | Confirm the time zone match when checking recent changes. |
| **Filter scope.** Profile-level filters (which catalogue or warehouse stock is in scope) may narrow which SKUs are compared. | Variable | Match the catalogue and stock-location filters. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** because this card spans Alibaba and BigCommerce, reconcile the listed SKUs against live BigCommerce stock rather than Alibaba alone. For divergence investigations across channels, use Vortex Mind.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Active Alibaba Listings for OOS SKUs on BC update?**
The card is real time and refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). Because it joins two channels, allow brief sync lag after a BigCommerce stock change before treating a SKU as cleared.

**Q: Why does my Alibaba dashboard show a different number?**
Alibaba's workbench shows your active listings but cannot see BigCommerce stock, so it has no equivalent count. Differences usually come from sync lag on recent stock changes, time-zone alignment, and which catalogue or warehouse is in scope. Reconcile against live BigCommerce stock.

**Q: How does this relate to the other cross-channel cards?**
This is the most urgent of the cross-channel set because each listing draws an inquiry you cannot fulfil. Read it next to Out-of-Stock Listings for Alibaba's own view and Buyer Messages Unread for where the dead-end RFQs land.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, the alert fires above 0 by default, since any exposed listing is actionable, and that threshold is configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Most suppliers keep it at zero and clear the list as soon as it appears.
