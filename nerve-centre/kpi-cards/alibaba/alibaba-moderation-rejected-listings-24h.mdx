## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Alibaba Moderation Rejected Listings (24h)** is a real-time alert metric tracked from Alibaba data. It surfaces product listings that Alibaba moderation has rejected in the last 24 hours, typically for IP infringement, restricted or prohibited goods, or image quality issues. For a B2B supplier a rejected listing is offline and unsearchable, and repeated rejections can weigh on your supplier standing. The card sits in the Nerve Centre category and pairs with the listing health and badge sibling cards listed below to build a complete diagnostic picture.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it counts** | The number of product listings rejected by Alibaba moderation in the past 24 hours, with each rejected listing and its stated reason shown in the alert list. |
| **Sample type** | Backend API data from Alibaba, refreshed on the standard data refresh. |
| **Why it matters** | A rejected listing stops appearing in search and inquiries, so revenue from that product pauses immediately. Catching rejections fast lets you fix and resubmit before they accumulate and before they touch your supplier standing. |
| **Reading the value** | Any value above zero needs attention. Open the list, read the rejection reason per listing, and remediate the highest-revenue products first. |

## Calculation

Vortex IQ reads your listing records from the Alibaba integration and identifies products whose moderation status changed to rejected within the last 24 hours. Each rejected listing is added to the alert list along with the reason Alibaba returned, such as IP infringement, restricted goods, or image quality. The card reports the rolling 24-hour rejection count. See the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Reconciling against Alibaba Seller dashboard

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Alibaba's own dashboard:**

Rejected listings appear in My Alibaba under Product Management, filtered to rejected or pending-review status, with the moderation reason shown per product. Confirm period boundaries and filter settings match the Vortex IQ profile to reconcile cleanly.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Period boundary.** Vortex IQ counts a rolling 24 hours; Alibaba's product list shows all rejected items regardless of when they were rejected. | Variable | Match the time range to the last 24 hours before comparing. |
| **Time zone.** Rejection timestamps are recorded in the account time zone; Vortex IQ aligns the 24-hour window to your merchant reporting time zone. | Marginal | Confirm time zone match. |
| **Filter scope.** Profile-level filters (storefront, product line) may narrow which listings Vortex IQ monitors. | Variable | Match filter settings. |

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Alibaba Moderation Rejected Listings (24h) update?**
This is a real-time alert card over a rolling 24-hour window, so it re-evaluates on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). For an immediate check, force a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Alibaba dashboard show a different number?**
The most common reasons are window differences (Vortex IQ shows only the last 24 hours while Product Management lists all rejected items), time-zone alignment of rejection timestamps, and filter scope (profile-level vs Alibaba's all-product view). Match these settings before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: How does Alibaba Moderation Rejected Listings (24h) relate to other Nerve Centre metrics?**
Fresh rejections here graduate into the standing Rejected / Off-Shelf Listings pool if left unfixed, and they often correlate with low Listing Quality Score and incomplete required attributes. Read it alongside those cards to find and fix the root cause.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, the rejection threshold and the categories you want flagged are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust them to match your catalogue size and business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
