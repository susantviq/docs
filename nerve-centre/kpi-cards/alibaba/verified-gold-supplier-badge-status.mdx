## Calculation

Reported automatically from your Alibaba data as the supplier-tier status the platform assigns to your account, surfaced by the Alibaba integration. The card holds the current tier and fires when the tier moves down a level. Tier is determined by Alibaba from a blend of verification, responsiveness, and performance signals rather than computed here. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
*A representative reading of **Verified / Gold Supplier Badge Status** for a typical Alibaba supplier.* Suppose the card has held GOLD for months, then on 12 Mar 26 it drops to VERIFIED and the alert fires. Checking the driver cards, RFQ Response Rate had slipped below the badge threshold the previous week and a run of late shipments was flagged, the combination Alibaba weighs when reassessing tier. The downgrade typically cuts inbox visibility and ranking, so inquiry volume softens soon after. The recovery path is to restore the response rate and shipping performance, then request reassessment. ## Reconciling against Alibaba Seller dashboard

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Alibaba's own dashboard:**

Open My Alibaba > Data Insights / Business Analysis and your account or membership overview, where Alibaba shows your current supplier tier and the metrics tied to it. Confirm period boundaries and filter settings match the Vortex IQ profile to reconcile cleanly.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Verified / Gold Supplier Badge Status update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). For real-time signals, force a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Alibaba dashboard show a different number?**
The most common reasons are timing of Alibaba's reassessment cycle, time-zone alignment, and which account the profile maps to if you run more than one storefront. Match these before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: How does Verified / Gold Supplier Badge Status relate to other Executive Command Centre metrics?**
This card is the outcome; the badge-threshold, RFQ response, and shipping cards are the inputs that move it. Watch them to anticipate a tier change before it happens. Single-metric reads can mislead; the diagnostic value is in the cross-reference.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. The default trigger fires whenever the tier drops a level. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
