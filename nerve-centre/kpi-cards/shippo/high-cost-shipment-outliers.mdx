## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> The individual Shippo labels in the trailing 7 days that cost more than twice your average parcel cost. A row-level table that surfaces the specific shipments quietly burning margin: oversized boxes, dimensional-weight surcharges, wrong-service selections, and far-zone expedited labels.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it tracks** | Each paid Shippo transaction whose `rate.amount` exceeds 2x the rolling average label cost, listed row by row with carrier, service, zone, weight and charge. |
| **Data source** | Shippo `GET /transactions` joined to `rate`; compared against the period mean. Table chart, one row per outlier shipment. |
| **Time window** | `7D` (rolling 7 days). |
| **Alert trigger** | `>2x avg` (a shipment costing more than twice the average parcel cost is flagged as an outlier row). |
| **Roles** | owner, finance |
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

## What it tracks

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
Average cost cards hide the parcels that hurt. A blended cost per shipment of, say, $7.40 can sit comfortably while a handful of $34 labels quietly eat the week's margin. High-Cost Shipment Outliers lists exactly those parcels: any label charged at more than 2x the period average. The usual culprits are dimensional-weight surcharges on light-but-bulky items, a far-zone (Zone 8 or 9) parcel sent on an expedited service when ground would have done, an oversize-package surcharge, or a manual rate override that bypassed your cheapest-rate selection rule. Each row shows the carrier, service, destination zone, billed weight and the charge, so a despatch lead can spot the pattern in seconds. Work this card alongside [Cost by Zone](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/shippo/cost-by-zone) to see whether outliers cluster in distant zones, and [Avg Shipping Cost](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/shippo/avg-shipping-cost) to size the gap between the typical parcel and the outliers.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}

## Reconciling against the source

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
Each flagged row maps to a single Shippo transaction; open it in the [Shippo dashboard](https://apps.goshippo.com/) under Shipments and check the rate breakdown and any carrier surcharges. Shippo bills in account-local time and the card stores in UTC, so a label printed near midnight can fall in a different day than the dashboard shows.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
