Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **21** KPI pulses for Shippo stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Shippo connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to teams using Shippo's multi-carrier shipping API to compare rates and print labels across USPS, UPS, DHL, FedEx, and dozens of regional carriers. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 21 KPI pulses focused on what Shippo controls — hero alerts on *Avg Shipping Cost*, *On-Time Delivery Rate*, *Late Shipments*, *Exception Rate*, *Open Claims*, and *Days to Token Expiry* — so a quietly expiring API key never breaks label generation overnight. **Vortex Mind** investigates when OTD slips on a route or cost-per-shipment drifts; **Ask Viq™** answers carrier-by-carrier comparisons in plain English with citations back to the source shipment.
