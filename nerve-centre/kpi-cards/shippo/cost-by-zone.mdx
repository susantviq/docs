## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> Despatch spend broken down by Shippo shipping zone over the trailing 30 days. Zones group destinations by distance band (USPS Zones 1 to 9, or the carrier-equivalent banding for UPS / FedEx / DHL), so you can see where your parcel spend concentrates and which zones are quietly eroding margin.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it tracks** | Total and average label cost grouped by destination zone, sourced from the `rate.amount` charged on each Shippo `transaction` and bucketed by the carrier zone returned on the shipment. |
| **Data source** | Shippo `GET /transactions` (paid labels) joined to `rate` and the resolved destination zone. Bar-horizontal chart, one bar per zone. |
| **Time window** | `30D` (rolling 30 days). |
| **Alert trigger** | None. This is a diagnostic distribution card, not an alerting card. |
| **Roles** | owner, finance |
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

## What it tracks

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
Cost by Zone answers one question for despatch and finance teams: where is your shipping budget actually going? A multi-carrier merchant on Shippo rarely ships evenly across zones. Most US DTC stores see 60 to 75 percent of volume land in nearby Zones 1 to 4 (cheap, fast) and a long tail in Zones 5 to 9 (expensive, slower). This card pulls the paid label cost from each Shippo transaction, reads the destination zone the carrier assigned, and sums spend per zone band. A bar creeping up in Zone 8 usually means a new customer cluster on the far coast, a rate-table change, or a carrier-selection rule sending distant parcels to the wrong service. Pair it with [High-Cost Shipment Outliers](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/shippo/high-cost-shipment-outliers) to find the individual parcels driving a heavy zone, and with [Cost Per Shipment Trend](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/shippo/cost-per-shipment-trend) to see whether the blended cost is drifting over time.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}

## Reconciling against the source

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
Cross-check against the [Shippo dashboard](https://apps.goshippo.com/) under Billing and the per-transaction label cost, filtered to the same 30-day window. Shippo bills in account-local time and the card stores in UTC, so boundary days can shift a handful of labels between zones in the totals. Refunded or voided labels drop out of Shippo billing on their own cadence, so a recently voided label may still appear here until the next refresh.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
