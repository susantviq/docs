## Calculation

Calculated automatically from your Newegg Marketplace data. The card reads your current Egg Badge tier from your seller account performance state and reports it directly, raising an alert when the tier moves down from its previous level. The badge itself is awarded by Newegg from a blend of seller performance inputs, so this card reflects Newegg's published tier rather than recomputing it. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Reconciling against Newegg Seller Portal

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in the Newegg Seller Portal:**

Your Egg Badge tier is shown on the Seller Portal account health and performance summary, alongside the performance inputs that feed it. This Vortex IQ card mirrors that published tier and adds a drop alert so you are notified the moment the tier changes rather than discovering it on your next Portal visit.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Egg Badge Status update?**
This is a real-time card that reads your published tier on each data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). The underlying badge, however, only changes when Newegg runs its recalculation cycle, so the value moves on Newegg's schedule, not yours.

**Q: Why does my Newegg Seller Portal show a different number?**
The most common reasons are recalculation timing (a recovered metric restores the badge only at the next Newegg cycle), read lag (a very recent change posts on the next Vortex IQ refresh), and tier labelling. Match these before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: How does this card relate to other executive metrics?**
It sits next to the Newegg Seller Health Score, the composite that underpins the tier, and to the account-health threshold alert that fires when the badge or its inputs slip. Read fulfilment and reputation siblings to see which inputs are moving the tier.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
