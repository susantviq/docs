Calculated automatically from your Newegg Marketplace data. The card evaluates three independent conditions on every refresh, MAP violation count, overdue warranty claim count, and Egg Badge tier movement, and reports a breach the moment any one crosses its threshold. MAP flags appear under the listing compliance and price-violation notices in Seller Portal. Warranty SLA timers sit in the RMA / warranty claim queue. Egg Badge tier is shown on the seller account health and performance summary. This single Vortex IQ card consolidates all three so you do not have to poll each Seller Portal area separately.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does MAP / Warranty-SLA / Egg-Badge Threshold Crossed update?**
This is a real-time alert card. It re-evaluates all three conditions on every data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations) and can be forced with a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Newegg Seller Portal show a different number?**
The most common reasons are evaluation timing (Vortex IQ is real time vs Seller Portal's batch posting of compliance notices), threshold scope (your configured sensitivity vs the raw flag), and Egg Badge recalculation lag. Match these before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: How does this alert relate to other seller-health metrics?**
It is a roll-up of three standing cards: MAP Violations, Open Warranty Claims (overdue), and Egg Badge Status. Use it as the tripwire, then open those siblings to see the detail behind each breach.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
