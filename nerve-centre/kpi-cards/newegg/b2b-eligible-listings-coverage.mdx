## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **B2B-Eligible Listings Coverage** is a listing-health metric tracked from Newegg Marketplace data. It measures the share of your B2B-relevant SKUs, the servers, networking gear, and bulk-friendly items that business buyers look for, that are actually flagged B2B-eligible so they surface in Newegg's bizmember filter. Any SKU a business buyer would want but that is not flagged is invisible in that filter, which is missed B2B revenue. The card sits in the listing-health cluster so owners and marketing can keep the catalogue's business reach high.

## Calculation

Calculated automatically from your Newegg Marketplace data. The card identifies the SKUs in your catalogue that are B2B-relevant, such as servers, networking equipment, and items suited to bulk purchase, then expresses the count flagged B2B-eligible as a percentage of that relevant set. SKUs that are not business-relevant in the first place are excluded from both numerator and denominator, so the value reflects coverage of the catalogue that actually matters to bizmember buyers. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Reconciling against Newegg Seller Portal

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in the Newegg Seller Portal:**

B2B eligibility is set and shown per item in the Seller Portal item management area, where each listing carries its B2B / bizmember flag. The Portal does not present a single coverage percentage across your B2B-relevant catalogue; this Vortex IQ card derives that ratio for you so you can track the gap at a glance.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Relevant-set definition.** Vortex IQ scopes the denominator to B2B-relevant SKUs; a Portal count over all items will read differently. | Variable | Confirm which SKUs you consider B2B-relevant. |
| **Flag timing.** Newly flagged items propagate on Newegg's cadence; a just-set flag may not yet appear in the Vortex IQ value. | Lower | Allow for flag propagation lag. |
| **Catalogue scope.** Profile-level filters may narrow the catalogue Vortex IQ evaluates. | Variable | Match filter settings. |

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does B2B-Eligible Listings Coverage update?**
This is a real-time card. It re-evaluates your B2B-eligibility flags on each data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations) and can be forced with a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Newegg Seller Portal show a different number?**
The Portal shows the B2B flag per item but does not compute a coverage percentage. Differences usually come down to the relevant-set definition (which SKUs you treat as B2B-relevant), flag propagation timing, and catalogue scope filters. Match these before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: How does this card relate to other listing-health metrics?**
It pairs directly with B2B (Bizmember) Revenue Share, which shows the revenue the coverage unlocks, and with Required Attribute Completeness, since incomplete attributes can block eligibility. Read them together to size the opportunity.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
