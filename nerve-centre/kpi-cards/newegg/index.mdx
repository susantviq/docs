The Newegg Marketplace connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to merchants selling on Newegg - the US-and-Canada electronics-focused marketplace strong on PC components, gaming, and consumer tech. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 17 KPI pulses including listings, sales, reputation, returns, and marketplace economics. **Vortex Mind** investigates when a number moves; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
