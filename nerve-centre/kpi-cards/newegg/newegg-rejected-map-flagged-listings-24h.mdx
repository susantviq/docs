## Calculation

Calculated automatically from your Newegg Marketplace data. The card scans item listing status over the trailing 24 hours and counts any listing that Newegg moved into a rejected state through content or compliance review, or flagged for a MAP issue. Listings that were already rejected before the window opened are not re-counted, so the value reflects fresh problems rather than a standing backlog. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Reconciling against Newegg Seller Portal

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in the Newegg Seller Portal:**

Rejected and flagged listings appear in the Seller Portal item management area, where each listing carries its approval status and the rejection or MAP reason from Newegg's review team. Compliance and price-violation notices may also surface separately under the listing compliance area. This Vortex IQ card consolidates the last 24 hours of those status changes into a single count so you do not have to scan the item list manually each day.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Window boundary.** Vortex IQ counts the trailing 24 hours; Seller Portal shows the full standing list of rejected items. | Lower | Match the time window before comparing counts. |
| **Review timing.** Newegg's content and compliance review posts decisions on its own cadence; a rejection may appear in Vortex IQ shortly after Newegg records it. | Variable | Allow for Newegg's review posting lag. |
| **Status overlap.** A listing both rejected and MAP-flagged counts once here but may appear under two notices in Seller Portal. | Higher in Portal | Confirm whether a listing carries multiple notices. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** complement with sibling cards in the same category for the full diagnostic picture. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Newegg Rejected / MAP-Flagged Listings (24h) update?**
This is a real-time alert card scoped to a 24-hour window. It re-evaluates listing status on each data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations) and can be forced with a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Newegg Seller Portal show a different number?**
The most common reasons are window boundary (Vortex IQ counts the trailing 24 hours vs Seller Portal's full standing list), review timing (Newegg posts review decisions on its own cadence), and status overlap (a listing rejected and MAP-flagged counts once here but may show two notices in Portal). Match these before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: How does this card relate to other listing-health metrics?**
It is the 24-hour leading edge of your standing rejected and MAP pools. Use this alert to catch fresh rejections fast, then open Rejected Listings, MAP Violations, and Required Attribute Completeness to work the underlying causes.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
