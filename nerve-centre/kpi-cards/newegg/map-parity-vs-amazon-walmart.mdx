## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **MAP Parity vs Amazon / Walmart** compares your prices for the same SKU across Newegg, Amazon, and Walmart against the brand's MAP (Minimum Advertised Price). Brands such as Intel, NVIDIA, and ASUS enforce MAP across every retail channel, not just one. If any marketplace shows a price below MAP, the brand can act against the seller on all channels, so a violation on Amazon or Walmart is your problem on Newegg too. Each table row compares a SKU's price across the three marketplaces. It sits in the Cross-Platform: Revenue at Risk Hero cluster so owners, finance, and marketing see parity breaks first.

## Calculation

Calculated automatically by comparing each matched SKU's price on Newegg, Amazon, and Walmart against the brand's MAP. The card counts a SKU as in violation when its advertised price on any of the three marketplaces falls below MAP, and the table breaks the comparison down per SKU across all three channels. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
*A representative reading of **MAP Parity vs Amazon / Walmart** for a typical merchant on Newegg Marketplace.* Suppose the card reads 3. Opening the table shows a graphics card holding MAP on Newegg and Walmart but listed below MAP on Amazon by a third-party repricer, a power supply below MAP on Walmart after an automated price match, and a CPU at MAP everywhere except a stray Newegg promo that dipped under the floor. Even though only one of the three breaches is on Newegg itself, the brand enforces MAP across channels, so all three put the seller's brand authorisation at risk. The merchant re-prices the Amazon and Walmart listings, pulls the Newegg promo back to MAP, and the count returns to zero. ## Reconciling against Newegg Seller Portal

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in the Newegg Seller Portal:**

For the Newegg side, open the flagged SKU in the Seller Portal item management area and read its live advertised price, plus any compliance or price-violation notices. For the Amazon and Walmart sides, check each marketplace's own seller console (Amazon Seller Central and Walmart Seller Center) for that SKU's price, since each is its own source of truth. Compare all three against the brand's published MAP. Because this is a cross-platform card, reconcile across the Newegg Seller Portal and both other marketplaces, not just one.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Capture timing.** Marketplace prices change constantly; a repricer move may post to one channel before Vortex IQ re-captures all three. | Variable | Allow for the refresh cadence, then re-check each marketplace. |
| **MAP reference.** The MAP figure can change per promotion window or brand update; an out-of-date MAP changes which prices count. | Variable | Confirm the current brand MAP for the SKU. |
| **Match key.** SKU or identifier mismatches across the three marketplaces can mask or inflate violations. | Variable | Confirm the SKU mapping is correct on all channels. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** because this card spans Newegg, Amazon, and Walmart, reconcile against the Newegg Seller Portal plus each other marketplace's own seller console as the source of truth. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does MAP Parity vs Amazon / Walmart update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations), re-capturing prices across the three marketplaces. For real-time signals, force a manual refresh from the dashboard, though marketplace price capture may lag a live repricer move.

**Q: Why does my Newegg Seller Portal show a different number?**
The Seller Portal shows only the Newegg price, so it cannot reveal a breach on Amazon or Walmart. Differences usually come from capture timing across channels, an out-of-date MAP reference, and SKU match-key mismatches. Check each marketplace's own console and the current brand MAP before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: How does this relate to the other cross-platform cards?**
It is the cross-marketplace pricing member of the Cross-Platform: Revenue at Risk cluster, while the drift card covers BC field sync and the OOS card covers availability. It also pairs with the Newegg-only MAP Violations card and the executive MAP / Warranty-SLA / Egg-Badge alert that breaches feed.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, alert thresholds are configurable per profile in the Alert Rules tab. Adjust the SKU-count threshold to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
