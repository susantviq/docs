## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **On-Time Shipping Rate** is the fulfilment reliability metric tracked from Newegg Marketplace data. It measures the share of orders shipped by their ship-by date. Newegg weighs this directly into seller health and Egg Badge eligibility, so a rate below threshold drags both, whether the order is seller-fulfilled or routed through SBN (Shipped By Newegg). Because it is one of the loudest inputs to account standing, this card sits in the hero set for owners and operations.

## Calculation

Calculated automatically from your Newegg Marketplace data. The card divides orders shipped on or before their ship-by date by total shippable orders over the 30-day window, expresses the result as a percentage, and compares it against the prior period. Both seller-fulfilled and SBN orders count. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
*A representative reading of **On-Time Shipping Rate** for a typical merchant on Newegg Marketplace.* Suppose the gauge usually reads around 98 percent, then drops to 93 percent over the window, falling under the 95 percent line. Tracing it shows a two-day stretch where a carrier pickup was missed and a batch of seller-fulfilled orders shipped a day late. SBN orders in the same window stayed on time. The merchant fixes the pickup schedule and, for high-volume SKUs, shifts more inventory into SBN so dispatch is handled by Newegg Logistics, and the rate climbs back above threshold the following week. ## Reconciling against Newegg Seller Portal

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in the Newegg Seller Portal:**

On-time shipping appears in the Seller Portal performance and account health section, scored against each order's ship-by date. Confirm whether the Seller Portal view blends seller-fulfilled and SBN orders the same way and that the period matches before reconciling against the Vortex IQ figure.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Period boundary.** Vortex IQ uses a 30-day rolling window; Seller Portal performance may use a fixed evaluation window. | Variable | Match the period range. |
| **Fulfilment scope.** Vortex IQ blends seller-fulfilled and SBN; a Seller Portal view may split them. | Variable | Confirm which order types each view counts. |
| **Carrier scan timing.** A shipment scanned just after the ship-by cutoff may post differently across systems. | Marginal | Allow for carrier scan posting lag. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** complement with sibling cards in the same category for the full diagnostic picture. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does On-Time Shipping Rate update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). For real-time signals, force a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Newegg Seller Portal show a different number?**
The most common reasons are period-boundary differences (Vortex IQ's 30-day rolling window vs Seller Portal's evaluation window), fulfilment scope (whether seller-fulfilled and SBN are blended), and carrier scan timing. Match these settings before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: How does On-Time Shipping Rate relate to other fulfilment metrics?**
It is the headline reliability figure that Late Shipments and Pending Shipment explain: late shipments are the misses behind a drop, and a pending-shipment spike is the early warning. It also feeds the Seller Health Score and Egg Badge.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
