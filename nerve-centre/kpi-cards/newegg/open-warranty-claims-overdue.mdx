## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Open Warranty Claims (overdue)** counts the Newegg warranty and RMA claims that have run past their resolution SLA deadline. Newegg sets a resolution SLA per claim type, and faster ones such as DOA (dead on arrival) run on a tighter clock than standard warranty returns. Every claim that slips past its deadline drags your seller health and puts your Egg Badge and brand-page placement at risk, which is why this card sits in the executive Hero cluster where owners, operations, and finance see it first.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it counts** | The number of open Newegg warranty and RMA claims that are past their resolution SLA deadline, as surfaced by the Newegg Marketplace integration. Each row in the list names the overdue claim and the order or item behind it. |
| **Sample type** | Backend API data from Newegg Marketplace, evaluated in real time against each claim's resolution SLA. |
| **Why it matters** | Overdue warranty claims directly lower seller-health inputs, can pull listings off brand pages, and drop your Egg Badge tier. Because SLAs vary by claim type and DOA runs fastest, a single missed deadline can compound quickly. |
| **Reading the value** | Zero is the healthy state. Any non-zero count means at least one claim has breached its SLA; open the list to see which claims and prioritise the fastest-clock types such as DOA first. |

## Calculation

Calculated automatically from your Newegg Marketplace data. On every refresh the card compares each open warranty or RMA claim against its resolution SLA deadline, which varies by claim type, and counts the claims that have passed it. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

Warranty and RMA claims with their SLA timers sit in the Seller Portal RMA / warranty claim queue. Each claim shows its type and remaining time to resolution. Filter the queue to overdue or past-due claims and confirm the claim-type SLA assumptions match the Vortex IQ profile to reconcile the count cleanly.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Open Warranty Claims (overdue) update?**
This is a real-time card. It re-evaluates each claim against its SLA deadline on every data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations) and can be forced with a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Newegg Seller Portal show a different number?**
The most common reasons are evaluation timing (Vortex IQ is real time vs the portal's batch queue updates), claim-type SLA mapping (DOA vs standard warranty deadlines), and just-resolved claims that have not yet cleared one view. Match these before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: How does this relate to other seller-health metrics?**
Overdue warranty claims are one of the three tripwires behind the MAP / Warranty-SLA / Egg-Badge Threshold Crossed alert, and they feed both the Egg Badge tier and the seller health score. Pair with DOA Claim Rate to see whether DOA is driving the backlog.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
