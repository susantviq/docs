## Calculation

Calculated automatically from your Newegg Marketplace data. The card combines Newegg's seller-health inputs, on-time shipping rate, order defect and cancellation rate, warranty claim SLA adherence, and MAP compliance, into a single composite reading on a 0 to 100 scale, refreshed on the standard data refresh and held against a rolling 7-day baseline. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Newegg Seller Health Score update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations) and holds the reading against a rolling 7-day baseline. For real-time signals, force a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Newegg Seller Portal show a different number?**
The most common reasons are composite weighting (Vortex IQ rolls several inputs into one score vs Seller Portal's separate component figures), evaluation timing (Vortex IQ's refresh vs Newegg's batch posting), and baseline window differences. Match these before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: How does Newegg Seller Health Score relate to other seller-health metrics?**
It is the roll-up of its component cards: On-Time Shipping Rate, Open Warranty Claims (overdue), and MAP Violations all feed it, and it gates Egg Badge Status. Use this score as the headline, then open the component siblings to see what drives the movement.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, alert thresholds are configurable per profile in the Alert Rules tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
