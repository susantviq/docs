## At a glance

It counts inventory for protected brands that is live without the brand authorisation Newegg requires. On Newegg, protected-brand inventory needs documented authorisation, and listings missing it are hidden from the brand's pages, which is where much of the high-intent traffic lands. ## Calculation

Calculated automatically from your Newegg Marketplace data. The card scans your live catalogue for listings tied to protected brands and counts those that do not carry the brand authorisation Newegg requires for them to appear on the brand's pages. Listings for brands that do not require authorisation are not counted, so the value reflects genuine compliance gaps rather than the whole catalogue. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Reconciling against Newegg Seller Portal

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in the Newegg Seller Portal:**

Brand authorisation status appears in the Seller Portal under brand and item management, where protected-brand listings carry an authorisation requirement and current status. Authorisation submissions and approvals are tracked in the same area. This Vortex IQ card counts the gaps for you so you do not have to audit each protected brand by hand.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Listings Without Brand Authorisation update?**
This is a real-time card. It re-evaluates your catalogue's brand-authorisation status on each data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations) and can be forced with a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Newegg Seller Portal show a different number?**
The most common reasons are approval timing (a just-approved brand may still read as a gap until it propagates), protected-brand scope (Vortex IQ counts only brands that require authorisation), and catalogue scope filters. Match these before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: How does this card relate to other listing-health metrics?**
It overlaps with Rejected Listings, since missing authorisation is a common rejection cause, and with the 24-hour rejection alert. Watch it alongside MAP Violations as the second brand-compliance tripwire on your catalogue.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, alert thresholds are configurable per profile in the Alert Rules tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
