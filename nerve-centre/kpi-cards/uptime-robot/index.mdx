Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **18** KPI pulses for Uptime Robot stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Uptime Robot connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to teams using Uptime Robot for low-cost uptime monitoring, port checks, and SSL-expiry alerts - popular with SMBs and indie operators who need free or cheap synthetic monitoring. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 18 KPI pulses covering *Alerts Firing*, *MTTA / MTTR*, *Incidents Open*, *Services Healthy / Degraded / Down*, *p95 / p99 Latency*, *SLA Compliance*, and *Top Alerting Services* - so an outage on the storefront flows into the same AI OS picture as conversion and traffic. **Vortex Mind** investigates when an uptime probe pattern shifts; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the monitor.
