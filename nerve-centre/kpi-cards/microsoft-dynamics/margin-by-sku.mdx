## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
US distributor on BC, 30D 14 Mar to 12 Apr 26. Bottom 5 by margin:

| SKU | Revenue | Margin% |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ELEC-LAPTOP-A14 | $1.84M | 16% |
| ELEC-MONITOR-27 | $620K | 22% |
| ELEC-DOCK-USB4 | $480K | 24% |

LAPTOP-A14 at 16% drags consolidated margin. Pair with [Margin Erosion Alerts](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/microsoft-dynamics/margin-erosion-alerts) for trend.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Negative margin SKUs?**
Possible from FIFO turnover or markdowns below cost. Card flags.

**How many SKUs ranked?**
Top 20 + Bottom 20 by default; configurable.

**Multi-Company per-SKU?**
Aggregated across Companies; can drill per-Company.
