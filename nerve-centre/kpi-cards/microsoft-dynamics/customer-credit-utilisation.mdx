> Outstanding A/R balance as percent of credit limit. High utilisation predicts credit-hold orders.

## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it counts** | `(Open Customer Ledger Entries balance + Open SO value) / Customer.Credit Limit` per Customer. Highlights customers approaching their limit. |
| **Tax treatment** | Gross. |
| **Currency** | Customer's Currency Code. |
| **Time window** | `RT` |
| **Alert trigger** | `>85% utilisation` |
| **Sentiment key** | `credit_util` |
| **Roles** | owner, finance |
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

## Calculation

Calculated automatically from your Microsoft Dynamics 365 data. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
US distributor, snapshot:

| Customer | Limit | Used (AR + SOs) | Utilisation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Acme Tech | $2,000K | $2,180K | 109% (already exceeded) |
| NorthEast | $500K | $480K | 96% |
| TechHub | $300K | $240K | 80% |

Acme already on Credit Hold. NorthEast next.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Customers without Credit Limit?**
Excluded (cannot compute %).

**100% threshold for alert?**
80% by default; tunable. The risk zone before automatic Credit Hold.

**Sub-Buyers contributing to parent limit?**
Configurable via field map.
