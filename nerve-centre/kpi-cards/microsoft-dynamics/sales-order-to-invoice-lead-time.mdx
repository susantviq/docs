## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**B2B benchmark?**
Net-30: 5 to 10 days from Order to Invoice. Net-60: 10 to 20 days.

**DTC benchmark?**
Hours to 1 day.

**Combine Shipments batch?**
Many BC tenants batch-invoice weekly via Combine Shipments. Lead time rises if the batch runs less often.
