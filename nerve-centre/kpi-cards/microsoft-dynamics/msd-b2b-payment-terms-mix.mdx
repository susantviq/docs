## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Healthy mix?**
Depends on industry. Wholesale electronics: predominantly Net-30 with some Net-60 for top accounts.

**Custom Payment Terms Codes?**
Mapped in field map. BC's standard codes (1M, 30D, COD, etc.) recognised by default.

**Migrating customers from Net-60 to Net-30 to reduce DSO?**
The card tracks the mix shift over time. A successful migration shows higher Net-30 share and falling DSO.
