## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Threshold tuning?**
20% MoM default. Stable BC tenants tighten to 10%; volatile tenants loosen to 30%.

**False positives from billing-cycle timing?**
Possible if a Combine Shipments batch is delayed; check the batch log first.
