## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> Top 10 Items by current Inventory Value. Pareto check: typically 5% of SKUs hold 60% of inventory value.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it counts** | `Item.Inventory × Item.Unit Cost` ranked descending, top 10. |
| **Currency** | Reporting Currency. |
| **Time window** | `RT` |
| **Roles** | owner, finance, operations |
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Healthy concentration?**
ABC analysis: A (top 5%) holds 60-80% of value, B (next 15%) holds 15-25%, C (bottom 80%) holds 5-15%.

**Top 10 vs Top 25?**
Default 10; configurable.

**Multi-Location: per-Location ranking?**
Aggregated by Item No. across Locations by default; per-Location available.
