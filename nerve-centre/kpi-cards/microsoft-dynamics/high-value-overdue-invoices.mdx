**Why may differ:**

| Reason | Direction | Why |

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Threshold tuning?**
$50K + 60d default. Larger merchants raise to $100K + 90d.

**Disputed invoices?**
Excluded if BC dispute flag set.
