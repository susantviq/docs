title: "Company Performance, Microsoft Dynamics 365"

**Metrics type:** [Card](/nerve-centre/overview#metrics-types-explained)

> Per-company revenue, margin, A/R, inventory roll-up. Available only when Multi-Company is enabled in Business Central.

## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it counts** | Per-Company snapshot: Revenue, Margin %, AR, Inventory Value. Multi-Company tenants only. |
| **Tax treatment** | Net of VAT for revenue. |
| **Currency** | Local + Reporting Currency. |
| **Time window** | `30D` |
| **Alert trigger** | `-` |
| **Roles** | owner, finance |
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

## Calculation

Calculated automatically from your Microsoft Dynamics 365 data. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
UK apparel parent + US + EU + AU Companies, snapshot 14 Apr 26.

| Company | Revenue 30D (GBP) | Margin% | AR | Inventory |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UK Apparel Ltd | £2,640K | 58% | £1,820K | £1,420K |
| US Apparel Inc | £1,423K | 52% | £980K | £680K |
| EU DTC BV | £974K | 56% | £620K | £420K |
| AU Apparel Pty | £218K | 48% | £180K | £102K |
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Single-Company tenants?**
Card hides.

**Eliminations applied?**
Yes when Consolidation Company selected.

**New Company added?**
Card auto-detects on next sync.
