## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
US distributor on BC, 90D window 14 Jan to 13 Apr 26.

| Rank | Customer | 90D Revenue (USD) | % of total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Acme Tech Distribution | $4,820,000 | 22% |
| 2 | NorthEast Components Inc | $2,140,000 | 10% |
| 3 | TechHub Wholesale | $1,820,000 | 8% |
| 4-10 | (other top customers) | $4,640,000 | 21% |
| **Top 10 total** | | **$13,420,000** | **61%** |

Top 10 = 61% of revenue. Top 1 = 22%. Concentration risk: losing Acme would create a 22% revenue hole. Treasury should ensure adequate facilities; sales team should diversify.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Concentration threshold for risk?**
Top 1 > 20% = high single-customer risk. Top 10 > 70% = high concentration; diversify or hedge.

**Top 10 vs Top 25?**
Card defaults to 10. Configurable.

**Multi-Company: same customer in two Companies?**
Aggregated by Customer No. across Companies. If your tenant has duplicate Customer No. semantics, configure dedup in field map.

**Anonymous DTC customers?**
DTC customers without a BC Customer record (handled via "DTC Default" customer) bucketed together; can be excluded from concentration calc.

**Privacy Blocked customers?**
Shown as "[Privacy Blocked]" per BC's privacy controls.
