## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

| | |

| **Time window** | `30D` |

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Which Dimension does the card use?**
Dimension 1 by default, or workspace-configured. Most BC tenants use Dim 1 for primary slice (Department, Brand, Channel).

**Multiple Dimensions: can I see crossing?**
Card defaults to single Dimension. Cross-Dimension (e.g. Channel × Brand) is on the roadmap.

**Postings without Dimension Value?**
Counted under "(unassigned)". A high unassigned bucket suggests a Dimension Posting Setup gap; see field map for filtering.

**AL Extension custom Dimensions?**
Yes if registered in Dimension Setup. The card uses BC standard `Dimension` table, AL Extensions extend without modification.

**Power BI parity?**
Power BI's Finance Performance pack with a Dimension slicer matches per Company.
