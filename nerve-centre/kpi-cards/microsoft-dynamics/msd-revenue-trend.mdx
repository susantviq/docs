## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> 90-day daily revenue trend: posted Sales Invoices + Sales Credit Memos summed by Posting Date. Shows momentum and seasonality at a glance.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it counts** | Daily `SUM(G/L Entry.Amount)` for revenue accounts (4000 to 4999 series) over 90 days. |
| **Tax treatment** | Net of VAT. |
| **Currency** | Multi-Company: each Company translated to Reporting Currency. |
| **Sales Credit Memos** | Deducted on the day posted. |
| **Time window** | `90D` |
| **Alert trigger** | `-` (paired with [Revenue Booked into GL](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/microsoft-dynamics/revenue-booked-into-gl) for actionable alerts) |
| **Roles** | owner, finance |
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

## Calculation

Calculated automatically from your Microsoft Dynamics 365 data. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
US wholesale electronics distributor, 90-day window 14 Jan to 13 Apr 26.

| Period | Daily avg revenue | Trend |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 14 Jan to 12 Feb | $238K/day | Baseline |
| 13 Feb to 14 Mar | $258K/day | +8% (Q1 push) |
| 15 Mar to 13 Apr | $246K/day | -5% (post-Q1 normalisation) |

The card surfaces three observations:

1. **Q1-end push visible in mid-March.** Sales reps closed deals before quarter-end, then sales paused as commission resets.
2. **Single-day spikes match B2B billing-cycle batch runs.** Some merchants run weekly batch invoicing (Mondays) which creates spike days.
3. **Sundays show zero or negligible revenue.** Confirms B2B-heavy mix; DTC-heavy mixes have flatter Sundays.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Why are some days zero?**
Weekend posting is rare in B2B. Public holidays show zero. The trendline stays in $/day so weekend zeros do not skew the rolling average.

**Daily vs weekly aggregation?**
Card defaults to daily. Configurable to weekly for low-volume Companies where daily noise dominates.

**FX impact on the trend?**
Multi-Company tenants: the FX rate moves day-to-day. A purely-FX-driven trend can appear without underlying revenue change. Toggle to view in single-currency Local for noise-free trend.

**Power BI parity?**
Power BI's Revenue Over Time tile shows the same trend per Company. Multi-Company aggregation needs custom DAX.

**Sales Credit Memos cause negative days?**
Yes if a single-day SCM exceeds Invoice posting that day. The trend can dip below zero on isolated days; smooths over the rolling average.
