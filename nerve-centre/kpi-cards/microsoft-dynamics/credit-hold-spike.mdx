## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Threshold tuning?**
2σ default. Tighter = 1σ. Looser = 3σ.

**Seasonal noise?**
The 90D baseline normalises seasonal patterns.
