## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**B2B vs DTC classification?**
Customer Posting Group field. Configure if your tenant uses different segmentation.

**Parent-Child grouping?**
Card uses Customer No. Parent-Child (Sub-Buyers) requires custom configuration via the Sub-Buyer field.

**Concentration risk threshold?**
Top 1 > 25% = high; review Treasury credit facility coverage.
