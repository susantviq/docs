## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Cancel Posted Invoice creates a SCM?**
Yes, BC's Cancel Posted Invoice generates a corrective SCM that nets the original to zero.

**Goodwill credits?**
Counted (any SCM, regardless of cause).

**Healthy SCM rate?**
2 to 6% of Posted Sales Invoice value for B2B; 8 to 18% for DTC.
