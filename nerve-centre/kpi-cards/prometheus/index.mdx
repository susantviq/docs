Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **18** KPI pulses for Prometheus stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Prometheus connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to teams running the open-source time-series monitoring system that's become the Kubernetes-native default for container metrics and alerting. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 18 KPI pulses covering *Error Rate*, *p95 / p99 Latency*, *Apdex Score*, *MTTA / MTTR*, *Incidents Open*, *Services Healthy / Degraded / Down*, *SLA Compliance*, and *Top Error Types* - so PromQL alerts flow into the same picture as conversion and order metrics. **Vortex Mind** investigates when alertmanager firing patterns shift; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the alert.
