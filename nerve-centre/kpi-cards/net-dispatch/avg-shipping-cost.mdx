## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> Average postage cost per Net Dispatch-routed parcel in GBP, post-discount and including standard surcharges. Reflects UK multi-carrier rate-shopping outcome: Net Dispatch's pooled rates plus per-shipment optimisation across Royal Mail, DPD, Evri, Yodel, ParcelForce. Typically 5 to 12% lower than direct-to-carrier UK average for similar volume.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it counts** | `SUM(label cost in GBP) / COUNT(shipments)` over the trailing 30 days. |
| **Cost components included** | Carrier base rate + fuel surcharge + carrier-specific accessorials (residential, remote-area, oversize) + Net Dispatch per-label fee. |
| **Discount handling** | Net Dispatch negotiates pooled rates with UK carriers; merchants typically pay 5-12% less than direct-to-carrier published rates. |
| **Service level scope** | All Net Dispatch-booked services pooled. |
| **Carrier-mix effect** | Strongest cost driver. Heavy Evri/Yodel use lowers the average dramatically (£1.50-£2.50/parcel) but hurts on-time. Heavy DPD use raises average (£3.50-£5.00/parcel) but lifts on-time. |
| **Annual rate increase** | Most UK carriers raise rates once-yearly, typically January-February (3-7% per cycle). Some (Royal Mail) have additional mid-year adjustments. |
| **Q4 surcharges** | UK carriers add Q4 peak surcharges October-January (£0.20-£1.50/parcel for high-volume); the card includes them. |
| **Currency** | GBP for UK domestic. International shipments may convert at booking-time FX. |
| **Time window** | `30D vsP` |
| **Alert trigger** | `+10% vsP` |
| **Roles** | owner, finance, operations |

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
A UK DTC fashion merchant via Net Dispatch. Reading taken at 09:00 GMT on 12 Mar 26 for the trailing 30 days vs the prior 30 days.

| Underlying Carrier | Shipments | Avg billed (Net Dispatch GBP) | Equivalent direct estimate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Royal Mail Tracked 48 | 5,820 | £3.10 | £3.40 (-9%) |
| Royal Mail Tracked 24 | 2,180 | £4.20 | £4.65 (-10%) |
| DPD Next Day | 1,420 | £4.40 | £4.90 (-10%) |
| Evri 3-5 Day | 3,940 | £1.95 | £2.20 (-11%) |
| Yodel Standard | 880 | £2.40 | £2.75 (-13%) |
| FedEx UK Express (international) | 220 | £14.80 | £16.20 (-9%) |
| **All carriers (this card)** | **14,460** | **£3.05** | **£3.40 (-10.3%)** |

The card reads **£3.05 (30D)** vs **£2.92 (prior 30D)**, +4.5% increase. Alert at +10% has not tripped. Five things to notice:

1. **Net Dispatch is saving the merchant ~£0.35/parcel vs direct.** At 14,460 monthly shipments, ~£5,060/month or £61K/year saving. This is the platform's value proposition.
2. **Evri at £1.95/parcel is the cheapest option but drags on-time.** The cost-vs-OTD trade is most acute on Evri/Yodel; merchants weighing the trade should look at this card alongside [On-Time Delivery Rate](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/net-dispatch/on-time-delivery-rate).
3. **The +4.5% climb is mostly January 2026 carrier rate increases.** Royal Mail published a 6.4% increase effective 1 Apr 26 (will affect April readings); DPD applied a 5.1% increase in January. Non-negotiable.
4. **No major service-mix shift.** Volume mix between carriers is similar to prior 30D; cost climb is rate-driven not mix-driven.
5. **Compare against on-time card.** Cost climb of 4.5% paired with on-time stable at 94.0% = "I'm paying 4-5% more for the same service quality". Acceptable; the trade flips negative if on-time degrades while cost climbs.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Net Dispatch saving vs direct-to-carrier UK?**
Typical 5-12% saving on rate-shopped volume. Driven by Net Dispatch's pooled rates with Royal Mail, DPD, Evri, Yodel. Trade-off: large enterprise UK shippers (£3M+/year) may negotiate stronger direct contracts.

**My cost climbed 10%, alert tripped. What happened?**
Three diagnostic steps. (1) [Cost Per Shipment Trend](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/net-dispatch/cost-per-shipment-trend) for shape. (2) [Shipments by Service](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/net-dispatch/shipments-by-service) for carrier-mix. (3) [Cost by Zone](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/net-dispatch/cost-by-zone) for regional shifts (Highland/Islands surcharges).

**Highlands and Islands surcharges?**
Most UK carriers add £3-£12/parcel surcharge to deliveries to Scottish Highlands & Islands, Northern Ireland (some), Channel Islands, Isle of Man. Net Dispatch passes the surcharge through; your average climbs if customer-mix shifts toward those regions.

**Should I configure Net Dispatch to use cheapest-only rate-shopping?**
For UK DTC under £2M/year shipping, usually yes ("cheapest meeting promise"). Trade-off: cheapest often selects Evri/Yodel which have weaker on-time. Configure rate-rules to prefer reliability for high-AOV orders.

**Q4 cost spike?**
UK carriers add Q4 peak surcharges October-January; expect 5-12% climb above steady-state. Combined with January rate increases at year-end, plan budgets for +10-+18% combined cost November-February.

**VAT treatment?**
UK merchant accounts typically receive ex-VAT pricing on shipping (VAT-recoverable for VAT-registered businesses). Card displays whichever the connector receives in API responses. Verify config via Net Dispatch CSM if your reconciliation looks off.

**Brexit cost change for UK→EU?**
Pre-Brexit, UK→EU was domestic-equivalent pricing. Post-Brexit, UK→EU shipments are international with customs declarations and (often) DDP duty handling. Cost climbed 30-60% on EU lanes; merchants who set up EU-domiciled subsidiaries (Netherlands) shipping from there avoid the international leg.

**Why is OnTrac/Lasership not in my UK Net Dispatch?**
OnTrac, Lasership, LSO are US regional carriers. UK regional carriers integrated into Net Dispatch typically include: Evri, Yodel, ParcelForce, DX, Tuffnells (for larger items). Net Dispatch's UK carrier portfolio differs from EasyPost's US portfolio.
