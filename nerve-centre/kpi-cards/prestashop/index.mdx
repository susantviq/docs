Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **68** KPI pulses for PrestaShop stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, the exact PrestaShop Webservice resource and field, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The PrestaShop connector brings European SMB and mid-market merchants the same executive view that Shopify and BigCommerce stores rely on. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 68 KPI pulses - the full order-flow surface (payment-error rate, unfulfilled backlog, fulfilment rate, refund and cancellation mix), inventory and margin health (out-of-stock, low stock, gross margin from `wholesale_price`), abandoned-cart recovery, and the cross-channel *Revenue at Risk* alerts that catch ad spend on broken funnels. **Vortex Mind** investigates when revenue or recovery dips; **Ask Viq** answers in plain English with citations back to the card. Sensitivity bands (good / warning / bad) are inherited from the ecommerce-platform model and tuned per card, so each pulse colours and alerts on the same thresholds used across the catalogue.

**13 hero cards** carry the headline KPIs and the real-time anomaly canaries. **4 cross-channel cards** light up when a PrestaShop store is paired with an ad, email or payment-gateway connector.

## What is PrestaShop?

PrestaShop is a French open-source ecommerce platform first released in 2007. It is PHP and MySQL based, has an active module marketplace called PrestaShop Addons, and powers a large share of independent online stores across France, Spain, Italy, Poland, and the wider EU. It runs in two flavours: self-hosted on a merchant's own infrastructure, or PrestaShop Cloud (Hosted), which bundles hosting, security updates, and support.

PrestaShop tends to be picked by SMB and mid-market merchants who want a feature-rich, locally adapted platform with strong multi-language, multi-currency, and EU-tax support, without the licence cost or operational weight of Adobe Commerce.

## Why Vortex IQ tracks PrestaShop

PrestaShop ships a serviceable back office with a built-in stats dashboard, but the executive view (revenue, refund rate, out-of-stock spike, churn risk, cross-channel revenue at risk) is not there out of the box. Larger PrestaShop merchants typically supplement with paid analytics modules, third-party BI tools, or custom SQL, all of which add cost and operational drag.

Vortex IQ Nerve Centre folds PrestaShop into the same KPI model used for Shopify, BigCommerce, and Adobe Commerce. The connector reads orders, order details, products, customers, stock and refunds through the PrestaShop Webservice API, then renders the same cards, alerts, and Ask Viq narratives the rest of the platform supports. PrestaShop stays the system of record. Vortex IQ is the always-on pulse layer.

## How the cards are sourced

Each KPI card documents the exact PrestaShop Webservice resource and field it reads (`orders`, `order_details`, `order_states`, `products`, `stock_availables`, `customers`, `carts`, `specific_prices`, `order_carriers`), the calculation, why it matters, and the playbook for when sentiment shifts. Order classification uses the PrestaShop order-state machine (paid states 2, 3, 4, 5, 9, 11; shipped 4, 5; refunded 7, 15; cancelled 6; payment error 8). A nightly sync snapshots every card into the KPI store so the dashboard loads instantly, and Vortex Mind audits run against the same snapshots.

PrestaShop 1.7 and 8.x are the initial focus, covering both self-hosted and PrestaShop Cloud (Hosted) instances. Older 1.6 instances are supported on request.
