The Bonanza connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to merchants selling on Bonanza - the long-tail US marketplace popular with crafters, vintage sellers, and indie merchants alongside eBay and Etsy. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 17 KPI pulses including listings, sales, reputation, returns, and marketplace economics. **Vortex Mind** investigates when a number moves; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
