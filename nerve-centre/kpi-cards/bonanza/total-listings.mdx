## Calculation

Vortex IQ counts every item listing in the booth regardless of status, summing active, inactive, and expired listings into one total, refreshed in real time. The figure rises as you add listings and falls when listings are deleted, but expiring or going inactive does not reduce it. It differs from Active Listings, which counts only the listings currently live and buyable. Because it is a structural count rather than a performance signal, there is no alert threshold. See the At a glance summary above and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
*A representative reading of **Total Listings** for a typical merchant on Bonanza.* On 14 Mar 26 a booth showed 1,400 total listings while Active Listings read 1,050, leaving roughly 350 inactive or expired. The interpretation was that about a quarter of the catalogue was not buyable, a mix of seasonal items and listings that had quietly expired. The action was to review the inactive set, relist the lines still worth carrying, and retire the rest to keep the catalogue clean. ## Reconciling against Bonanza

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Bonanza's own dashboard:**

Open your booth dashboard and review the selling and items area with the status filter cleared, so active, inactive, and expired listings all show. That all-status count is what Vortex IQ reports as Total Listings.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Total Listings update?**
It recalculates on the standard data refresh, typically every 30 to 60 minutes, and tracks the catalogue in real time. Force a manual refresh from the dashboard if you need the latest count immediately.

**Q: Why does my Bonanza dashboard show a different number?**
The most common reason is filter scope: if your Bonanza view is filtered to active listings only, it will read lower than Total Listings, which includes inactive and expired. Time-zone alignment and period boundaries can also nudge the figure.

**Q: How is this different from Active Listings?**
Total Listings counts every listing regardless of status, while Active Listings counts only those currently live and buyable. The gap between them is your inactive and expired inventory, which is worth reviewing periodically.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
