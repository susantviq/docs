## Calculation

Each Bonanza order in the window is attributed to the buyer's shipping state, then summed per state. The result is a per-state count rendered as a choropleth, with intensity scaled to the order volume in each state. See At a glance for the window and the worked example below for a representative reading.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Orders by State update?**
It follows the trailing 30-day window and refreshes on the standard data refresh, typically every 30 to 60 minutes. You can also trigger a manual refresh to pull the latest order records.

**Q: Why does my Bonanza dashboard show a different number?**
Most differences come from period boundaries, time zone alignment, or profile-level filters such as booth or test-order exclusions. Match the window and filters and the totals should line up.

**Q: How are orders with no state or international buyers handled?**
Orders without a recognised US state, including international buyers, are not attributed to a state and so do not appear on the US choropleth, which can make the map total slightly lower than your overall order count.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
