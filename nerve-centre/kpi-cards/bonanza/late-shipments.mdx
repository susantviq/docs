## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

It lists every order that has passed its ship-by deadline without a tracked dispatch, so a slipping order surfaces the moment it goes overdue rather than after a buyer complains. It sits in the Nerve Centre alert layer and pairs with the on-time and pending-shipment cards below to give operations a full dispatch picture.

## Calculation

This is a real-time alert rather than a rolling metric. Vortex IQ compares each open Bonanza order's ship-by deadline against the current time and lists any order that has passed its deadline without a recorded dispatch. The list refreshes as orders are shipped or new ones go overdue. See the At a glance summary above for the trigger condition and the worked example below for a typical fire.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
*A representative reading of **Late Shipments** for a typical merchant on Bonanza.* On 14 Mar 26 a booth that normally clears dispatch by mid-morning shows 3 orders on the Late Shipments alert after a carrier pickup was missed the previous afternoon. The merchant opens the list, sees all three are same-warehouse items, prints labels and dispatches them by noon, clearing the alert and protecting the booth rating. Left unattended, those three orders would likely have triggered buyer messages and a dent in on-time shipping rate. ## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Late Shipments update?**
This is a real-time alert. Vortex IQ re-checks ship-by deadlines on each data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations) and lists newly overdue orders immediately. Force a manual refresh from the dashboard for an instant re-check after you dispatch.

**Q: Why does my Bonanza dashboard show a different number?**
The most common reasons are time-zone alignment (an order may be "due today" in one zone and overdue in another), tracking-upload lag (an order you shipped may still show as overdue until tracking syncs), and filter scope. Match these settings before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: An order shows here but I already shipped it - why?**
Bonanza marks an order dispatched once tracking is attached and synced. If you shipped without uploading tracking, or tracking is still propagating, the order stays on the list until the dispatch record reaches Vortex IQ on the next refresh.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
