## Calculation

Vortex IQ combines several booth-standing signals into a weighted index scaled from 0 to 100. The inputs include listing quality as captured by the BRA score, fulfilment performance such as on-time shipping, booth rating, and Importer sync health, evaluated on a real-time basis with a 7-day view for trend. A high score means the component signals are broadly healthy; a low score means one or more are dragging. See the At a glance summary above for the 70-point line and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Reconciling against Bonanza

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Bonanza's own dashboard:**

Bonanza does not publish a single equivalent score, so reconcile against its components: the booth dashboard for rating and standing, selling and items for listing quality and BRA, and the Importer status area for sync health. Together those underpin the composite.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Bonanza Booth Health Score update?**
It is computed in real time with a 7-day view for trend and recomputes on the standard data refresh, typically every 30 to 60 minutes. Force a manual refresh from the dashboard if you need it recalculated immediately.

**Q: Why does my Bonanza dashboard show a different number?**
Bonanza shows no single equivalent, so differences come from period-boundary choices (Vortex IQ blends real-time and 7-day views), time-zone alignment, and filter scope (profile-level versus Bonanza's all-booth view). Reconcile against the components instead of one figure.

**Q: My score dropped but each metric still looks okay. Why?**
The composite is weighted, so a single weak signal, such as a sync gap, can pull the score below 70 even when other metrics sit just inside their own thresholds. Drill into the component siblings to find the laggard.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, alert thresholds are configurable per profile in the Alert Rules tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
