## At a glance

## Calculation

Vortex IQ sums the Bonanza fees applied to your sales over the window and divides by gross revenue for the same period, then expresses the result as a percentage. Because Bonanza's headline FOV sits around 3.5% on Bonanza-only sales, a reading close to that is expected for a booth selling purely through Bonanza. Advertising and promotional placements add cost on top, lifting the effective percentage. See the At a glance summary above for the baseline and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Bonanza Fee % of Revenue update?**
It recalculates on the standard data refresh, typically every 30 to 60 minutes, across the rolling 30-day window versus prior period. Force a manual refresh from the dashboard if you need the latest figure immediately.

**Q: Why does my Bonanza dashboard show a different number?**
The usual reasons are period-boundary differences (Vortex IQ uses a 30-day rolling window versus Bonanza's calendar months), time-zone alignment, and filter scope (profile-level versus Bonanza's all-booth view). Match these before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: Why is my fee percentage above the 3.5% FOV baseline?**
The ~3.5% Final Offer Value applies to Bonanza-only sales. Opting into Bonanza's advertising or promotion program adds cost on top, so the effective percentage rises. Review which placements are active if the gauge climbs.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
