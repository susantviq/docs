## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Importer Sync / BRA / Rating Threshold Crossed update?**
This is a real-time alert. Vortex IQ re-checks the three health signals on each data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations) and fires the instant a threshold is crossed. Force a manual refresh from the dashboard for an immediate re-check.

**Q: Why does my Bonanza dashboard show a different number?**
The most common reasons are snapshot timing (Bonanza may show a last-computed value while Vortex IQ tracks the live state), time-zone alignment, and filter scope. Bonanza also recomputes BRA and rating on its own cadence, so a brief lag between the two is normal.

**Q: Which of the three drivers should I act on first?**
Open the alert to see the named driver. A low Importer sync usually has the fastest fix (re-run the mirror), a low BRA points to listing-quality gaps, and a low booth rating points to fulfilment or service issues that take longer to recover.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
