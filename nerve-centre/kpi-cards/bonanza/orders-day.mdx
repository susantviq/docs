## Calculation

Every Bonanza order is bucketed to its order date, then counted per day across the window to form a daily series. The chart plots one point per day so the trend and any spikes are visible. See At a glance for the window and the worked example below for a representative reading.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Orders / Day update?**
It follows the trailing 30-day window and refreshes on the standard data refresh, typically every 30 to 60 minutes. The current day is partial until it closes, so the final point will keep rising through the day. You can also trigger a manual refresh.

**Q: Why does my Bonanza dashboard show a different number?**
Differences usually come from period boundaries, time zone alignment, or profile-level filters such as booth or test-order exclusions. A day that straddles your time zone boundary can shift an order from one bucket to the next.

**Q: Why does the last day on the chart look low?**
The most recent day is still in progress, so its count reflects only the orders placed so far. It is not a true daily total until that day closes.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
