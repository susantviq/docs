## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **On-Time Shipping Rate** tracks the share of orders you ship by their ship-by deadline. On Bonanza, dispatching on time protects your booth standing and the buyer trust that drives repeat sales and good feedback. A reading near 100% is the goal; below 95% starts to put both standing and reputation at risk. It is one of the clearest operational signals of whether fulfilment is keeping pace with orders.

## Calculation

Vortex IQ counts orders shipped on or before their ship-by deadline over the window and divides by the total orders due to ship in that period, expressed as a percentage. Orders dispatched after the deadline lower the rate. The comparison runs against the prior period so you can see whether fulfilment is improving or slipping. See the At a glance summary above for the 95% line and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Reconciling against Bonanza

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Bonanza's own dashboard:**

Open your booth dashboard and review the selling and orders area, where Bonanza shows each order's ship-by date and the date it was marked shipped. Comparing those dates over the period reconstructs the on-time rate.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does On-Time Shipping Rate update?**
It recalculates on the standard data refresh, typically every 30 to 60 minutes, across the rolling 30-day window versus prior period. Force a manual refresh from the dashboard if you need the latest figure immediately.

**Q: Why does my Bonanza dashboard show a different number?**
The usual reasons are period-boundary differences (Vortex IQ uses a 30-day rolling window versus Bonanza's calendar months), time-zone alignment, and filter scope (profile-level versus Bonanza's all-booth view). Match these before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: An order shows shipped but still counts as late. Why?**
On-time status compares the shipped date against the order's ship-by deadline, so an order dispatched after its deadline counts as late even though it is now shipped. Pull the pending-shipment queue forward to stay ahead of deadlines.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, alert thresholds are configurable per profile in the Alert Rules tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
