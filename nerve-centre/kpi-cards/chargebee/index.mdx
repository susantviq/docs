Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **16** KPI pulses for Chargebee stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Chargebee connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to subscription brands using Chargebee for recurring billing, dunning, and revenue operations across the US, UK, EU, and APAC. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 16 KPI pulses focused on the payment-flow side of subscription health — *Success Rate*, *Decline Rate*, *3DS Abandonment Rate*, *Refund Rate*, *Chargeback Rate*, *Dispute Rate*, *Avg Settlement Time*, *Pending Payouts*, *Top Decline Reasons*, and *Top Payment Methods* — so failed renewals don't quietly bleed MRR. **Vortex Mind** investigates when decline-reason mix shifts or settlement timing slips; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the source transaction.
