Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **18** KPI pulses for Pingdom stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Pingdom connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to teams using SolarWinds Pingdom for synthetic uptime monitoring, page-speed checks, and real-user monitoring across global probe locations. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 18 KPI pulses covering *Alerts Firing*, *MTTA / MTTR*, *Incidents Open*, *Incidents Resolved (24h)*, *Services Healthy / Degraded / Down*, *p95 / p99 Latency*, *Apdex Score*, and *SLA Compliance* — so a probe failing in São Paulo surfaces alongside the conversion drop it caused. **Vortex Mind** investigates when uptime drift correlates with commerce metrics; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the check.
