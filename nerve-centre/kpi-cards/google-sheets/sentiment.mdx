[KPI Cards](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards) / [Google Sheets](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/google-sheets) / **Sentiment**

Cards carrying a sensitivity band flip to Action Needed when the live value crosses the bad threshold. Bands are shared across connectors on the same archetype and tunable per store in Alert Rules.

| Card | Direction | Good | Bad |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Hours Since Last Edit](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/google-sheets/hours-since-last-edit) | Lower is better | 24 | 168 |
| [Error-Cell Rate %](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/google-sheets/error-cell-rate) | Lower is better | 0 | 1 |
| [Blank-Row Rate %](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/google-sheets/blank-row-rate) | Lower is better | 2 | 10 |
| [Source Sheet Not Updated in >7 Days](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/google-sheets/source-sheet-not-updated-in-7-days) | Lower is better | 24 | 168 |
