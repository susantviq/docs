[KPI Cards](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards) / **Google Sheets** · [Sentiment](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/google-sheets/sentiment)

Vortex IQ watches a source-of-truth Google Sheet for freshness and integrity: hours since last edit, error-cell rate, blank-row rate, schema drift, and volume. Cards derive from the Sheets API v4 (metadata and values) plus Drive metadata for freshness. The connector authenticates via a merchant OAuth connection or a service account, and never writes to the sheet.
