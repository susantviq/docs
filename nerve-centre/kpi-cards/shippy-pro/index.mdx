Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **24** KPI pulses for ShippyPro stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The ShippyPro connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to merchants using ShippyPro's European multi-carrier platform to ship across 170+ couriers with rate-shopping, returns, and post-purchase tracking. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 24 KPI pulses with hero alerts on *Avg Shipping Cost*, *On-Time Delivery Rate*, *Label-Generation Success Rate*, *Late Shipments*, *Exception Rate*, *Open Claims*, *Days to Token Expiry*, plus a cross-channel *ShippyPro OTD by Sales Channel* card so you can see whether marketplace orders ship slower than D2C. **Vortex Mind** investigates when the rate-shop winner mix shifts or label-gen success dips; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the source label.
