## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Fruugo Merchant Health Score** is a composite 0-100 index of your account standing, blending on-time shipping, cancellation, feed ingestion, VAT compliance, and review signals into one number. Because Fruugo can throttle distribution or suspend selling across its country marketplaces when performance slips, this single score is the earliest warning that your standing across the platform is at risk. Sitting in the Executive Command Centre, it is the headline owners, finance, and operations watch, with a configurable floor at 70 below which account standing is in jeopardy.

## Calculation

The card blends several account-health inputs, on-time shipping, cancellation rate, feed ingestion success, VAT compliance, and review performance, into a single weighted 0-100 score, evaluated in real time over a recent 7-day basis so a single bad day does not whipsaw the result. Each input contributes proportionally, so the score moves toward whichever signal is weakest at the time.

## Reconciling against Fruugo Merchant Portal

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Fruugo's own dashboard:** Open the Fruugo Merchant Portal and review the account performance or seller health area, where Fruugo reports its own view of shipping, cancellation, and compliance metrics. This is the authoritative source for the inputs, even though Fruugo does not necessarily publish a single blended score identical to this one.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Composite weighting: Vortex IQ blends inputs into one 0-100 score, the portal may report each metric separately without a single index | Either | Compare the underlying inputs rather than expecting the portal to show an identical headline number. |
| Smoothing window: the score uses a recent 7-day basis, the portal may use a different lookback for some metrics | Either | Align the lookback per metric before judging whether an input genuinely differs. |
| One input recovered after the last feed import but the score has not fully re-evaluated yet | Vortex IQ lower | Re-check after the next import cycle; lagging inputs settle once the underlying signal refreshes. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** Because this card is a composite, reconcile it against its inputs: [`fru_on_time_ship_rate`](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/fruugo/on-time-shipping-rate), [`fru_cancellation_rate`](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/fruugo/cancellation-rate), and [`fru_feed_ingestion_health`](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/fruugo/feed-ingestion-success-last-24h), then let Vortex Mind attribute the move to whichever signal shifted.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does this number update?**
It updates in real time as its inputs change, smoothed over a recent 7-day basis so a single off day does not swing it. Inputs tied to listings and feeds only move when Fruugo's twice-daily import re-evaluates your catalogue.

**Q: Why does the Fruugo Merchant Portal show different performance figures?**
The portal typically reports each performance metric separately rather than as one blended index, and it may use a different lookback for some metrics. Compare the individual inputs rather than expecting an identical headline score.

**Q: How does this relate to Revenue at Risk and the input cards?**
This card is the composite health view, while [`fru_revenue_at_risk`](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/fruugo/revenue-at-risk) puts a money figure on the same kinds of problems, and the input cards such as [`fru_on_time_ship_rate`](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/fruugo/on-time-shipping-rate) show each driver in isolation. They tend to move together when standing slips.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes. The default fires below 70, the band where Fruugo standing is commonly at risk, but the threshold is configurable. Cautious merchants raise it to alert earlier, giving operations more runway to fix an input before the score reaches a level Fruugo acts on.
