## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Listings Drifting from BC** is a cross-platform surface that catches SKUs where the live Fruugo listing has diverged from the BigCommerce source of truth on price, title, image, or stock. BigCommerce is where your catalogue is authored; Fruugo is where it sells across 40+ countries, with Feedonomics in between, so drift means the two no longer agree. Each drifted SKU risks oversell, mispricing, or buyer complaints in markets you cannot watch by hand. It alerts when more than 10 SKUs are out of sync, the configurable default.

## Calculation

The card matches each Fruugo listing to its BigCommerce source SKU and compares the key fields - price, title, image, and stock - between the two. Any SKU where the live Fruugo value no longer matches the current BC record is counted as drifted, with the specific field and both values recorded. The comparison runs across the trailing 30 days so transient mid-feed states settle before a SKU is flagged.

## Reconciling against Fruugo Merchant Portal

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Fruugo's own dashboard:** The Fruugo Merchant Portal shows the live Fruugo side of each listing - the price, title, image, and stock currently published per locale. The canonical comparison, though, is multi-source: this card reads the Fruugo listing against the BigCommerce source of truth and the Feedonomics feed that connects them, so the Portal alone cannot show drift on its own.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A BC change is mid-flight through Feedonomics and not yet on Fruugo | Vortex IQ higher | Wait for the next twice-daily import, then re-check before treating it as drift. |
| Fruugo deliberately localises a title or image per country | Vortex IQ higher | Mark intentional localisation as expected so it is excluded from drift. |
| BC record updated after the last feed snapshot was taken | Either | Re-run the comparison once the feed reflects the newest BC state. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** This is a multi-source check by design - Fruugo versus BC versus Feedonomics. Pair it with `fru_xc_listed_but_oos_on_bc` and `fru_xc_feed_rejection_vs_listings` to separate stock drift from feed faults, and let Vortex Mind pinpoint whether the divergence began in BC, in Feedonomics, or on Fruugo.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does this card update?**
It re-evaluates after each twice-daily Fruugo import and as BigCommerce records change, comparing across a trailing 30-day window.

**Q: Why might the Fruugo Merchant Portal show a different picture?**
The Portal only shows the live Fruugo side. Drift is only visible when Fruugo is compared against the BigCommerce source and the Feedonomics feed, which the Portal does not surface.

**Q: How does this relate to the sibling cards?**
This card catches any field drift; `fru_xc_listed_but_oos_on_bc` isolates the stock case, `fru_xc_price_parity` compares price against rival marketplaces, and `fru_revenue_at_risk` quantifies the financial exposure.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes. The default fires above 10 drifted SKUs, but you can tune the count and choose which fields - price, title, image, stock - count as drift for your catalogue.
