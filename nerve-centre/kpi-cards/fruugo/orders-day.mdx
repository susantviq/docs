## Calculation

The card counts orders placed on each calendar day across all your active Fruugo country marketplaces and plots them as a daily line over the rolling window. Orders are attributed to the day they were placed, so the trend reflects demand timing rather than dispatch or settlement timing.

## Reconciling against Fruugo Merchant Portal

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Fruugo's own dashboard:** In the Fruugo Merchant Portal, open the orders or sales reporting area and view the daily order count for the same date range. The portal's day-by-day order list is the authoritative source for this trend.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day boundaries differ by time zone, shifting orders placed near midnight into a neighbouring day | Either | Compare both views in the same time zone, especially for spike days. |
| This card attributes orders to placement day, while a portal report may group by paid or confirmed day | Either | Confirm which event the portal report is dating orders by before matching day totals. |
| Cancellations removing an order from the count retroactively | Vortex IQ lower | Re-check after cancellations settle; a recent day can drop slightly as orders cancel. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** Read this with [`fru_pending_ship`](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/fruugo/pending-shipment) and [`fru_on_time_ship_rate`](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/fruugo/on-time-shipping-rate) so a demand spike does not quietly become an SLA miss, and let Vortex Mind attribute the movement to specific countries or products.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does this number update?**
The trend extends as new orders arrive, with the most recent day filling in through the trading day, so today's point keeps rising until the day closes.

**Q: Why does the Fruugo Merchant Portal show different daily totals?**
The usual culprits are time-zone day boundaries and which order event the day is dated by - placement versus paid or confirmed - plus cancellations that remove an order from a day after the fact. Align time zone and dating basis before comparing.

**Q: How does this relate to the Orders card and Pending Shipment?**
[`fru_order_count`](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/fruugo/orders) gives the window total while this card shows its daily shape, and [`fru_pending_ship`](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/fruugo/pending-shipment) tells you whether a rising line is outrunning your dispatch capacity.

**Q: Can I set an alert on this card?**
It ships without a default alert, but the threshold is configurable. A common setup is alerting when a day exceeds a multiple of the recent average, giving early warning of a demand surge that will strain fulfilment.
