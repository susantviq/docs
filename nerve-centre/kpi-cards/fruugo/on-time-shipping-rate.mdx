## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **On-Time Shipping Rate** is the share of orders you dispatched on or before Fruugo's ship-by deadline over the window. It is one of the most heavily weighted account-health metrics Fruugo tracks, so falling below the default 95% threshold risks penalties and a weakened merchant standing. For a cross-border seller juggling orders across many countries, this Fulfilment card is the single clearest read on whether your dispatch operation is keeping pace.

## Calculation

The card counts orders that were marked dispatched on or before their Fruugo ship-by deadline, divides that by the total orders due to ship in the window, and expresses it as a percentage compared against the prior equivalent period. An order dispatched after its deadline counts against the rate even if it eventually shipped.

## Reconciling against Fruugo Merchant Portal

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Fruugo's own dashboard:** In the Fruugo Merchant Portal, open the account-health or performance section, where Fruugo reports your dispatch performance against ship-by deadlines, and the orders area where each order's dispatch and deadline timestamps are shown. The account-health figure is the one that governs penalties.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Different 30-day boundary than Fruugo's account-health window | Either | Align date ranges; Fruugo's performance window may not be a clean trailing 30 days. |
| Ship-by deadline interpreted at order time zone versus your local time zone | Either | Confirm which time zone the deadline is measured in before treating a borderline miss as a real one. |
| An order dispatched and then later marked late after a carrier scan correction | Vortex IQ higher temporarily | Re-check after carrier data settles; late corrections can move an order from on-time to late. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** Pair this with [`fru_alert_dispatch_sla_miss`](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/fruugo/late-shipments) and [`fru_pending_ship`](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/fruugo/pending-shipment) to see backlog turning into misses, and let Vortex Mind isolate the routes or SKUs dragging the rate down.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does this number update?**
It recalculates as orders are dispatched and as deadlines pass, against a rolling 30-day window versus the prior period, so even a no-order day can shift it slightly as days roll off the window.

**Q: Why does the Fruugo Merchant Portal show a different on-time rate?**
Fruugo's account-health window may differ from a clean trailing 30 days, and time-zone interpretation of the ship-by deadline or a late carrier-scan correction can move borderline orders. Align the window and time zone before assuming an error.

**Q: How does this relate to Late Shipments and the Health Score?**
[`fru_alert_dispatch_sla_miss`](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/fruugo/late-shipments) counts the individual misses that pull this rate down, and [`fru_marketplace_health_score`](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/fruugo/fruugo-merchant-health-score) is the broader score this rate feeds into. Reading all three together separates a one-off backlog from a structural problem.

**Q: Can I change the 95% alert threshold?**
Yes. 95% is the default and reflects a common Fruugo expectation, but it is configurable. Merchants who want a safety margin above Fruugo's own trigger point often set the alert a little higher so they act before the marketplace does.
