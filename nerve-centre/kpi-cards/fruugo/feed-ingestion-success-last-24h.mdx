## At a glance

A reading below 95% signals a schema or VAT-metadata regression that is silently dropping listings before they ever reach buyers. ## Calculation

The gauge divides the number of feed lines Fruugo accepted by the total lines submitted across the twice-daily imports in the trailing 24 hours, expressed as a percentage. Rejected and skipped lines, whether from schema faults, missing VAT metadata, or policy breaches, count against the success rate. Because Fruugo imports the same feed for all locales, a single recurring fault can pull the whole percentage down.

## Reconciling against Fruugo Merchant Portal

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Fruugo's own dashboard:** Open the Fruugo Merchant Portal and review the feed import history for the latest twice-daily ingestion. The accepted and rejected line counts there are the raw inputs to this gauge. The Portal reports per-import; Vortex IQ rolls the last 24 hours into a single percentage.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Portal shows a single import; this gauge blends both imports in 24 hours | Either | Average the Portal's two latest imports before comparing. |
| Portal counts warnings differently from hard rejections | Vortex IQ lower | Confirm whether warning lines are treated as failures in your setup. |
| Latest import still processing when the Portal was viewed | Either | Re-check once the Portal marks the import complete. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** Read this gauge with `fru_xc_feed_rejection_vs_listings` to see whether failures start upstream at Feedonomics, and `fru_rejected_listings` to see which listings the failures removed from sale. Vortex Mind traces a dip back to the specific transform or field at fault.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does this card update?**
It refreshes after each of Fruugo's twice-daily imports, keeping a rolling 24-hour view that blends the two most recent ingestion results.

**Q: Why might the Fruugo Merchant Portal show a different picture?**
The Portal reports each import separately, while this gauge blends the last 24 hours into one figure. How warning lines versus hard rejections are counted can also shift the percentage.

**Q: How does this relate to the sibling cards?**
This gauge measures the pipe's overall health; `fru_xc_feed_rejection_vs_listings` shows whether failures begin upstream in Feedonomics, and `fru_rejected_listings` shows the listings removed as a result.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes. The default trips below 95%, but you can tighten or loosen it to reflect how much line-level churn is normal for your catalogue and feed.
