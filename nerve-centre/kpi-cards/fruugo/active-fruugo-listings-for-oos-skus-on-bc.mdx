## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Active Fruugo Listings for OOS SKUs on BC** is a cross-channel surface that catches listings still sellable on Fruugo whose SKU has gone out of stock on BigCommerce, your source of truth. When BC stock and the live Fruugo listing diverge, every order against those SKUs is a direct oversell that ends in a cancellation. Because Fruugo cancellations feed straight into your account health across 40+ markets, this is a live financial and standing risk. It alerts on any such SKU above zero, the configurable default.

## Calculation

The card matches each active Fruugo listing to its BigCommerce source SKU and flags any case where the BC record shows zero available stock while the Fruugo listing remains sellable. It reads the live state on both sides rather than a rolling average, because the risk is immediate: any order placed against such a SKU cannot be fulfilled. The gap usually opens when a BC stock drop has not yet propagated through Feedonomics into Fruugo's twice-daily import.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does this card update?**
It is a real-time surface, re-matching live Fruugo listings against BigCommerce stock continuously and after each twice-daily import.

**Q: Why might the Fruugo Merchant Portal show a different picture?**
The Portal shows only the Fruugo side, which is the side that is stale here. The oversell risk is only visible when the live Fruugo listing is compared against the BigCommerce stock position.

**Q: How does this relate to the sibling cards?**
This card is the oversell trip-wire; `fru_out_of_stock` shows Fruugo-side stock, `fru_cancellation_rate` shows the cancellations that follow unfulfillable orders, and `fru_revenue_at_risk` quantifies the exposure.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
The default fires on any SKU above zero. You can add a tolerance for intentional buffer stock or scope the match to specific catalogues if some BC stock states are expected to differ.
