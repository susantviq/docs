## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Pending Shipment** is the count of orders awaiting dispatch right now, across all your Fruugo country marketplaces. It is the live backlog gauge that tells you whether dispatch is keeping pace with demand. The default alert fires when it climbs above twice your 30-day average, which is the early warning that a fulfilment backlog is forming before it turns into late shipments and account-health damage.

## Calculation

The card counts orders that have been accepted but not yet marked as dispatched at the moment of the snapshot, across every active Fruugo country marketplace. The alert compares the live count against a rolling 30-day average of the same backlog, firing when the current queue exceeds twice that baseline.

## Reconciling against Fruugo Merchant Portal

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Fruugo's own dashboard:** In the Fruugo Merchant Portal, open the orders area and filter to orders awaiting dispatch or in a to-be-shipped state. That open-order queue is the authoritative count this card mirrors in real time.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Snapshot timing: an order dispatched seconds before or after the snapshot moves the count by one | Either | Refresh both views together; small live counts move constantly. |
| Vortex IQ counts every accepted-but-undispatched order, while a portal filter may exclude orders still pending payment confirmation | Vortex IQ higher | Match the order-status filter on both sides, including or excluding payment-pending orders. |
| An order cancelled while still in the queue leaves it on both sides at slightly different times | Either | Re-check after cancellations settle to confirm the queue figure. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** Read this with [`fru_on_time_ship_rate`](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/fruugo/on-time-shipping-rate) and [`fru_alert_dispatch_sla_miss`](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/fruugo/late-shipments) to see whether a backlog is already converting into misses, and let Vortex Mind tie the spike back to a demand surge or a capacity gap.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does this number update?**
It is a live snapshot of the dispatch queue, so it moves in near real time as new orders arrive and as you mark orders dispatched.

**Q: Why does the Fruugo Merchant Portal show a different pending count?**
Usually it is the order-status filter and snapshot timing: the portal view may exclude orders still pending payment confirmation that this card counts, and the live count shifts second by second as orders dispatch.

**Q: How does this relate to On-Time Shipping Rate and Late Shipments?**
Pending Shipment is the leading indicator: a backlog here precedes a drop in [`fru_on_time_ship_rate`](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/fruugo/on-time-shipping-rate) and a rise in [`fru_alert_dispatch_sla_miss`](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/fruugo/late-shipments). Clearing the queue early keeps those two healthy.

**Q: Can I change the 2x average alert threshold?**
Yes. The 2x-of-30-day-average default is configurable, both the multiple and the baseline window. Merchants with tight ship-by windows often lower the multiple so the alert fires on a smaller backlog.
