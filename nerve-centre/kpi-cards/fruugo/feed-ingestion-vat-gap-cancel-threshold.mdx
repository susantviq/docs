## Calculation

The card evaluates three independent conditions on every refresh and lists each that is currently in breach. Feed ingestion is the share of the most recent twice-daily import lines that Fruugo accepted; VAT gaps are listings lacking the per-country VAT registration, OSS threshold flags, or HS commodity codes Fruugo requires before distribution; cancellation rate is cancelled orders over total orders in the rolling window. A row appears whenever any one condition crosses its configurable default of feed below 95%, VAT gaps above 0, or cancellation above 3%.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does this card update?**
It is a real-time surface. The feed condition refreshes on each twice-daily ingestion result, while VAT-gap and cancellation conditions re-evaluate continuously as listings and orders change.

**Q: Why might the Fruugo Merchant Portal show a different picture?**
The Portal reports each signal in a separate area on its own cadence. Vortex IQ merges all three into one view and reflects the newest import, so it can flag a breach minutes before the Portal publishes a matching warning.

**Q: How does this relate to the sibling cards?**
This card is the composite trip-wire; the siblings are the detail. `fru_feed_ingestion_health`, `fru_vat_compliance_gaps`, and `fru_cancellation_rate` each isolate one of the three triggers so you can drill into the specific cause.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes. The default is feed below 95%, VAT gaps above 0, or cancellation above 3%, but each of the three conditions can be tuned independently to match your risk tolerance and Fruugo agreement.
