Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **17** KPI pulses for Fruugo stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Fruugo connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to merchants selling on Fruugo - the UK-based cross-border marketplace that lists products in 46 countries with localised currency and language handling. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 17 KPI pulses including listings, sales, reputation, returns, and marketplace economics. **Vortex Mind** investigates when a number moves; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
