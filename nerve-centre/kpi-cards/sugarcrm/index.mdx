The SugarCRM connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to teams on SugarCRM - long-established mid-market CRM popular with manufacturing, financial services, and B2B needing customisable workflows. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 29 KPI pulses including subscriber, deliverability, engagement, and revenue-attribution metrics. **Vortex Mind** investigates when a number moves; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
