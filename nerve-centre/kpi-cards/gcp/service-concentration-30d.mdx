## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it measures** | Top service 30d net_cost / total 30d net_cost x 100; how concentrated the bill is in one service. |
| **Source** | GCP Billing Export (BigQuery) (derived) |
| **Time window** | `30D` |
| **Alert trigger** | `-` |
| **Roles** | owner, finance |
| **Chart** | gauge |

> Requires the Standard billing export to BigQuery and a service account with BigQuery read access; without it this card shows no data.

## Calculation

```
Top service 30d net_cost / total 30d net_cost x 100; how concentrated the bill is in one service.
```

Computed by the Google Cloud Platform engine from daily cost buckets over a 60-day window (current 30 days plus the previous 30 for trend comparisons), refreshed on the standard sync. The engine degrades to no data rather than estimating.

### Tracked live in Vortex IQ Nerve Centre

Connect Google Cloud Platform in [Settings, Connectors](https://app.vortexiq.ai/v2/settings?tab=sources) and this card renders live alongside the rest of the connector's metrics.
