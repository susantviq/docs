## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it measures** | 0-100 composite over whatever is readable: token exchange ok (100) + pct of projects ACTIVE (/v1/projects) + pct of billing accounts open (/v1/billingAccounts) + spend-trend band from the billing export when configured. Null when nothing beyond the token is readable. |

## Calculation

```
0-100 composite over whatever is readable: token exchange ok (100) + pct of projects ACTIVE (/v1/projects) + pct of billing accounts open (/v1/billingAccounts) + spend-trend band from the billing export when configured. Null when nothing beyond the token is readable.
```

Computed by the Google Cloud Platform engine from daily cost buckets over a 60-day window (current 30 days plus the previous 30 for trend comparisons), refreshed on the standard sync. The engine degrades to no data rather than estimating.

### Tracked live in Vortex IQ Nerve Centre

Connect Google Cloud Platform in [Settings, Connectors](https://app.vortexiq.ai/v2/settings?tab=sources) and this card renders live alongside the rest of the connector's metrics.
