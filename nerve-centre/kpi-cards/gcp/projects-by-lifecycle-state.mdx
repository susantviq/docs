## Calculation

```
projects[] grouped by lifecycleState from GET /v1/projects.
```

Computed by the Google Cloud Platform engine from daily cost buckets over a 60-day window (current 30 days plus the previous 30 for trend comparisons), refreshed on the standard sync. The engine degrades to no data rather than estimating.

### Tracked live in Vortex IQ Nerve Centre

Connect Google Cloud Platform in [Settings, Connectors](https://app.vortexiq.ai/v2/settings?tab=sources) and this card renders live alongside the rest of the connector's metrics.
