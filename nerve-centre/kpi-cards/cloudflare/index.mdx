Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **14** KPI pulses for Cloudflare stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Cloudflare connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to merchants on Cloudflare's CDN, security, and edge-compute platform - but the Nerve Centre focuses on *front-end performance outcomes*, the part that affects ranking and conversion. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 14 KPI pulses on *Core Web Vitals* (*LCP*, *INP*, *CLS*, *TTFB*), *CWV Pass Rate*, *Lighthouse Performance / SEO / Accessibility / Best Practices*, *Total Page Weight*, *Speed Index*, *Total Blocking Time*, and *Render-Blocking Resources*. **Vortex Mind** investigates when a deployment regresses Lighthouse scores or CWV pass rate dips; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the page.
