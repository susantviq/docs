## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
"Helle Mode" Klarna daily volume, last 7 days ending 02 May 26.

| Date | Volume EUR | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mon 27 Apr 26 | 28,400 | Mid-week baseline |
| Tue 28 Apr 26 | 31,200 | Continued |
| Wed 29 Apr 26 | 29,800 | |
| Thu 30 Apr 26 | 47,600 | End-of-month payday cluster begins |
| Fri 01 May 26 | 52,800 | Payday peak |
| Sat 02 May 26 | 38,400 | Tailing |

What the merchant should notice:

1. **Thursday-Friday end-of-month spike of 60-80% over baseline.** German monthly-paid workers receive salary on the last business day; Pay-in-30-days purchases cluster post-payday. This is the signature Klarna pattern.
2. **Saturday and Sunday lower for Klarna than for cards.** Klarna is more "considered purchase" than impulse-card; weekend mobile-impulse buyers index higher on cards.
3. **Klarna Day campaigns (typically biannual) can spike a single day 3-5x baseline.** Helle Mode should track whether they participated.
4. **A 15% drop in 7-day rolling window triggers alert.** Common cause: Klarna placement in checkout was de-emphasised (a UX experiment moved Klarna below the fold), or competitive BNPL added (Afterpay or Klarna's biggest competitor in-market).

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Why does my Klarna trend lag my card-trend by 1-2 days?**
Klarna's authorisation can be days before capture (merchants typically capture on shipment). Card flow is auth-and-capture immediate. The Klarna trend therefore drifts later; for retailers shipping next day, the gap is 1 day.

**End-of-month spike, German pattern only?**
Strongest in DE/AT/NL where monthly payday is dominant. UK/US shows weaker spike (biweekly/semi-monthly payroll dominates).

**Klarna Day campaigns, when?**
Typically biannual (spring + autumn). Klarna communicates dates to participating merchants; brands not in the catalogue still see indirect lift.

**Single day at 5x baseline, what causes?**
(1) Klarna in-app feature promotion (your products surfaced to 150M+ users), (2) merchant-side flash sale, (3) influencer pushing Klarna-paid checkout. Cross-check with marketing calendar.

**Holiday volume zero, Klarna issue?**
Unlikely. Klarna operates 24/7. More commonly the merchant's commerce platform was down or paused.

**Year-over-year, Klarna penetration grows?**
Yes typically. Klarna market share in DE/UK/US grew 15-25% YoY through 2024-2025; Klarna share of merchant revenue typically rises with platform maturity.

**Klarna NYSE listing 2025, trend impact?**
None. Operations unchanged; trend reflects merchant-side and customer-side dynamics only.
