## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> Average days from Klarna order capture to merchant payout. Klarna pays merchants on configured schedule (typically daily, weekly, or biweekly).

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it counts** | `AVG(payout_date - capture_date)`. |
| **Standard schedule** | Daily payout: 1-2 days. Weekly: 3-7 days. Biweekly: 7-14 days. |
| **Time window** | `30D`. |
| **Roles** | owner, finance |
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Can I switch to daily payout?**
Yes; merchants can request schedule change in Klarna Merchant Portal. Daily payouts may have a small fee.

**Why slower than card processing?**
Klarna pays from its own balance after captures aggregate; the schedule is Klarna-controlled, not acquirer-controlled.

**Hold on payouts?**
Klarna may hold a reserve for new merchants or high-risk verticals; visible in Settlements view.
