## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> Klarna's chargeback equivalent: "buyer protection" claims raised by customers (item not received, not as described). Klarna handles the consumer-side investigation; merchant defends with evidence.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it counts** | `COUNT(buyer_protection_claims) / COUNT(captured)` per period. |
| **Healthy** | &lt;0.5%. Klarna's BNPL chargeback rate is generally lower than card chargebacks because Klarna controls customer relationship and resolves disputes pre-escalation. |
| **Sentiment key** | `gauge_inverse: good<=0.5, warn>0.9` |

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Klarna chargeback vs card chargeback?**
Klarna's "buyer protection" claim is similar in intent (customer disputes after sale) but the resolution flow is Klarna-managed, not Visa/Mastercard-managed. Generally faster resolution and lower fees.

**Klarna fees on chargeback?**
Per-case fee (typically EUR 5-15, lower than Visa/MC's USD 15-25). Lost case forfeits the funds.

**Win rate?**
70-85% with strong evidence (shipping proof, signed delivery, product photos).

**Customer didn't pay Klarna, my problem?**
No. Klarna assumes consumer credit risk; non-payment is Klarna's loss.
