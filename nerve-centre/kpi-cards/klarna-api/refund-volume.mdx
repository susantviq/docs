## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Refund volume larger than payout, how does Klarna handle?**
Klarna debits merchant's pending payout balance first. If insufficient, Klarna invoices the merchant for the gap.

**Partial refund supported?**
Yes; any amount up to original via API.

**Klarna processing fee on refund?**
Small fee (typically EUR 0.10 to EUR 0.30 per refund); the original merchant fee on the sale is generally not refunded.
