> Days since the oldest unpaid Klarna capture. Stuck-money signal.

| | |
| --- | --- |

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Oldest 14 days, problem?**
For weekly-payout merchants, 14 days is at the limit; investigate. For daily-payout, alarm.

**Reserve hold, looks like stuck payout?**
Klarna may hold reserve for new or high-risk merchants. Visible in Settlements as "reserved".
