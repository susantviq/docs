## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
"Helle Mode" 7-day Klarna decline breakdown.

| Reason | Count | Share | Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| INSUFFICIENT_DATA (thin file) | 58 | 35.6% | Customer too new to Klarna or no credit history. No fix; suggest card fallback. |
| EXISTING_DEBT (over-extended) | 41 | 25.2% | Customer has open Klarna balances elsewhere. No fix. |
| ADDRESS_VERIFICATION | 28 | 17.2% | Address mismatch. Improve checkout autofill / address validation. |
| FRAUD_FLAG | 18 | 11.0% | Klarna risk model flagged. No appeal; legitimate fraud-prevention. |
| MAX_AMOUNT_EXCEEDED | 13 | 8.0% | Customer exceeded Klarna purchase limit. |
| OTHER | 5 | 3.0% | Misc edge cases. |

What the merchant should notice:

1. **Address verification at 17% is the only actionable category.** Improve checkout address validation (Google Address Autofill, Loqate); recover roughly 60-70% of these.
2. **Insufficient data + Existing debt = 60% of declines.** Both customer-side; no merchant fix.
3. **Fraud flag at 11% is low (good).** Above 20% suggests merchant attracts higher-fraud-risk traffic.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Why are reasons so vague?**
Consumer-credit-privacy regulation. Klarna cannot disclose specific risk signals to the merchant.

**Address verification, can I improve it?**
Yes. Implement Google Address Autofill, Loqate, or similar; clean addresses recover 60-70% of these.

**Existing debt, anything I can do?**
No. The customer has open Klarna balances elsewhere. They need to settle before increasing exposure.

**Fraud flag at 5% suddenly jumped to 25%, what gives?**
Targeted fraud attempt at the merchant. Klarna's risk model caught it. The merchant should review web traffic for campaign anomalies.

**Slice it has a unique decline reason?**
Yes: SLICE_IT_CREDIT_CHECK_FAILED. Customer chose Slice it but didn't pass the harder credit check. They can retry with Pay in 4 or Pay in 30.
