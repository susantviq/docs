## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Pending growing while volume stable, why?**
Payout schedule slowed (Klarna shifted you to longer interval) or reserve hold increased.

**Reserve held, separate?**
Yes; some Portal views split reserve from pending.

**Working capital plan?**
Plan for ~7 days of revenue in flight if on weekly schedule.
