Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **39** KPI pulses for WooCommerce stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The WooCommerce connector handles the WordPress + commerce stack realities most Woo merchants actually live with - plugin sprawl, version drift, security patch posture. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 39 KPI pulses for WooCommerce stores in the Vortex IQ **AI OS** catalogue, including the WordPress Stack Health cluster (*Plugins Out-of-Date*, *Plugin Security-Patch Breach*, *WooCommerce Core Version*, *SSL / HTTPS Status*) and cross-channel cards like *Pagespeed-Caused Cart-Abandonment Loss* and *Refund-Driven Customer Churn*. **Vortex Mind** investigates root cause; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask anything in plain English with citations back to the card.
