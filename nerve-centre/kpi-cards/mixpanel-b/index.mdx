Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **13** KPI pulses for Mixpanel (B) stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Mixpanel (B) connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to teams using Mixpanel as a secondary product-analytics source - a parallel manifest to the primary Mixpanel integration that exposes a slimmer 13-KPI core for storefront engagement and conversion tracking. The **Nerve Centre** tracks *Real-Time Users*, *Sessions*, *Bounce Rate*, *Pageviews per Session*, *Avg Session Duration*, *New / Returning Users*, *Session Conversion Rate*, *Cart Abandonment Rate*, *Goal Completions*, and *Top Pages / Top Traffic Sources*. **Vortex Mind** investigates when bounce-rate spikes correlate with a traffic-source change; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the session.
