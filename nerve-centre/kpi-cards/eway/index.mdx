Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **16** KPI pulses for eWAY stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The eWAY connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to merchants in Australia, New Zealand, the UK, and Singapore using eWAY's payment gateway for card processing, hosted checkout, and recurring billing. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 16 KPI pulses covering *Success Rate*, *Decline Rate*, *3DS Abandonment Rate*, *Refund Rate*, *Chargeback Rate*, *Dispute Rate*, *Avg Settlement Time*, *Pending Payouts*, *Top Decline Reasons*, and *Top Payment Methods*. **Vortex Mind** investigates when 3DS abandonment spikes or settlement lag stretches; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the transaction.
