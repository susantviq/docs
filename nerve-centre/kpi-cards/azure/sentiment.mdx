Cards carrying a sensitivity band flip to Action Needed when the live value crosses the bad threshold. Bands are shared across cloud_platform connectors and tunable per store in Alert Rules.

| Card | Direction | Good | Bad | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Azure Spend (30d)](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/azure/azure-spend-30d) | Trend inverse (rising spend = bad) | 10 | 50 | % change vsP |

| [Spend Spike Alert](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/azure/spend-spike-alert) | Trend inverse (rising spend = bad) | 10 | 50 | % change vsP |
