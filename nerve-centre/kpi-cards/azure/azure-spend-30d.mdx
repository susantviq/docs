## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it measures** | Sum of Cost over the last 30d of daily rows from the CostManagement/query 60d Daily ActualCost response; change vs the previous 30d window. Cost Management Reader role required. |
| **Source** | Azure Cost Management (costs) |
| **Time window** | `30D vsP` |
| **Alert trigger** | `up >50% vsP` |
| **Sensitivity band** | `ai_spend_trend`: good 10, bad 50 (trend inverse (rising spend = bad)) |
| **Roles** | owner, finance, engineering |
| **Chart** | kpi |

> Requires the Cost Management Reader role granted to the app registration on the subscription; without it this card shows no data.

## Calculation

```
Sum of Cost over the last 30d of daily rows from the CostManagement/query 60d Daily ActualCost response; change vs the previous 30d window. Cost Management Reader role required.
```

Computed by the Microsoft Azure engine from daily cost buckets over a 60-day window (current 30 days plus the previous 30 for trend comparisons), refreshed on the standard sync. The engine degrades to no data rather than estimating.

### Tracked live in Vortex IQ Nerve Centre

Connect Microsoft Azure in [Settings, Connectors](https://app.vortexiq.ai/v2/settings?tab=sources) and this card renders live alongside the rest of the connector's metrics.
