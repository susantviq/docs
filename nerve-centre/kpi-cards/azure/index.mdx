[KPI Cards](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards) / **Microsoft Azure** · [Sentiment](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/azure/sentiment)

Vortex IQ tracks the Azure spend of the connected subscription: spend level and trend, cost by service, daily spend, run rate and concentration. Cards derive from the subscription read plus one daily Cost Management ActualCost query grouped by service. Spend cards require the Cost Management Reader role on the subscription.
