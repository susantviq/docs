## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> A 90-day sparkline of your average rated cost per Japan Post consignment, so you can see whether unit despatch cost is drifting up, holding flat, or stepping at a tariff change. This is the slow-moving margin signal that sits underneath the daily noise of [Avg Shipping Cost](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/japan-post/avg-shipping-cost).
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

## What it tracks

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:whatittracks */}
The card plots total Japan Post shipping spend divided by consignment count, sampled across the trailing 90 days. A smooth gentle slope usually reflects a shift in despatch mix (more EMS, more remote-prefecture Yu-Pack, heavier parcels); a sharp step almost always reflects a Japan Post tariff change, the annual Yu-Pack pricing review typically lands in March and can move whole prefecture-zone bands at once, and EMS rates are revised periodically too. Reading the trend over 90 days lets you separate a genuine cost-creep that needs a packaging or carrier-mix response from a one-month blip driven by a heavy-parcel promotion or a seasonal spike such as Oshogatsu (New Year) gifting. There is no alert on this card; it is a watch metric. For the spend geography behind the trend use [Cost by Zone](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/japan-post/cost-by-zone), and for the individual parcels distorting an upward month use [High-Cost Shipment Outliers](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/japan-post/high-cost-shipment-outliers).
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:whatittracks */}

## Reconciling against the source

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
Cross-check the trend against your monthly Japan Post billing statements and the rated charges in the Yu-Pack Print / EMS despatch records, aligning to the same tariff period (billing is in JST); a step in the line usually maps to a published Japan Post price revision rather than a data issue. See [Avg Shipping Cost](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/japan-post/avg-shipping-cost) for the period-over-period point reading.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
