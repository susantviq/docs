## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> Total Japan Post shipping spend broken down by destination zone over the trailing 30 days, shown as a horizontal bar so you can see at a glance which zones absorb most of your despatch budget. Japan Post prices Yu-Pack by prefecture-zone domestically and by country-band for EMS, so this card is where the geography of your cost lives.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

## What it tracks

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:whatittracks */}
This card sums the rated shipping cost of each Japan Post consignment in the last 30 days and groups it by zone. Domestically that means the Yu-Pack prefecture-zone bands (Hokkaido and Okinawa sit in the most expensive bands; the Kanto and Kansai core sit cheaper); for EMS it means the destination country-band (Asia, Oceania / North America / Central America / Middle East / Europe, and so on). The longest bars tell you where consolidation, packaging optimisation, or a service-tier change would save the most yen. Because Japan Post runs an annual Yu-Pack pricing review (typically effective in March), a prefecture can be reclassified into a different zone between periods, so a bar that jumps without a volume change is usually a tariff change, not a despatch-mix change. For per-zone unit economics rather than total spend, pair this with [Yu-Pack Cost by Prefecture Zone](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/japan-post/yu-pack-cost-by-prefecture-zone) and [Avg Shipping Cost](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/japan-post/avg-shipping-cost).
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:whatittracks */}

## Reconciling against the source

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
Cross-check against your Japan Post billing statement and the rated charges in the Yu-Pack Print / EMS despatch records; note that Japan Post bills in JST and a prefecture's zone band can change at the annual March pricing review, so compare like tariff periods. See [Cost Per Shipment Trend](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/japan-post/cost-per-shipment-trend) for the trend view and [High-Cost Shipment Outliers](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/japan-post/high-cost-shipment-outliers) for the individual parcels driving a zone's total.
