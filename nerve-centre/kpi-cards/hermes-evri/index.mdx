Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **25** KPI pulses for Evri (formerly Hermes UK) stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Evri (formerly Hermes UK) connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to UK merchants shipping with Evri's home-delivery, ParcelShop drop-off, and locker network - the country's largest pure-play parcel carrier by volume. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 25 KPI pulses with hero alerts on *Avg Shipping Cost*, *On-Time Delivery Rate*, *Late Shipments*, *Exception Rate*, *Open Claims*, *Days to Token Expiry*, *Redelivery Attempts > 3*, plus cross-channel *Evri OTD by Sales Channel* and *ParcelShop Drop-off Failure Rate* so failed handovers don't quietly accumulate. **Vortex Mind** investigates when the lost-parcel rate spikes or redeliveries pile up; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the consignment.
