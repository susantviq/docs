Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **13** KPI pulses for TikTok stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The TikTok connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to brands tracking organic TikTok as a customer-acquisition channel - the storefront traffic that flows from bio links, TikTok Shop links, and Linktree-style aggregators rather than native post-level reach or for-you-page placement. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 13 KPI pulses on *Sessions*, *Real-Time Users*, *New / Returning Users*, *Bounce Rate*, *Pageviews per Session*, *Avg Session Duration*, *Session Conversion Rate*, *Cart Abandonment Rate*, *Goal Completions*, and *Top Pages / Top Traffic Sources*. **Vortex Mind** investigates when a viral video drives traffic that bounces; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the session.
