Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **14** KPI pulses for WordPress stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The WordPress connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to merchants and content teams running WordPress — the platform powering 40%+ of the open web and the dominant CMS for content-led commerce, WooCommerce stores, and editorial sites. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 14 KPI pulses on *Core Web Vitals* (*LCP*, *INP*, *CLS*, *TTFB*), *CWV Pass Rate*, *Lighthouse Performance / SEO / Accessibility / Best Practices*, *Total Page Weight*, *Speed Index*, *Total Blocking Time*, and *Render-Blocking Resources* — so plugin bloat and theme regressions surface alongside the SEO ranking impact they cause. **Vortex Mind** investigates when mobile CWV slips below pass; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the page.
