Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **65** KPI pulses for AdRoll stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The AdRoll connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to merchants using AdRoll's retargeting and prospecting platform - strong on cross-network display and CRM-segment retargeting. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 65 KPI pulses including the standard ad hero cards (*ROAS*, *Wasted Spend*, *Zero-Conversion Spend*, *Spend vs Budget*) plus campaign / device / geography / dayparting cuts. **Vortex Mind** investigates when retargeting ROAS drops; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
