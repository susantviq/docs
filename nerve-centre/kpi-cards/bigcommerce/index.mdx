Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **115** KPI pulses for BigCommerce stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The BigCommerce connector is one of the deepest commerce integrations in the Vortex IQ **AI OS** catalogue. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 115 KPI pulses for BigCommerce stores - including the multi-channel revenue mix (DTC, Amazon, eBay), payment-decline recovery surface (Declined Transaction Rate, Incomplete Cart Rate, *Size of the Prize* unrecovered value), and the cross-channel *Active Ads on Out-of-Stock SKUs* alert that catches paid spend on broken funnels. **Vortex Mind** investigates when revenue or recovery dips; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask anything in plain English with citations back to the card.
