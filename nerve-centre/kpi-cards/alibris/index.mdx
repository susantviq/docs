Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **52** KPI pulses for Alibris stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Alibris connector covers the long-established US marketplace for books, music, and movies - the main competitor to AbeBooks for indie booksellers and antiquarian dealers. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 52 KPI pulses for Alibris accounts in the Vortex IQ **AI OS** catalogue - including hero alerts (*Feed Cycle Failure*, *Last Successful Upload*, *Late Order Processing Queue*, *Listings Processed With Errors*, *Pending Dispatch*, *Suspended/Pending Listings*, *Dispatch SLA Compliance*) plus cross-platform cards that fuse Alibris with the broader book trade (*ISBN Drift vs AbeBooks + Amazon*, *Alibris Share of Book-Trade Revenue*, *Rare-Book Price Floor Watch*). **Vortex Mind** investigates when feed cycles fail or rare-book pricing drifts; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
