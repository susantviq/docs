Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **52** KPI pulses for AbeBooks stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The AbeBooks connector covers the Amazon-owned global marketplace for books - used and rare books, antiquarian sellers, university booksellers. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 52 KPI pulses for AbeBooks accounts in the Vortex IQ **AI OS** catalogue - including hero alerts (*Feed Cycle Failure*, *Last Successful Upload*, *Late Order Processing Queue*, *Listings Processed With Errors*, *Pending Dispatch*, *Suspended/Pending Listings*, *Dispatch SLA Compliance*) plus cross-channel cards that fuse AbeBooks with the broader book trade (*ISBN Drift vs Alibris + Amazon*, *AbeBooks Share of Book-Trade Revenue*, *Rare-Book Price Floor Watch*). **Vortex Mind** investigates when feed cycles fail or rare-book pricing drifts; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
