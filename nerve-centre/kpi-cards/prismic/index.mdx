Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **14** KPI pulses for Prismic stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Prismic connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to teams using Prismic's slice-based headless CMS, popular with marketing-led brands that need component-level content authoring on Next.js, Nuxt, and SvelteKit. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 14 KPI pulses on *Core Web Vitals* (*LCP*, *INP*, *CLS*, *TTFB*), *CWV Pass Rate*, *Lighthouse Performance / SEO / Accessibility / Best Practices*, *Total Page Weight*, *Speed Index*, *Total Blocking Time*, and *Render-Blocking Resources*. **Vortex Mind** investigates when a slice change regresses CLS; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the page.
