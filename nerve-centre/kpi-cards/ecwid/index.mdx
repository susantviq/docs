Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **12** KPI pulses for Ecwid stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Ecwid connector brings Vortex IQ **AI OS** monitoring to Ecwid stores - typically smaller catalogues running on a hosted shopping cart that embeds into other sites. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 12 KPI pulses (Total Revenue, Conversion Rate, Refund Rate, Out-of-Stock Products, AOV, plus customer-health and inventory-health cards). **Vortex Mind** investigates when a number moves; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask anything in plain English with citations back to the source card.
