Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **35** KPI pulses for Intercom. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Intercom connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to teams running Intercom as their **customer-support inbox** — the conversation, messaging, and ticketing layer between a store and its customers. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 35 KPI pulses focused on the questions a support or CX lead asks every morning: *is anything breaching our first-response SLA right now*, *how fast are we replying and resolving by team and agent*, *is CSAT holding*, and *is the backlog growing*. Hero alerts cover **SLA breaches**, **unanswered conversations**, **CSAT**, and the cross-platform kill-shots that tie support load to revenue — **support spikes on failed Adyen payments**, **complaints on out-of-stock SKUs**, and **high-value customers left waiting**. **Vortex Mind** investigates when first-response slips or CSAT dips; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the source card.
