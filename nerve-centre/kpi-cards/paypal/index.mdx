Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **77** KPI pulses for PayPal stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The PayPal connector covers PayPal account health where merchants care most - auth rates, decline patterns, dispute exposure, recoverable revenue. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 77 KPI pulses for PayPal in the Vortex IQ **AI OS** catalogue, including hero alerts (*Payment Health Score*, *Transaction Success Rate*, *Decline Rate Spike Alert*, *Open Disputes*) plus cross-channel cards (*Decline Spike vs Checkout Funnel Drop*, *Recoverable Revenue (decline-driven)*, *Item-Not-Received vs Shipping Delays*). **Vortex Mind** investigates when success rate dips or INR cases burst; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
