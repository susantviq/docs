Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **102** KPI pulses for Amazon Ads stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Amazon Ads connector covers Amazon's full advertising stack - Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display, plus DSP. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 102 KPI pulses for Amazon Ads accounts in the Vortex IQ **AI OS** catalogue - including hero alerts (*ACOS*, *TACOS*, *ROAS*, *Active Ads on Out-of-Stock SKUs*, *Active Ads on No-Buy-Box ASINs*, *Dayparting Waste*, *Wasted Spend*, *Zero-Conversion Spend*) plus the *DSP vs Sponsored Spend Mix* and *Harvest Queue* surfaces no Amazon-only dashboard joins natively. **Vortex Mind** investigates when ACOS climbs or wasted spend concentrates; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English (*"which ASINs are wasting Amazon ad spend this week?"*) and act through your agents.
