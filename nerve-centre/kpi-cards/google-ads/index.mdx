Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **75** KPI pulses for Google Ads stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Google Ads connector is the busiest paid-channel integration in the Vortex IQ **AI OS** catalogue. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 75 KPI pulses for Google Ads accounts - ROAS at account and campaign level, *Wasted Spend* and *Zero-Conversion Spend* hero alerts, plus the cross-channel *Active Ads on Out-of-Stock SKUs* alert that catches budget burning on SKUs that can't convert. **Vortex Mind** explains why ROAS drops or where waste is concentrated; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask *"are we wasting spend on any keywords?"* in plain English and act on the answer through your agents.
