Audits campaign performance, wasted spend, and negative-keyword opportunities.

## What this audit checks

### Campaign efficiency
- ROAS per campaign
- Cost per conversion
- Quality score range
- Disapproved ads

### Keyword hygiene
- Wasted spend on non-converting terms
- Negative-keyword candidates from search terms
- Broad-match exposure

### Budget & pacing
- Budget utilisation per campaign
- Lost impression share (budget)
- Lost impression share (rank)

## Data sources

- `POST https://googleads.googleapis.com/v17/customers/{customer_id}/googleAds:searchStream` - Run GAQL queries for campaigns / keywords / search terms
- `POST https://oauth2.googleapis.com/token` - Refresh OAuth access token
