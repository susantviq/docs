The Agile CRM connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to small businesses on Agile CRM - all-in-one sales + marketing + service CRM popular with budget-conscious SMB needing a single tool across the customer lifecycle. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 29 KPI pulses including subscriber, deliverability, engagement, and revenue-attribution metrics. **Vortex Mind** investigates when a number moves; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
