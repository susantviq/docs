## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> Days Since Last Payout tracks how long it has been since Walmart last settled funds into your account. Walmart pays sellers on a roughly bi-weekly cycle, so this number tells you whether settlements are arriving on the expected rhythm. A figure that climbs past the normal cadence is an early warning of a payment hold, banking issue, or account standing problem.

## Calculation

Vortex IQ reads the date of your most recent completed Walmart Marketplace payout and measures the elapsed days up to the current moment. Because the window is real time, the value increments each day until the next settlement clears and resets it. As noted in the At a glance summary, the unit is a simple day count. The worked example below shows how the figure crossing its bi-weekly cadence prompts a check.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Days Since Last Payout update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). For real-time signals, force a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Walmart Seller Center show a different number?**
A payout that has just been initiated may not yet show as completed in the data feed, so the count can lag the settlement you see processing in Seller Center. Time-zone differences at the day boundary can also shift the count by a day.

**Q: Is a high value always a problem?**
Not necessarily. The number naturally rises between scheduled bi-weekly settlements and resets when the next payout clears. It only warrants action when it clearly exceeds your normal cycle length.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, alert thresholds are configurable per profile in the Alert Rules tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
