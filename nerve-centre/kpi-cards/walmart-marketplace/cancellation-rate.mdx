## Calculation

Vortex IQ divides cancelled orders by total orders over the selected window and compares the result against the prior period to show direction. The figure is presented as a gauge against Walmart's published cancellation threshold so you can see how much headroom remains. As the At a glance summary notes, seller-initiated cancellations are the ones that hurt; the worked example below shows how a stock-out can push the rate over the line.

## Reconciling against Walmart Seller Center

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Walmart Seller Center:**

Verify against the Performance or Scorecard area, where Walmart publishes the cancellation rate that feeds account health, and the Order Management area for the underlying cancelled orders. The scorecard also shows the threshold currently applied.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Period boundary.** Vortex IQ uses 30D vsP rolling by default; Seller Center may use calendar periods. | Variable | Match the period range. |
| **Time zone.** Seller Center uses the account time zone; Vortex IQ aligns to the merchant reporting time zone. | Marginal | Confirm time zone match. |
| **Filter scope.** Profile-level filters (fulfilment type, test orders) may narrow the Vortex IQ view. | Variable | Match filter settings. |

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Cancellation Rate update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). For real-time signals, force a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Walmart Seller Center show a different number?**
Seller Center applies the cancellation rate over its own scorecard window and may attribute buyer-initiated cancellations differently from Vortex IQ, which uses a rolling period. Time-zone and filter scope (fulfilment type or test orders) can also shift which cancellations are counted.

**Q: Do buyer-requested cancellations count against my rate?**
Walmart treats the metric primarily around seller-initiated cancellations; buyer-requested cancellations made before fulfilment are generally handled differently, so check the scorecard detail if your two figures diverge.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, alert thresholds are configurable per profile in the Alert Rules tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
