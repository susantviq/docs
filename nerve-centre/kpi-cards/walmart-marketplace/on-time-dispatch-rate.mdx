## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> On-Time Dispatch Rate is the share of Walmart Marketplace orders you ship by the expected ship date. It is one of the metrics Walmart watches most closely, because it feeds directly into your Seller Scorecard and Pro Seller eligibility. Slipping below the SLA quietly cuts your search visibility long before any suspension notice arrives.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it counts** | The percentage of Walmart Marketplace orders dispatched on or before the expected ship date in the window. |
| **Sample type** | Backend API data from Walmart Marketplace, refreshed on the standard data refresh. |
| **Why it matters** | Walmart treats on-time dispatch as a core SLA; a falling rate reduces algorithmic visibility and, if sustained, puts Pro Seller status and account standing at risk. |
| **Reading the value** | Higher is better; a value trending down toward the SLA floor is an early warning that fulfilment is slipping. |
| **Currency** | percent |
| **Time window** | `30D vsP` |
| **Alert trigger** | `<99% (Walmart SLA)` |
| **Sentiment key** | `wal_sla_compliance` |
| **Roles** | owner, operations |
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

## Calculation

The value is derived by comparing the number of Walmart Marketplace orders dispatched on or before their expected ship date against the total number of orders due to ship in the selected window, then expressing the result as a percentage. Orders are aligned to the merchant reporting time zone before the comparison. See the At a glance summary for the window and unit, and the worked example below for how the figure reads in practice.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
*A representative reading of **On-Time Dispatch Rate** for a typical merchant on Walmart Marketplace.* On 12 Mar 26 a seller shipping around 1,200 orders in the trailing 30 days sees the rate slip from 99.4% to 98.1%, with roughly 23 orders dispatched a day late. That is still above a hard suspension line but already below the SLA the card watches, so Walmart begins trimming search placement on the affected items. The action is to check which carrier or warehouse cut-off caused the late batch and tighten the pick-and-pack schedule before the rate erodes further. ## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does On-Time Dispatch Rate update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). For real-time signals, force a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Walmart Seller Center show a different number?**
Seller Center often reports on-time shipment over a fixed calendar window and in the account time zone, while Vortex IQ uses a rolling 30-day window aligned to your reporting time zone. Matching the period and time zone usually closes the gap.

**Q: Does WFS fulfilment count toward this rate?**
Walmart Fulfillment Services orders are handled by Walmart, so seller-controlled dispatch timing applies mainly to seller-fulfilled orders. Use profile filters if you want to isolate seller-fulfilled performance.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, alert thresholds are configurable per profile in the Alert Rules tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
