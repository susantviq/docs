## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Pro Seller Status update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). For real-time signals, force a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Walmart Seller Center show a different number?**
Walmart re-evaluates eligibility on its own cadence, so a status change can show in one place slightly before the other depending on when each was last refreshed. Time-zone and profile-filter differences can also affect the underlying metric snapshots.

**Q: What does a parity violation flag actually mean for the badge?**
Walmart compares your item prices against the same products elsewhere, including Amazon. If a flagged item is priced uncompetitively, it can suppress that listing and, if widespread, jeopardise the Pro Seller badge until parity is restored.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, alert thresholds are configurable per profile in the Alert Rules tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
