## Calculation

Vortex IQ reads open orders from Walmart Marketplace and compares each order's estimated ship date against the current time. Any order whose ship-by moment has already elapsed but which has not yet been dispatched is added to the count. The card is real time, so it updates as orders age into breach or are shipped out of it, which is why the worked example below treats it as a live action queue rather than a historical rate.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Late Dispatches Risking Pro Seller Status update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). For real-time signals, force a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Walmart Seller Center show a different number?**
Seller Center may count an order as on-time the moment a tracking number is uploaded, while Vortex IQ holds it in the breach list until the dispatch confirmation propagates. Time-zone and filter scope (fulfilment type or test orders) can also change which orders appear past ship-by.

**Q: Does WFS-fulfilled volume show up in this card?**
WFS orders are dispatched by Walmart, so they are not part of your seller-fulfilled dispatch SLA; this card focuses on the seller-fulfilled orders you ship yourself.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, alert thresholds are configurable per profile in the Alert Rules tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
