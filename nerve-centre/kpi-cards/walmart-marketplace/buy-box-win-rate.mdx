## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> This card shows how often your offers win the Walmart Buy Box, the default add-to-cart spot on a shared product page. Walmart often awards the Buy Box to the lowest-price seller, including WFS offers and Walmart first-party, so a drop here usually means you have been undercut on price or fulfilment promise. Sellers watch it because Buy Box ownership is where the vast majority of marketplace sales actually happen.

## Calculation

Vortex IQ measures the share of your eligible offers that currently hold the Buy Box across the selected window and compares it against the prior period to show direction. The result is rendered as a gauge so you can see at a glance how close you are to the threshold. As the At a glance summary notes, Walmart's Buy Box logic leans heavily on price and fulfilment promise; the worked example below shows how a competitor's repricing move can pull this figure down quickly.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Buy-Box Win Rate update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). For real-time signals, force a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Walmart Seller Center show a different number?**
Buy Box ownership changes continuously as competitors reprice, so Seller Center and Vortex IQ can sample slightly different moments, and the two may use different period boundaries. Filter scope (fulfilment type or test orders) can also change which offers are included.

**Q: Why am I losing the Buy Box even when my listing is high quality?**
Walmart weights price and fulfilment promise heavily, so a lower-priced rival or a WFS offer with a faster delivery estimate can take the Buy Box even when your listing quality is strong; compare against the price parity and WFS sibling cards.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
