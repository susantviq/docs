## Calculation

Vortex IQ takes Walmart order revenue over the selected window and divides it by order count within each destination state to produce a per-state average. The result is rendered as a bar comparison so high and low states stand out at a glance. As described in the At a glance summary, read each bar against your blended AOV; the worked example below shows how a single state can skew the overall figure.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does AOV by State update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). For real-time signals, force a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Walmart Seller Center show a different number?**
Seller Center may attribute orders to region differently or use a calendar period, while Vortex IQ uses a rolling 30-day window grouped by destination state. Time-zone and filter scope (fulfilment type or test orders) can also move a few orders between states or periods.

**Q: Which state is used when billing and shipping addresses differ?**
This card groups by the order's shipping destination, since that is what reflects where demand physically lands, not the buyer's billing region.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
