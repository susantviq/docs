## Calculation

Vortex IQ reads the outcome of new item setup submissions to Walmart Marketplace and counts those flagged with item setup errors over the trailing 24 hours. Each rejected item is tallied once for the window, so the value reflects the volume of listings that failed to publish recently rather than a cumulative backlog. Because the trigger fires above zero, even a single rejection surfaces, as shown in the worked example below.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Walmart Rejected New Listings (24h) update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). For real-time signals, force a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Walmart Seller Center show a different number?**
Seller Center may group rejections by submission batch rather than by the trailing 24-hour clock, so a batch straddling the window boundary can split across views. Time-zone and filter scope (fulfilment type or test items) can also shift which rejections fall inside the window.

**Q: Does a resubmitted listing that succeeds clear it from the count?**
The 24-hour count reflects rejections that occurred in the window, so a later successful resubmission shows up as a published listing elsewhere but does not retroactively erase the original rejection event.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
