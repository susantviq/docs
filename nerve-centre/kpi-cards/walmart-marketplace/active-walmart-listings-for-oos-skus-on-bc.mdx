## Calculation

The value is derived by matching active Walmart Marketplace listings against their BigCommerce source records and counting the SKUs that are still live on Walmart while showing zero available stock in BigCommerce. It is a real-time cross-channel snapshot that depends on both connectors being linked. See the At a glance summary for the unit, and the worked example below for how the count reads in practice.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Active Walmart Listings for OOS SKUs on BC update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). For real-time signals, force a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Walmart Seller Center show a different number?**
Seller Center only sees the Walmart inventory level and has no view of BigCommerce stock, so it cannot flag this mismatch on its own. The count exists only in Vortex IQ because it compares both sides.

**Q: What is the fastest way to clear this count?**
Push an inventory update that zeroes the affected Walmart listings, then fix the sync so BigCommerce stockouts propagate promptly. The count clears once Walmart reflects the true availability.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
