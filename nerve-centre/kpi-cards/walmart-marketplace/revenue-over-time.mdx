## Calculation

Vortex IQ aggregates your Walmart Marketplace gross sales across the selected 90-day window and plots them as an area series over time. Each point reflects the sales recorded for that interval, so the shape of the area shows momentum rather than a single snapshot. As noted in the At a glance summary, the value is expressed in the store currency. The worked example below shows how reading the trend shape informs a planning decision.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Revenue Over Time update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). For real-time signals, force a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Walmart Seller Center show a different number?**
Seller Center reports may use calendar periods that differ from the rolling 90-day window, and refunds or adjustments can post on dates that shift individual points. Time-zone and profile-filter differences can also change which sales fall into each interval.

**Q: Does the trend show gross or net revenue?**
This card plots gross sales over time. To see the take-home trend after Walmart fees and refunds, pair it with the Net Revenue card.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, alert thresholds are configurable per profile in the Alert Rules tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
