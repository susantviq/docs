## Calculation

Vortex IQ counts your Walmart Marketplace orders for each calendar day in the selected 30-day window and plots them as a daily series. This produces the line chart you see, where each point is one day's order total. As noted in the At a glance summary, the unit is a plain order count per day. The worked example below shows how a spike in the line translates into an operational action.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
*A representative reading of **Orders / Day** for a typical merchant on Walmart Marketplace.* On 12 Mar 26 a seller averaging around 40 orders a day sees the line jump to roughly 110 after a Walmart promotional placement went live. Dispatch is staffed for the normal baseline, so pending dispatch starts to build. ## Reconciling against Walmart Seller Center

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Walmart Seller Center:**

Verify against the Order Management area, where you can filter orders by date and tally them per day. Walmart's sales reports also let you confirm daily order counts over a chosen range.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Period boundary.** Vortex IQ uses 30D rolling by default; Seller Center may use calendar periods. | Variable | Match the period range. |
| **Time zone.** Seller Center uses the account time zone; Vortex IQ aligns to the merchant reporting time zone. | Marginal | Confirm time zone match. |
| **Filter scope.** Profile-level filters (fulfilment type, test orders) may narrow the Vortex IQ view. | Variable | Match filter settings. |

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Orders / Day update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). For real-time signals, force a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Walmart Seller Center show a different number?**
The day boundary depends on time zone, so an order placed near midnight can fall on a different day in Seller Center than in Vortex IQ. Test-order and fulfilment filters can also change which orders are counted on a given day.

**Q: Why does a day sometimes show fewer orders than I expected?**
Cancelled or test orders may be excluded depending on your profile filters, and very recent orders for the current day may still be settling into the data on the next refresh.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
