## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Walmart Account Health (LQS / SLA / Cancel) Crossed Threshold update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). For real-time signals, force a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Walmart Seller Center show a different number?**
Walmart's scorecard often reports each KPI on a calendar-aligned rolling window while Vortex IQ evaluates the live position, so a breach may appear here slightly before or after the scorecard updates. Time-zone and filter scope (fulfilment type or test orders) can also shift which orders count toward a component.

**Q: Which KPI tripped the alert when the count is 1?**
Open the card detail to see the per-component breakdown; only the breached KPI is flagged, so you know whether to chase dispatch, cancellations, or listing quality first.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
