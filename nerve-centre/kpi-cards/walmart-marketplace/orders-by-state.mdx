## Calculation

Vortex IQ groups your Walmart Marketplace orders for the selected 30-day window by the shipping destination state and counts the orders in each. The result is a per-state distribution rendered as a choropleth, where each state's shade reflects its share of total volume. As described in the At a glance summary, the unit is a simple order count per region. The worked example below shows how the distribution informs a fulfilment decision.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Orders by State update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). For real-time signals, force a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Walmart Seller Center show a different number?**
Seller Center may count by a calendar period rather than the rolling 30-day window, and orders that straddle the window edge can land on different sides. Time-zone alignment and any test-order or fulfilment filters can also change which orders are included.

**Q: Which state is used when billing and shipping addresses differ?**
The card groups by the shipping destination state, since that is what drives fulfilment placement and delivery time. Billing geography is not used for this breakdown.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
