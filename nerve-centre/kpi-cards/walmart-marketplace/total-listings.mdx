## Calculation

The value is derived by counting every Walmart Marketplace listing on the account at the time of the refresh, with no filtering by status, quality, or availability. It is a real-time snapshot rather than a windowed measure. See the At a glance summary for the unit, and the worked example below for how the count reads in practice.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
*A representative reading of **Total Listings** for a typical merchant on Walmart Marketplace.* On 12 Mar 26 a seller normally carrying around 2,400 items sees Total Listings drop to 2,180 overnight. Nothing was deliberately retired, so the dip points to a feed that failed to refresh a batch of SKUs rather than a real catalogue change. ## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Total Listings update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). For real-time signals, force a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Walmart Seller Center show a different number?**
Seller Center counts may exclude certain statuses or apply a default filter, while Vortex IQ counts every listing regardless of state. Clearing status filters in Seller Center usually aligns the two.

**Q: Does this count include variants and bundles?**
It counts each listed item as Walmart records it, so variant-level and bundle items are included if they exist as separate listings. Check how your feed maps variants if the number looks higher than expected.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
