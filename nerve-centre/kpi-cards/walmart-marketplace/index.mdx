The Walmart Marketplace connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to sellers on Walmart's third-party marketplace. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 17 KPI pulses for Walmart Marketplace stores including *Total Revenue*, *Listing Quality Score*, *Avg Review Rating*, *Dispute Rate*, *Marketplace Fees Paid*, *Listings Expiring Soon*, and *Pending Payouts*. **Vortex Mind** investigates when standards or revenue dip; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the card.
