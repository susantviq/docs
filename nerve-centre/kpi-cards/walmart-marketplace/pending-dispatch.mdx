## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> Pending Dispatch is the live count of paid Walmart Marketplace orders that have not yet been shipped. It is your real-time fulfilment backlog and the single most direct lever on whether you meet Walmart's on-time dispatch SLA. Let it climb and you risk late-dispatch penalties, order defects, and a hit to your Pro Seller standing.

## Calculation

Vortex IQ counts the seller-fulfilled Walmart Marketplace orders that are paid but not yet marked shipped at the current moment. Because the window is real time, the figure reflects the live backlog rather than a historical period, and the alert compares it against your trailing 30-day average. As noted in the At a glance summary, the unit is a plain order count. The worked example below shows how a backlog past the alert line drives an immediate action.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Pending Dispatch update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). For real-time signals, force a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Walmart Seller Center show a different number?**
There can be a short lag between an order being marked shipped and that status reaching the data feed, so a recently dispatched order may still appear pending for one refresh. Time-zone and fulfilment-filter differences can also shift the live count.

**Q: Does this include WFS orders?**
The backlog focuses on seller-fulfilled orders, where you control the shipment and the dispatch SLA. WFS orders are fulfilled by Walmart, so they are not part of your dispatch queue.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, alert thresholds are configurable per profile in the Alert Rules tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
