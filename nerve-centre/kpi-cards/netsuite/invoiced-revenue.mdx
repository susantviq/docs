## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> Sum of Invoice transaction amounts in the period. The Invoice-only slice of total booked revenue, excluding Cash Sales and revenue journals. The metric AR-driven analysis runs on.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it counts** | `SUM(Transaction.Amount)` for `Transaction.Type = 'CustInvc'` posted in the period, all subsidiaries the role can read. |
| **Tax treatment** | **Net of tax**, sums revenue lines only. |
| **Shipping** | Included if shipping is mapped to a revenue Item record. |
| **Discounts** | Already deducted at line level. |
| **Credit Memos** | NOT included on this card. Credit Memos are tracked separately on [Credit Memos Value](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/netsuite/ns-credit-memos-value). |
| **Cash Sales** | NOT included. Cash Sales (POS, prepaid web orders) bypass Invoice and book directly to revenue. They appear on [Revenue Booked into GL](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/netsuite/revenue-booked-into-gl). |
| **Cancelled / voided invoices** | Excluded. |
| **Currency** | OneWorld: reporting currency at transaction-date FX. Single-subsidiary: native currency. |
| **Subsidiary scope** | Respects dashboard filter. |
| **Time window** | `30D vsP` |
| **Alert trigger** | `drop >15% vsP`, sentiment key `revenue_trend` |
| **Roles** | owner, finance |
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
A US wholesale distributor on NetSuite OneWorld. 30-day window 14 Mar 26 to 12 Apr 26. Reporting currency USD.

| Subsidiary | Invoice count | Invoiced revenue (USD) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| US Distribution Inc | 482 | $5,840,000 |
| CA Distribution Ltd (Canada) | 84 | $720,000 |
| **Invoiced Revenue (this card)** | **566** | **$6,560,000** |

| Comparison | Amount | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invoiced Revenue (this card) | $6,560,000 | Invoice transactions only |
| Cash Sales (separate) | $310,000 | Prepaid web orders, POS |
| Credit Memos (separate, deducted on GL card) | ($128,000) | Returns processed in window |
| **Revenue Booked into GL** | **$6,742,000** | Sum of all three |

Five things to notice:

1. **Invoiced Revenue is the biggest slice** of total revenue for B2B-skewed businesses (typically 95-99% of total). For DTC-skewed businesses on NetSuite, Cash Sales can be 30-50%.
2. **The 566 invoices count is more useful than the dollar number for AR analysis.** Average invoice value (~$11.6K) tells you customer-mix concentration. Compare to [Average SO Value](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/netsuite/average-sales-order-value).
3. **Last period was $6,890,000.** This period is down 4.8% vsP, well below the alert threshold. The trend chart shows the slope.
4. **Cash Sales of $310K are typically retail POS or web-prepaid orders.** They're real revenue but they don't create AR, so they don't show up here. For an AR-driven analysis (DSO, aging), this card is the right denominator.
5. **The $128K Credit Memo reduction shows separately.** This card is gross of refunds; the [Revenue Booked into GL](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/netsuite/revenue-booked-into-gl) card is net.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}

## Reconciling against the vendor's own dashboard

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in NetSuite:**

> **Reports → Sales → Invoice Register**
> **Saved search**: `Transaction.Type = 'CustInvc' AND Posting = T AND TransactionDate BETWEEN [start, end]`

The Invoice Register report should match this card to the dollar.

Adjacent reports that look related but aren't:

- **Sales by Item**: includes Cash Sales and Credit Memos by default; unfiltered totals do not match.
- **Income Statement**: includes everything; matches the GL Booked card, not this one.
- **Customer Aging**: balance view, not period flow.

**Why our number may legitimately differ from NetSuite reports:**

| Reason | Direction | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cash Sale exclusion** | Card lower than Income Statement | This card is Invoice-only; Income Statement includes Cash Sales. |
| **Credit Memo treatment** | Card higher than net-of-CM views | Credit Memos are separate. Net invoice activity needs CM subtraction. |
| **Subsidiary scope** | Either | Card defaults to all subsidiaries the role can read. |
| **FX rate cadence** | Small | Transaction-date FX vs period-average FX. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:**

| Card | Relationship | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [shopify.total_revenue](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/shopify/total-revenue) | Commerce > Invoiced (with lag) | Commerce gross feeds the SO queue; Invoiced is downstream. Difference is the SO-to-Invoice cycle time × inflow rate. |
| [bigcommerce.total_revenue](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/bigcommerce/total-revenue) | Larger lag | B2B Net-30 means Invoiced lags commerce by 10-30 days. |
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Why is this card different from Revenue Booked into GL?**
Two reasons: (1) this card excludes Cash Sales (POS, prepaid web orders that bypass Invoice); (2) this card is gross of Credit Memos. The GL Booked card is the consolidated view; this is the Invoice-only slice.

**Should I use this or GL Booked for board reporting?**
GL Booked. That's the audit-grade total revenue figure. Use Invoiced for AR analysis (DSO, aging, collection efficiency).

**Why is the count more useful than the dollar value sometimes?**
Invoice count tracks operational throughput. A merchant could see flat dollar Invoiced Revenue but rising count, indicating smaller-basket B2B customers replacing larger ones. Count + AOV = decomposition.

**Recurring billing on subscriptions?**
Each billing cycle creates a new Invoice. So a $120K annual contract billed monthly produces 12 Invoices of $10K each. The card sums them in the period they post.

**ASC 606, what hits this card?**
Invoiced amount, not recognised revenue. ARM-deferred revenue still appears here at full Invoice amount; deferral lives on the Balance Sheet. The GL Booked card is the recognised view.

**B2B credit-hold, does it suppress Invoiced Revenue?**
Yes, indirectly. SOs on credit hold don't bill, so they don't create Invoices, so they don't appear here. This is the leading indicator: Invoiced Revenue dropping while open SOs are rising is the classic credit-bottleneck signal.

**Does Cash Application affect this card?**
No. Cash Application changes the AR balance but not the Invoiced Revenue total. This card is the period-flow of new Invoices created.

**Multi-currency, how does FX work?**
Each Invoice is translated to reporting currency at transaction-date rate. OneWorld accounts: see the [Revenue by Currency](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/netsuite/ns-revenue-by-currency) card for the FX-mix view.
