## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> Total on-hand inventory valued at NetSuite's costing method (Avg, FIFO, LIFO, Standard, or Lot Numbered). The working-capital line on the Balance Sheet.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it counts** | `SUM(Item.QuantityOnHand × Item.AverageCost)` for inventory items at every Location, all subsidiaries the role can read. |
| **Costing method** | NetSuite supports Average, FIFO, LIFO, Standard, Lot Numbered, Serial. Card uses whatever the Item record's costing method dictates. |
| **Tax treatment** | n/a (inventory is at cost, no tax). |
| **Shipping** | Inbound landed cost included if the Item record's costing includes Landed Cost allocation. |
| **In-transit** | Configurable. Inventory in transit (received from supplier, not yet received at warehouse) appears in Transit Locations only if NetSuite's transfer-order workflow is in use. |
| **Drop-ship** | Excluded (drop-ship inventory is owned by supplier). |
| **Currency** | OneWorld: each subsidiary's inventory is valued in its base currency, summed in reporting currency at current FX. |
| **Subsidiary scope** | Respects dashboard filter. |
| **Time window** | `RT` |
| **Alert trigger** | None at headline; the [Dead Stock](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/netsuite/dead-stock-value) and [Aging](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/netsuite/inventory-aging) cards carry alerts |
| **Roles** | owner, finance, operations |
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

Calculated automatically from your NetSuite data. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
A US wholesale apparel distributor on NetSuite. Snapshot 14 Apr 26.

| Location | Items count | Total value (USD) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| US East Warehouse | 8,420 | $14,260,000 |
| US West Warehouse | 6,180 | $9,810,000 |
| Canada DC | 2,840 | $3,140,000 |
| In-transit | 980 | $1,420,000 |
| **Total Inventory Value** | **18,420** | **$28,630,000** |

Five things to notice:

1. **$28.6M in inventory** is the working-capital snapshot. As a percentage of annual revenue (assume $80M COGS), that's ~4.3 months of stock. Industry average for wholesale apparel is 3-4 months; slightly above.
2. **In-transit $1.42M** is goods received from overseas suppliers, in containers en-route to warehouses. This sits on the Balance Sheet but isn't sellable yet.
3. **East vs West split (60/40)** matches the company's geographic sales mix. Imbalance signals fulfilment lag.
4. **Canada DC is small ($3.14M)** but operates as a fulfilment node for Canadian customers. Often understocked; check [Low Stock Alerts](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/netsuite/low-stock-alerts) per location.
5. **Pair with [Inventory Turnover](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/netsuite/inventory-turnover-ratio).** $28.6M of inventory turning 4 times per year = $114M COGS implied. If actual COGS is $80M, turnover is 2.8x and inventory is bloated.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}

## Reconciling against the vendor's own dashboard

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in NetSuite:**

> **Reports → Inventory → Inventory Valuation Summary**
> **Reports → Financial → Balance Sheet** (Inventory line)
> **Lists → Accounting → Items** with "Quantity Available" + "Average Cost" columns

The Inventory Valuation Summary is the gold standard. The Balance Sheet line should match this card to within rounding.

**Why our number may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **In-transit inclusion** | Either | Some accounts exclude in-transit from the Inventory Valuation report. Card includes by default. |
| **WIP / partial-build** | Either | If you use NetSuite Manufacturing, WIP value can be excluded from valuation reports but included in BS. |
| **FX cadence** | Small | Current spot vs report-saved rate. |
| **Subsidiary scope** | Either | Card defaults to all subs. |
| **Costing method update lag** | Small | Average cost recalculations happen on transaction; card respects current value. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:**

| Card | Relationship | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [shopify.inventory_value](/nerve-centre/shopify/inventory_value) | Different definition | Shopify's inventory value is at "cost" set on each Variant; not always landed cost. Use NetSuite as truth. |
| [bigcommerce.inventory_value](/nerve-centre/bigcommerce/inventory_value) | Same caveat | BC inventory cost field is per-variant; landed cost rarely reflected. |
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**What's a healthy inventory ratio?**
Wholesale apparel: 3-4 months of COGS. Electronics: 2-3 months. Food/beverage: &lt;1 month. Compare to industry benchmarks; the absolute number isn't the metric, the ratio to COGS is.

**Average vs FIFO vs Standard, which is "right"?**
Whatever your accounting policy says. NetSuite supports all. The card uses whatever your Item records dictate. Average is most common for mid-market commerce; FIFO for lot-tracked goods; Standard for manufactured items.

**In-transit, included or excluded?**
Default included. The Item record's location-level breakdown shows the split. Exclude via field map if your treasury policy treats in-transit as supplier liability.

**Multi-currency inventory, how is it valued?**
Each subsidiary holds inventory in its base currency. Consolidated total in reporting currency at current FX. Inventory FX revaluation runs at month-end.

**WIP (Work-in-Progress) for manufacturing?**
Included if NetSuite Manufacturing is enabled. WIP carries the partially-built items at cost.

**OneWorld vs single-subsidiary?**
Identical. OneWorld just consolidates across subsidiaries.

**Why is Total Inventory rising while sales are flat?**
Three usual reasons: (1) over-ordering from suppliers; (2) demand drop on previously-fast SKUs; (3) longer lead times causing safety stock to balloon. Drill into [Inventory Aging](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/netsuite/inventory-aging) and [Dead Stock](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/netsuite/dead-stock-value).

**Stock revaluation impact?**
NetSuite's standard cost recalculation can adjust inventory value. The card respects current state; a revaluation run will move the headline.

**Lot / serial-numbered items?**
Valued at lot-specific cost. Card shows cumulative.
