## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> Real-time alert table: invoices >$50,000 that are >60 days overdue. The "phone today" list for the AR clerk and Controller. Each row is one invoice; columns include customer, invoice amount, days overdue, customer's total AR balance, customer credit utilisation, last-contact-date. Sized to the highest-priority horizon for AR effort: a single $50k+ invoice 60+d overdue is more important than 50 small overdue invoices combined.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Trigger threshold** | `Invoice WHERE amount > $50,000 AND days_overdue > 60`. Threshold dollar amount configurable per merchant; defaults set for mid-market distribution. |
| **Signal interpretation** | High-value invoices >60d overdue are typically NOT slipping through routine processes; somebody at the customer is intentionally delaying. Causes: (a) customer in financial trouble (bankruptcy precursor); (b) dispute (unresolved billing or quality issue); (c) AP team turnover at the customer; (d) the customer's bank-line is restrictive and they are stretching payables. |
| **Recommended action** | (1) Phone the customer's AR / AP contact within 24h of alert firing. (2) Cross-reference [Customer Credit Utilisation](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/netsuite/customer-credit-utilisation): if customer is at 100% utilisation, place new orders on credit hold. (3) Cross-reference [Customer Churn Signals](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/netsuite/customer-churn-signals): if also showing churn signals, this is bankruptcy / churn precursor; escalate to credit committee. |
| **Time window** | `RT` |
| **Roles** | owner, finance, AR clerk, Controller |
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
A US wholesale distributor on NetSuite OneWorld. Snapshot 04 May 26.

**Alert payload:** 6 invoices >$50k and >60 days overdue. Total at risk: $1,420,000.

| Invoice | Customer | Amount | Days overdue | Credit util | Last contact | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| INV-84412 | Premier Construction | $480,000 | 78 | 100% | 14 days ago | AP contact on parental leave |
| INV-84520 | Cobb Industrial | $284,000 | 92 | 95% | 6 days ago | Dispute on freight charges |
| INV-84481 | Hartwell Junior Stores | $186,000 | 68 | 88% | 22 days ago | Sales rep transition |
| INV-84455 | Bertram & Sons | $182,000 | 71 | 92% | 8 days ago | Cash-flow stretching |
| INV-84539 | Western Steel Co | $168,000 | 64 | 78% | 11 days ago | Recent CS escalation |
| INV-84612 | Coastal Marine Supply | $120,000 | 62 | 84% | 4 days ago | Q1 collection cycle |

**Investigation actions:**

1. **Premier Construction $480k**: AP contact on parental leave 4 weeks. Action: AR clerk to phone the secondary contact (CFO directly). Escalate to procurement director if needed. Estimated recovery within 14 days.
2. **Cobb Industrial $284k**: dispute on freight charges. Action: Sales rep + Controller joint call to resolve dispute, issue credit memo for legitimate freight credit, secure remainder. Estimated recovery $250k within 7 days.
3. **Hartwell Junior Stores $186k**: sales rep transition disrupted relationship. Action: re-introduce new sales rep + AR clerk joint call. Recovery within 14 days.
4. **Bertram & Sons $182k**: customer is intentionally stretching payables (cash flow tight). Action: payment plan discussion (4-week structured) + place new orders on credit hold pending. Recovery $182k over 4 weeks.
5. **Western Steel $168k**: recent CS escalation (damaged shipment claim). Action: resolve the underlying complaint, then collect.
6. **Coastal Marine $120k**: routine slow-payer (Q1 always lags). Less urgent; standard reminder cadence.

**Total estimated recovery within 30 days:** $1,140,000 of the $1,420,000.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**The same invoices show up week after week. Why?**
Because they remain unpaid; the alert is real-time and reflects the live AR state. Aged unpaid invoices stay on the alert until paid, written off, or sold to a collection agency.

**Should I write off invoices >180 days overdue?**
Depends on collection-agency strategy. Most merchants write off >90% of invoices >180 days old as bad debt; before that, persistent collection effort is warranted. Cross-reference your bad-debt history.

**Can I see this per-sales-rep for accountability?**
Yes via Ask Viq. Useful when sales-rep compensation is tied to collected revenue (some commission plans claw back unpaid revenue beyond N days).

**My OneWorld account: per-subsidiary?**
Yes. Each subsidiary's overdue list runs independently.

**An invoice is overdue but I know the customer is paying tomorrow. Suppress?**
Configure a "promise to pay" custom field. Vortex IQ suppresses alerts on invoices with a future-dated promise. Auto-resolves when the date passes.

**Why $50k and 60d as defaults?**
Empirical. >$50k is "material" for a mid-market AR balance; &lt;60 days is "routine" delinquency. Together, the intersection is the priority-call list. Tune per merchant.
