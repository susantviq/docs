Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **24** KPI pulses for DHL InExpress stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The DHL InExpress connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to merchants shipping international parcels via DHL's reseller network for cross-border express service into 220+ countries. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 24 KPI pulses with hero alerts on *Avg Shipping Cost*, *On-Time Delivery Rate*, *International Express OTD*, *Late Shipments*, *Exception Rate*, *Open Claims*, *Days to Token Expiry*, plus a cross-channel *Customs Dwell Time by Lane* card so a slow border lane surfaces before customers escalate. **Vortex Mind** investigates duty-billing mismatches and route degradation; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the consignment.
