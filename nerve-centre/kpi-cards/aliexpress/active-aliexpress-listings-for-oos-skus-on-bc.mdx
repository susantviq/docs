## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Active AliExpress Listings for OOS SKUs on BC** counts SKUs that are out of stock in BigCommerce but still active and buyable on AliExpress. BigCommerce is the source of truth for stock, so a SKU sold out on BC has nothing to fulfil, yet the AliExpress listing keeps taking orders. On a cross-border marketplace this is the most dispute-prone kind of oversell: AliExpress buyers already accept long shipping lead times, so they will wait, but they will not tolerate a cancellation after waiting. Every listing in this count is a cancellation and a dispute waiting to happen, which damages seller ratings on top of the lost order. The alert fires the moment the count rises above zero, because even one of these is a live liability.

## Reconciling against AliExpress Seller Center

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in AliExpress Seller Center:**

In Product Management the affected SKUs will still show as active with stock available, because Seller Center only knows the AliExpress side. The out-of-stock truth lives in BigCommerce, and the stock sync usually runs through the Feedonomics feed layer, so confirm the BC stock level and the last successful sync rather than trusting Seller Center alone.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sync lag.** A BC stock change may be mid-propagation, so the listing is briefly counted before AliExpress catches up. | Marginal | Recheck after the next refresh before pausing the listing. |
| **Buffer or safety stock.** Some merchants intentionally keep AliExpress sellable on a buffer beyond BC zero. | Variable | Whitelist intentional buffer SKUs so they stop counting. |
| **Separate fulfilment stock.** A SKU may be fulfilled from a warehouse BC does not track. | Variable | Confirm the true fulfilment source before treating it as an oversell. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** reconcile the AliExpress listing state, the BigCommerce stock level, and the last Feedonomics sync to confirm a true oversell rather than a sync gap. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Active AliExpress Listings for OOS SKUs on BC update?**
It refreshes in near real time on the standard data refresh, so a new oversell risk surfaces within a refresh cycle of the BC stock hitting zero. Force a manual refresh after a known sell-out to catch it immediately.

**Q: Why does my AliExpress Seller Center show a different number?**
Seller Center shows the listing as active with stock because it cannot see the BigCommerce source. The count reflects BC being out of stock, so reconcile against BC stock and the last Feedonomics sync, not Seller Center.

**Q: How does Active AliExpress Listings for OOS SKUs on BC relate to other metrics?**
It is the sharpest of the cross-channel revenue-at-risk cards. Pair it with Listings Drifting from BC for the broader stock-drift picture and with Dispute Rate to see the downstream cost of unfulfilled orders.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
