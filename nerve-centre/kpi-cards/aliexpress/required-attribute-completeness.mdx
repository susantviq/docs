## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Required-Attribute Completeness** measures the share of your AliExpress listings that have filled in every attribute their category requires, things like size, material, brand, and other category-specific fields. AliExpress uses these attributes to power search, filters, and category placement, so incomplete listings lose visibility and can be down-ranked or blocked at audit. For an owner or operations lead this gauge is a direct lever on discoverability, because attributes are far cheaper to fix than ad spend and pay off across every impression. It sits close to `aex_listing_quality_score` for the broader content picture and `aex_alert_listing_rejection_spike`, since missing attributes are a common rejection trigger.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it counts** | The percentage of active listings that have all of their category-required attributes populated. |
| **Sample type** | Backend API data from AliExpress, refreshed continuously as listings are edited and re-evaluated. |
| **Why it matters** | Complete attributes drive search and filter visibility; incomplete ones cost discoverability and can lead to down-ranking or rejection. |
| **Reading the value** | Read it as a health gauge where higher is better and a dip means newly added or edited listings have gaps. Watch for drops after bulk uploads. |
| **Currency** | Percent. |
| **Time window** | `RT` |
| **Alert trigger** | `<90%` |
| **Sentiment key** | `aex_attribute_completeness` |
| **Roles** | owner, operations |
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

## Calculation

Vortex IQ checks each active listing against the required attribute set for its AliExpress category and reports the proportion that are fully populated as a single percentage. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
*A representative reading of **Required-Attribute Completeness** for a typical mid-size AliExpress catalogue.* On 17 Mar 26 the gauge reads 86 percent, below the 90 percent threshold, after a batch of new apparel listings went live without the material and size-type fields. The operations lead drills in, sees the gap is concentrated in one category, applies the missing attributes in a bulk edit, and watches the gauge climb back above threshold over the next refresh. Because the fix lands before audit, the affected listings regain filter visibility without ever being rejected. ## Reconciling against AliExpress Seller Center

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in AliExpress Seller Center:**

Open Product Management, edit a sample listing, and review the category attribute panel where required fields are marked, or use any listing diagnostics view that flags incomplete products.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Category rule changes.** AliExpress can add required attributes to a category, instantly lowering completeness. | Variable | Re-check the category requirements after any audit-rule update. |
| **Optional vs required scope.** Seller Center may show optional fields alongside required ones. | Marginal | Compare only the genuinely required fields when reconciling. |
| **Edit lag.** A bulk edit takes a sync to reflect in the gauge. | Marginal | Allow one refresh cycle before treating a fix as missed. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** If listings come from a source catalogue, compare against `aex_listings_drifting_from_bc` to confirm the gap is on AliExpress and not inherited from the feed. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Required-Attribute Completeness update?**
It updates in near real time, re-evaluating listings as they are created or edited, so a bulk attribute fix shows up in the gauge within the next sync.

**Q: Why does my AliExpress Seller Center show a different number?**
Seller Center may count optional fields, apply a different category rule version, or lag your most recent edits; period boundary, time zone, and field scope all contribute to small gaps.

**Q: How does Required-Attribute Completeness relate to other listing health metrics?**
It is a key driver behind `aex_listing_quality_score` and a leading indicator for `aex_alert_listing_rejection_spike`.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, alert thresholds are configurable per profile in the Alert Rules tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
