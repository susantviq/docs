## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Store Level (Tier1/Gold/Platinum)** reports your AliExpress store level, the seller-tier ranking the marketplace assigns from your overall performance. Higher tiers, in the Gold, Platinum, and Diamond style progression, earn trust badges, better search placement, and a measurable buyer-trust premium at the point of decision. For an owner or marketing lead this is a strategic standing pulse rather than a daily worklist, because a tier is hard-won over months of consistent fulfilment and ratings, and a drop quietly cuts your search visibility and conversion at the same time. It pairs with `aex_aliexpress_seller_health_score`, the composite that drives the tier, and with `aex_detailed_seller_ratings_match_comm_ship`, one of its key inputs.

## Reconciling against AliExpress Seller Center

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in AliExpress Seller Center:**

Open the store performance or seller-level overview, where AliExpress displays your current tier and the metrics that determine it.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Review cadence.** AliExpress recalculates the tier on its own periodic cycle, so the card mirrors the last published level. | Variable | Treat the tier as a periodic verdict, not a live-moving number. |
| **Input lag.** The underlying ratings and fulfilment inputs update before the tier itself is re-evaluated. | Variable | Watch the input cards for early warning ahead of a tier change. |
| **Naming differences.** Tier labels may differ by program or region. | Marginal | Confirm the label scheme your store is enrolled in when comparing. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** There is no direct cross-connector equivalent, but the fulfilment and dispute inputs can be sanity-checked against your connected store data. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Store Level (Tier1/Gold/Platinum) update?**
The card refreshes in near real time, but the tier itself only changes when AliExpress completes its periodic performance review, so the value reflects the last published level.

**Q: Why does my AliExpress Seller Center show a different number?**
Differences usually come from the review cadence, input lag before the tier is re-evaluated, or tier-naming differences between programs or regions.

**Q: How does Store Level (Tier1/Gold/Platinum) relate to other performance metrics?**
It is the headline outcome; `aex_aliexpress_seller_health_score`, `aex_detailed_seller_ratings_match_comm_ship`, `aex_shipping_time_accuracy`, and `aex_dispute_rate` are the inputs that move it.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
