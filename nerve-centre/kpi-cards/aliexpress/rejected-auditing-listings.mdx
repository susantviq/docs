## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Rejected / Auditing Listings** counts every AliExpress listing that the platform has either placed under audit or rejected outright. AliExpress reviews listings for intellectual property infringement, restricted or prohibited goods, image quality, and required attributes, and while a listing is in auditing it is pending and a rejected listing is hidden from buyers until the seller corrects it. For an owner or operations lead this is a direct revenue-protection pulse, because a hidden listing earns nothing and a rejection that goes unactioned quietly removes a product from search. It pairs with `aex_required_attribute_completeness`, which often explains why a listing was held, and with `aex_active_listings`, the storefront count that rejections silently erode.

## Calculation

Vortex IQ counts the listings AliExpress reports as currently in an auditing or rejected state, the platform's own labels for products held in review or blocked from display. Any listing in either state is surfaced as an action row. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
*A representative reading of **Rejected / Auditing Listings** for a typical cross-border AliExpress store.* On 09 Mar 26 the card jumps from 0 to 5 and the alert fires. The operations lead opens the list and finds four listings rejected for low-resolution main images and one held for a missing brand attribute. They re-shoot and re-upload the four images, complete the attribute on the fifth, and resubmit all five for audit the same day. By 11 Mar 26 the listings have cleared review and the card is back to zero, restoring the products to search before any meaningful sales were lost. ## Reconciling against AliExpress Seller Center

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in AliExpress Seller Center:**

Open the Products area and filter to listings in review or rejected status; the platform usually annotates each with the rejection reason and required correction.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Audit-state lag.** A resubmitted listing may still show as pending until the platform completes its review. | Variable | Allow for review time before treating a flagged listing as unresolved. |
| **Filter scope.** Seller Center views can carry category or status filters that exclude some listings. | Variable | Clear filters so both views cover the same listing set. |
| **Reason grouping.** Seller Center may separate auditing from rejected; this card combines both into one worklist. | Marginal | Use the Seller Center reason annotation to split the count when triaging. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** If you syndicate listings from a connected store, compare against your BigCommerce or Shopify product status to confirm whether the source product itself is the cause. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Rejected / Auditing Listings update?**
It refreshes in near real time, so a listing clears from the count within the next sync once AliExpress moves it back to an active state.

**Q: Why does my AliExpress Seller Center show a different number?**
Differences usually come from audit-state lag, a category or status filter applied in Seller Center, or the way Seller Center splits auditing from rejected while this card combines them.

**Q: How does Rejected / Auditing Listings relate to other listing-health metrics?**
It is the enforcement endpoint of a chain; `aex_required_attribute_completeness` and `aex_listing_quality_score` are upstream causes, and `aex_active_listings` is the storefront total it reduces.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, alert thresholds are configurable per profile in the Alert Rules tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
