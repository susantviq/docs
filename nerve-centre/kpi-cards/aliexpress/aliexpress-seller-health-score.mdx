## Calculation

Vortex IQ pulls your AliExpress account-health inputs, including Detailed Seller Ratings, dispute rate, on-time dispatch, and listing compliance, normalises each onto a common scale, and combines them into a single weighted composite expressed out of 100. The score refreshes as the underlying inputs update, with rolling-window inputs reflected on a 7-day reference. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Reconciling against AliExpress Seller Center

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in AliExpress Seller Center:**

Open the Service Scores or store performance dashboard where AliExpress reports your account health, Detailed Seller Ratings, and dispute figures, the same inputs that feed this composite.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Composite weighting.** AliExpress publishes inputs separately; Vortex IQ blends them into one score, so the headline number will not match any single Seller Center figure. | Variable | Read the composite for direction and drill into siblings for the exact input. |
| **Refresh cadence.** Some Seller Center inputs update on their own periodic schedule rather than in real time. | Marginal | Allow each input its own reporting lag before treating a gap as an error. |
| **Window framing.** Rolling-window inputs are referenced over 7 days here, which can differ from the closed window Seller Center reports. | Variable | Confirm both views cover the same period when comparing a specific input. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** Compare the score against your fulfilment and dispute activity on any connected BigCommerce or Shopify store to confirm the operational picture lines up. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does AliExpress Seller Health Score update?**
It refreshes in real time as its inputs change, with rolling-window inputs reflected over a 7-day reference, so an improvement in dispatch or disputes lifts the score at the next sync.

**Q: Why does my AliExpress Seller Center show a different number?**
Seller Center reports each health input separately, while this card blends them into one composite; the weighting, each input's own refresh cadence, and window framing all explain why the headline number differs.

**Q: How does AliExpress Seller Health Score relate to other account metrics?**
It is the roll-up of the same inputs surfaced individually in `aex_dispute_rate`, `aex_detail_rating_summary`, and `aex_late_shipments`, and it ultimately defends `aex_store_level_tier`.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
