## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Detailed Seller Ratings (Match / Comm / Ship)** tracks the three axes buyers score you on after every AliExpress order: how well the item matched its description, how good your communication was, and how fast you shipped. These Detailed Seller Ratings feed your store level and influence search placement, so they are reputation and discoverability rolled into one. For an owner, marketer, or operations lead this card is a strategic mirror, because a single weak axis can tell you whether the problem is your listings, your service, or your dispatch. It pairs with `aex_seller_feedback_score` for the overall reputation view and `aex_store_level_tier` for the placement consequence.

## Calculation

Vortex IQ reads the buyer-submitted Detailed Seller Ratings on each of the three axes over the 30-day window and compares them with the previous period to show direction of travel. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Reconciling against AliExpress Seller Center

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in AliExpress Seller Center:**

Open your store reputation or seller-rating dashboard, where the three Detailed Seller Rating axes are reported alongside their recent trend.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Rating window.** Seller Center may show a different rolling period than this 30-day view. | Variable | Match the comparison window before reading a gap as real movement. |
| **Late ratings.** Buyers can rate well after delivery, so scores keep settling. | Marginal | Allow recent orders to mature before treating a dip as final. |
| **Axis weighting.** How an overall score is derived from the three axes can differ. | Marginal | Compare axis by axis rather than relying on a single blended number. |

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Detailed Seller Ratings (Match / Comm / Ship) update?**
It refreshes across the 30-day window as new buyer ratings arrive, so scores move gradually rather than in sharp steps, and the comparison to the prior period shifts with each refresh.

**Q: Why does my AliExpress Seller Center show a different number?**
Seller Center may use a different rolling window, weight the axes differently when forming an overall score, or include ratings that are still settling; period boundary and time zone account for the rest.

**Q: How does Detailed Seller Ratings (Match / Comm / Ship) relate to other economics metrics?**
The three axes roll up into `aex_seller_feedback_score` and feed `aex_store_level_tier`, while a weak axis often surfaces first in `aex_dispute_rate` or `aex_alert_dispatch_sla_miss`.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
