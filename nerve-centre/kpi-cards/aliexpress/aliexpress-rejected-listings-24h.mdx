## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **AliExpress Rejected Listings (24h)** counts the listings AliExpress has rejected during its audit in the last day, whether for suspected IP infringement, restricted or prohibited goods, poor image quality, or missing required attributes. A rejected listing is hidden from buyers until you fix and resubmit it, so a sudden spike usually points at a single root cause such as a bad feed push, a new image template, or a category rule change. For an owner or operations lead this card is an early-warning siren on catalogue health, because hidden listings stop selling the moment they fail audit. It works hand in hand with `aex_rejected_auditing_listings` for the standing backlog and `aex_required_attribute_completeness` for the most common fixable cause.

## Reconciling against AliExpress Seller Center

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in AliExpress Seller Center:**

Open Product Management and filter to listings in a rejected or failed-audit state, then review the rejection reason shown against each item.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Window framing.** This card is strictly the trailing 24 hours, while Seller Center may show all rejected items regardless of age. | Variable | Compare against a same-day filter in Seller Center, not the full backlog. |
| **Audit timing.** Rejections post as the audit completes, which may straddle the window edge. | Marginal | Allow a short settling period before treating counts as final. |
| **Resubmission churn.** A listing fixed and re-rejected can appear more than once in a day. | Marginal | Treat repeat rejections as a single unresolved issue when triaging. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** If listings originate from a connected catalogue, compare against `aex_listings_drifting_from_bc` to see whether a source-feed change triggered the rejections. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does AliExpress Rejected Listings (24h) update?**
It tracks a rolling 24-hour window and refreshes as new audit outcomes arrive, so a fresh rejection appears within the next sync and an item ages out of the count a day after it was rejected.

**Q: Why does my AliExpress Seller Center show a different number?**
Seller Center often lists every rejected item regardless of age, while this card is limited to the last 24 hours; differences come from window scope, the Seller Center time zone, and which audit states are included in the filter.

**Q: How does AliExpress Rejected Listings (24h) relate to other listing health metrics?**
It is the acute signal feeding the standing view in `aex_rejected_auditing_listings`, and its most common root cause is tracked by `aex_required_attribute_completeness`.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
