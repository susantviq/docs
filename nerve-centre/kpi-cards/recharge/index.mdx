Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **16** KPI pulses for Recharge stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Recharge connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to Shopify-first subscription brands using Recharge for recurring orders, subscription management, and customer-portal flows. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 16 KPI pulses on the payment side of subscription health — *Success Rate*, *Decline Rate*, *3DS Abandonment Rate*, *Refund Rate*, *Chargeback Rate*, *Dispute Rate*, *Avg Settlement Time*, *Pending Payouts*, *Top Decline Reasons*, and *Top Payment Methods* — so renewal failures surface alongside D2C order metrics. **Vortex Mind** investigates when decline patterns concentrate on a card scheme or BIN range; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the transaction.
