The Constant Contact connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to small businesses on Constant Contact's email marketing platform - long-established US tool popular with retail, restaurants, non-profits, and local-service brands. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 29 KPI pulses including subscriber, deliverability, engagement, and revenue-attribution metrics. **Vortex Mind** investigates when a number moves; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
