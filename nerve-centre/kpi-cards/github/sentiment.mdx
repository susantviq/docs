[KPI Cards](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards) / [GitHub](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/github) / **Sentiment**

Cards carrying a sensitivity band flip to Action Needed when the live value crosses the bad threshold. Bands are shared across connectors on the same archetype and tunable per store in Alert Rules.

| Card | Direction | Good | Bad |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Pipeline Health Score](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/github/pipeline-health-score) | Higher is better | 90 | 70 |
| [Commits (30d)](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/github/commits-30d) | Trend (drop is the alarm) | 0 | -50 |
| [CI Pass Rate (30d)](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/github/ci-pass-rate-30d) | Higher is better | 95 | 80 |
| [Median Open PR Age](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/github/median-open-pr-age) | Lower is better | 3 | 14 |
| [Deploy Cadence Trend](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/github/deploy-cadence-trend) | Trend (drop is the alarm) | 0 | -50 |
| [Deploy Stall Alert](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/github/deploy-stall-alert) | Trend (drop is the alarm) | 0 | -50 |
| [PR Age Alert](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/github/pr-age-alert) | Lower is better | 3 | 14 |
