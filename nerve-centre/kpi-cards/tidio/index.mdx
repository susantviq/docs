Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **23** KPI pulses for Tidio stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Tidio connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to ecommerce brands running Tidio — the live-chat + chatbot platform purpose-built for Shopify and small-store ecommerce. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 23 KPI pulses focused on **how Tidio handles findings the AI OS pushes back to it**: hero alerts on *VortexIQ Findings Open*, *Finding Resolution Rate (90d)*, *Abandoned Findings (>14d no movement)*, plus throughput and backlog-health metrics. **Vortex Mind** investigates when findings stall or resolution rate dips; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
