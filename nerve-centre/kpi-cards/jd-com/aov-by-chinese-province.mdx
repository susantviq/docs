## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **AOV by Chinese Province** breaks your average order value down across the Chinese provinces, municipalities and autonomous regions your JD.com buyers ship to. China's regional markets are not uniform: tier-1 cities like Beijing, Shanghai and the Guangdong coast tend to carry higher basket values than inland and western provinces, and provincial demand patterns shift the mix of what people buy. Reading AOV by province tells you where your premium buyers are, where bundling or free-shipping thresholds could lift baskets, and where regional pricing or assortment is leaving money on the table.

## Calculation

For each Chinese province over the trailing 30-day window, the card divides total order revenue (in CNY) by the number of orders shipped to that province, giving a per-province average order value. Provinces are derived from the buyer's shipping address, so an order is attributed to the destination region rather than the buyer's registered address. The bar chart ranks provinces so the highest and lowest baskets are immediately visible against the blended national AOV.

## Reconciling against the vendor's own dashboard

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in JD.com's own dashboard:**

JD's seller analytics include regional and geographic breakdowns of orders and sales, usually under a buyer-distribution or regional-sales report. AOV is typically derived there rather than shown directly, so reconcile by checking that the per-province revenue and order counts match before comparing the computed average.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Province attribution.** Vortex IQ attributes by shipping destination; the vendor view may split by billing or buyer-registered region. | Variable | Confirm the attribution basis matches. |

| **Order definition.** Cancelled or refunded orders may be treated differently in the AOV denominator. | Variable | Align the order-status filter. |

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does AOV by Chinese Province update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). The 30-day window means individual orders have a small marginal effect on each province's average.

**Q: A small province shows a wild AOV. Why?**
Provinces with very few orders in the window produce a high-variance average, so a single large or small basket can swing the figure. Read low-volume provinces alongside the orders-by-province sibling card and treat their AOV as indicative rather than precise.

**Q: Why does my JD dashboard show a different provincial split?**
The usual reasons are attribution basis (shipping destination vs registered region), period boundary (30-day rolling vs calendar month), and how cancelled or refunded orders are handled in the average. Match these before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
This card is a comparison view and ships without an alert by default. If you want to be alerted when a key province's AOV drops below a baseline, configure a threshold per profile in the Alert Rules tab.
