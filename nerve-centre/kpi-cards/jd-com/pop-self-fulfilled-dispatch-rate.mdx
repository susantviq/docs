## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **POP Self-Fulfilled Dispatch Rate** measures, for the orders you ship yourself rather than through JD Logistics, the share you dispatched inside JD's ship-deadline window. This is the speed metric you control directly. JD Direct (1P) and JD-Logistics orders are handled by JD, but for POP marketplace orders fulfilled in-house, JD holds you to a dispatch clock, and your hit rate against it feeds the Logistics axis of your DSR. Fall below roughly 90% and you are accumulating late dispatches, eroding ratings, and risking Premium Buyer eligibility. It sits in the Fulfilment family and reads best beside the pending-shipment, SLA, and late-dispatch cards below.

## Calculation

POP self-fulfilled orders dispatched within JD's ship-deadline divided by total POP self-fulfilled orders in the window, as a percentage. An order is on-time when its dispatch scan is recorded before the deadline JD sets for that order. Orders shipped after the deadline count against the rate; cancelled-before-dispatch orders drop out. JD Logistics and JD Direct (1P) orders are excluded, because JD, not you, controls their dispatch, the JD Logistics SLA card covers those. The result is a clean read on your own dispatch performance.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
*A representative reading of **POP Self-Fulfilled Dispatch Rate** for a typical merchant on JD.com.* Suppose 1,800 of your 2,000 self-fulfilled orders shipped before their deadline last 30 days. That is 90%, right on the floor and down from 94% the prior period. The gauge sits amber with a downward arrow. Cross-referencing pending-shipment shows a backlog that built during a promotion, and the late dispatches cluster on orders placed late in the day that missed the same-day handover cut-off. Two fixes follow: move the dispatch cut-off earlier so late-day orders still make the deadline, and add capacity during promotion peaks. Recovering the rate above 90% protects the Logistics DSR before it slips. ## Reconciling against the vendor's own dashboard

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in JD.com's own dashboard:**

In JD's Seller Centre, the on-time dispatch or ship-rate metric under fulfilment performance is the direct equivalent, scoped to seller-fulfilled orders. Confirm the report isolates POP self-fulfilment (excluding JD Logistics and 1P) and that the period matches the Vortex IQ 30-day window.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Dispatch vs delivery.** This card measures on-time dispatch, not on-time delivery; do not compare it against a delivery-based figure. | Variable | Confirm the vendor metric is dispatch-based. |
| **Order scope.** Vortex IQ isolates POP self-fulfilment; JD reports may fold in JD Logistics orders. | Variable | Exclude JD Logistics and 1P in the vendor filter. |
| **Deadline basis.** JD sets per-order ship-deadlines; ensure the vendor report uses the same deadline definition. | Variable | Confirm the deadline definition. |
| **Period and time zone.** Vortex IQ uses a 30-day rolling window in China Standard Time. | Marginal | Match the date range and time zone. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** read with pending-shipment to see whether a falling rate is caused by a backlog, and with the late-dispatch card to confirm the DSR impact. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does POP Self-Fulfilled Dispatch Rate update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). Dispatch scans can lag the physical handover by a short window. For real-time signals, force a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my JD.com dashboard show a different number?**
The common causes are dispatch-versus-delivery basis (this card is dispatch-based), order scope (whether JD Logistics and 1P orders are folded in), and the deadline definition. Match those three before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: My delivery times look fine but this rate dropped. Why?**
This card measures dispatch against the ship-deadline, not delivery. You can dispatch late but still have parcels arrive on time if the carrier moves fast. The dispatch clock is what JD scores for the Logistics DSR, so a late dispatch counts against you even when delivery lands well.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, the floor (default 90%) is configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Tighten it if you compete on dispatch speed, or align it to JD's current ship-rate requirement for your category rather than relying on the generic default.
