The JD.com connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to brands selling on JD.com - China's second-largest ecommerce platform after Alibaba/Tmall, strong on first-party logistics and electronics + appliances categories. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 17 KPI pulses including listings, sales, reputation, returns, and marketplace economics. **Vortex Mind** investigates when a number moves; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
