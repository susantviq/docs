Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **18** KPI pulses for Grafana stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Grafana connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to teams using Grafana as the open-source visualisation layer over Prometheus, Loki, Tempo, and other data sources for metrics, logs, and traces. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 18 KPI pulses covering *Error Rate*, *p95 / p99 Latency*, *Apdex Score*, *MTTA / MTTR*, *Incidents Open*, *Services Healthy / Degraded / Down*, *SLA Compliance*, and *Top Error Types* - so dashboard panels flow into the AI OS without re-pivoting in Grafana itself. **Vortex Mind** investigates when alert volume spikes on a service; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the panel.
