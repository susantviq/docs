Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **96** KPI pulses for Google Search Console properties. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Google Search Console connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to SEO teams - the canonical source for organic search clicks, impressions, average position, CTR and indexing health. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 96 KPI pulses for GSC properties, spanning search performance (clicks, impressions, CTR, position), keyword and query analysis, page and geographic performance, indexing and sitemap coverage, rich results, and cross-channel revenue-at-risk signals. **Vortex Mind** investigates when organic visibility moves; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
