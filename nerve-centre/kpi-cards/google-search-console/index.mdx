Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **13** KPI pulses for Google Search Console stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

The Google Search Console connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to SEO teams - the canonical source for organic search impressions, clicks, position, and indexing health. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 13 KPI pulses for GSC properties (Sessions, New Users, Returning Users, Real-Time Users, Avg Session Duration, Pageviews per Session, Bounce Rate, Cart Abandonment Rate, Session Conversion Rate, Goal Completions, Top Pages, Top Traffic Sources). **Vortex Mind** investigates when organic visibility moves; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
