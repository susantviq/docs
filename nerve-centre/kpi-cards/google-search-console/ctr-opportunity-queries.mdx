## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **CTR Opportunity Queries** is a ranked table, broken down by row (one query per row), of search terms whose click-through rate falls short of the CTR expected for their average position. Each row carries impressions, position, actual CTR, expected CTR and the estimated recoverable clicks, turning the abstract "improve your CTR" advice into a query-by-query target list sorted by upside.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

## What it tracks

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:whatitracks */}
The card reads Google Search Console Search Analytics data with the `query` dimension (clicks, impressions, average position) and compares each query's actual CTR against the expected CTR for its position band. Opportunity is sized as `impressions × (expected CTR − actual CTR)`, the clicks the query forfeits at its current ranking. Because it is query-level, it tells you which exact search intents your snippet is failing to capture, which is more actionable than a page-level view when one URL ranks for many queries: you can tune the title and meta description (or add structured data to win a richer search appearance) toward the highest-opportunity intents. It is the query-dimension twin of [CTR Opportunity Pages](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/google-search-console/ctr-opportunity-pages); pair it with [Branded vs Non-Branded](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/google-search-console/branded-vs-non-branded) so you do not chase CTR on branded terms that already convert, and with [CTR by Position Bucket](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/google-search-console/ctr-by-position-bucket) for the benchmark behind each expected-CTR figure.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:whatitracks */}

## Reconciling against the source

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
Reconcile the raw inputs in the Search Console Performance report, **Queries** tab, with Clicks, Impressions, CTR and Position enabled, matching property, Web search type and date range. The expected-CTR and recoverable-clicks columns are Vortex IQ calculations and will not appear in Google's UI. Keep in mind the data is 2 to 3 days delayed, rare queries are anonymised (so some low-volume terms never surface), and the UI and per-query export both cap at 1,000 rows.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
