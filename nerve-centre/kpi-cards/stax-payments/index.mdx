Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **16** KPI pulses for Stax Payments stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Stax Payments connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to merchants using Stax (formerly Fattmerchant) for flat-rate subscription-pricing card processing across in-person, online, and B2B channels in the US. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 16 KPI pulses covering *Success Rate*, *Decline Rate*, *3DS Abandonment Rate*, *Refund Rate*, *Chargeback Rate*, *Dispute Rate*, *Avg Settlement Time*, *Pending Payouts*, *Top Decline Reasons*, and *Top Payment Methods*. **Vortex Mind** investigates when decline patterns shift or chargebacks spike; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the transaction.
