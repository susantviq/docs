## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> Average EasyPost label cost broken out by shipping zone (the distance band between your despatch origin and the destination) over the trailing 30 days. Carrier pricing is zone-based, so this horizontal bar chart shows where your parcels are expensive: a heavy tail in the far zones points at distant customers, a missing warehouse, or a rate-shop rule that is not picking the cheapest carrier for that lane.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

## What it tracks

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:whattracks */}
Grounded in the card's `detail` ("Cost by Zone."): the card reads the bought rate on every EasyPost shipment (`GET /v2/shipments`, the `selected_rate` plus surcharges and the per-label fee) and groups it by the zone derived from origin and destination postcodes. For US carriers this is the USPS / UPS / FedEx zone (Zone 1 nearby through Zone 8 furthest); for international it groups by destination region. Each bar is the average cost per parcel in that zone over the 30-day window, so a longer bar means a more expensive lane, not necessarily more volume. Read it next to [Shipments by Destination](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/easypost/shipments-by-destination) to weight cost by where your parcels actually go, and next to [Avg Shipping Cost](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/easypost/avg-shipping-cost) for the blended headline. A persistent far-zone cost bulge is the classic signal to evaluate a second despatch location or a regional carrier for that band. This card carries no alert; it is a diagnostic surface, not a pager.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:whattracks */}

## Reconciling against the source

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
Cross-check against the EasyPost dashboard billing view (filter shipments by date and group by zone) and against each underlying carrier's own zone-rate chart in the carrier portal. Small differences come from surcharge timing and the EasyPost per-label fee; large gaps usually mean a rate-shop rule is overriding the cheapest carrier for that zone. For a like-for-like comparison against direct carrier contracts see [Cost per Parcel by Zone vs Direct-Carrier Pricing](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/easypost/cost-per-parcel-by-zone-vs-direct-carrier-pricing).
