Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **23** KPI pulses for Monday.com stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Monday.com connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to teams running Monday — the visual work-OS popular with mid-market across sales, marketing, and operations. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 23 KPI pulses focused on how Monday handles findings the AI OS pushes back to it: hero alerts on *VortexIQ Findings Open*, *Finding Resolution Rate (90d)*, *Abandoned Findings (>14d no movement)*, plus throughput and backlog-health metrics. **Vortex Mind** investigates when findings stall or resolution rate dips; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
