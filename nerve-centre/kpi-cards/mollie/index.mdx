The Mollie connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to merchants on Europe's most popular alternative payments platform - strong on iDEAL, Bancontact, and other regional EU methods alongside cards. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 16 KPI pulses for Mollie accounts (Success Rate, Decline Rate, Refund Rate, Chargeback Rate, Dispute Rate, settlement metrics, Top Decline Reasons, Top Payment Methods, Volume Trend). **Vortex Mind** investigates when a number moves; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
