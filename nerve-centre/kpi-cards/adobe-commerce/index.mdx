Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **59** KPI pulses for Adobe Commerce stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Adobe Commerce (Magento) connector is built for the multi-store, multi-environment B2B + B2C complexity Adobe merchants live in. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 59 KPI pulses for Adobe Commerce stores - including the B2B-specific cards (*B2B Accounts Gone Quiet*, *B2B AOV vs B2C AOV*, *B2B Revenue Share*) that Adobe Analytics doesn't surface natively, plus the cross-platform *Catalogue Drift (Adobe ↔ Amazon)* alert. **Vortex Mind** explains why a B2B account went quiet or why catalogue drift opened up; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English, scoped per Profile so the B2B and DTC questions stay separate.
