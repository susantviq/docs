Audits product catalog completeness and inventory hygiene against the connector's commerce API.

## What this audit checks

### Product completeness
- Missing meta description (SEO)
- Missing product weight / shipping data
- Missing or short product titles
- Zero-priced visible products
- Missing alt text on product images

### Inventory tracking
- Inventory tracking enabled per variant
- Out-of-stock products still visible
- Negative or implausible stock levels

### Schema & structured data
- Product schema markup present
- Breadcrumb structure validity

### Order & customer data hygiene
- Orders stuck in unfulfilled or failed payment state
- Customers with unverified email addresses
- Refund and cancellation rates within expected band
