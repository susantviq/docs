Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **77** KPI pulses for Amazon (Selling Partner) stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Amazon (Selling Partner) connector is one of the deepest marketplace integrations in the Vortex IQ **AI OS** catalogue. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 77 KPI pulses for Amazon sellers - including hero alerts (*Buy-Box Loss Burst*, *New Suppressions (24h)*, *Account Health Status*, *Estimated Revenue Lost to Buy-Box Loss*, *Suppressed Listings*) plus cross-platform kill-shots (*Ad Spend on Out-of-Stock ASINs*, *MAP Violation Risk vs DTC*, *Channel Mix Amazon vs DTC*, *Catalogue Drift vs DTC*). **Vortex Mind** investigates when an ASIN moves on Buy Box, suppression, or revenue; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English (*"which ASINs are at risk this week?"*) and act through your agents.
