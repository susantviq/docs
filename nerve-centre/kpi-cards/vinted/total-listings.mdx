## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Total Listings** is a listing-health metric tracked from Vinted data. It is the full count of listings in your shop, the inventory base every other listing-health and discovery metric is measured against. On Vinted, catalogue size correlates with reach: more well-made listings mean more entry points into search and more chances to be favourited. Tracking the total tells you whether your catalogue is growing, holding, or shrinking faster than you replenish.

| | |
| --- | --- |

| **Sample type** | Backend API data from Vinted, refreshed on the standard data refresh. |

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
*A representative reading of **Total Listings** for a typical Pro seller on Vinted.* The shop shows 640 total listings. Set against 600 active listings, that means around 40 are not currently live, drafts, paused, or recently sold and not yet cleared. A month ago the total was 580, so the catalogue is growing at a healthy listing cadence. The read here is reassuring: stock is being added faster than it sells, keeping reach climbing. ## Reconciling against Vinted

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Vinted's own dashboard:**

Vinted shows your listing count on your shop profile and in your wardrobe view. Reconcile by checking the count shown on your profile against this card, noting whether your profile count includes only live items or also drafts and sold items.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Scope.** Vortex IQ's total may include states (drafts, paused) that the public shop count excludes. | Higher than the live count | Confirm which states each count includes. |
| **Refresh timing.** A just-added or just-sold listing may not reflect until the next refresh. | Marginal | Allow for short refresh lag. |
| **Sold handling.** Recently sold items may linger in the total briefly before clearing. | Marginal | Allow for sold items clearing on a slight delay. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** complement with active listings to separate the live catalogue from the full total. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Total Listings update?**
It refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). After a bulk upload, force a manual refresh to see the new total.

**Q: Why does my Vinted dashboard show a different number?**
The usual reason is scope: Vinted's public shop count may show only live items, while the total here can include drafts or paused listings. Refresh timing and sold-item handling also cause small differences. Confirm scope before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: How does Total Listings relate to other listing-health metrics?**
It is the denominator for active, stale, completeness, and photo-coverage measures. Read it next to active listings to see how much of the catalogue is actually working for you in search.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
This card is informational and has no default alert, but you can configure sensitivity per profile in the Sensitivity tab to be flagged if the catalogue shrinks beyond a set rate.
