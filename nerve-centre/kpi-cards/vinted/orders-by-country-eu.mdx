## Reconciling against Vinted

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Vinted's own dashboard:**

Vinted does not publish a per-country order map for sellers. The nearest reference is your sold-items history, where each order shows the buyer's country. Reconcile by tallying recent orders per country and comparing the distribution against this card.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Period boundary.** Vortex IQ uses 30-day rolling by default; a manual export may use calendar months. | Variable | Match the period range. |
| **Order definition.** Vortex IQ counts completed orders; pending or cancelled orders may be handled differently. | Variable | Confirm which order states are included. |
| **Country attribution.** Attribution is by buyer country; ambiguous destinations can shift edge cases. | Marginal | Confirm the attribution basis. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** complement with AOV by country and revenue mix for the full geography picture. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Orders by Country (EU) update?**
It refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). For an immediate read, force a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Vinted dashboard show a different number?**
Vinted has no native per-country order map, so differences come from period boundaries, which order states are counted, and country attribution. Align these before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: How does Orders by Country (EU) relate to other geography metrics?**
Read it with AOV by country and revenue mix: volume, value, and share together show which markets matter and why. High order volume at low value reads very differently from low volume at high value.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
This card is informational and has no default alert, but you can configure alert rules per profile in the Alert Rules tab if you want to be flagged when a country's order share shifts.
