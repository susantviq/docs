Treat it as a same-day action item rather than a metric to monitor.

## Reconciling against Vinted

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Vinted's own dashboard:**

Vinted surfaces the underlying signals in the seller profile and shop reviews area, where the public rating and recent buyer feedback are visible, and in the order or complaint history where buyer-protection cases appear. There is no single "threshold crossed" view in Vinted, so reconcile by checking the rating shown on your shop and counting recent complaint cases against your configured band.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Threshold definition.** Vortex IQ uses the band set in your profile, which may be stricter than Vinted's internal standing rules. | Variable | Align your profile band with the standing level you want to defend. |
| **Rating refresh lag.** Vinted's public rating can update on a slight delay versus the live API read. | Marginal | Allow for short lag near a breach boundary. |
| **Complaint window.** Vortex IQ evaluates the rolling complaint rate; Vinted may weight recency differently. | Variable | Confirm the complaint window matches. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** complement with the complaint-rate and feedback-rating siblings for the full diagnostic picture. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Buyer-Protection / Rating Threshold Crossed update?**
It is evaluated in real time against the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). For an immediate read after resolving complaints, force a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Vinted dashboard show a different number?**
Vinted has no equivalent single alert, so differences usually come from your configured threshold band being stricter than Vinted's internal standing rules, plus short refresh lag on the public rating. Match the band and allow for lag before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: How does Buyer-Protection / Rating Threshold Crossed relate to other reputation metrics?**
It is a composite trip-wire built on complaint rate and feedback rating. Track it alongside those two siblings and the shop health score to understand both the trigger and the downstream visibility impact.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, the complaint-rate band and rating floor are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Set them to defend the shop standing your business needs rather than relying on the generic default.
