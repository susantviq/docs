The Vinted connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to merchants selling on Vinted - Europe's leading C2C resale fashion marketplace, strong on circular fashion across the UK, France, Germany, Italy, and Poland. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 17 KPI pulses including listings, sales, reputation, returns, and marketplace economics. **Vortex Mind** investigates when a number moves; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
