## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Revenue Over Time** is a sales metric tracked from Vinted data. It plots revenue across a 90-day window so you can see trajectory rather than a single snapshot. For a resale shop, the trend line reveals seasonality, the lift from a listing refresh or promotion, and any quiet drift downward that a point-in-time figure would hide. It is the card to open first when someone asks whether the shop is growing.

## Calculation

Calculated automatically from your Vinted data. Vortex IQ aggregates revenue per period across the selected window and plots it as a continuous area series. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
*A representative reading of **Revenue Over Time** for a typical Pro seller on Vinted.* Across a 90-day window the area chart climbs steadily through the first two months, peaks after a Wardrobe Spotlight push in mid-period, then drifts down over the final fortnight. The drift lines up with listing freshness decaying: a batch of items uploaded early in the window has passed the two-week boost mark and is getting fewer impressions. The action is to refresh or re-promote the aging listings to arrest the decline before it deepens. The peak confirms the promotion worked, which informs the next push. ## Reconciling against Vinted

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Vinted's own dashboard:**

Vinted shows your sold-items history and balance activity, which you can group by period to reconstruct a revenue trend. There is no native trend chart for sellers, so reconcile by tallying revenue per week for a sample span and comparing the shape against this card.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Period boundary.** Vortex IQ uses a rolling 90-day window; a manual tally may use calendar months. | Variable | Match the period range. |
| **Revenue basis.** Vortex IQ measures sale value; a manual tally might include buyer-protection fees. | Variable | Compare on sale value, not buyer totals. |
| **Time zone.** Daily bins follow the reporting time zone. | Marginal | Confirm the time zone for binning. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** complement with total revenue and orders per day for the full sales picture. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Revenue Over Time update?**
It refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations), with the current period filling in through the day. Force a manual refresh for the latest read.

**Q: Why does my Vinted dashboard show a different number?**
Vinted has no native trend chart, so differences come from the rolling versus calendar window, whether buyer-protection fees are in the revenue basis, and the binning time zone. Align these before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: How does Revenue Over Time relate to other sales metrics?**
It is the trajectory behind the headline total. Read it with orders per day and AOV to see whether a move is driven by volume or price, and with the stale-listings card when a decline looks like freshness decay.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
This card is a trend view with no default alert, but you can configure sensitivity per profile in the Alert Rules tab to be flagged when the trend turns down beyond a set rate.
