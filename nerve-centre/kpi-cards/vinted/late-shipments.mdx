## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Late Shipments** is a dispatch-SLA alert tracked from Vinted data. It lists every paid order that has passed its ship-by deadline without a tracked label scan. On Vinted, the buyer pays at purchase and the clock starts immediately, so a missed dispatch window risks order cancellation, a refund to the buyer, and a dent in your reliability signals. This card exists so a slipping order is caught while there is still time to post it.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it counts** | A live list of orders that are paid but not yet dispatched past their ship-by deadline. Each order is shown individually so you can pack and post the specific items at risk. |
| **Sample type** | Backend API data from Vinted, evaluated in real time against each order's ship deadline. |
| **Why it matters** | Late dispatch is the most common controllable cause of cancellations and poor reviews on Vinted. Buyers who paid expect a prompt label scan, and repeated misses erode shop standing. |
| **Reading the value** | A value above zero means at least one paid order is past its dispatch deadline. Open the list to see which orders need a shipping label scanned today. |

## Calculation

Calculated automatically from your Vinted data. Vortex IQ compares each paid order's ship-by deadline against its current dispatch status and lists any order that has crossed the deadline without a label scan. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
*A representative reading of **Late Shipments** for a typical Pro seller on Vinted.* On a Monday morning the card shows three orders past their ship-by deadline: two were bought over the weekend while the seller was away from the packing bench, and one slipped because the chosen label type needed a drop-off the seller missed. The fix is immediate, pack and scan the three labels today, then message each buyer with the tracking link so they hold rather than cancel. The structural fix is a tighter weekend packing routine. ## Reconciling against Vinted

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Vinted's own dashboard:**

Vinted shows the dispatch clock inside each sold order, where the ship-by deadline and current shipping status are visible, and in the "to ship" or sold items list. Reconcile by counting orders flagged as awaiting dispatch past their deadline against this card's list.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Scan timing.** A label scanned at a drop-off may register on the carrier before Vinted updates status. | Marginal | Allow for short scan-to-status lag. |
| **Deadline definition.** Vortex IQ reads each order's ship-by deadline; Vinted may show a countdown rounded to the hour. | Marginal | Compare against the per-order deadline, not the rounded countdown. |
| **Filter scope.** Profile-level filters may narrow which orders Vortex IQ evaluates. | Variable | Match filter settings. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** complement with pending shipment and the ships-within-5-days rate for the full dispatch picture. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Late Shipments update?**
It is evaluated in real time on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). After scanning labels, force a manual refresh to clear resolved orders from the list.

**Q: Why does my Vinted dashboard show a different number?**
The usual reasons are scan-to-status lag (a label scanned at drop-off can take time to register), deadline rounding in Vinted's countdown display, and filter scope. Allow for short lag and match filters before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: How does Late Shipments relate to other fulfilment metrics?**
It is the acute version of pending shipment: an order that was awaiting dispatch and has now breached its deadline. Track it alongside pending shipment and the ships-within-5-days rate to see both the queue and the SLA outcome.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
The card fires per order past deadline by default, but the supporting bands are configurable per profile in the Alert Rules tab. Tune them to match your dispatch capacity rather than relying on the generic default.
