## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Orders / Day** is a fulfilment metric tracked from Vinted data. It plots daily order volume over time, giving a clear picture of sales rhythm and the packing load that follows it. On Vinted, each order starts a dispatch clock immediately, so daily order pace is also a workload forecast: a spike today is a packing surge tomorrow. The trend line is the simplest way to see momentum, seasonality, and weekday patterns.

## Calculation

Calculated automatically from your Vinted data. Vortex IQ counts completed orders per calendar day over the selected period and plots the series as a line. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Reconciling against Vinted

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Vinted's own dashboard:**

Vinted shows your sold items with sale dates, which you can group by day. There is no native orders-per-day chart for sellers, so reconcile by counting sold items per day for a sample window and comparing the shape against this card.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Day boundary.** Vortex IQ bins orders by the reporting time zone; a manual count may use a different zone. | Marginal | Confirm the time zone for day boundaries. |
| **Order definition.** Vortex IQ counts completed orders; cancelled orders may be excluded. | Variable | Confirm which states are counted. |
| **Period boundary.** Vortex IQ uses a rolling window; manual exports may use calendar ranges. | Variable | Match the period range. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** complement with total orders and pending shipment for the full pace-and-load picture. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Orders / Day update?**
It refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations), with the current day filling in through the day. Force a manual refresh for the latest count.

**Q: Why does my Vinted dashboard show a different number?**
Vinted has no native daily chart, so differences come from the day-boundary time zone, which order states are counted, and the period window. Align these before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: How does Orders / Day relate to other fulfilment metrics?**
It is the leading indicator of dispatch load: today's spike is tomorrow's packing surge. Track it with pending shipment and late shipments so a sales high does not turn into an SLA miss.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
This card is a trend view with no default alert, but you can configure sensitivity per profile in the Sensitivity tab to be flagged when daily volume spikes beyond your packing capacity.
