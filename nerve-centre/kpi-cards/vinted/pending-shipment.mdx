## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Pending Shipment** is a fulfilment metric tracked from Vinted data. It counts paid orders still waiting to be dispatched. On Vinted the buyer pays up front and the ship-by clock starts at once, so the pending queue is the work standing between you and a clean dispatch record. A queue that grows faster than you can pack is the direct precursor to late shipments, cancellations, and unhappy buyers.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it counts** | The live number of paid orders awaiting dispatch, before any have breached their ship-by deadline. |
| **Sample type** | Backend API data from Vinted, refreshed on the standard data refresh. |
| **Why it matters** | The pending queue is your packing backlog. Kept low it means dispatch is keeping pace; allowed to swell it converts into late-shipment alerts and the cancellations and rating damage that follow. |
| **Reading the value** | Lower is better relative to your normal pace. A queue well above your typical level, especially more than twice the recent average, signals a packing crunch that needs clearing today. |
| **Currency** | count |
| **Time window** | `Real time` |
| **Alert trigger** | Fires when the pending queue rises above roughly twice the recent average, signalling a dispatch backlog. |
| **Sentiment key** | `vin_pending_ship` |
| **Roles** | owner, operations |
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

## Calculation

Calculated automatically from your Vinted data. Vortex IQ counts paid orders that have not yet been dispatched, comparing the live count against your recent average to judge whether the queue is building. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
*A representative reading of **Pending Shipment** for a typical Pro seller on Vinted.* The queue normally sits around 8 orders, cleared daily. After a weekend away it reads 21, more than twice the recent average, so the alert fires. None are late yet, but several are close to their ship-by deadline. The action is straightforward: clear the backlog today, prioritising the orders nearest their deadline, before they tip into the late-shipments list. The structural fix is a weekend packing plan or a holiday-mode pause so the queue never builds to this point. ## Reconciling against Vinted

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Vinted's own dashboard:**

Vinted shows orders awaiting dispatch in your sold-items or "to ship" view, each with its ship-by countdown. Reconcile by counting orders shown as paid but not yet dispatched against this card.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Scan timing.** A just-scanned label may still show pending until status updates. | Marginal | Allow for short scan-to-status lag. |
| **Queue definition.** Vortex IQ counts paid-not-dispatched; orders mid-cancellation may be handled differently. | Variable | Confirm how cancelling orders are treated. |
| **Refresh timing.** A live read may sit between the queue and the next refresh. | Marginal | Force a refresh for the latest count. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** complement with late shipments and the ships-within-5-days rate for the full dispatch picture. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Pending Shipment update?**
It refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). After scanning labels, force a manual refresh to see the queue fall.

**Q: Why does my Vinted dashboard show a different number?**
The usual reasons are scan-to-status lag, how orders mid-cancellation are counted, and refresh timing. Allow for lag and confirm the queue definition before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: How does Pending Shipment relate to other fulfilment metrics?**
It is the queue; late shipments is what happens when the queue is not cleared in time. Track them together with order pace so you can staff packing ahead of demand rather than reacting to breaches.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, the multiple-of-average that triggers the backlog alert is configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Set it to the queue depth your packing capacity can clear within the ship-by window.
