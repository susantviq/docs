## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **AOV by Country (EU)** is a geography metric tracked from Vinted data. It breaks average sale price down by the EU country marketplace a buyer purchased from, so you can see where higher-value sales come from and where pricing or assortment leaves money on the table. Vinted runs separate per-country marketplaces with their own buyer demand and shipping economics, so a single blended average can hide important country-level differences.

## Calculation

Calculated automatically from your Vinted data. Vortex IQ groups completed sales by EU buyer country over the selected period and divides total sale value by order count within each country. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Reconciling against Vinted

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Vinted's own dashboard:**

Vinted does not expose a native per-country AOV report for sellers. The nearest reference is your sold-items history, where each order shows sale price and the buyer's country, which you can group manually. Reconcile by spot-checking a country's recent sales against this card's average for that country.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Period boundary.** Vortex IQ uses 30-day rolling by default; a manual export may span calendar months. | Variable | Match the period range. |
| **Country attribution.** Vortex IQ attributes by buyer country; mixed shipping destinations can shift edge cases. | Marginal | Confirm the attribution basis. |
| **Buyer-protection treatment.** AOV reflects sale price, not buyer-paid protection fees, which a manual tally might include. | Lower | Compare on sale price, not buyer total. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** complement with orders by country and revenue mix for the full geography picture. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does AOV by Country (EU) update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). For an immediate read, force a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Vinted dashboard show a different number?**
Vinted has no native per-country AOV view, so differences usually come from period-boundary choices, country attribution, and whether a manual tally includes buyer-protection fees in the sale value. Match the period and compare on sale price before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: How does AOV by Country (EU) relate to other geography metrics?**
Read it next to orders by country and revenue mix: value, volume, and share together tell you which markets to prioritise. A high AOV in a low-volume country may be worth scaling.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
This card is informational and has no default alert, but you can configure alert rules per profile in the Alert Rules tab if you want to be flagged when a country's AOV drifts.
