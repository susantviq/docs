## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Listings Drifting from BC** is a cross-channel metric tracked from Vinted data alongside your BigCommerce catalogue. It lists items whose Vinted listing has diverged from the source BigCommerce record, in price, title, condition, or availability, so the same product tells two different stories on two channels. Drift erodes trust, creates pricing arbitrage against you, and risks selling something on Vinted that is no longer correct on BigCommerce. Each drifting SKU is a small revenue-at-risk item.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it counts** | The number of SKUs where the Vinted listing has drifted from the BigCommerce source record over the selected period, broken down by row so you can see the field that diverged. |
| **Sample type** | Backend API data from Vinted compared against the BigCommerce catalogue, refreshed on the standard data refresh. |
| **Why it matters** | When the two channels disagree, you can oversell, mis-price, or misrepresent condition. Drift left unchecked becomes refunds, complaints, and reconciliation work. |
| **Reading the value** | Lower is better. A count above the safe number of SKUs means catalogue sync is slipping and the divergent items need to be re-aligned to the BigCommerce source. |
| **Currency** | count |
| **Time window** | `30D` |
| **Alert trigger** | Fires when more than the configured number of SKUs have drifted from BigCommerce. |
| **Sentiment key** | `vin_xc_catalogue_drift_vs_bc` |
| **Roles** | owner, marketing, operations |
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

## Reconciling against Vinted

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Vinted's own dashboard:**

Vinted shows the current state of each listing, but it has no view of your BigCommerce source, so it cannot show drift on its own. Reconcile by opening a flagged SKU on Vinted and comparing the named field against the same product in BigCommerce.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sync timing.** A recent BigCommerce change may not have propagated to Vinted yet, so brief drift is expected. | Higher right after a change | Allow a sync cycle before treating drift as a problem. |
| **Tolerance.** Vortex IQ flags drift beyond a tolerance; tiny rounding or formatting differences may sit below it. | Variable | Confirm the tolerance for each field. |
| **Match basis.** Drift detection relies on matching Vinted listings to BigCommerce SKUs; unmatched items are excluded. | Variable | Confirm the SKU match coverage. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** this card is itself the cross-channel check; complement it with the sold-elsewhere and feed-validation siblings. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Listings Drifting from BC update?**
It refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). After re-syncing, force a manual refresh to confirm the drift count falls.

**Q: Why does my Vinted dashboard show a different number?**
Vinted cannot show drift at all, since it has no view of BigCommerce. Any difference versus your own check comes from sync timing (brief expected drift after a change), the per-field tolerance, and which SKUs are matched. Allow a sync cycle before acting.

**Q: How does Listings Drifting from BC relate to other cross-channel metrics?**
It is the broad catalogue-sync check. The sold-elsewhere card is the acute availability case, and the feed-validation card catches the upstream cause. Track all three to keep Vinted aligned with the source catalogue.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, the SKU count that triggers the alert and the per-field tolerance are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Tune them to your sync cadence and catalogue size.
