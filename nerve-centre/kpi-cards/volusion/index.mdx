## What is Volusion?

Volusion is one of the older hosted ecommerce platforms in the US market, founded in 1999, well before the Shopify generation. It served SMB retailers with an all-in-one stack: hosted storefront, built-in checkout, basic CRM, simple marketing tools, and reporting, all bundled in one monthly fee. Its pitch was "everything you need built in" rather than "an extension marketplace for everything you might need".

Market share has declined as Shopify, BigCommerce, and Squarespace Commerce captured most of the new SMB pipeline, but a meaningful install base of legacy Volusion merchants is still actively trading. They are typically US-based, multi-year merchants with established catalogues, real revenue, and (very often) a deliberate decision not to undertake a full replatform project.

## Why Vortex IQ tracks Volusion

Long-running Volusion merchants share a specific problem. The platform's reporting has not kept pace with what teams now expect (refund rate trend, OOS spike alerts, churn risk, cross-channel revenue at risk) and the cost of switching to a new platform is real: theme work, app re-integration, SEO risk, and disruption to the running business. Many merchants would rather upgrade their visibility than upgrade their platform.

Vortex IQ Nerve Centre gives them that path. The connector reads orders, products, customers, and stock through Volusion's API and CSV export endpoints, normalises into the same KPI model used for Shopify and BigCommerce, then surfaces the same executive cards, alerts, and Ask Viq narratives. Merchants keep their Volusion store running and get the modern pulse layer on top, with the option to replatform later from a position of better data.

## What you can monitor (when KPI cards launch)

Planned coverage maps Volusion's order, product, customer, and inventory data onto the same KPI structure used elsewhere in the Nerve Centre:

- Total revenue, order count, average order value, and trend across 7, 30, and 90 day windows.
- Order status mix (new, processing, shipped, returned, cancelled) with status-spike alerting.
- Refund and return rate, refunded order count, and refund value, with rate-spike alerting.
- Inventory health: out of stock, low stock, and products with no stock tracking.
- Catalogue health: products missing image, description, manufacturer, or SEO meta.
- Customer health: new vs returning customers, repeat purchase rate, top customers by spend, and churn risk on inactive customers.
- Payment method and shipping method revenue breakdowns.
- Tax collected by state, useful given Volusion's US-heavy merchant base and US sales tax obligations.

Cross-channel cards (ad spend on OOS SKUs, email share of revenue, pagespeed-driven cart loss, marketplace drift) light up when a Volusion store is paired with Google Ads, Amazon, Klaviyo, or a website performance source.

## Detailed KPI cards in active development

The Volusion adapter is in build. Each KPI card documents the exact Volusion API or export resource used, the calculation, why it matters, and the playbook for when sentiment moves. Cards follow the hero / non-hero / cross-channel layout used across the Nerve Centre, sized for the kind of multi-year SMB catalogue typical on Volusion.

Volusion's current API and the CSV-based generic export endpoints are both in scope, so older stores without full API coverage can still light up the dashboard.
