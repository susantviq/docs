[KPI Cards](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards) / [X (Twitter)](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/twitter) / **Audit**

Native organic X presence audit, re-authored 14 Jul 26 alongside the twitter.yaml card rebuild (the previous version audited a GA-referral view the X API cannot serve). Answers four questions: (1) is the OAuth token / account healthy and refreshable, (2) did the account quietly stop posting (cadence collapse is the top organic decay), (3) is the audience actually engaging with what gets posted, and (4) is reach / impressions trending down vs the prior period. Cross-channel social-to-commerce pairing joins the analytics sibling once the social-referral handler ships.


## Authentication & access

- OAuth 2.0 access token valid (auth on /2/users/me) and refresh token present so the 2-hour token re-mints
- Required scopes granted (users.read, tweet.read, offline.access); missing tweet.read degrades every post card
- API tier supports reading posts (Basic or above; the Free tier is write-only + users/me)
- Connected account resolves to a live X profile with a non-zero audience

## Posting cadence

- No post in the last 14 days while the account has followers (silent cadence collapse)
- Posts in the last 30 days below 2 (cadence under the survival floor)
- Posting cadence down more than 40% vs the prior period

## Engagement quality

- Engagement rate below 1% across the last 30 days of posts
- Engagement rate down more than 30% vs the prior period
- Replies share of engagement near zero (broadcasting without conversation)

## Reach & audience

- Impressions down more than 20% vs the prior period (reach decay; user-context token required)
- Follower:following ratio below 1 on a brand account (follow-back growth pattern)
- Follower count shrinking vs the prior period

## Cross-channel: social-to-commerce (pending handler)

- Posting-cadence collapse coinciding with a drop in the analytics sibling's x.com / t.co referral sessions
- High engagement posts driving no storefront sessions (link/CTA gap; sibling analytics)
