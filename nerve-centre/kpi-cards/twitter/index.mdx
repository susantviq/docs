Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **13** KPI pulses for X (Twitter) stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The X (Twitter) connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to brands tracking organic X as a customer-acquisition channel - the traffic that flows from tweets, bio links, and replies to the merchant's storefront, not native post engagement metrics. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 13 KPI pulses on *Sessions*, *Real-Time Users*, *New / Returning Users*, *Bounce Rate*, *Pageviews per Session*, *Avg Session Duration*, *Session Conversion Rate*, *Cart Abandonment Rate*, *Goal Completions*, and *Top Pages / Top Traffic Sources*. **Vortex Mind** investigates when an X referral spike fails to convert; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the session.
