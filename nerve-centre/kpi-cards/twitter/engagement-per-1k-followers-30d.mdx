## Calculation

```
Total 30d engagement / followers x 1000; size-normalised engagement.
```

Computed by the X (Twitter) engine from `/2/users/me` and the account's recent posts (`/2/users/{id}/tweets` with `public_metrics`), refreshed on the standard sync. Reading posts requires the X API Basic tier; the engine degrades to no data rather than estimating.
