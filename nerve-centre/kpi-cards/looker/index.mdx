Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **13** KPI pulses for Looker stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Looker connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to teams on Looker (Google Cloud's enterprise BI platform) - strong on LookML semantic models, governed dashboards, and embedded analytics. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 13 KPI pulses surfaced from Looker explores (Sessions, New Users, Returning Users, Real-Time Users, Avg Session Duration, Pageviews per Session, Bounce Rate, Cart Abandonment Rate, Session Conversion Rate, Goal Completions, Top Pages, Top Traffic Sources). **Vortex Mind** investigates when a number moves; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
