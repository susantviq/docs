## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> The total **dollar amount captured but not yet acquirer-funded** to the merchant's bank account. The "in-flight" balance: customer has paid, but the acquirer hasn't disbursed yet.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it counts** | `SUM(settleAmount)` for captures with `transactionStatus = settledSuccessfully` where the acquirer has not yet deposited (within typical 1 to 5 business day window). |
| **Reserve effect** | Reserve-held funds excluded; this card represents in-flight only. |
| **eCheck.Net (ACH)** | Pending balances higher proportion (3 to 5 day NACHA lag). |
| **Currency** | USD-dominant; multi-currency stacked. |
| **Time window** | Snapshot. |
| **Roles** | owner, finance |
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
"Heartland Hardware Co." snapshot end of day 02 May 26 (Friday).

| Method | Pending balance | Funding day |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visa / MC captures last 2 business days | USD 184,200 | Mon 05 May 26 (T+1 from Thu and Fri) |
| Amex captures | USD 38,400 | Tue 06 May 26 |
| eCheck.Net captures last 5 business days | USD 28,400 | Spread Mon to Wed |
| **Total pending** | **USD 251,000** | |

For Heartland (USD 1.87M monthly volume), USD 251,000 in flight at any time is roughly 4 days of revenue, consistent with T+2 average settlement. A growing pending balance with stable revenue indicates settlement is slowing; investigate.

## Reconciling against the vendor's own dashboard

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look:**

Authorize.Net Dashboard does not show pending balance directly; sum settled-but-not-yet-deposited from **Reports → Settled Transactions** with status filter, or use the acquirer's daily settlement report.

**Why our number may differ:**

| Reason | Direction | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Reserve withholding** | Theirs higher | We exclude reserved funds. |
| **Time zone** | Boundary days | Pacific batch cut-off vs UTC. |
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Pending balance growing while volume stable, why?**
Settlement is slowing. Causes: (1) acquirer reconciliation issue, (2) increased reserve withholding, (3) chargeback hold cluster.

**Friday pending always higher, normal?**
Yes. Captures Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday all fund Monday or Tuesday at the earliest. Friday-end snapshots show 3 to 4 days of pending.

**Working-capital implications?**
Plan for 2 to 4 days of revenue always in flight. Heartland Hardware needs USD 250k in working capital just to cover the gap between customer-paid and bank-deposited.

**Same-day funding, eliminates pending?**
Most. Same-day-funded card captures clear the same day; only ACH and weekend captures show as pending.

**Reserve held, separate from this?**
Yes. Reserve is a long-term hold (90 days typical); this card shows short-term in-flight only.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
