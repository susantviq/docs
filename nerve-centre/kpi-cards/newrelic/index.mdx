**[Nerve Centre KPIs](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/newrelic) · [Audit Profile](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/newrelic/audit) · [Sentiment Settings](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/newrelic/sentiment)**

Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **75** KPI pulses for New Relic stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The New Relic connector is the second-deepest observability integration in the Vortex IQ **AI OS** catalogue - 75 KPI pulses covering full-stack APM, browser monitoring, synthetics, infrastructure, and logs for teams using New Relic One. The **Nerve Centre** runs hero alerts on *Revenue at Risk (live)*, *SLO Burn Rate (1h)*, *Active Incidents*, *Critical-Path Tests Status*, *p95 Response Time*, *Error Rate*, and *Operational Health Score*, plus cross-platform cards on *Cart Abandonment During 5xx Spikes*, *Checkout App Health × Sales*, and *Revenue Lost / Min (active incidents)*. **Vortex Mind** investigates Apdex anomalies, deploy regressions, and conditions in 'No Signal' state; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the trace.
