## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> Pacing check, comparing actual Quora spend against the configured budget cap. **Quora typically under-paces** more aggressively than other platforms because question pools are finite (only so many users read "best CRM for SaaS" each day) and the auction can't absorb extra spend without broadening to lower-quality questions. Overshoots are rare; under-pacing at 60, 80% of cap is the norm on most accounts.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it counts** | `actual_spend ÷ configured_budget` over the current period. Configured budget read from campaign daily/lifetime caps in Quora Ads Manager. |
| **API level** | Account-level by default. |
| **Currency** | Account currency. |
| **Daily vs lifetime budget** | Daily resets in account TZ; lifetime accumulates across the campaign duration. |
| **Pacing characteristic** | Quora is the most consistently under-pacing of all the social-ad platforms because question-context audiences saturate. **Expect 60, 85% of monthly cap delivered**, not 100, 110%. |
| **Account-level Spending Limit** | Configurable in Billing > Spending Limit. Hits cap → all campaigns pause. |
| **Time window** | `T/30D`. |
| **Alert trigger** | `>90% used before 80% of period`. **Rarely fires on Quora**; consider tuning to fire on under-pacing instead. |
| **Roles** | owner, marketing, finance |

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
A US B2B SaaS account running Quora as a primary mid-funnel channel. Account-level Spending Limit: $12,000 / month. Per-campaign daily budgets sum to $400 / day target. 30-day window 02 Apr 26 to 01 May 26.

| Day | Cumulative spend | Cumulative budget | % used | % of period elapsed | Card status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Day 7 | $2,200 | $2,800 | 79% | 23% | Under-pacing (typical) |
| Day 14 | $4,400 | $5,600 | 79% | 47% | On track for ~78% of cap |
| Day 21 | $6,600 | $8,400 | 79% | 70% | Stable under-pacing |
| Day 28 | $8,400 | $11,200 | 75% | 93% | Tightening as auction broadens |
| Day 30 | $9,000 | $12,000 | 75% | 100% | Final: 75% of cap used |

What's happening:

1. **The account consistently delivers ~75% of cap on Quora**, well below the 90, 100% typical on Meta or TikTok. **This is the norm**, not a config error. Quora's question-context targeting can't deliver more spend on a saturated question pool.
2. **By day 7, the priority questions are saturated**: the top 10, 15 high-intent questions ("best CRM for SaaS", "Mailchimp alternatives", etc.) only have so many readers per day. Daily delivery from those questions plateaus at ~$280, $320 of the $400 daily target.
3. **The remaining ~$100/day** is spent on broader topic-targeted or longer-tail question audiences. CPCs there are lower but conversion rate drops too; the spend lands but the ROAS reads weaker.
4. **By day 28, even the broader auctions are saturating**, daily delivery drops to $240 and the algorithm runs out of inventory it can profitably serve. The remaining $3,000 of monthly cap goes unused.
5. **Action**: this account is healthy. To push toward 100% delivery on Quora would require:
   - Adding new questions (research adjacent topics, longer-tail queries).
   - Testing Promoted Answers (different inventory pool).
   - Broadening topic targeting (lower ROAS but higher volume).
   - Accepting higher CPCs on the existing question pool.

**Most accounts shouldn't push to 100%**, the unspent budget represents the auction's natural ceiling on Quora's audience.

Quick sanity tests:

- 70, 85% used at 100% of period = healthy Quora pacing.
- 95% used at 80% of period = unusual on Quora; check for daily-budget-config errors or a competitor exiting the auction (rare).
- 50% used at 100% of period = audience too narrow OR Quality Score erosion suppressing impressions; investigate.
- Spending Limit hit early on Quora = misconfigured daily budgets; rare.
- Pacing under by >40% with stable ROAS = healthy ceiling reached.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Why does Quora consistently under-deliver against my budget?**
Question-context targeting on Quora is naturally inventory-bound. Only so many users read "best CRM for SaaS" each day; the auction can't manufacture more impressions on a saturated question pool. **Expect 60, 85% of monthly cap delivered**, not 100%. Pushing to 100% requires expanding to lower-quality questions, which usually hurts ROAS.

**My account hits 100% delivery, should I be worried?**
Slightly yes. 100% delivery on Quora usually means:
- Questions targeted are very broad (low-intent topic targeting eating budget).
- Daily budgets too low relative to question-pool inventory.
- Quality Score artificially high pushing you to win all auctions you bid on.

**How do I increase Quora delivery?**
Four levers, in order of preference:
1. **Add new questions.** Research adjacent questions, longer-tail queries, "vs" comparison questions.
2. **Test Promoted Answers.** Different inventory pool from text/image ads.
3. **Improve Quality Score.** Better landing pages, tighter ad-question relevance.
4. **Broaden topic targeting** (lower ROAS, higher volume).

**Quora ROAS is healthy at 4x but pacing is at 70%, should I raise the cap?**
**Probably no.** If pacing is at 70% with healthy ROAS, the auction has reached its natural ceiling on your current question targeting. Raising the cap won't generate more impressions, it'll just leave more budget unused. Instead, **broaden the question pool** (add new question targets) to grow inventory, then pacing will rise.

**What's the difference between daily and lifetime budget on Quora?**
- **Daily**: resets each midnight in account TZ. Standard for evergreen campaigns.
- **Lifetime**: hard cap across campaign duration. Better for date-bounded flights.

For Quora's slow-paced auction, **daily budgets are usually fine**; the algorithm rarely overshoots, so daily caps act as soft ceilings.

**My account-level Spending Limit hit; what now?**
Same as other platforms:
1. Raise the limit in Billing > Spending Limit.
2. Investigate (rare on Quora; usually a per-campaign daily-budget config error).
3. Re-pace; expect 24, 48 hours of recovery.

**Why is Quora pacing the most predictable of all platforms?**
Three factors:
1. **Auction is less competitive.** Fewer advertisers bidding for each question.
2. **Slower algorithm.** Quora's optimisation is conservative, doesn't aggressively pour budget into single ads.
3. **Inventory-bound by question pool.** Hard ceiling on impressions per question per day.

**Should I switch the alert to fire on under-pacing instead of over-pacing?**
**Yes for Quora**, the default >90% used before 80% of period almost never fires. Under-pacing alerts (e.g. &lt;70% used at 100% of period) catch genuine problems (audience too narrow, Quality Score erosion, ad suspensions) earlier. **Tune the alert direction** for Quora.

**Sponsored / custom-deal flights, do they appear in pacing?**
Mostly yes. Quora's Reporting API includes most spend types. The exception is heavily-customised brand deals negotiated through the Quora rep team, which sometimes route through a parallel billing system; check with your rep.
