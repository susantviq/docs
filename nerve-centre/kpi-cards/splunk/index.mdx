**[Nerve Centre KPIs](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/splunk) · [Audit Profile](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/splunk/audit) · [Sentiment Settings](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/splunk/sentiment)**

Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **18** KPI pulses for Splunk stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Splunk connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to enterprise teams using Splunk for log analytics, SIEM, and operational intelligence at scale across distributed environments. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 18 KPI pulses covering *Error Rate*, *p95 / p99 Latency*, *Apdex Score*, *MTTA / MTTR*, *Incidents Open*, *Services Healthy / Degraded / Down*, *SLA Compliance*, and *Top Error Types* - so commerce-impacting alerts surface in the AI OS, not buried in Splunk dashboards only the ops team checks. **Vortex Mind** investigates when alerting concentration shifts to a single service; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the source event.
