## At a glance

## Calculation

The card scans your live Mercari orders, comparing each paid-and-unshipped order's ship-by deadline against the current time. Any order whose deadline has already passed without a tracking scan or dispatch confirmation is counted. Because it evaluates in real time, the count rises the instant a deadline lapses and falls as soon as you mark the order shipped.

In the Mercari app or web, open your Sold or To Ship orders. Each order shows its ship-by date and a countdown, and overdue orders are flagged for action. Compare that list against the count the alert reports.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Late Shipments Risking Fast Shipper Badge update?**
It is real-time, re-evaluating on every standard data refresh. An order is added the moment its deadline passes and removed as soon as you confirm dispatch.

**Q: Why does my Mercari dashboard show a different number?**
Mercari may still show an order as "to ship" right at the deadline boundary while the alert has already counted it as late, or a tracking scan may clear it on one side before the other syncs. Refresh both and re-check.

**Q: Does marking an order shipped clear it immediately?**
On the next refresh, yes. Once Mercari registers the dispatch or a tracking scan, the order drops out of the count.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
