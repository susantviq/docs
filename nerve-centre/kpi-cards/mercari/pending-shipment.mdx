## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Pending Shipment** counts orders that buyers have paid for but you have not yet shipped. Let this backlog grow past roughly twice your normal level and you risk late dispatch, sour ratings, and losing the Fast Shipper badge.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it counts** | Paid Mercari orders awaiting dispatch, where the label is unprinted or the parcel has no carrier acceptance scan yet. |
| **Sample type** | Backend API data from Mercari, refreshed on the standard data refresh. |
| **Why it matters** | Mercari rewards prompt shipping. A backlog above about 2x your 30-day average means orders are stacking up faster than you can dispatch, which threatens ratings and Fast Shipper status. |
| **Reading the value** | Lower is healthier. Read it against your 30-day baseline rather than as an absolute. A brief spike after a sale event is normal; a sustained one is not. |
| **Currency** | number |
| **Time window** | `RT` |
| **Alert trigger** | `>2x 30D avg` |
| **Sentiment key** | `mer_pending_ship` |
| **Roles** | owner, operations |
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

## Calculation

Vortex IQ counts every Mercari order that has been paid but not yet handed to the carrier, then compares the live count against the rolling 30-day average of your pending queue. The alert fires when the current backlog runs at more than twice that average. Orders drop off the count once a shipping label receives its first carrier acceptance scan.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
*A representative reading of **Pending Shipment** for a typical Mercari reseller.* A sneaker reseller usually carries 3 to 4 unshipped orders at any moment. After a weekend Smart Pricing drop, the card jumps to 11 on 14 Jun 26, well past the `>2x 30D avg` line. The seller blocks out an afternoon, prints labels in a batch, and clears 9 parcels to a carrier acceptance scan that day, pulling the count back to 2. Acting fast protects the 3-day window and keeps the Fast Shipper badge intact. Cross-check against the ships-within-3-days rate to see whether the backlog has already started eating into on-time dispatch. When the number surges, Vortex Mind traces upstream causes such as a sale event or a paused fulfilment routine, and Ask Viq answers plain-English questions like "which of my pending orders are closest to the ship deadline?"

## Reconciling against Mercari

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Mercari's own dashboard:**

In the Mercari app, open your Selling or To Do view, where orders awaiting a shipping label or carrier drop-off are grouped together. Each shows a ship-by date. The count of items in that to-ship state is what this card mirrors.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Period boundary.** An order paid moments before the refresh may appear here before it shows in your queue, or vice versa. | Variable | Match the period range. |
| **Time zone.** Ship-by deadlines are set in Mercari's time zone, which can shift which orders look overdue. | Marginal | Confirm time zone match. |
| **Filter scope.** A label printed but not yet scanned by the carrier still counts as pending until acceptance. | Variable | Match filter settings. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** Compare against your carrier's pickup or drop-off log to confirm which labels have actually been accepted. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Pending Shipment update?**
It refreshes on the standard Vortex IQ data refresh, so newly paid orders appear and shipped ones drop off close to real time.

**Q: Why does my Mercari dashboard show a different number?**
Mercari may group by ship-by date or separate label-printed from not-started orders, while this card counts every paid-but-unshipped order against your 30-day baseline. A label printed without a carrier scan still counts here.

**Q: Does printing a label clear an order from this count?**
No. The order leaves the count only when the carrier records its first acceptance scan. A printed but undropped label still sits in the backlog, which keeps the card honest about real dispatch progress.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
