## Calculation

Vortex IQ divides the number of orders shipped within 3 days of purchase by the total orders in the trailing 30-day window, expressed as a percentage, and shows the change against the prior comparable period. Shipping is timed from purchase to the carrier's first acceptance scan, so printing a label alone does not count as shipped. Orders still inside their 3-day window are excluded until they ship or the window closes.

## Reconciling against Mercari

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Mercari's own dashboard:**

Mercari surfaces shipping speed through your seller profile and the Fast Shipper badge status, and individual order pages show the time from purchase to the first carrier scan. There is no single percentage shown the same way this card frames it, so reconcile by sampling recent orders against their ship-by performance.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Period boundary.** The rate uses a rolling 30-day window, so an old late order ages out and lifts the rate without any new action. | Variable | Match the period range. |
| **Time zone.** The 3-day window is measured in Mercari's time zone, which can flip a borderline order between on-time and late. | Marginal | Confirm time zone match. |
| **Filter scope.** Shipping is timed to the carrier acceptance scan, not the label print, so a printed-but-undropped label counts as not yet shipped. | Variable | Match filter settings. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** Compare against your carrier's scan log to confirm exactly when each parcel was accepted. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Ships-Within-3-Days Rate update?**
It refreshes on the standard Vortex IQ data refresh, recalculating across the trailing 30-day window as orders ship and older ones age out.

**Q: Why does my Mercari dashboard show a different number?**
Mercari frames shipping speed through your badge status and per-order timings rather than a single 30-day percentage. Differences usually come from the window length or from whether label printing versus carrier scan is treated as shipped.

**Q: Does printing a label count as shipping on time?**
No. The clock stops at the carrier's first acceptance scan, not when you print the label. A printed label that sits undropped past day 3 counts as a late ship, which matches how Mercari judges promptness.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, alert thresholds are configurable per profile in the Alert Rules tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
