## Calculation

Vortex IQ sums the sale value of every Mercari order or listing currently carrying a near-term risk flag: orders approaching or past the ship-by deadline, orders with an open dispute or cancellation request, listings suspended by Mercari, and items flagged as potentially sold elsewhere. Each at-risk item is counted at its order or listing price, and the total updates as items enter or clear their risk state. An item counts once even if it carries more than one flag.

## Reconciling against Mercari

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Mercari's own dashboard:**

There is no single at-risk total in Mercari's own views. The contributing signals live across your To Do list for ship-by deadlines, your messages and order pages for disputes, and notification banners for suspended listings. This card consolidates those into one figure, so reconcile by adding up the values of the flagged items you find in each place.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Period boundary.** An item that enters or clears a risk state between refreshes will move the total at the next sync. | Variable | Match the period range. |
| **Time zone.** Ship-by deadlines that define late-dispatch risk are set in Mercari's time zone. | Marginal | Confirm time zone match. |
| **Filter scope.** Mercari surfaces each risk type in a different place, so a manual tally depends on checking every relevant view. | Variable | Match filter settings. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** If an item is flagged as sold elsewhere, compare against your other sales channels to confirm the conflict. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Revenue at Risk (live) update?**
It refreshes on the standard Vortex IQ data refresh, recalculating as orders and listings enter or leave a risk state.

**Q: Why does my Mercari dashboard show a different number?**
Mercari has no equivalent single total. This card aggregates several risk signals that Mercari surfaces in separate places, so the only way to match it is to add up the flagged items across each view.

**Q: Does an item with two problems get counted twice?**
No. Each at-risk order or listing is counted once at its sale price, even if it carries multiple flags such as a late ship-by and an open dispute, so the total reflects unique dollars at risk.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, alert thresholds are configurable per profile in the Alert Rules tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
