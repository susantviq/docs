## Calculation

Vortex IQ groups each completed order in the trailing 30 days by the buyer's country and counts orders per country. The totals are mapped onto a choropleth where shading intensity reflects relative order volume. For most Mercari sellers a single domestic market dominates, so the map reads as a strong concentration with little elsewhere. Orders without a resolvable country are grouped separately rather than discarded.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
*A representative reading of **Orders by Country** for a typical Mercari reseller.* Suppose a US-based seller's map shows 312 orders from the United States and a handful, say 4, from elsewhere. That domestic concentration is exactly what to expect on Mercari, so the card mostly confirms healthy local demand. The few outliers are worth a quick check: are they legitimate forwarders or proxy buyers, and does your shipping setup cover them cleanly? There is no alert on this card, so treat it as context rather than a trigger. Cross-reference `mer_aov_by_country` to see whether any non-domestic orders carry a different value, and `mer_total_transactions` for the overall order base. ## Reconciling against Mercari

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Mercari's own dashboard:**

Mercari does not offer a country map natively. You can infer buyer location from individual shipping addresses in your order history, but there is no aggregated geographic view. This card builds that picture for you.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Period boundary.** Orders near the edge of the 30-day window may fall in or out depending on the exact cutoff. | Variable | Match the period range. |
| **Time zone.** Order timestamps may be recorded in a different time zone than your reporting view. | Marginal | Confirm time zone match. |
| **Filter scope.** Country is taken from the buyer's order, so orders with an unresolved country are grouped apart. | Variable | Match filter settings. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** If you sell on other marketplaces, compare geographic mix there, but expect Mercari to skew far more domestic than open international platforms. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Orders by Country update?**
It refreshes on the standard data refresh and reflects orders from the trailing 30 days.

**Q: Why does my Mercari dashboard show a different number?**
Mercari has no aggregated country report, so any tally you build from individual addresses depends on how you read each order. This card groups them consistently.

**Q: Why is almost everything one country?**
That is normal for Mercari, which is a predominantly domestic marketplace. A near-single-country map is the expected pattern, not a data fault.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
