Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **17** KPI pulses for Mercari stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

The Mercari connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to merchants selling on Mercari - the Japan + US C2C marketplace popular for second-hand goods, fashion, and collectibles. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 17 KPI pulses including listings, sales, reputation, returns, and marketplace economics. **Vortex Mind** investigates when a number moves; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
