## Calculation

Vortex IQ matches each Mercari listing to the same unique item on Depop or Vinted and compares the listed prices. Where the spread between the highest and lowest price across the matched channels exceeds the parity threshold, the item is counted and shown as a table row with the per-channel prices. The comparison runs across the window so brief, in-progress repricing settles out. Because this is a multi-marketplace comparison, an item can read fine on Mercari alone yet still appear here when a rival app prices it very differently.

## Reconciling against Mercari

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Mercari's own dashboard:**

Mercari shows only your Mercari price, with no awareness of what the same item costs on Depop or Vinted. To check parity by hand you would open the item on each app and compare the prices yourself. The comparison here is Mercari versus rival resale apps, which means the Mercari dashboard alone will not show the gap because it has no view of the other marketplaces.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Period boundary.** A reprice on one app near the window edge may shift which items breach the spread. | Variable | Match the period range. |
| **Time zone.** Price-change timestamps across the apps may sit in different zones. | Marginal | Confirm time zone match. |
| **Filter scope.** Only items matched across Mercari and a rival app are compared; single-channel items are excluded. | Variable | Match filter settings. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** Because this compares Mercari against Depop and Vinted, remember that each app charges different fees, so a deliberate spread may sometimes be correct; the card flags the gap so you can decide whether it is intentional. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Same Item Priced Differently vs Depop / Vinted update?**
It refreshes on the standard Vortex IQ data refresh, re-matching cross-listed items and recomputing the price spread over the 30-day window.

**Q: Why does my Mercari dashboard show a different number?**
Mercari cannot see Depop or Vinted prices, so it has no spread to report. The card exists precisely because the comparison is invisible from inside Mercari.

**Q: Is a price spread always a problem?**
Not always. Different apps charge different fees, so a measured spread can be intentional. The card flags items above the threshold so you can judge each one rather than assuming every gap is an error.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Adjust to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
