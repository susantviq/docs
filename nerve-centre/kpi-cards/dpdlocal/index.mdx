Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **51** KPI pulses for DPDLocal stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The DPDLocal connector is the deepest carrier integration in the Vortex IQ **AI OS** catalogue — 51 KPI pulses covering DPD's UK reseller channel for SMB merchants and 3PLs booking collections, premium services, and Sunday delivery. The **Nerve Centre** runs hero alerts on *Avg Shipping Cost*, *On-Time Delivery Rate*, *Late Shipments*, *Exception Rate*, *Open Claims*, *Days to Token Expiry*, plus cross-channel cards on *Late-Delivery Revenue at Risk*, *3PL vs Carrier Delay Attribution*, and *Open Claims Without Jira Ticket* so disputed shipments never fall through the gap. **Vortex Mind** investigates failed pickups, route degradation, and ageing claims; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English — "which postcodes are bleeding OTD this week?" — with citations back to the consignment.
