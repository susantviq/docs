Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **78** KPI pulses for Shopify stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Shopify connector is the most-used integration in the Vortex IQ **AI OS** catalogue. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 78 KPI pulses from your Shopify store in real time - revenue, orders, refunds, inventory, fulfilment, customers, plus the cross-channel signals that fuse Shopify with your ads, marketplaces, and email programmes. **Vortex Mind** investigates when a number moves and explains why; **Ask Viq™** lets you query any of these in plain English (*"why did refunds spike on Tuesday?"*) with citations back to the source card.
