[KPI Cards](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards) / **Telegram**

Telegram is connected to Vortex IQ as a delivery channel via a bot. These cards confirm the bot is alive and, when a chat id is configured, report the channel or group member count. A Telegram bot cannot read subscriber growth or post views (those are owner-only in the Telegram app), so the connector surfaces bot and channel health rather than reach analytics.
