## Calculation

```
getMe.username (fallback first_name) rendered as 'Connected as @username'. When the token is revoked getMe fails and the card degrades to no_data (auth_failed).
```

Computed live by the Telegram engine on the standard sync. The engine degrades to no data rather than estimating: if the source is unreadable, the card says so instead of guessing.

### Tracked live in Vortex IQ Nerve Centre

Connect Telegram in [Settings, Connectors](https://app.vortexiq.ai/v2/settings?tab=sources) and this card renders live alongside the rest of the connector's metrics.
