Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **80** KPI pulses for Stripe stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Stripe connector is the most-used payments integration in the Vortex IQ **AI OS** catalogue. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 80 KPI pulses for Stripe accounts - including hero alerts (*Authorisation Success Rate*, *Payment Health Score*, *Decline Rate Spike Alert*, *Dispute Threshold Watch*) plus cross-channel cards that fuse Stripe with checkout (*Decline Spike vs Checkout Funnel Drop*, *Recoverable Revenue (decline-driven)*, *3DS Friction Revenue Loss*, *Chargeback Risk Forecast*). **Vortex Mind** investigates when authorisation drops or disputes spike; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English (*"why did Stripe decline rate climb yesterday?"*) with citations back to the source card.
