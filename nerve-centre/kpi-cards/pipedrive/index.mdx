The Pipedrive connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to small-business sales teams on Pipedrive — strong on visual sales pipelines, deal-stage tracking, and lightweight CRM for revenue-led teams. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 29 KPI pulses including subscriber, deliverability, engagement, and revenue-attribution metrics. **Vortex Mind** investigates when a number moves; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
