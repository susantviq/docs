Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **16** KPI pulses for Worldpay stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

The Worldpay connector covers the enterprise card-processing platform from FIS, popular with UK and European retailers needing wide acquirer coverage. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 16 KPI pulses for Worldpay accounts (Success Rate, Decline Rate, Refund Rate, Chargeback Rate, Dispute Rate, settlement metrics, Top Decline Reasons, Top Payment Methods, Volume Trend). **Vortex Mind** investigates when a number moves; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
