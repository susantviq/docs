Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **48** KPI pulses for Costco SIP stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Costco SIP connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to brands selling into Costco via the Supplier Inventory Portal - the EDI feed-driven channel through which Costco buyers manage POs, item masters, and ASNs. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 48 KPI pulses focused on the operational discipline that keeps a Costco account healthy: hero alerts on *Item Delisting Burst (24h)*, *POs Missing ASN*, *Last Successful Inbound*, and *Items Delisted This Week*, plus cross-platform cards on *Costco vs DTC Pricing Parity*, *Costco POs via ShipBob (SLA)*, and *Costco Issues in Jira* - so a delisting cascade or a missing overnight feed surfaces before the buyer escalates. **Vortex Mind** investigates when chargeback risk creeps up or item-master sync errors concentrate; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the source PO or item.
