**[Nerve Centre KPIs](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/datadog) · [Audit Profile](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/datadog/audit) · [Sentiment Settings](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/datadog/sentiment)**

Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **80** KPI pulses for Datadog stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Datadog connector is the deepest observability integration in the Vortex IQ **AI OS** catalogue - 80 KPI pulses spanning APM, real-user monitoring, infrastructure, logs, synthetics, and SLO management for engineering teams running production e-commerce. The **Nerve Centre** runs hero alerts on *Revenue at Risk (live)*, *SLO Burn Rate (1h)*, *Synthetic Uptime*, *Active Incidents*, *p95 Response Time*, *Error Rate*, *Frustrated User Sessions*, and *Operational Health Score*, plus cross-channel cards on *Cart Abandonment During 5xx Spikes*, *Checkout Service Health × Sales*, and *Revenue Lost / Min (active incidents)* - so an SRE outage and the commercial loss it caused read off the same screen. **Vortex Mind** investigates when SLO burn accelerates or a deploy marker correlates with latency; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English - "what broke during the last incident?" - with citations back to the trace.
