Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **52** KPI pulses for Shopline stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Shopline connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to merchants on the Asia-Pacific commerce platform. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 52 KPI pulses for Shopline stores - including hero alerts (*Auth Token Health*, *Refund Rate*, *Zero/Negative Stock Variants*) plus cross-platform cards that fuse Shopline with Amazon (*Catalogue Drift vs Amazon*, *Marketplace Revenue Share*) and email (*Email-Attributed Revenue Share*). **Vortex Mind** investigates when a number moves; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
