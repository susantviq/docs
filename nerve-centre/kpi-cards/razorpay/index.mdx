Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **16** KPI pulses for Razorpay stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Razorpay connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to Indian merchants using Razorpay for UPI, cards, net-banking, EMI, and pay-later flows - the dominant payments gateway for D2C and SMB e-commerce in India. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 16 KPI pulses covering *Success Rate*, *Decline Rate*, *3DS Abandonment Rate*, *Refund Rate*, *Chargeback Rate*, *Dispute Rate*, *Avg Settlement Time*, *Pending Payouts*, *Top Decline Reasons*, and *Top Payment Methods*. **Vortex Mind** investigates when UPI vs card success diverges or a specific bank rail underperforms; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the transaction.
