Audits campaign performance, address-book hygiene, and automation programmes.

## What this audit checks

### Address books
- Active vs suppressed contacts
- Bounced contacts %
- Recent growth rate

### Campaigns
- Recent campaign open rate
- Click-through rate
- Hard-bounce rate

### Automations
- Active programs count
- Programs with zero contacts
- Welcome / abandoned cart programs present

## Data sources

- `GET https://r{region}-api.dotdigital.com/v2/address-books` - Address book inventory + counts
- `GET https://r{region}-api.dotdigital.com/v2/campaigns` - Campaign metadata + send stats
- `GET https://r{region}-api.dotdigital.com/v2/programs` - Automation program inventory
