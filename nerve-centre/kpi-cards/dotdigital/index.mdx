**[Nerve Centre KPIs](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/dotdigital) · [Audit Profile](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/dotdigital/audit) · [Sentiment Settings](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/dotdigital/sentiment)**

Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **62** KPI pulses for Dotdigital stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Dotdigital connector covers the enterprise email + SMS + cross-channel platform popular with UK and European retail brands. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 61 KPI pulses for Dotdigital accounts in the Vortex IQ **AI OS** catalogue - including hero alerts (*Email-Attributed Revenue*, *Programme vs Campaign Revenue Mix*, *RFM Segment Distribution*, *Bounce Rate*, *Bounce Spike*, *Spam Complaint Rate*, *Abandoned-Cart Programme Status*) plus cross-channel cards (*Abandoned-Cart Recovery Value*, *Email Share of Total Store Revenue*, *Top Sends → Purchase Lag*, *Unsubs Within 7d of Purchase*). **Vortex Mind** investigates when programme revenue dips or RFM cohorts shift; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:data_availability */}
> **A note on data availability.** Cards populate from your connected Dotdigital account:
> - **Revenue cards** (total revenue, programme/campaign revenue mix) need e-commerce **revenue tracking** passed to Dotdigital. Accounts that send order counts but no revenue value will show these as zero.
> - **Cross-channel cards** light up once a store (BigCommerce/Shopify/etc.) is connected on the same workspace.
> - **RFM / suppressed / dormant / segment cards** come from the contacts/profiles dataset and are kept off the default dashboard (listed here as roadmap) until that sync lands.
