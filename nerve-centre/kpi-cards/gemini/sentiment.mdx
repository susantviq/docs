[KPI Cards](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards) / [Google Gemini](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/gemini) / **Sentiment**

Cards carrying a sensitivity band flip to Action Needed when the live value crosses the bad threshold. Bands are shared across connectors on the same archetype and tunable per store in Alert Rules.

| Card | Direction | Good | Bad |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Gemini API Health](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/gemini/gemini-api-health) | Higher is better | 90 | 70 |
| [Gemini Spend (30d)](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/gemini/gemini-spend-30d) | Trend (rise is the alarm) | 10 | 50 |
| [Gemini Usage Trend (30d)](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/gemini/gemini-usage-trend-30d) | Trend (drop is the alarm) | 0 | -50 |
