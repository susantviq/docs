Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **18** KPI pulses for Dynatrace stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Dynatrace connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to enterprise teams using Dynatrace's AI-driven full-stack APM with Davis causal analysis across applications, infrastructure, and digital experience. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 18 KPI pulses covering *Error Rate*, *p95 / p99 Latency*, *Apdex Score*, *MTTA / MTTR*, *Incidents Open*, *Services Healthy / Degraded / Down*, *SLA Compliance*, and *Top Error Types* - so Dynatrace problems land in the same AI OS feed as commerce alerts. **Vortex Mind** investigates when MTTR or alerting concentration shifts; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the problem ticket.
