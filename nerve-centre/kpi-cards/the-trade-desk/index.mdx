Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **65** KPI pulses for The Trade Desk stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Trade Desk connector covers TTD's enterprise demand-side platform - programmatic display, video, audio, and CTV across the open internet. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 65 KPI pulses for TTD accounts in the Vortex IQ **AI OS** catalogue including the standard ad hero cards (*ROAS*, *Wasted Spend*, *Zero-Conversion Spend*, *Spend vs Budget*) plus campaign / device / geography / dayparting cuts. **Vortex Mind** investigates when ROAS drops across channels; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
