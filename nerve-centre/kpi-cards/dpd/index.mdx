Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **24** KPI pulses for DPD stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The DPD connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to UK and EU merchants shipping with DPD's predictive 1-hour delivery slot service across home delivery, Pickup, and Brexit-affected cross-border lanes. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 24 KPI pulses with hero alerts on *Avg Shipping Cost*, *On-Time Delivery Rate*, *Predict Slot Accuracy (30-min / 1-hour)*, *Late Shipments*, *Exception Rate*, *Open Claims*, *Days to Token Expiry*, plus cross-channel *DPD OTD by Sales Channel* and *UK→EU Cross-Border Exception Rate* so Brexit-lane health surfaces alongside domestic OTD. **Vortex Mind** investigates when slot-accuracy slips or a specific destination zone drifts; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the parcel.
