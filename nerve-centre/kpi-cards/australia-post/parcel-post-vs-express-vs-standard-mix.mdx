## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> The share of your Australia Post volume split across the three core service tiers: Parcel Post (the economy default), Express Post (the guaranteed-speed premium), and Standard / other products. Shown as a donut over the trailing 30 days. It is the single clearest read on the cost-versus-speed trade your despatch operation is actually making, parcel by parcel.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

## What it tracks

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:what_it_tracks */}
The card reads the Australia Post product code on each outbound consignment from the Shipping & Tracking API over the rolling 30-day window, buckets each into Parcel Post, Express Post, or Standard / other, and shows each bucket's share of total volume. The mix is a direct lever on your shipping cost: Express Post can run two to three times the Parcel Post rate per parcel, so a few points of share moving between tiers is a real swing in despatch spend.

Watch the mix for drift. A rising Express share lifts cost and your exposure to the next-business-day guarantee (and its refund clause); a rising Parcel Post share saves money but lengthens transit and can dent the customer experience on time-sensitive orders. A sudden shift usually traces to a checkout-copy change, a free-shipping-threshold change, or a default service swap in your fulfilment setup. Pair it with [Express Post Address-Eligibility Rate](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/australia-post/express-post-address-eligibility-rate) to check you are only selling the guarantee where it applies, [Express Post Next-Business-Day OTD](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/australia-post/express-post-next-business-day-otd) for the premium tier's performance, [Avg Shipping Cost](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/australia-post/avg-shipping-cost) for the cost consequence, and [Avg Transit (days)](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/australia-post/avg-transit-days) for the speed consequence.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:what_it_tracks */}

## Reconciling against the source

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
Cross-check the split against your consignment export in **MyPost Business** or the **Australia Post Merchant Portal**, grouped by product code over the same 30-day window, outbound only. Differences are normally period-boundary or timezone effects: Australia Post scan and lodgement timestamps are in carrier-local time (AEST / AEDT) and can lag ingestion by minutes to a few hours.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
