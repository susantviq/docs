Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **62** KPI pulses for OnBuy stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The OnBuy connector covers the UK marketplace specifically, with 62 KPI pulses tracked in the Vortex IQ **AI OS** catalogue. The **Nerve Centre** runs hero alerts (*Late Dispatch Queue*, *Listings Suspension Burst*, *Suspended Listings*, *Dispatch SLA Compliance*) plus cross-channel cards that fuse OnBuy with Amazon UK (*Amazon UK Top SKUs Missing on OnBuy*, *Catalogue Drift vs Amazon UK*, *OnBuy vs DTC Price Gap*) - the latter is one of the highest-ROI single-action recommendations the AI OS makes for UK multi-marketplace sellers. **Vortex Mind** investigates when SLA slips or listings get suspended; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the card.
