The SendGrid connector (Twilio's email API) brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to teams running transactional email at scale through SendGrid - the most-used transactional email API in commerce, used for order confirmations, shipping notifications, and account messaging. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 29 KPI pulses for SendGrid accounts including deliverability, bounce, and engagement metrics. **Vortex Mind** investigates when a number moves; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
