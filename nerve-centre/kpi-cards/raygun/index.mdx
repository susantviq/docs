Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **18** KPI pulses for Raygun stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Raygun connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to teams using Raygun for crash reporting, real-user monitoring, and application performance across web and mobile - a New Zealand-built error-tracking suite popular with mid-market engineering teams. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 18 KPI pulses covering *Error Rate*, *p95 / p99 Latency*, *Apdex Score*, *MTTA / MTTR*, *Incidents Open*, *Services Healthy / Degraded / Down*, *SLA Compliance*, and *Top Error Types*. **Vortex Mind** investigates when error patterns shift; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the source error.
