Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **16** KPI pulses for Recurly stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Recurly connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to subscription brands using Recurly for recurring billing, dunning, and revenue recognition across consumer and B2B SaaS. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 16 KPI pulses on the payment-flow side — *Success Rate*, *Decline Rate*, *3DS Abandonment Rate*, *Refund Rate*, *Chargeback Rate*, *Dispute Rate*, *Avg Settlement Time*, *Pending Payouts*, *Top Decline Reasons*, and *Top Payment Methods* — so involuntary churn from declined renewals doesn't go unattributed. **Vortex Mind** investigates when chargeback rate creeps toward thresholds; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the transaction.
