Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **23** KPI pulses for Zendesk stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Zendesk connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to teams running Zendesk Support - the dominant enterprise + mid-market customer-service platform. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 23 KPI pulses focused on **how Zendesk handles findings the AI OS pushes back to it**: hero alerts on *VortexIQ Findings Open*, *Finding Resolution Rate (90d)*, *Abandoned Findings (>14d no movement)*, plus throughput (*Avg Cycle Time*, *Avg Lead Time*) and backlog health (*Blocked Tickets*, *Oldest Open*, *Backlog by Priority/Status*). **Vortex Mind** investigates when findings stall or resolution rate dips; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
