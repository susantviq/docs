Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **18** KPI pulses for Atlassian Statuspage stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Atlassian Statuspage connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to teams using Statuspage to communicate incident status to customers, partners, and internal teams via public and private status pages. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 18 KPI pulses covering *Error Rate*, *p95 / p99 Latency*, *Apdex Score*, *MTTA / MTTR*, *Incidents Open*, *Incidents Resolved (24h)*, *Services Healthy / Degraded / Down*, and *SLA Compliance* — so customer-facing incidents and internal alerts read off the same picture. **Vortex Mind** investigates when public incidents go unresolved past target; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the incident.
