Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **16** KPI pulses for GoCardless stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The GoCardless connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to merchants taking direct debit and bank-to-bank payments - the dominant pattern for subscription, B2B, and high-AOV recurring billing. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 16 KPI pulses for GoCardless accounts (Success Rate, Decline Rate, Refund Rate, Chargeback Rate, Dispute Rate, settlement metrics, Top Decline Reasons, Top Payment Methods, Volume Trend). **Vortex Mind** investigates when a number moves; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
