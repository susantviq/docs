Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **21** KPI pulses for Deutsche Post stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Deutsche Post connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to merchants shipping with Germany's national postal carrier via DHL Paket, Warenpost, and international letter and parcel services across the EU. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 21 KPI pulses with hero alerts on *Avg Shipping Cost*, *On-Time Delivery Rate*, *Late Shipments*, *Exception Rate*, *Open Claims*, and *Days to Token Expiry*. **Vortex Mind** investigates when zone costs drift or first-attempt delivery dips; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the source shipment.
