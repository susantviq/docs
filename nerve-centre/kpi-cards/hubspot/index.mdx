Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **68** KPI pulses for HubSpot stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The HubSpot connector is the most-used integrated CRM + marketing platform in the SMB-to-mid-market segment. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 68 KPI pulses for HubSpot accounts in the Vortex IQ **AI OS** catalogue - including hero alerts (*Total Deal Pipeline Value*, *Pipeline Drop Alert*, *Deals Closing This Month*, *Pipeline Value by Stage*, *Lifecycle Stage Distribution*, *Workflow Error Spike*, *Workflows With Errors*, *Total Contacts*, *Marketing Emails Sent*) plus cross-platform cards (*Email-Attributed Commerce Revenue*, *Lifecycle Stage → First Purchase Conversion*, *Pipeline-vs-Realised Revenue Gap*). **Vortex Mind** investigates when pipeline drops or workflows break; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English (*"why did the pipeline value fall this week?"*) with citations back to the source card.
