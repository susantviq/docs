| | |

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
30-day Austin bookshop tender mix:

| Tender | Count | % of count | Volume share |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Contactless tap (CARD) | 2,420 | 42.8% | USD 55,800 |
| Chip + PIN (CARD) | 980 | 17.3% | USD 22,600 |
| Cash | 1,180 | 20.9% | USD 14,200 |
| Square Online (EXTERNAL, card) | 612 | 10.8% | USD 21,300 |
| Apple/Google Pay (WALLET, POS) | 240 | 4.2% | USD 5,200 |
| Cash App Pay | 88 | 1.6% | USD 2,940 |
| Magstripe swipe | 20 | 0.4% | USD 460 |
| Tap to Pay on iPhone | 95 | 1.7% | USD 2,180 |
| Manual entry (Virtual Terminal) | 14 | 0.2% | USD 4,800 |

Reads:

1. **Tap is now the dominant tender at 43%.** US contactless adoption was &lt;20% in 2019; mid-60s as of 2026. Merchants who haven't enabled NFC leave material conversion behind.
2. **Magstripe share at 0.4% is healthy.** Anything &lt;1% is normal residual; >5% means the chip reader has a fault.
3. **Cash at 21% is high for ecom-led brand but normal for a bookshop.** Books are low-margin walk-in tender.
4. **Cash App Pay at 1.6% of count, 2.3% of online volume.** In younger demographics it can hit 10, 15% of online volume.
5. **Tap to Pay on iPhone at 1.7%** is emerging tender; for sole proprietors at markets this is meaningful new revenue.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in the Square Dashboard:**

> **Reports → Sales by Item / Sales Summary** has tender breakdown tiles ("Card payments", "Cash", "Other").

The Dashboard's "Other" bucket includes Cash App Pay, Square Gift Cards, and external sources; this card splits them out.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**"Why is contactless not 100% of my tap-eligible transactions?"**
Some customers still prefer chip + PIN (older demographics, perceived security). Some banks issue cards without NFC. Some carriers' cases interfere with the NFC antenna.

**"How do I push customers toward Apple Pay / Google Pay?"**
Express checkout buttons on Square Online, Apple Pay-first promo at the till. Wallet adoption has secular growth.

**"Cash App Pay only US, will my international customers see it?"**
Cash App Pay is US-first (with limited UK pilot). Non-US shoppers won't see it.

**"Square Gift Cards, are they a tender here?"**
Yes; gift card redemptions appear as `source_type = GIFT_CARD`. Merchants with gift programs see 5, 15% of holiday volume on this rail.

**"Magstripe at 8%, what's wrong?"**
Either chip reader hardware fault (replace it) or the cashier defaulting to swipe out of habit. Run a 1-week reader-status check via Square Dashboard's Devices view.

**"Tap to Pay on iPhone, does it count as POS or as Online?"**
POS card-present (`source_type = CARD`, `entry_method = CONTACTLESS`).

**"Multi-location, does the mix vary by location?"**
Yes, hugely. A flagship downtown store sees more wallet + tap; a suburban store sees more chip + cash.

**"Manual entry / Virtual Terminal at 0.2%, what's that?"**
Phone orders entered by the cashier into Square Virtual Terminal. High decline rate due to absence of physical card.
