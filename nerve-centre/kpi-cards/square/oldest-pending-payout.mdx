**Card class:** Non-Hero  •  **Category:** Payment Gateway

## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> Days since the last successful payout from Square to your linked bank. Healthy: 1, 2 days (T+1 schedule). >3 days suggests weekend / holiday closure or schedule change. >7 days suggests an account review or banking issue.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it counts** | `(now - last_successful_payout.created_at)` in calendar days. |
| **Currency** | Currency-neutral. |
| **Time window** | Snapshot. |
| **Alert trigger** | `>3 days` absolute (excluding weekend / holiday adjusted). Sentiment inverse-gauge: good &lt;=2, warn >=5. |
| **Roles** | owner, finance, operations |
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
The bookshop, snapshot 02 May 26:

```text
Last payout       = 02 May 26 02:00 CT
Now               = 02 May 26 16:00 CT
Payout age        = 0.6 days

Healthy. T+1 schedule, deposit landed at 02:00, today's volume in flight for tomorrow.
```

A merchant with payout age of 5 days on a Tuesday would be unusual: the previous Thursday was the last payout, suggesting Friday Saturday Sunday Monday Tuesday all had no payout, which means either schedule was paused or no volume was available to deposit.

## Reconciling against the vendor's own dashboard

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in the Square Dashboard:**

> **Balance → All deposits** with newest-first sort. Compare top-row date to today.

**Common divergences:** time-zone (your account's vs UTC).

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** payout age varies per PSP; Stripe defaults to T+2, Square to T+1, so even healthy multi-rail merchants see different ages. Compare each rail to its own benchmark.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**"Payout age of 4 on a Tuesday morning, normal?"**
Possibly. Friday's payout (T+1 from Thursday) lands Friday 02:00. Saturday + Sunday no payouts (banking weekend). Monday's payout (T+1 from Friday) lands Monday 02:00. So Tuesday morning's "age" reads ~1 day. If you're seeing 4 days, something changed.

**"Bank holiday impact?"**
US: Federal Reserve holidays close ACH (typically 10, 11 days/year). UK: bank holidays close BACS / FPS. EU: per-country bank holidays. Square's payout pauses through these, age increases by the holiday count.

**"Why might Square pause my payouts?"**
Risk review (sudden volume surge, dispute spike, suspicious activity), bank-account verification issue (closed bank account at your end), or an active fraud investigation.

**"Same-day Instant Deposit, does that reset age?"**
Yes; Instant Deposit creates a payout record. Age resets to &lt;1 day after each Instant Deposit.

**"Multi-currency?"**
Per-currency age (a USD-only stream may be on T+1; a GBP stream on T+2).

**"Closed bank account, what happens?"**
Square retains the funds, blocks payouts; merchant must update banking details before payouts resume. Age grows daily until resolved.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
