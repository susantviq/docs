## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

Frustration, scroll depth, and conversion all behave differently by device, so knowing your split tells you which experience deserves the most attention. If 70% of sessions are mobile, a desktop-only bug matters less than a mobile checkout glitch, and this donut is what frames that judgement.

## Calculation

Calculated automatically from your FullStory data. Vortex IQ groups captured sessions by the device category FullStory records for each session and shows the share of each for the period. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}

## Reconciling against the vendor's own dashboard

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in FullStory's own dashboard:**

FullStory lets you segment sessions by device in session search and its reporting. Confirm period boundaries and segment filters match the Vortex IQ profile to reconcile cleanly.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Device classification.** Where tablets are grouped (with mobile or separate) can differ between views. | Variable | Align the device categories. |
| **Period boundary.** Vortex IQ uses 30-day rolling by default; FullStory dashboards may use calendar periods. | Variable | Match the period range. |
| **Segment scope.** Bot or internal traffic exclusion may differ. | Variable | Match segment and filter settings. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** complement with the captured-sessions and frustration cards. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Sessions by Device Type update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). For real-time signals, force a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does the split differ from my analytics tool?**
Capture sampling, consent gating, and ad blockers affect device categories unevenly, so the FullStory device mix can differ slightly from raw analytics. Treat it as the mix of captured sessions specifically.

**Q: Why does my FullStory dashboard show a different number?**
The most common reasons are device classification, period boundaries, and segment scope. Match these before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
This is a distribution card rather than a threshold metric, so there is no single alert; use it as context for the cards that do alert.
