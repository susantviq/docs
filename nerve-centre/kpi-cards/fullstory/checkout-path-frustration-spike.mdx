## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Checkout-Path Frustration Spike** is the real-time alert that fires when frustration events on cart and checkout pages surge within a short window. It is the watchdog version of the checkout-frustration metric: instead of waiting for you to open the dashboard, it raises an alert the moment friction at the point of purchase jumps. Because checkout is where money changes hands, this is one of the alerts worth waking up for, a sudden spike usually means a deploy or third-party script just broke the buy flow.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Checkout-Path Frustration Spike update?**
It evaluates in near real time against the alert rule, so entries appear as spikes are detected rather than on the standard refresh cadence.

**Q: Will it fire on a normal busy hour?**
The rule compares against a recent baseline, so a proportional rise during a busy hour should not trigger on its own. It fires when frustration jumps out of line with traffic.

**Q: Why does my FullStory dashboard show a different alert pattern?**
The most common reasons are the evaluation window, checkout URL definition, and baseline method. Align these before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, the spike sensitivity and window are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Tune them so the alert stays meaningful for your traffic level.
