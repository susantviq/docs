**Card class:** Card

> Estimated cost of holding aged inventory. Joined with commerce sell-through to flag dead stock with cash impact.

## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> Estimated monthly cost of holding inventory: capital cost + warehousing + insurance + obsolescence accrual. Typically 1.5-2.5% of inventory value per month.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it counts** | `Inventory_Value × monthly_carrying_rate` (default 2%). Configurable rate. |
| **Currency** | Reporting ledger. |
| **Time window** | `30D` |
| **Roles** | owner, finance |
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

## Calculation

Calculated automatically from your Oracle ERP Cloud data. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
$277M inventory × 2% / month = **$5.54M / month carrying cost**, ~$66M / year. Significant P&L hit; reducing aged stock by 20% saves $13M / year.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
