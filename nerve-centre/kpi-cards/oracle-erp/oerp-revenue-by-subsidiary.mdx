## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> Revenue split across Oracle Business Units. Available when multiple BUs are connected. Bar chart for entity-level revenue concentration view.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it counts** | `SUM(revenue) GROUP BY business_unit`. |
| **Currency** | Reporting ledger consolidated. |
| **Time window** | `30D` |
| **Roles** | owner, finance |

## Calculation

Calculated automatically from your Oracle ERP Cloud data. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
A US Fortune 500 retailer:
| BU | Revenue 30D |
| --- | --- |
| US Retail Inc | $48M |
| UK Retail Plc | $24M |
| EU DTC NV | $12M |
| Mexico Distribucion | $8M |

Five things: (1) US dominance; (2) FX consolidation impact; (3) per-BU vsP trend; (4) margin profile differs by BU; (5) inter-co flows excluded post-Financial Consolidation Hub.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
