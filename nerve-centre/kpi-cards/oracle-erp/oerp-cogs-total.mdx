## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> Total Cost of Goods Sold over the period, drawn from Oracle's COGS natural-account postings.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it counts** | `SUM(GL_JE_LINES posted to 5xxxx COGS accounts)` in window. |
| **Currency** | Reporting ledger. |
| **Time window** | `30D vsP` |
| **Roles** | owner, finance |
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

## Calculation

Calculated automatically from your Oracle ERP Cloud data. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
30D COGS $94.8M vs revenue $124.2M = 76.3% COGS ratio (23.7% gross margin). Pair with [Gross Margin %](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/oracle-erp/gross-margin-percentage).
