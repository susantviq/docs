## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
A US Fortune 500 distributor. Snapshot 14 Apr 26.

| Customer | Credit limit | Outstanding | Utilisation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ACME Industries | $5,000,000 | $4,820,000 | 96.4% |
| Beta Wholesale | $3,000,000 | $2,880,000 | 96.0% |
| Gamma Retail | $4,000,000 | $3,520,000 | 88.0% |
| Delta Manufacturing | $2,000,000 | $1,820,000 | 91.0% |

Five things to notice:

1. **4 customers over 85% threshold.** Each is one large order away from credit hold.
2. **Action options:** expand credit limit (if customer is healthy) or push collections to free up.
3. **Revenue at risk:** if these customers cannot order, expected forward revenue declines.
4. **Pair with [DSO](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/oracle-erp/days-sales-outstanding) trend** for predictive horizon.
5. **Track velocity:** rising utilisation week-over-week is the leading signal.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
