**Card class:** Card

> Net intercompany A/R minus A/P across business units. Should net to zero post-elimination.

## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> Net intercompany AR minus AP across Business Units. Should net to zero post-Financial Consolidation Hub elimination. Non-zero indicates booking errors or pending eliminations.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it counts** | `SUM(IC AR) - SUM(IC AP)` across BUs. |
| **Currency** | Reporting ledger. |
| **Time window** | Real-time. |
| **Roles** | owner, finance |
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

## Calculation

Calculated automatically from your Oracle ERP Cloud data. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
Pre-elimination IC AR $48M, IC AP $47.2M, net $0.8M. Post-elimination = $0 expected. The $0.8M residual is a known timing difference (AR booked Day 1, AP booked Day 2 across BUs). Will resolve at next consolidation run.
