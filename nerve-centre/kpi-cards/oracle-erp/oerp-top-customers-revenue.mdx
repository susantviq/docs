## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
A US Fortune 500 retailer. 90D window. Top-20 customers represent 42% of revenue ($168M of $400M). Concentrated mix of B2B wholesale + a handful of large enterprise accounts.

Five things to notice:

1. **Concentration risk:** top 5 = 24% of revenue.
2. **Mix:** 14 B2B + 6 enterprise B2C.
3. **Pair with churn signals**.
4. **Per-channel** view available.
5. **Historical trend** in a customer's rank tells the relationship story.
