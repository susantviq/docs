## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> Anomaly alert: credit hold count or value rising >2 standard deviations above baseline. Signals tightening credit policy or worsening customer payment behaviour.

| **Currency** | Reporting ledger. |

## Calculation

Calculated automatically from your Oracle ERP Cloud data. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
Baseline 22 holds / week; this week 48 holds. 2.3σ above baseline = alert fires. Drill: 18 of the 26 new holds are from one customer cohort tied to seasonal-end timing. Not systemic but worth tracking.
