## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> Count of unique customers who placed at least one Sales Order in the period. Tracks customer base health.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it counts** | `COUNT(DISTINCT customer_id WHERE so_count > 0)` in window. |
| **Currency** | n/a. |
| **Time window** | `30D` |
| **Roles** | owner, finance |
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

## Calculation

Calculated automatically from your Oracle ERP Cloud data. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
A US Fortune 500 distributor on Oracle ERP Cloud. 30D window.

| | This period | Prior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Active customers | 1,820 | 1,768 |

Five things to notice:

1. **3% growth vsP** is healthy.
2. **Customer concentration** matters as much as count.
3. **Mix of new vs repeat:** drill-down separates.
4. **Per-channel** (DTC, B2B, marketplace) reveals where growth is.
5. **Pair with [Customer Churn Signals](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/oracle-erp/customer-churn-signals)** for retention view.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Distinct customer = parent or child account?**
Parent by default; configurable.

**Cancelled-only customers count?**
No, requires at least one non-cancelled SO.

**Multi-BU?**
Yes via filter.
