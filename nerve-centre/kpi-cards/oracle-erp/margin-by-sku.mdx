## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> Per-SKU margin distribution. Identifies high-margin and below-break-even SKUs. Pareto for assortment review.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it counts** | `(revenue - cogs) / revenue` per SKU over window. Includes all SKUs with sales. |
| **Currency** | Reporting ledger. |
| **Time window** | `30D` |
| **Alert trigger** | `any below break-even` |
| **Roles** | owner, finance |
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}

## Calculation

Calculated automatically from your Oracle ERP Cloud data. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
8 SKUs below break-even (negative margin). Top loss-makers: heavily discounted clearance items. Action: clearance-out, retire from active assortment, or repricing.
{/* /HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
