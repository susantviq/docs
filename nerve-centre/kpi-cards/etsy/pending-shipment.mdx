## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Pending Shipment** is a live count of paid Etsy orders that have been bought and confirmed but not yet marked as dispatched in Shop Manager. Every order in this bucket is a clock that is already running against the ship-by date the buyer was promised at checkout. An Etsy seller cares because the on-time dispatch rate is one of the three pillars of the Star Seller badge, and because a buyer who waits too long for a shipping notification is far more likely to open a case or leave a low star rating. A healthy shop keeps this number flat and small relative to daily order volume; a worrying shop shows it climbing day over day, which usually means orders are arriving faster than they are going out the door.

## Calculation

Vortex IQ counts every Etsy order in a paid and confirmed state that has not yet been moved to a shipped or completed state. Cancelled and refunded orders drop out of the count once Etsy records the status change. The card is a point-in-time snapshot, so it rises as new paid orders land and falls as you mark parcels dispatched. The alert fires when the live count exceeds twice your trailing 30-day average, which adapts the trigger to your own normal volume rather than a fixed threshold.

## Reconciling against Etsy Shop Manager

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Etsy Shop Manager:**

Open Shop Manager > Orders & Shipping and filter to the New or Not Shipped view. The count of orders in that view is the direct counterpart to this card. You can cross-check individual ship-by dates there before working the queue.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Refresh timing.** The card is a snapshot from the last data refresh, while Shop Manager is live to the second. | Variable | Allow for orders that arrived or shipped in the gap between refreshes; re-check after the next refresh. |
| **Status lag.** An order marked shipped in Etsy can take a moment to propagate to the API. | Marginal | Treat very recent dispatches as already cleared even if the count has not yet dropped. |
| **Cancellations and refunds.** An order cancelled after payment leaves the pending bucket once Etsy records it. | Marginal | Reconcile against the Orders & Shipping cancelled filter if the counts diverge. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** If you fulfil Etsy orders from the same stock as another channel, use Vortex Mind to trace whether a pending spike on Etsy lines up with a fulfilment slowdown shared across connectors.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: Does this count orders that are paid but on a made-to-order or long processing time?**
Yes. Any paid order not yet marked shipped is counted, including made-to-order pieces. Read the number against your stated processing times, because a made-to-order shop will legitimately carry a larger steady backlog than a ship-from-stock shop.

**Q: Will marking an order as shipped immediately drop the count?**
It drops on the next data refresh once Etsy confirms the status. The change is usually quick, but the card is not live to the second the way Shop Manager is.

**Q: Why does my Etsy Shop Manager show a different number?**
Most often it is a period boundary or timing difference. The card reflects the last refresh while Shop Manager is live, and time zone differences can shift which orders fall inside a given day. Filter scope also matters, so make sure you are comparing against the Not Shipped view rather than all open orders.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes. Sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. A high-volume shop may want the multiple raised above 2x, while a small shop may prefer a low absolute cap so any handful of un-shipped orders is flagged.
