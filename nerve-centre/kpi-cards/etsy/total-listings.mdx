## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Total Listings** is the full count of every listing your Etsy shop holds, spanning active, expired, draft, and sold-out states as applicable. For an Etsy seller it is the headline measure of catalogue breadth: more well-made listings means more entry points into Etsy search, since each listing carries its own title, 13 tags, and attributes. A healthy number that grows steadily with fresh, on-brand product is good. A number that has stalled, or that hides a large pile of expired and draft listings doing no selling work, is a sign the catalogue needs attention. Because Etsy listings expire and renew on a 4-month cycle at $0.20 each, the total is also a quiet driver of fixed listing cost.

## Calculation

Vortex IQ counts the listings returned for your shop and reports the total at the moment of refresh. The figure includes listings in non-active states (such as expired and draft) where Etsy exposes them, which is why it is usually higher than the active-only listing count. Because it is a real-time snapshot, the number can move whenever you publish, expire, renew, or delete a listing. No averaging or windowing is applied.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
*Illustrative numbers.* On 14 Jun 26 a homeware seller sees Total Listings at 480, while the active-listings card reads only 360. The 120-listing gap is mostly expired stock the seller forgot about. They open Shop Manager, bulk-renew the 40 best-selling expired listings for $0.20 each, delete 50 seasonal items that will not return, and move the rest to draft for a refresh. Total Listings settles at 430 with a cleaner active base. To understand why so many had expired unnoticed, the seller opens Vortex Mind to trace the renewal pattern, then uses Ask Viq to ask "which expired listings sold best last year?" in plain English before deciding what to bring back.

## Reconciling against Etsy Shop Manager

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Etsy Shop Manager:**

Open Shop Manager, then Listings. The state filters along the top (Active, Draft, Expired, Sold Out, Inactive) let you tally each bucket. Add the buckets together to compare against the Vortex IQ total, since the Active tab alone will read lower.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **State scope.** Shop Manager defaults to the Active tab while Vortex IQ counts all states. | Vortex IQ higher | Sum all state tabs in Shop Manager before comparing. |
| **Refresh timing.** A listing published, renewed, or deleted after the last refresh is not yet reflected. | Variable | Re-check after the next standard data refresh. |
| **Draft and inactive handling.** Drafts and inactive items may be excluded from some Shop Manager views. | Variable | Confirm which state tabs you included in your manual tally. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** If your Etsy catalogue is fed from another system, open Vortex Mind to trace whether a listing total mismatch traces back to a sync or publish step upstream.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: Does this count only listings that are selling?**
No. Total Listings deliberately includes non-active states so you can see the full catalogue. Use the active-listings card for the selling-only subset.

**Q: Why did my total jump up on the same day with no new products?**
Renewing or re-activating expired listings moves them back into countable states, which can lift the total without any new product being created.

**Q: Why does my Etsy Shop Manager show a different number?**
Shop Manager usually opens on the Active tab, which under-counts. Time zone and refresh timing can also cause small gaps if you compare immediately after editing listings. Sum all state tabs and re-check after the next refresh.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes. Alert thresholds are configurable per profile in the Alert Rules tab. This card ships without an alert by default, but you can set one to flag, for example, a sudden drop in total listings that may indicate accidental bulk deletion.
