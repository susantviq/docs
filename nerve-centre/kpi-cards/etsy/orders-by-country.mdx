## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Orders by Country** breaks your Etsy order count down by the buyer's country and plots it as a choropleth map, so you can see at a glance where your demand actually comes from. An Etsy seller cares because country mix drives the decisions that quietly make or break margin: which shipping profiles to offer, where to set delivery times, which markets justify localised titles and tags, and where customs or postage costs are eating into otherwise healthy orders. Good looks like a mix that matches your shipping setup and your growth ambitions. Bad looks like a heavy concentration in markets you ship to slowly or expensively, or a fast-growing country you have no shipping profile or processing buffer for.

## Calculation

Each order in the trailing 30 days is attributed to the buyer's destination country and counted. The card totals those counts per country and renders them as a choropleth, where colour intensity reflects order volume. It counts orders rather than revenue, so a country with many small orders can outweigh one with a few large ones; pair it with revenue and average-order-value cards when you want the money view rather than the volume view.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
*Illustrative numbers.* On 23 Jun 26 a print shop opens Orders by Country expecting its usual home-market dominance, but sees Germany has climbed to roughly a quarter of the last 30 days of orders, illustratively up from a tenth a quarter earlier. The seller checks their shipping profiles, finds Germany on a slow and pricey rate, and adds a faster tracked option plus a German-language line in the titles and tags of their best sellers. ## Reconciling against Etsy Shop Manager

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Etsy Shop Manager:**

Reconcile against Shop Manager > Stats, which shows where your visits and orders come from by region, and Shop Manager > Orders & Shipping, where each order shows its destination country. The Stats view is the closest native equivalent to this card's geographic breakdown.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Country basis.** The card attributes by buyer destination country; gift orders may ship to a different country than the buyer's account. | Variable | Treat destination as the shipping reality and confirm gift orders separately. |
| **Window alignment.** The card uses a trailing 30 days, while Stats lets you pick custom ranges. | Marginal | Match the Stats date range to the card window before comparing. |
| **Cancelled orders.** A cancelled order may drop from one view sooner than the other. | Marginal | Reconcile against a settled period to avoid in-flight cancellations. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** If you sell the same range on other marketplaces, use Vortex Mind to compare country mix across channels and spot a market that is strong on Etsy but untapped elsewhere.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: Does the map show revenue or order count?**
Order count. A country can dominate the map on many low-value orders. Pair it with the average-order-value and revenue siblings when you need the money picture.

**Q: A gift order shipped abroad, which country does it count under?**
The card attributes by the destination country of the order, so a gift shipped to another country counts under that destination, which is usually what you want for shipping decisions.

**Q: Why does my Etsy Shop Manager show a different number?**
Shop Manager Stats may use a different date range and time zone than the card's trailing 30-day window, and it can blend visits with orders in some views. Match the range and look at the orders metric specifically before comparing.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
This card has no alert by default because country mix is descriptive rather than pass-or-fail. You can still configure a sensitivity rule per profile in the Sensitivity tab, for example to flag when a single country's share of orders moves sharply.
