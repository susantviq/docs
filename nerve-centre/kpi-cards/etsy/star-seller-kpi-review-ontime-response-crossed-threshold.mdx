## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Star Seller KPI (Review / OnTime / Response) Crossed Threshold** is a composite alert that watches Etsy's three Star Seller criteria at once: average review rating, on-time shipping, and message response speed. Etsy awards the Star Seller badge only when all three stay above their bars over the trailing review window, so a single metric slipping below the line is enough to cost you the badge and the search-visibility lift that comes with it. An Etsy seller cares because the badge is a trust signal buyers actively filter and shop by. Good looks like all three KPIs comfortably above their thresholds with headroom; bad looks like any one of them touching or crossing the line, because the badge is all-or-nothing.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What it counts** | The number of Star Seller criteria currently below threshold (review rating under 4.8, on-time dispatch under 95%, or message response slower than 24 hours). |
| **Sample type** | Backend API data from Etsy, refreshed on the standard data refresh. |
| **Why it matters** | Losing the Star Seller badge removes a visible trust marker and the associated search boost, which can dent click-through and conversion across your whole shop, not just one listing. |
| **Reading the value** | 0 means all three criteria are safe. Any value above 0 names how many criteria have crossed; treat 1 as urgent because Star Seller requires all three. |
| **Currency** | number |
| **Time window** | `RT` |
| **Alert trigger** | `review<4.8 OR ontime<95% OR response<24h` |
| **Sentiment key** | `ets_alert_account_health_drop` |

## Calculation

Vortex IQ pulls the three Star Seller inputs Etsy exposes for your shop: the rolling average star rating from buyer reviews, the share of orders dispatched by their estimated ship date, and your typical first-message response time. Each input is compared to its Star Seller bar (4.8 average rating, 95% on-time, response within 24 hours). The card returns a count of how many of the three are currently below the bar. Because Etsy treats the badge as all-or-nothing, the card escalates to an alert the moment any single criterion crosses, rather than waiting for an average.

## Reconciling against Etsy Shop Manager

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Etsy Shop Manager:**

Open Shop Manager > Star Seller dashboard to see Etsy's own read on all three criteria with their progress bars. Cross-check the review average under Shop Manager > Stats and your message response indicator in Shop Manager > Messages.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Review-window boundary.** Etsy's Star Seller window and the card's refresh may not align to the same minute. | Marginal | Treat a fresh crossing as a heads-up and confirm against the Star Seller dashboard before acting. |
| **Pending reviews not yet posted.** A buyer's recent review may be counted on one side before the other. | Variable | Recheck after the next refresh; the gap usually closes within a cycle. |
| **Response time uses different rounding.** Etsy and the card may round median response differently near the 24-hour mark. | Marginal | When response sits close to the bar, clear the inbox proactively rather than relying on the exact figure. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** If review or dispatch trends move in step with a parallel marketplace, open Vortex Mind to investigate whether a shared fulfilment or staffing issue is the root cause.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: Does crossing one criterion really lose the whole badge?**
Yes. Etsy requires all three Star Seller criteria to be met at once, so the card treats any single crossing as badge-threatening rather than averaging the three together.

**Q: How quickly can I recover the badge after a slip?**
The badge recalculates on Etsy's review cadence, so once the failing criterion is back above its bar it can be restored at the next assessment. The card returning to 0 tells you the underlying numbers are safe again.

**Q: Why does my Etsy Shop Manager show a different number?**
Usually a period-boundary or time-zone difference: the Star Seller dashboard and the card may sample at slightly different points, and pending reviews or recently dispatched orders can land on one side first. The values converge after a refresh cycle.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes. Sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. You can tighten the buffer so the card warns before a criterion actually touches Etsy's bar, giving your team lead time to react.
