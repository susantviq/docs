## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Etsy Shop Health Score** is a single composite number that rolls up the signals Etsy buyers and the Etsy search algorithm care about most: your review rate, your on-time shipping rate, the share of your catalogue that is active and sellable, and the stability of your listings in Etsy search. An Etsy seller cares because the score is an early warning that tells you, in one glance, whether the shop is drifting toward a Star Seller badge loss, a search visibility dip, or a buyer-experience problem before any single metric trips its own alert. A healthy shop sits comfortably in the high range with no component dragging the average down. A struggling shop shows a falling score driven by one weak pillar, for example a run of late shipments or a wave of expired listings, that the composite surfaces before it becomes a revenue problem.

## Calculation

The score blends four Etsy pillars into one index: the rate at which orders earn reviews, the share of orders shipped within your declared processing window, the proportion of your listings that are active and in stock rather than expired or sold out, and how stable your listings are in Etsy search over the window. Each pillar is normalised and weighted, then combined into a single value. Because it is a composite, a strong score can hide one weak pillar, and a falling score always traces back to one or more underlying components rather than the index itself.

## Reconciling against Etsy Shop Manager

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Etsy Shop Manager:**

There is no single Shop Health Score inside Etsy, so reconcile pillar by pillar. Check Shop Manager > Star Seller dashboard for on-time shipping and review signals, Shop Manager > Listings for the active and expired counts that drive the sellable pillar, and Shop Manager > Stats for search visibility trends that feed the rank-stability pillar.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Composite weighting.** Vortex IQ combines four pillars into one index, while Etsy reports each metric separately. | Variable | Compare each pillar to its matching Etsy view rather than expecting a single matching number. |
| **Window alignment.** The card uses a real-time or 7-day view; Etsy's Star Seller dashboard runs on its own rolling assessment window. | Marginal | Allow for the boundary difference when a pillar looks slightly off. |
| **Sellable definition.** The active-listing pillar excludes expired and sold-out listings that Etsy may still list in your catalogue total. | Variable | Reconcile against the active-only filter in Shop Manager > Listings. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** If your Etsy catalogue is fed from another platform, use Vortex Mind to confirm that expired or drifted listings on the source system are not silently lowering the sellable pillar.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: My score dropped but every individual metric looks fine, why?**
A composite can fall on a small dip across several pillars at once even when no single metric trips its own alert. Open each pillar's sibling card to find the combined cause.

**Q: Is the score the same as Etsy's Star Seller status?**
No. Star Seller is a pass or fail badge on Etsy's own criteria. The health score is a continuous early-warning index that includes Star Seller signals plus listing and search health, so it moves before the badge does.

**Q: Why does my Etsy Shop Manager show a different number?**
Etsy never shows a single combined health score, and each pillar inside Etsy runs on its own period and time zone. Reconcile pillar by pillar against the matching Shop Manager view rather than against one figure.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes. Sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. You can raise the 70 line if you want earlier warning, or set component-aware sensitivity so a single weak pillar triggers a flag even when the composite still reads healthy.
