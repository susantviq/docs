Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **17** KPI pulses for Etsy stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Etsy connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to Etsy sellers - handmade, vintage, and craft-supply brands running on the marketplace. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 17 KPI pulses for Etsy stores including *Total Revenue*, *Listing Quality Score*, *Avg Review Rating*, *Dispute Rate*, *Marketplace Fees Paid*, *Listings Expiring Soon*, and *Pending Payouts*. **Vortex Mind** investigates when listing quality, dispute rate, or fees move; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the card.
