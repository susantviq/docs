## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Listings with SEO-Weak Titles** counts the listings whose titles are working against you in Etsy search. A title is flagged when it is too short to carry meaningful search terms, when it is missing the key words buyers actually type, or when it leans on brand-only or repeated text that Etsy cannot match to real queries. An Etsy seller cares because the title, alongside tags and attributes, is one of the strongest signals Etsy uses to rank a listing, and a weak title quietly suppresses placement and views. Etsy allows up to 140 characters in a title, so there is room to be specific. Good looks like a small flagged count; bad looks like a large share of the catalogue carrying lazy or thin titles that bleed search visibility.

## Calculation

Vortex IQ inspects the title of each active listing and flags it when it fails one or more title-quality checks: insufficient length to carry search terms, absence of the relevant key terms a buyer would search, or over-reliance on brand-only or repeated phrasing. The card reports the count of flagged listings and the alert fires when that count passes 10% of your active catalogue, so the trigger scales with shop size rather than a fixed number. Etsy's 140-character title limit defines the headroom the checks measure against.

*Illustrative numbers.* On 14 Jun 26 a stationery shop with 200 active listings sees the card read 34, which is 17% of the catalogue and over the 10% trigger, so the alert fires. ## Reconciling against Etsy Shop Manager

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Etsy Shop Manager:**

Open Shop Manager > Listings to review and edit the titles themselves, and Shop Manager > Stats to see the search terms and views each listing is drawing. There is no single Etsy screen that labels a title as weak, so the card is doing the assessment Etsy leaves to you.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Etsy has no weak-title report.** The flag is Vortex IQ's quality assessment, not an Etsy field. | Variable | Use Shop Manager to act on each flagged listing; expect no matching Etsy count. |
| **Judgement on key terms.** What counts as a missing key term depends on the niche and current buyer language. | Variable | Sanity-check flags against the search terms shown in Stats. |
| **Recent edits.** A title fixed in Shop Manager clears on the next refresh, not instantly. | Marginal | Re-check the count after the next data refresh. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** If product titles flow from another system, use Vortex Mind to trace whether weak Etsy titles originate upstream and are repeating across channels.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: My titles look fine to me. Why are they flagged?**
The checks judge titles for Etsy search, not readability. A title that reads well to a human can still be too short, miss the terms buyers actually type, or repeat brand text the algorithm cannot match. Compare each flag against the search terms in Stats.

**Q: Will fixing titles guarantee a ranking jump?**
No single change guarantees a ranking, because Etsy weighs titles alongside tags, attributes, listing quality, and engagement. Strong titles remove a known drag and give the other signals a chance to work.

**Q: Why does my Etsy Shop Manager show a different number?**
Etsy does not publish a weak-title count, so there is nothing to match directly. Differences against your own tally usually come from edits made since the last refresh and from differing views on which key terms matter. Re-check after a refresh and align on the search terms shown in Stats.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes. Sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. A shop investing heavily in search may tighten the trigger below 10%, while a shop mid-rewrite may loosen it to avoid repeated alerts during the cleanup.
