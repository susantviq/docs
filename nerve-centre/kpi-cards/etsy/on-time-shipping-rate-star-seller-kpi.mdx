## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **On-Time Shipping Rate (Star Seller KPI)** is the share of your orders that were marked shipped, with tracking where required, inside the processing window you declared on each listing. It is one of the named criteria Etsy uses to award the Star Seller badge, and Etsy expects at least 95% of orders to ship on time. An Etsy seller cares because falling below the 95% line puts the badge at risk, and the badge drives buyer trust, conversion, and search prominence. Good looks like a rate sitting comfortably above 95% with margin to absorb the odd late order. Bad looks like a rate hovering near or below 95%, where a single late batch can tip you out of badge eligibility.

## Calculation

The rate is the count of orders shipped on time divided by total shippable orders in the window, expressed as a percentage. An order counts as on time when it is marked shipped, with valid tracking where Etsy requires tracking, on or before the end of the processing window you declared on the listing. The current 30 days is compared against the prior comparable period, and the alert fires when the rate falls below the 95% Star Seller threshold.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
*Illustrative numbers.* A leather-goods shop normally runs at 98% on-time. On 23 Jun 26 the card shows the rate has slipped to 94% and tripped the alert below 95%. The owner opens the card and sees a cluster of orders shipped a day late after a courier collection was missed. They batch-ship the remaining open orders the same day, add tracking to each, and pad the declared processing window on their slowest-to-make listings to build buffer. Within the rolling window the rate climbs back above 95% and the badge stays safe. ## Reconciling against Etsy Shop Manager

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Etsy Shop Manager:**

Reconcile against Shop Manager > Star Seller dashboard, which shows your on-time shipping percentage against the 95% target for Etsy's rolling assessment window. Cross-check individual orders under Shop Manager > Orders & Shipping, where each order shows its ship-by date and whether tracking was added.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Assessment window.** Etsy's Star Seller dashboard runs on its own rolling period, while the card uses a trailing 30 days. | Marginal | Expect a small gap near the period edges; trend direction matters more than the exact figure. |
| **Tracking requirement.** An order shipped on time but missing required tracking may not count as on time. | Variable | Confirm tracking was added on orders that look on time but score against you. |
| **Processing window edits.** Changing a listing's declared window changes the ship-by date used for new orders. | Variable | Reconcile each order against the window that applied at purchase, not the current one. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** If your fulfilment is driven from another system, use Vortex Mind to confirm that the late orders trace to a dispatch delay there rather than to Etsy marking.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: I shipped the item but it still counts as late, why?**
On-time is judged against the declared processing window and the marked ship date, with tracking where Etsy requires it. If you shipped physically but marked it late, or omitted required tracking, Etsy may not credit it as on time.

**Q: How much buffer above 95% should I hold?**
Because the metric is a rolling rate, a single late batch in a low-volume week can swing it sharply. Aiming for a comfortable margin above 95% rather than sitting on the line protects the badge through quiet weeks.

**Q: Why does my Etsy Shop Manager show a different number?**
The Star Seller dashboard uses Etsy's own rolling assessment window and time zone, while the card uses a trailing 30-day view. Near period boundaries the two can read slightly differently even when both are correct.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes. Sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. Many sellers set the alert above 95%, for example at 96% or 97%, so they get an early warning before they actually breach the Star Seller line.
