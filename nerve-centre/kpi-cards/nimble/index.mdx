Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **29** KPI pulses for Nimble stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Nimble connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to teams on Nimble - the social-CRM platform that pulls contact context from social profiles and email signatures, popular with relationship-led sales teams. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 29 KPI pulses including subscriber, deliverability, engagement, and revenue-attribution metrics. **Vortex Mind** investigates when a number moves; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
