Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **18** KPI pulses for Better Uptime stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Better Uptime connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to teams using Better Stack's modern uptime monitoring, status pages, and on-call routing - a developer-focused alternative to legacy tools with sub-30s checks and screenshot-on-fail diagnostics. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 18 KPI pulses covering *Alerts Firing*, *MTTA / MTTR*, *Incidents Open*, *Services Healthy / Degraded / Down*, *p95 / p99 Latency*, *SLA Compliance*, and *Top Alerting Services*. **Vortex Mind** investigates when probes fail in patterns that suggest regional issues; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the monitor.
