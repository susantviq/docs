## Calculation

```
Count of messages delivered in the last 30d. Degrades to no_data (reason not_tracked): an incoming webhook exposes no history/read method, so sent volume cannot be counted.
```

Computed live by the Slack engine on the standard sync. The engine degrades to no data rather than estimating: if the source is unreadable, the card says so instead of guessing.

### Tracked live in Vortex IQ Nerve Centre

Connect Slack in [Settings, Connectors](https://app.vortexiq.ai/v2/settings?tab=sources) and this card renders live alongside the rest of the connector's metrics.
