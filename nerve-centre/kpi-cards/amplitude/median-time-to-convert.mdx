## At a glance

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:tldr */}
> **Median Time to Convert** is the median elapsed time between entering the primary purchase funnel and completing it in Amplitude, taken across users who converted. It captures the tempo of the journey, which often shifts before the conversion rate itself moves. The card pairs with sibling metrics in the Analytics category to build a complete diagnostic picture; cross-reference the related cards listed below for context.

## Calculation

Calculated automatically from your Amplitude data. For each user who completed the primary purchase funnel, Amplitude measures the elapsed time from the first step to the final step, then reports the median across those converters over the selected window. This card surfaces that median for the 30-day window and compares it against the prior period. The median is used so that a small number of very slow converters does not skew the value. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
*A representative reading of **Median Time to Convert** for a typical merchant on Amplitude.* Suppose half of converting users complete the funnel within 18 minutes this period, versus 12 minutes in the prior period. The 6-minute increase suggests rising hesitation, perhaps a slower page, a confusing new layout, or higher-consideration traffic. The conversion rate may still look stable, which is why this leading signal is useful. Use Vortex Mind to trace whether the slowdown ties to a specific step, device, or release; for natural-language exploration, ask Ask Viq how time to convert differs between new and returning users.

## Reconciling against Amplitude

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Amplitude's own dashboard:**

Open the Funnels chart for the primary purchase funnel and switch to the time-to-convert view, then read the median rather than the average. Confirm the step definitions and conversion window match the Vortex IQ profile. The Dashboards section may pin a saved funnel, and Event Segmentation can verify the underlying step event counts.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Period boundary.** Vortex IQ uses a 30-day rolling window by default; an Amplitude funnel may be set to calendar periods. | Variable | Match the date range on the Amplitude funnel to a rolling 30 days. |
| **Time zone.** Amplitude buckets events by the project time zone; Vortex IQ aligns to the merchant reporting time zone. | Marginal | Confirm the project time zone matches your reporting time zone. |
| **Filter or segment scope.** Profile-level filters (platform, B2B, test users) change which converters are included in the median. | Variable | Apply the same segment to the Amplitude funnel. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** complement with sibling cards in the same category for the full diagnostic picture. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does Median Time to Convert update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). For real-time signals, force a manual refresh from the dashboard.

**Q: Why does my Amplitude dashboard show a different number?**
The most common reasons are reading the average instead of the median, step-definition and conversion-window differences, period-boundary differences (Vortex IQ uses 30-day rolling vs an Amplitude funnel set to calendar months), time-zone alignment, and segment scope. Match these settings before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: How does Median Time to Convert relate to other Amplitude metrics?**
It is the tempo behind the funnel outcome. Read it with funnel conversion and step drop-off to see whether slowing journeys are turning into lost conversions. Single-metric reads can mislead; the diagnostic value is in the cross-reference.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, sensitivity thresholds are configurable per profile in the Sensitivity tab. This card ships without a default alert, but you can add one tuned to your typical conversion tempo rather than relying on a generic default.
