## At a glance

It is the long-run durability test: the point on the retention curve where casual users have churned and only genuinely habitual ones remain. ## Calculation

Calculated automatically from your Amplitude data. Amplitude groups users into cohorts by their first event, then measures the percentage who returned and performed the retention event on day 30. This card reports that day-30 percentage over a 90-day window and compares it against the prior period. Because a cohort needs a full 30 days to be eligible, the metric trends slowly and reflects mature, long-run behaviour. See the At a glance summary above for what the metric tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Worked example

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:worked_example */}
*A representative reading of **D30 Retention %** for a typical merchant on Amplitude.* Suppose cohorts from the last quarter return at 11 percent on day 30, against an alert floor of 15 percent and a prior-period reading of 16 percent. The slip below the floor signals that long-term durability is eroding: users are trying the store but fewer are forming a lasting habit. Because D30 moves slowly, a confirmed decline here is meaningful and rarely noise. ## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: How often does D30 Retention % update?**
The card refreshes on the standard data refresh (typically every 30-60 minutes for live integrations). However, any given cohort's D30 only finalises once a full 30 days have elapsed, so the metric inherently lags recent activity.

**Q: Why does my Amplitude dashboard show a different number?**
The most common reasons are choosing day-range retention instead of N-day, different start and return event definitions, cohort-range differences (Vortex IQ uses a 90-day window), time-zone day boundaries, and segment scope. Match these settings before assuming a real divergence.

**Q: How does D30 Retention % relate to other Amplitude metrics?**
D30 is the durable tail that D1 and D7 lead into. Read it with those earlier points and the full curve to judge whether early stickiness translates into long-term habit. Single-metric reads can mislead; the diagnostic value is in the cross-reference.

**Q: Can I customise the alert threshold?**
Yes, alert thresholds are configurable per profile in the Alert Rules tab. The default trips below 15 percent; adjust it to match your business baseline rather than relying on the generic default.
