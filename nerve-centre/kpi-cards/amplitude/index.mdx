The Amplitude connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to product teams on Amplitude - the enterprise product analytics platform strong on retention curves, behavioural cohorts, and predictive segmentation. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 13 KPI pulses for Amplitude projects (Sessions, New Users, Returning Users, Real-Time Users, Avg Session Duration, Pageviews per Session, Bounce Rate, Cart Abandonment Rate, Session Conversion Rate, Goal Completions, Top Pages, Top Traffic Sources). **Vortex Mind** investigates when a number moves; **Ask Viq™** lets you ask in plain English with citations back to the source card.
