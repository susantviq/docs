## Calculation

For each significant landing page, this card pairs a performance signal with the bounce rate Amplitude records for sessions that begin on that page, presenting the result as a sortable table over the selected period. The alert flags rows where a slow page and a high bounce rate occur together, since that combination is the strongest indicator of experience-driven drop-off. See the At a glance summary above for what the card tracks and the worked example below for a typical reading.

## Reconciling against Amplitude

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:reconcile */}
**Where to look in Amplitude's own dashboard:**

Use Event Segmentation or a Pathfinder report grouped by landing page to read bounce behaviour per page, and pull the page performance signal from your performance tooling. This card combines two different systems, so the performance side lives outside Amplitude. Amplitude Dashboards can hold the bounce view; compare it against the same 30-day window in Vortex IQ. Confirm how a landing page and a bounce are defined before deciding the readings disagree.

**Why the Vortex IQ value may legitimately differ:**

Because this is a cross-platform card, you are comparing Amplitude event data with a separate landing-page performance source. Definitional differences between the two systems are expected, not a fault.

| Reason | Direction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Period boundary.** The bounce window and the performance sampling window may not align exactly. | Variable | Match both to the same 30-day window. |
| **Time zone.** Amplitude uses the project time zone; Vortex IQ aligns to the merchant reporting time zone. | Marginal | Confirm time zone match. |
| **Definition and scope.** What counts as a bounce, and which pages count as landing pages, can differ between systems and profile filters. | Variable | Align the bounce and landing-page definitions. |

**Cross-connector reconciliation:** because two systems are compared, expect baseline differences and focus on the rows where both signals move together. For divergence investigations, use Vortex Mind.

## Known limitations / merchant FAQs

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:faqs */}
**Q: Why does Amplitude's bounce rate differ from my performance tool's view?**
The two come from different systems with different definitions. Amplitude measures session behaviour, while a performance tool measures load and render timing. A row matters most when both signals are weak together, regardless of small definitional gaps.

**Q: A page is slow but has a low bounce rate. Should I still fix it?**
Performance is worth improving on its own, but this card prioritises rows where slow performance and high bounce co-occur, because those are where speed is most clearly costing you sessions. Use the broader performance cards for slow-but-sticky pages.

**Q: How do I know which page to fix first?**
Sort the table by the pages with both the worst performance and the highest bounce against meaningful traffic. A high-bounce page with little traffic recovers less than a moderately bad page that receives a large campaign audience.

**Q: Can I customise the co-occurrence threshold?**
Yes, the performance and bounce thresholds that define a flagged row are configurable per profile in the Alert Rules tab. Tune them to your store's typical landing-page baselines rather than relying on the generic default.
