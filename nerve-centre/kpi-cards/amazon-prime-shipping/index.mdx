Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **25** KPI pulses for Amazon Prime Shipping (SFP) stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Amazon Prime Shipping (SFP) connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to merchants enrolled in Seller Fulfilled Prime - shipping their own warehouse stock to Prime customers under Amazon's strict 1-day / 2-day delivery promise and 99% OTD threshold. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 25 KPI pulses with hero alerts on *Avg Shipping Cost*, *On-Time Delivery Rate*, *SFP On-Time Delivery vs 99% Threshold*, *Prime 1-Day / 2-Day Promise Miss Rate*, *Late Shipments*, *Exception Rate*, *Open Claims*, *Days to Token Expiry*, plus cross-channel *Late-Shipment Buy Box Loss* and *SFP Eligibility Risk by ASIN* - because losing Prime eligibility loses the listing. **Vortex Mind** investigates when ASINs trend toward the 99% breach line; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the order.
