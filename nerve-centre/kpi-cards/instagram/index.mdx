Vortex IQ Nerve Centre tracks **13** KPI pulses for Instagram stores. Each metric has its own page: definition, calculation, why it matters, and what to do when it moves.

{/* HUMAN_AUTHORED:landing_positioning */}
The Instagram connector brings the Vortex IQ **AI OS** to brands tracking organic Instagram as a customer-acquisition channel - specifically, the *traffic that flows from Instagram to the merchant's storefront*, not native post-level metrics like reach or follower growth. The **Nerve Centre** tracks 13 KPI pulses on *Sessions*, *Real-Time Users*, *New / Returning Users*, *Bounce Rate*, *Pageviews per Session*, *Avg Session Duration*, *Session Conversion Rate*, *Cart Abandonment Rate*, *Goal Completions*, and *Top Pages / Top Traffic Sources* - so a viral Reel translates into measurable storefront behaviour, not just impressions. **Vortex Mind** investigates when bounce-rate from Instagram traffic spikes; **Ask Viq™** answers in plain English with citations back to the session.
