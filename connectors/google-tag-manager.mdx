## Audit risks and opportunities

A fix status appears only where the action, inputs, approval, verification and recovery controls are mapped. Candidate remediations are never executable.

| Check | Severity | Outcome | Why it matters | Fix status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Core Web Vitals failing on mobile (LCP &gt; 2.5s OR CLS &gt; 0.1 OR INP &gt; 200ms)** | critical | Grow revenue | Failing Core Web Vitals on mobile suppresses rankings sitewide and correlates directly with conversion loss; this is the field data Google actually uses. | Report only |
| **CWV pass rate below 75% on mobile** | high | Customer experience | When fewer than three quarters of sampled pages pass Core Web Vitals, slow experiences are the norm rather than the exception for real visitors. | Report only |
| **Lighthouse performance score below 50 on mobile** | high | Grow revenue | A mobile performance score below 50 means most shoppers meet a visibly slow store; speed is a ranking factor and a conversion factor at once. | Report only |
| **Lighthouse SEO score below 90** | medium | Grow revenue | Lighthouse SEO failures are basic crawlability and metadata problems that suppress pages regardless of how good the content is. | Report only |
| **Lighthouse accessibility score below 90** | medium | Customer experience | An accessibility score below 90 means common assistive-technology journeys are breaking; lost customers and legal exposure in several markets. | Report only |
| **Render-blocking resources blocking &gt; 500ms** | medium | Run operations | Render-blocking resources delay the first paint beyond half a second; the store feels slower than it needs to be on every single visit. | Report only |
