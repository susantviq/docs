    ### Audit risks and opportunities

    A fix status appears only where the action, inputs, approval, verification and recovery controls are mapped. Candidate remediations are never executable. Open a check for the detail.

    <AccordionGroup>
      <Accordion title="CRM API token invalid or scope insufficient">

        **Severity** critical &middot; **Outcome** Catalogue quality &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Once this credential fails, VortexIQ stops seeing deals, contacts and pipeline activity from this CRM entirely, and every pipeline and data-quality finding here goes stale from that moment, silently, until someone notices the numbers stopped updating.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `CRM-AUTH-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Median lead response time above 8h">

        **Severity** high &middot; **Outcome** Grow revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Leads answered after 8 hours convert at a fraction of the rate of leads answered in minutes; speed-to-lead is the cheapest conversion lever in the pipeline.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `CRM-LEAD-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Stalled open deals above threshold (no activity in 14 days)">

        **Severity** high &middot; **Outcome** Run operations &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Deals with no activity for two weeks are quietly dying; a nudge list ordered by value recovers some before they close-lose themselves.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `CRM-PIPE-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="CRM data hygiene below 80% (missing required fields)">

        **Severity** medium &middot; **Outcome** Run operations &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        More than 1 in 5 records is missing a required field. Incomplete records make segmentation, reporting and any automation keyed off those fields (routing, scoring, follow-up sequences) unreliable, so the gap compounds into every process built on top of this data, not just the records themselves.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `CRM-DATA-001`

      </Accordion>
