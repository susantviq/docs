## Audit risks and opportunities

A fix status appears only where the action, inputs, approval, verification and recovery controls are mapped. Candidate remediations are never executable.

| Check | Severity | Outcome | Why it matters | Fix status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Site views dropped > 20% vs prior period** | high | Grow revenue | Site views dropping over 20 percent needs a cause before it needs a reaction: seasonality, tracking, or a real ranking loss all look identical at the top line. | Report only |
| **Comment moderation backlog above 20** | medium | Protect revenue | A moderation backlog above 20 leaves genuine questions unanswered in public and spam visible on the store's own pages. | Report only |
| **Over 40% of content not updated in 12 months** | medium | Catalogue quality | When over 40 percent of content has not been touched in a year, decay outpaces creation; rankings quietly rot page by page. | Report only |
| **Publishing pipeline stalled (no post in 30 days)** | medium | Run operations | A stalled publishing pipeline slowly starves organic traffic; content compounds only while it keeps shipping. | Candidate remediation |
