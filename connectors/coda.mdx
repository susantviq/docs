        **Severity** high &middot; **Outcome** Run operations &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        A source that has not updated in over a day means every conclusion drawn from it describes yesterday; the staleness is invisible unless surfaced.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `BYO-FRESH-001`

        **Severity** medium &middot; **Outcome** Catalogue quality &middot; **Fix status** Report only
