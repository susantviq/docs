    ### Audit risks and opportunities

    A fix status appears only where the action, inputs, approval, verification and recovery controls are mapped. Candidate remediations are never executable. Open a check for the detail.

    <AccordionGroup>
      <Accordion title="Engagement rate below 1%">

        **Severity** medium &middot; **Outcome** Grow revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Engagement below 1 percent means content is reaching feeds and being scrolled past; format and hook need work before spend goes behind it.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `SOCIAL-ENGAGEMENT-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Reach dropped &gt; 20% vs prior period">

        **Severity** medium &middot; **Outcome** Run operations &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Reach dropping over 20 percent usually means the algorithm has deprioritised the account or a format change landed badly; catching it early keeps the slide shallow.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `SOCIAL-REACH-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Posting cadence collapsed (no post in 14 days)">

        **Severity** low &middot; **Outcome** Grow revenue &middot; **Fix status** Candidate remediation

        Posting cadence collapsing to zero lets the audience's attention lapse; reach compounds only while the account stays present.

        A likely corrective operation exists, but its mapping and recovery are not yet complete, so execution is not offered. Evidence and recommended manual steps are provided.

        Reference: `SOCIAL-CADENCE-001`

      </Accordion>
