    ### Audit risks and opportunities

    A fix status appears only where the action, inputs, approval, verification and recovery controls are mapped. Candidate remediations are never executable. Open a check for the detail.

      <Accordion title="Dispute rate above 1% (Visa/Mastercard threshold)">

        **Severity** critical &middot; **Outcome** Protect revenue &middot; **Fix status** Candidate remediation

        1% is the threshold Visa and Mastercard use to flag a merchant as excessive, and crossing it puts the account at risk of monitoring programs, extra fees, and in sustained cases, the ability to accept those card networks at all, on top of losing the disputed sales themselves.

        A likely corrective operation exists, but its mapping and recovery are not yet complete, so execution is not offered. Evidence and recommended manual steps are provided.

        Reference: `PAYMENT-DISPUTE-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Decline rate above 8%">

        **Severity** high &middot; **Outcome** Protect revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        More than 8% of payment attempts are being declined. Every decline is a customer who was ready to pay and did not complete the sale, so this is lost revenue at the very last step of the funnel, after every earlier marketing and conversion cost has already been paid.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `PAYMENT-DECLINE-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Refund rate spiked &gt; 50% vs 7-day average">

        **Severity** high &middot; **Outcome** Protect revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Refunds jumped more than 50% above the recent weekly average. A spike this fast and this size usually has one specific, recent cause worth finding quickly, before it settles into an ongoing pattern that is harder to trace back to its origin.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `PAYMENT-REFUND-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="3DS challenge friction , &gt; 30% of qualifying transactions abandoned">

        **Severity** medium &middot; **Outcome** Protect revenue &middot; **Fix status** Candidate remediation

        More than 3 in 10 shoppers who reach a 3D Secure identity challenge are abandoning it rather than completing it. These are customers who already committed to buying and entered payment details, so this is revenue lost at the very last step, to friction rather than to lack of interest.

        A likely corrective operation exists, but its mapping and recovery are not yet complete, so execution is not offered. Evidence and recommended manual steps are provided.

        Reference: `PAYMENT-3DS-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Payout overdue &gt; 5 days vs typical schedule">

        **Severity** medium &middot; **Outcome** Grow revenue &middot; **Fix status** Candidate remediation

        A payout is running more than 5 days behind this gateway's usual schedule. That is revenue already earned that has not reached the store's bank account, which affects cash flow directly regardless of how sales themselves are performing.
