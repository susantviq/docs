    ### Audit risks and opportunities

    A fix status appears only where the action, inputs, approval, verification and recovery controls are mapped. Candidate remediations are never executable. Open a check for the detail.

    <AccordionGroup>
      <Accordion title="Spam complaint rate &gt; 0.1%">

        **Severity** critical &middot; **Outcome** Protect revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Spam complaints are the fastest way to damage sender reputation with mailbox providers; a rate this high can get future campaigns throttled or routed to spam even for recipients who never complained, which drags down deliverability for the whole list, not just the segment that complained.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `EM-DEL-002`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Abandoned-cart flow not live">

        **Severity** high &middot; **Outcome** Protect revenue &middot; **Fix status** Candidate remediation

        Abandoned-cart recovery is usually the single highest-ROI automation in email: high-intent shoppers who nearly bought are contacted at exactly the right moment. Without it they are never contacted at all.

        A likely corrective operation exists, but its mapping and recovery are not yet complete, so execution is not offered. Evidence and recommended manual steps are provided.

        Reference: `EM-FLOW-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Bounce rate &gt; 5%">

        **Severity** high &middot; **Outcome** Protect revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        More than 1 in 20 emails is bouncing. Beyond the immediate lost reach, mailbox providers watch bounce rate as a sender-reputation signal, and a rate this high risks future emails, even to good addresses, landing in spam instead of the inbox.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `EM-DEL-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Delivery rate &lt; 95%">

        **Severity** high &middot; **Outcome** Protect revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        More than 1 in 20 emails is not reaching an inbox at all, whether blocked, bounced, or filtered. Every campaign, promotion or transactional email built on this list is reaching a materially smaller audience than the send count suggests.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `EM-DEL-003`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Welcome flow not live">

        **Severity** medium &middot; **Outcome** Grow revenue &middot; **Fix status** Candidate remediation

        New subscribers are at their most engaged in their first days; without a live welcome flow that window closes unconverted.

        A likely corrective operation exists, but its mapping and recovery are not yet complete, so execution is not offered. Evidence and recommended manual steps are provided.

        Reference: `EM-FLOW-002`
