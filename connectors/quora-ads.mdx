    ### Audit risks and opportunities

    A fix status appears only where the action, inputs, approval, verification and recovery controls are mapped. Candidate remediations are never executable. Open a check for the detail.

    <AccordionGroup>
      <Accordion title="Conversion tracking broken (clicks &gt; 0 but conversions = 0)">

        **Severity** critical &middot; **Outcome** Grow revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Over 100 clicks with zero recorded conversions almost never means genuinely zero sales; it means the ad platform cannot see the sales that are happening. Every bid strategy that optimises toward conversions is currently optimising against no signal at all, which makes the campaign's spend effectivel

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `AD-TRACK-002`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Wasted spend , campaigns with ROAS &lt; 1 over 30d">

        **Severity** critical &middot; **Outcome** Grow revenue &middot; **Fix status** Candidate remediation

        A campaign returning less than $1 of revenue for every $1 spent is losing money on every dollar put into it, before accounting for product cost or fulfilment. Over 30 days that is not a bad day, it is a sustained drain on ad budget that could be funding a campaign that actually returns a profit.

        A likely corrective operation exists, but its mapping and recovery are not yet complete, so execution is not offered. Evidence and recommended manual steps are provided.

        Reference: `AD-SPEND-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Missing UTM tags in ad destination URLs">

        **Severity** high &middot; **Outcome** Control risk and change &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Ads landing without UTM tags cannot be attributed, so the spend disappears from every report and the channel looks worse than it is.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `AD-TRACK-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Zero-conversion spend detected (B2B pipeline leak)">

        **Severity** high &middot; **Outcome** Protect revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Keywords that spend without ever converting are the purest form of wasted budget, and the list is exact: these terms, this spend, zero return.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `QRA-WASTE-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Budget &gt; 80% used before mid-month">

        **Severity** medium &middot; **Outcome** Grow revenue &middot; **Fix status** Candidate remediation

        More than 80% of this month's budget is gone before the month is half over. At this pace the campaign runs out of budget and stops serving for the back half of the month, losing whatever sales it would have driven during that gap.

        A likely corrective operation exists, but its mapping and recovery are not yet complete, so execution is not offered. Evidence and recommended manual steps are provided.

        Reference: `AD-SPEND-002`
