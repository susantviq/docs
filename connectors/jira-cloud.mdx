    ### Audit risks and opportunities

    A fix status appears only where the action, inputs, approval, verification and recovery controls are mapped. Candidate remediations are never executable. Open a check for the detail.

    <AccordionGroup>
      <Accordion title="API token / credential expired or about to expire">

        **Severity** critical &middot; **Outcome** Control risk and change &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Once this credential expires, VortexIQ stops seeing ticket and project activity from this tool entirely, and every backlog, capacity and cycle-time finding here goes stale from that moment, silently, until someone notices the numbers stopped updating.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `PM-AUTH-001`

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `PM-ABANDONED-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Assignee overloaded (&gt;10 open tickets)">

        **Severity** medium &middot; **Outcome** Protect revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        At least one person is carrying more than 10 open tickets at once. Beyond the burnout risk, this usually means their tickets move slower than the team average simply from context-switching, which shows up later as missed deadlines that look unrelated to the real cause.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `PM-CAPACITY-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Backlog blocked-tickets count growing">

        **Severity** medium &middot; **Outcome** Grow revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Blocked tickets grew more than 50% versus the prior period. Blocked work does not disappear, it queues up, so a growth rate this size now usually means a larger backlog crunch and slower delivery in the weeks ahead, once those tickets eventually unblock all at once.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `PM-BACKLOG-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Cycle time degrading &gt; 25% week-over-week">

        **Severity** medium &middot; **Outcome** Run operations &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Tickets are taking over 25% longer to complete than they did the prior week. A shift this fast usually has a specific, findable cause, catching it this week is far easier than after it has become the new normal and nobody remembers what changed.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `PM-CYCLE-001`
