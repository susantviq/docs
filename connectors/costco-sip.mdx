    A fix status appears only where the action, inputs, approval, verification and recovery controls are mapped. Candidate remediations are never executable. Open a check for the detail.

      <Accordion title="Listings missing required attributes (varies per marketplace)">

        **Severity** high &middot; **Outcome** Grow revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        A listing missing a required attribute is commonly suppressed, down-ranked in marketplace search, or blocked from certain placements entirely, so it can be losing sales without showing any error the seller would notice on the listing itself.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `MARKETPLACE-LISTING-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Marketplace fee % drifted &gt; 2pp vs last quarter">

        **Severity** medium &middot; **Outcome** Grow revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        The share of revenue this marketplace takes as fees has shifted by more than 2 percentage points from last quarter. On thin-margin products, a shift this size can move a listing from profitable to break-even without anything on the store side changing at all.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `MARKETPLACE-FEE-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Payout overdue &gt; 7 days vs marketplace's typical schedule">

        **Severity** medium &middot; **Outcome** Run operations &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        A payout is running more than 7 days behind this marketplace's usual schedule. That is cash the store has already earned sitting outside its bank account, which matters directly to cash flow regardless of how the underlying sales are performing.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `MARKETPLACE-PAYOUT-001`
