## Audit risks and opportunities

A fix status appears only where the action, inputs, approval, verification and recovery controls are mapped. Candidate remediations are never executable.

| Check | Severity | Outcome | Why it matters | Fix status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **API token expired or about to expire** | critical | Control risk and change | With invalid credentials the tool has stopped collecting entirely; every metric that depends on it is silently stale. | Report only |
| **Stockout with active demand (OOS where committed > 0)** | critical | Protect revenue | Stockouts on items with committed demand are immediate lost sales plus cancellation work; the highest-velocity SKUs deserve the earliest warning. | Candidate remediation |
| **On-time delivery rate below 90%** | high | Grow revenue | On-time delivery below 90 percent erodes repeat purchase intent, the cheapest revenue a store has. | Report only |
| **Orders breaching SLA in flight (any > 0)** | high | Protect revenue | Orders breaching SLA while still in flight can often be rescued if flagged now; after delivery they become refunds and complaints instead. | Candidate remediation |
| **SLA compliance below 90%** | high | Run operations | SLA compliance below 90 percent means late delivery is systemic, not incidental; contractual penalties and churn both follow. | Report only |
| **Carrier OTD below 85%** | medium | Run operations | A carrier delivering on time less than 85 percent of the time is damaging the store's promise; reroute volume or renegotiate. | Report only |
| **Return rate above 8%** | medium | Catalogue quality | Returns above 8 percent usually concentrate in specific products or size ranges; finding the cluster turns a cost centre into a fixable defect. | Report only |
| **Shipping cost per order up >10% vs prior period** | medium | Run operations | Shipping cost per order rising faster than basket value quietly eats margin on every single shipment. | Candidate remediation |
| **Warehouse capacity utilisation > 85%** | medium | Run operations | Warehouse utilisation above 85 percent leaves no surge headroom; peak days start breaching SLA the moment volume spikes. | Candidate remediation |
