## Audit risks and opportunities

A fix status appears only where the action, inputs, approval, verification and recovery controls are mapped. Candidate remediations are never executable.

| Check | Severity | Outcome | Why it matters | Fix status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Token invalid, expired or lacking the report scope the cards need** | high | Control risk and change | With invalid credentials the tool has stopped collecting entirely; every metric that depends on it is silently stale. | Report only |
| **Active users dropped more than 30% vs the prior period** | medium | Grow revenue | Active users dropping by a third signals adoption failure or licence waste; both are worth catching within the billing cycle. | Report only |
| **Message volume dropped more than 40% vs the prior period (team going quiet)** | medium | Grow revenue | Message volume collapsing usually means the team moved channels or an integration broke; either way the record of work is going dark. | Report only |
