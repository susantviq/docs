    ### Audit risks and opportunities

    A fix status appears only where the action, inputs, approval, verification and recovery controls are mapped. Candidate remediations are never executable. Open a check for the detail.

    <AccordionGroup>
      <Accordion title="Token invalid, expired or lacking the report scope the cards need">

        **Severity** high &middot; **Outcome** Control risk and change &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        With invalid credentials the tool has stopped collecting entirely; every metric that depends on it is silently stale.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `COLLAB-AUTH-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Active users dropped more than 30% vs the prior period">

        **Severity** medium &middot; **Outcome** Grow revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Active users dropping by a third signals adoption failure or licence waste; both are worth catching within the billing cycle.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `COLLAB-USERS-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Message volume dropped more than 40% vs the prior period (team going quiet)">

        **Severity** medium &middot; **Outcome** Grow revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Message volume collapsing usually means the team moved channels or an integration broke; either way the record of work is going dark.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `COLLAB-MSG-001`

      </Accordion>
