## Audit risks and opportunities

A fix status appears only where the action, inputs, approval, verification and recovery controls are mapped. Candidate remediations are never executable.

| Check | Severity | Outcome | Why it matters | Fix status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **CSAT below 70%** | high | Grow revenue | CSAT below 70 percent predicts churn before it shows in revenue; the transcripts behind the score say exactly what to fix. | Report only |
| **First-response SLA breached on open conversations** | high | Run operations | Breached first-response SLAs compound: late answers create chase messages, which create more queue, which creates later answers. | Report only |
| **Median first-response time above 4h** | medium | Protect revenue | Customers are waiting a median of over 4 hours just to hear back for the first time, before their actual issue is even addressed. Slow first response is one of the most common drivers of a b | Report only |
| **Open-conversation backlog rising > 20% week-over-week** | medium | Run operations | The open-conversation backlog grew more than 20% in a week. A growing backlog means response and resolution times for everyone in the queue are about to get worse, not just the customers who | Report only |
