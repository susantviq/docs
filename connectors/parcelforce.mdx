    ### Audit risks and opportunities

    A fix status appears only where the action, inputs, approval, verification and recovery controls are mapped. Candidate remediations are never executable. Open a check for the detail.

    <AccordionGroup>
      <Accordion title="API token expired or about to expire (&lt;14 days)">

        **Severity** critical &middot; **Outcome** Run operations &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Once this token expires, VortexIQ stops receiving tracking and delivery data from this carrier entirely, and every finding in this report that depends on courier data goes stale from that moment, silently, until someone notices shipments look wrong.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `COURIER-AUTH-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Exception rate above 3%">

        **Severity** high &middot; **Outcome** Protect revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        More than 3% of shipments with this carrier are hitting an exception, a failed delivery attempt, damage, an address problem, a lost parcel, each of which usually turns into a support ticket and, often, a refund or reshipment cost the store absorbs.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `COURIER-EXCEPTION-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="On-time delivery rate below 90%">

        **Severity** high &middot; **Outcome** Protect revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        More than 1 in 10 shipments with this carrier is arriving late. Late delivery is one of the most common drivers of customer support contacts, refund requests and negative reviews, and it lands on the store's reputation even though the carrier caused it.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `COURIER-OTD-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Open claims unresolved &gt; 7 days">

        **Severity** high &middot; **Outcome** Run operations &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Courier claims open beyond 7 days are money owed sitting unclaimed; carriers rely on claimants giving up.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `COURIER-CLAIM-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Avg cost per shipment up &gt; 10% vs prior period">

        **Severity** medium &middot; **Outcome** Run operations &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Average cost per shipment with this carrier rose more than 10% compared to the prior period. Shipping is rarely re-priced into the product on the same timeline it changes, so a jump this size quietly erodes margin on every order this carrier touches until pricing or carrier choice is revisited.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `COURIER-COST-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Failed-delivery rate above 2%">

        **Severity** medium &middot; **Outcome** Protect revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Failed deliveries above 2 percent create support contacts, redelivery costs and refunds; address validation and carrier choice are the usual levers.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `COURIER-FAIL-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="OTD trending down &gt;5pp WoW">

        **Severity** medium &middot; **Outcome** Run operations &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        On-time delivery dropped more than 5 percentage points in a single week. A move this fast usually has a specific, recent cause (a service disruption, a capacity issue at the carrier, a seasonal volume spike), which is worth catching now, before it settles into a new, worse normal that customers star

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `COURIER-OTD-002`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Single-carrier dependency on a route (&gt;80% volume)">

        **Severity** medium &middot; **Outcome** Run operations &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Over 80% of shipping volume on one route runs through a single carrier. If that carrier has an outage, a rate hike, or a capacity problem on that route, there is no fallback already in place, and the store finds out at the worst possible time, mid-disruption, rather than in advance.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `COURIER-DEPENDENCY-001`
