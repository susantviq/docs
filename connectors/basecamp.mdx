    ### Audit risks and opportunities

    A fix status appears only where the action, inputs, approval, verification and recovery controls are mapped. Candidate remediations are never executable. Open a check for the detail.

      <Accordion title="API token / credential expired or about to expire">

        **Severity** critical &middot; **Outcome** Control risk and change &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Once this credential expires, VortexIQ stops seeing ticket and project activity from this tool entirely, and every backlog, capacity and cycle-time finding here goes stale from that moment, silently, until someone notices the numbers stopped updating.
