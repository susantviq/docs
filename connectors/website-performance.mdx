    ### Audit risks and opportunities

    A fix status appears only where the action, inputs, approval, verification and recovery controls are mapped. Candidate remediations are never executable. Open a check for the detail.

    <AccordionGroup>
      <Accordion title="Core Web Vitals failing on mobile (LCP &gt; 2.5s OR CLS &gt; 0.1 OR INP &gt; 200ms)">

        **Severity** critical &middot; **Outcome** Grow revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Failing Core Web Vitals on mobile suppresses rankings sitewide and correlates directly with conversion loss; this is the field data Google actually uses.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `WEBPERF-CWV-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="CWV pass rate below 75% on mobile">

        **Severity** high &middot; **Outcome** Customer experience &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        When fewer than three quarters of sampled pages pass Core Web Vitals, slow experiences are the norm rather than the exception for real visitors.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `WEBPERF-CWV-002`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Lighthouse performance score below 50 on mobile">

        **Severity** high &middot; **Outcome** Grow revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        A mobile performance score below 50 means most shoppers meet a visibly slow store; speed is a ranking factor and a conversion factor at once.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `WEBPERF-PERF-001`

        **Severity** medium &middot; **Outcome** Run operations &middot; **Fix status** Report only
