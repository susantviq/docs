## Audit risks and opportunities

A fix status appears only where the action, inputs, approval, verification and recovery controls are mapped. Candidate remediations are never executable.

| Check | Severity | Outcome | Why it matters | Fix status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **CI pass rate below 80%** | high | Run operations | More than 1 in 5 CI runs is failing. At this rate, a genuinely broken change is more likely to slip through unnoticed (lost in the normal noise of failures) and developers waste time re-runn | Report only |
| **Delivery cadence dropped more than 50% vs the prior period** | medium | Grow revenue | Deploys or merges have dropped by more than half compared to the prior period. That usually means fixes and improvements, including fixes for issues this same audit has flagged elsewhere, ar | Report only |
| **Median open pull-request age above 14 days** | medium | Run operations | Half of open pull requests have been waiting more than two weeks. Work that is done but not merged is not delivering any value yet, and the longer a PR sits open, the more it tends to drift  | Report only |
