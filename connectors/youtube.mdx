## Audit risks and opportunities

A fix status appears only where the action, inputs, approval, verification and recovery controls are mapped. Candidate remediations are never executable.

| Check | Severity | Outcome | Why it matters | Fix status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Engagement rate below 1%** | medium | Grow revenue | Engagement below 1 percent means content is reaching feeds and being scrolled past; format and hook need work before spend goes behind it. | Report only |
| **Reach dropped > 20% vs prior period** | medium | Run operations | Reach dropping over 20 percent usually means the algorithm has deprioritised the account or a format change landed badly; catching it early keeps the slide shallow. | Report only |
| **Posting cadence collapsed (no post in 14 days)** | low | Grow revenue | Posting cadence collapsing to zero lets the audience's attention lapse; reach compounds only while the account stays present. | Report only |
