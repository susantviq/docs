    ### Audit risks and opportunities

    A fix status appears only where the action, inputs, approval, verification and recovery controls are mapped. Candidate remediations are never executable. Open a check for the detail.

    <AccordionGroup>
      <Accordion title="Key events not configured (purchase event firing but conversions = 0)">

        **Severity** critical &middot; **Outcome** Grow revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        The property has meaningful traffic but reports zero conversions, which almost never means zero sales; it means the tool cannot see the sales that are happening. Every conversion-based report and any ad spend optimised against this tool's conversion signal is blind until this is fixed.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `ANALYTICS-TRACK-003`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Attribution divergence with ecommerce platform &gt; 25% over 30d">

        **Severity** high &middot; **Outcome** Protect revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Analytics showing conversions while the store records zero orders (or the reverse) means one of the two systems is wrong; every decision made on the divergent numbers is at risk.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `ANALYTICS-ATTR-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Mobile Search Performance Gap">

        **Severity** medium &middot; **Outcome** Grow revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Ranking worse on mobile than desktop for the same queries points at mobile experience problems, and mobile is where most ecommerce searches happen.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `GSC03`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="No Rich Results Detected in Search">

        **Severity** medium &middot; **Outcome** Grow revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        No rich results means competitors' listings show stars and prices where this store shows plain text on the same results page.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `GSC04`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Sitemap(s) With Errors / Warnings">

        **Severity** medium &middot; **Outcome** Catalogue quality &middot; **Fix status** Candidate remediation

        Sitemap errors mean Google's map of the store is wrong; new products and updates propagate late or not at all.

        A likely corrective operation exists, but its mapping and recovery are not yet complete, so execution is not offered. Evidence and recommended manual steps are provided.

        Reference: `GSC05`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Low Indexing Coverage">

        **Severity** low &middot; **Outcome** Run operations &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Pages Google has seen but not indexed earn nothing; low coverage usually traces to quality thresholds, redirects or crawl blocks that are fixable.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `GSC01`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Pages With High Impressions But &lt; 1% CTR">

        **Severity** low &middot; **Outcome** Grow revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        High impressions with under 1 percent click-through means the store is being seen and skipped; titles and snippets on these exact pages are the highest-leverage rewrite list available.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `GSC02`
