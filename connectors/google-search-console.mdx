## Audit risks and opportunities

A fix status appears only where the action, inputs, approval, verification and recovery controls are mapped. Candidate remediations are never executable.

| Check | Severity | Outcome | Why it matters | Fix status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Key events not configured (purchase event firing but conversions = 0)** | critical | Grow revenue | The property has meaningful traffic but reports zero conversions, which almost never means zero sales; it means the tool cannot see the sales that are happening. Every conversion-based repor | Report only |
| **Attribution divergence with ecommerce platform > 25% over 30d** | high | Protect revenue | Analytics showing conversions while the store records zero orders (or the reverse) means one of the two systems is wrong; every decision made on the divergent numbers is at risk. | Report only |
| **Mobile Search Performance Gap** | medium | Grow revenue | Ranking worse on mobile than desktop for the same queries points at mobile experience problems, and mobile is where most ecommerce searches happen. | Report only |
| **No Rich Results Detected in Search** | medium | Grow revenue | No rich results means competitors' listings show stars and prices where this store shows plain text on the same results page. | Report only |
| **Sitemap(s) With Errors / Warnings** | medium | Catalogue quality | Sitemap errors mean Google's map of the store is wrong; new products and updates propagate late or not at all. | Candidate remediation |
| **Low Indexing Coverage** | low | Run operations | Pages Google has seen but not indexed earn nothing; low coverage usually traces to quality thresholds, redirects or crawl blocks that are fixable. | Report only |
| **Pages With High Impressions But < 1% CTR** | low | Grow revenue | High impressions with under 1 percent click-through means the store is being seen and skipped; titles and snippets on these exact pages are the highest-leverage rewrite list available. | Report only |
