    ### Audit risks and opportunities

    A fix status appears only where the action, inputs, approval, verification and recovery controls are mapped. Candidate remediations are never executable. Open a check for the detail.

    <AccordionGroup>
      <Accordion title="Key events not configured (purchase event firing but conversions = 0)">

        **Severity** critical &middot; **Outcome** Grow revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        The property has meaningful traffic but reports zero conversions, which almost never means zero sales; it means the tool cannot see the sales that are happening. Every conversion-based report and any ad spend optimised against this tool's conversion signal is blind until this is fixed.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `ANALYTICS-TRACK-003`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Attribution divergence with ecommerce platform &gt; 25% over 30d">

        **Severity** high &middot; **Outcome** Protect revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Analytics showing conversions while the store records zero orders (or the reverse) means one of the two systems is wrong; every decision made on the divergent numbers is at risk.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `ANALYTICS-ATTR-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Cart abandonment rate above 75%">

        **Severity** high &middot; **Outcome** Protect revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Three out of four shoppers who add something to their cart leave without buying it. These are the closest prospects the store has, people who already decided they wanted a specific product, so this is lost revenue that was nearly captured, not cold traffic that never showed interest.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `ANALYTICS-CART-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Goal Stopped Converting">

        **Severity** high &middot; **Outcome** Grow revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Goals present but never completing typically means the goal or its tracking broke, not that every visitor changed their mind on the same day.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `PLA01`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Plausible Conversions but Zero Ecom Orders">

        **Severity** high &middot; **Outcome** Protect revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Analytics showing conversions while the store records zero orders (or the reverse) means one of the two systems is wrong; every decision made on the divergent numbers is at risk.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `PLA05`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Session Conversion Rate Below Floor">

        **Severity** high &middot; **Outcome** Grow revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Session conversion below the floor while traffic holds means the store is buying visits it cannot convert; the cause is usually specific and findable.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `PLA02`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Session conversion rate below 1%">

        **Severity** high &middot; **Outcome** Grow revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Fewer than 1 in 100 sessions convert. At this rate the store needs a large multiple of today's traffic just to hold revenue flat, so the most efficient lever right now is fixing conversion, not buying more visitors who will convert at the same low rate.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `ANALYTICS-TRACK-002`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Average engagement time below 30 seconds">

        **Severity** medium &middot; **Outcome** Grow revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Under 30 seconds of engaged time is barely enough to read a headline, let alone consider a product. Visitors this disengaged are unlikely to convert on this visit or return for another, so traffic that looks healthy in a session count is not translating into attention.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `ANALYTICS-ENGAGE-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Bounce Rate Spike">

        **Severity** medium &middot; **Outcome** Protect revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        A bounce-rate spike after a release or campaign change is the earliest cheap warning that something on the landing experience broke.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `PLA03`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Bounce rate above 70% over 30d">

        **Severity** medium &middot; **Outcome** Protect revenue &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        Seven in ten visitors are leaving after one page. Whatever is bringing them, an ad, a search result, a link, is not being matched by what they find when they arrive, and every one of those visits was still a cost (ad spend, content effort, SEO ranking) that produced no second page view.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `ANALYTICS-TRACK-001`

      </Accordion>

        **Severity** medium &middot; **Outcome** Control risk and change &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        A sharp drop in captured events or sessions usually means a tracking break after a site change, not a real traffic collapse; decisions made on the partial data will be wrong.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.
