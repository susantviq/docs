    A fix status appears only where the action, inputs, approval, verification and recovery controls are mapped. Candidate remediations are never executable. Open a check for the detail.

    <AccordionGroup>
      <Accordion title="Data-source API token invalid or access revoked">

        Once this token fails, VortexIQ stops pulling data from this source entirely, and every card and finding built on it goes stale from that moment, silently, until someone notices the numbers stopped updating.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `BYO-AUTH-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Source not updated in over 24h (stale data)">

        **Severity** high &middot; **Outcome** Run operations &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        A source that has not updated in over a day means every conclusion drawn from it describes yesterday; the staleness is invisible unless surfaced.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.

        Reference: `BYO-FRESH-001`

      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Required-field empty rate above 15%">

        **Severity** medium &middot; **Outcome** Catalogue quality &middot; **Fix status** Report only

        When required fields are empty in more than 15 percent of rows, every downstream calculation inherits the gaps and quietly under-reports.

        Vortex IQ detects and explains this; resolution is manual, with evidence and recommended steps.
