Connecting Amazon Ads to Vortex IQ gives the AI OS a live read on your Amazon Ads data, feeding the Nerve Centre dashboard, Vortex Mind diagnostics, and Ask Viq.

## What data Vortex IQ reads from Amazon Ads

- **Campaign**
- **AdGroup**
- **ProductAd**
- **Keyword**
- **SearchTerm**
- **ReportRow**

## How to connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connected Sources">
    In your Vortex IQ workspace, go to **Settings → Connected Sources**. You need Admin role in the workspace to connect sources.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Amazon Ads card">
    Locate the Amazon Ads connector card and click **+ Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorise with Amazon Ads">
    You are redirected to Amazon Ads's own authorisation page (`https://www.amazon.com/ap/oa`). Sign in and approve the requested permissions. Vortex IQ requests these scopes: `advertising::campaign_management`, `advertising::reporting`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to Vortex IQ">
    Amazon Ads redirects you back to Vortex IQ once authorisation completes. The connector card flips to Connected automatically; no credentials are copied by hand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the first data pull">
    KPI cards begin populating once the first sync completes. Timing depends on the Amazon Ads API response size and rate limits.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Available KPI cards

Vortex IQ populates **96** Nerve Centre cards from your Amazon Ads data. See the [Amazon Ads KPI card reference](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/amazon-ads) for the full list with definitions, calculations, and sentiment bands.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Credential verification fails">
    Re-check each value against the Amazon Ads developer console for typos or trailing whitespace. If a scope or permission is missing, Amazon Ads will typically return a 401 or 403 on the verification call rather than a generic failure.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Cards show no data after connecting">
    Allow time for the first sync to complete; large accounts can take longer. If cards are still empty after an hour, disconnect and reconnect to confirm the credentials were saved correctly.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
