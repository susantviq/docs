Vortex IQ's connector graph spans 215 integrations across 11 connector types. Every connector you add in **Settings → Connected Sources** populates Nerve Centre with KPI cards, feeds Vortex Mind diagnostics, and becomes queryable through Ask Viq. The value of the platform grows with each connector you add-cross-channel findings are only possible when the graph has data from multiple sources.

To connect any source, go to **Settings → Connected Sources → Add Source**, search or browse by type, and follow the OAuth or credential flow for that connector.

## Connect a source

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connected Sources">
    Go to **Settings → Connected Sources** and click **Add Source**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your connector">
    Use the search bar or browse by type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    Follow the OAuth pop-up or enter API credentials depending on the connector type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. Nerve Centre cards for that connector appear after the initial data pull (typically 2-10 minutes for OAuth sources, up to 30 minutes for high-volume credential-based sources).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Start by connecting your primary commerce platform, at least one ad platform, and your payment gateway. These three together unlock the most powerful Vortex Mind cross-channel diagnostics.
</Tip>
