Vortex IQ's connector graph spans 239 connectors across 19 categories, the same grouping you see in **Settings -> Connected Sources**. Every connector you add populates Nerve Centre with KPI cards, feeds Vortex Mind diagnostics, and becomes queryable through Ask Viq. The value of the platform grows with each connector you add, since cross-channel findings are only possible when the graph has data from multiple sources.

To connect any source, go to **Settings -> Connected Sources -> Add Source**, search or browse by type, and follow the OAuth or credential flow for that connector. Connectors without a linked card page are live in the app with documentation on the way.

## Connect a source

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connected Sources">
    Go to **Settings -> Connected Sources** and click **Add Source**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your connector">
    Use the search bar or browse by type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    Follow the OAuth pop-up or enter API credentials depending on the connector type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. Nerve Centre cards for that connector appear after the initial data pull (typically 2-10 minutes for OAuth sources, up to 30 minutes for high-volume credential-based sources).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Start by connecting your primary commerce platform, at least one ad platform, and your payment gateway. These three together unlock the most powerful Vortex Mind cross-channel diagnostics.
</Tip>
