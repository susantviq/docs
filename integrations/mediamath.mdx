Connecting MediaMath (T1) to Vortex IQ gives the AI OS a live read on your MediaMath (T1) data, feeding the Nerve Centre dashboard, Vortex Mind diagnostics, and Ask Viq.

## What data Vortex IQ reads from MediaMath (T1)

- **AdAccount**
- **Campaign**
- **AdCreative**

## How to connect

<Note>
  MediaMath (T1) uses credential-based authentication rather than OAuth. You provide these values directly rather than approving a redirect.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connected Sources">
    In your Vortex IQ workspace, go to **Settings → Connected Sources**. You need Admin role in the workspace to connect sources.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the MediaMath (T1) card">
    Locate the MediaMath (T1) connector card and click **+ Connect**. The connector drawer opens with a credential form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Gather your credentials from MediaMath (T1)">
    You will need:

    - **T1 Username**: T1 user account with API access. Engineering must verify post-2023 support.
    - **T1 Password**: T1 user password. Historical session-cookie login, engineering must verify current flow.
    - **Organization ID**: T1 organization ID, find under Admin → Organization Settings (when accessible).
    - **Agency ID**: T1 agency ID under the organization.
    - **Advertiser ID**: T1 advertiser ID under the agency. One per brand/client.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter credentials in Vortex IQ">
    Paste each value into its matching field in the connector drawer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify and connect">
    Click **Verify and Connect**. Vortex IQ makes a test API call to validate the credentials before saving them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Vortex IQ only ever requests read-only access. Where MediaMath (T1) offers scoped or read-only API keys, use one.
</Note>

## Available KPI cards

Vortex IQ populates **72** Nerve Centre cards from your MediaMath (T1) data. See the [MediaMath (T1) KPI card reference](/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/mediamath) for the full list with definitions, calculations, and sentiment bands.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Credential verification fails">
    Re-check each value against the MediaMath (T1) developer console for typos or trailing whitespace. If a scope or permission is missing, MediaMath (T1) will typically return a 401 or 403 on the verification call rather than a generic failure.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Cards show no data after connecting">
    Allow time for the first sync to complete; large accounts can take longer. If cards are still empty after an hour, disconnect and reconnect to confirm the credentials were saved correctly.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
