Connecting Adobe Commerce (formerly Magento) to Vortex IQ gives the AI OS access to your order stream, product catalogue, customer records, and fulfilment data. Adobe Commerce is the platform of choice for mid-market and enterprise merchants with complex B2B, multi-website, or custom checkout requirements - and Vortex IQ surfaces the KPI cards that matter for that operating context, including B2B-specific metrics that simpler analytics tools miss entirely.

## What data Vortex IQ reads from Adobe Commerce

Vortex IQ connects to the Adobe Commerce REST API and ingests the following data:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Orders and revenue">
    - Order totals across all websites and store views configured in Adobe Commerce
    - Order state and status (new, processing, complete, closed, cancelled, holded)
    - Payment method and gateway per order
    - Shipping amounts, methods, and carrier assignments
    - Discount codes and discount amounts
    - Refunds (credit memos) and return status
    - Tax amounts and tax class per line item
    - Guest vs customer account orders
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Products and inventory">
    - Product variants and configurable product options with current stock quantities
    - Product status (enabled, disabled)
    - Product type (simple, configurable, bundle, downloadable)
    - SKU, category, and attribute sets
    - Out-of-stock state per variant
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="B2B data (Adobe Commerce B2B edition)">
    - Company accounts and account status
    - B2B revenue share vs B2C revenue
    - B2B average order value delta vs B2C AOV
    - Account-level silence signals (companies that have stopped ordering)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Customers">
    - Customer records with email state
    - Order frequency and geography
    - Guest vs account-registered breakdown
    - Repeat purchase rate and churn signals
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  Adobe Commerce uses OAuth 1.0a authentication (the standard Adobe Commerce/Magento Integrations model), not a single bearer token. You will need five values from an Adobe Commerce Admin integration: your store URL, Consumer Key, Consumer Secret, Access Token, and Access Token Secret. Vortex IQ shows step-by-step instructions for generating all five inline in the connector drawer.
</Note>

## How to connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connected Sources">
    In your Vortex IQ workspace, go to **Settings → Connected Sources**. You need Admin role in the workspace to connect sources.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Adobe Commerce card">
    Locate the Adobe Commerce connector card and click **+ Connect**. The connector drawer opens with a credential form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an integration in Adobe Commerce">
    In your Adobe Commerce Admin, go to **System → Extensions → Integrations → Add New Integration**. Set the integration name, then under **API** grant read access to: Orders, Products, Inventory, Customers, and Store.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate the integration and copy all four OAuth values">
    Save, then click **Activate** to authorize the integration - Adobe Commerce generates all four OAuth 1.0a credentials at once: **Consumer Key**, **Consumer Secret**, **Access Token**, and **Access Token Secret**. Copy all four; the Consumer Secret and Access Token Secret are shown only once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your store URL and the four OAuth credentials">
    In the Vortex IQ connector form, enter your Adobe Commerce **Store URL** (for example, `https://store.yoursite.com`) plus the **Consumer Key**, **Consumer Secret**, **Access Token**, and **Access Token Secret** you copied.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify and connect">
    Click **Verify and Connect**. Vortex IQ signs a test API call with your OAuth 1.0a credentials to validate them against the store URL. On success, the card flips to Connected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the first data pull">
    Adobe Commerce KPI cards appear in Nerve Centre within 5 to 15 minutes. Full historical order backfill takes 1 to 4 hours.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Grant the integration **read-only** access to all required resources. Vortex IQ does not require write access to any Adobe Commerce resource. For multi-website setups, the OAuth credentials cover all websites within the same Adobe Commerce installation.
</Note>

## Available KPI cards

Vortex IQ populates Nerve Centre with Adobe Commerce KPI cards across the following categories.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Revenue and growth" icon="chart-line">
    **Total Revenue** - Gross revenue across all websites and store views for the period, compared to the prior period. Alerts on a drop greater than 15% vs prior 30 days.

    **Revenue Over Time** - Daily revenue trend sparkline.

    **Average Order Value** - Revenue ÷ Order Count, with trend.

    **Revenue by Day of Week** - Identifies highest-revenue days for scheduling.

    **Discount % of Revenue** - Discount value as a share of gross revenue.

    **Daily Order Trends** - Daily order volume with period comparison.
  </Card>
  <Card title="B2B-specific cards" icon="building">
    **B2B Revenue Share** - B2B company-account revenue as a percentage of total Adobe Commerce revenue.

    **B2B AOV Delta** - Difference between B2B average order value and B2C AOV. Indicates customer mix changes.

    **B2B Account Silence** - Company accounts that have not placed an order within a configurable look-back window. An early churn signal for wholesale accounts.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Orders and fulfilment" icon="truck">
    **Order Count** - Total orders placed in the period.

    **Orders Over Time** - Daily order volume sparkline.

    **Order State Breakdown** - Distribution of orders across Adobe Commerce order states.

    **Unfulfilled Orders** - Orders awaiting processing or shipment.

    **Fulfilment Rate** - Share of orders fulfilled within the expected window.

    **Fulfilment Over Time** - Fulfilment rate trend.

    **Cancellation Rate** - Cancelled orders as a share of total.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Customers" icon="users">
    **Customer Count** - Total active customers.

    **New Customers** - First-time buyers in the period.

    **Repeat Rate** - Share of customers who bought more than once.

    **Guest vs Account** - Breakdown of guest checkout vs registered account orders.

    **Churn Risk** - Customers showing lapse signals.

    **Customer Segments** - Breakdown by RFM-style segment.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Refunds and returns" icon="arrow-left">
    **Refund Rate** - Credit memos as a share of orders in the period.

    **Refund Value** - Total value of credit memos issued.

    **Refunds Over Time** - Daily refund trend.

    **Return Status** - Breakdown of return requests by state.

    **Refund Spike Alert** - Fires when refund rate rises sharply.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Alerts and anomalies" icon="bell">
    **Revenue Drop Alert** - Revenue falls more than 15% vs prior period.

    **OOS Spike Alert** - Out-of-stock count rises sharply.

    **Fulfilment Delay Alert** - Fulfilment rate drops below threshold.

    **Cart Abandon Spike Alert** - Cart abandonment rate rises sharply.

    **Refund Spike Alert** - Refund rate rises above threshold.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Cross-platform cards

When you connect ad platforms or other data sources alongside Adobe Commerce, Vortex IQ unlocks additional cross-platform cards:

| Card | What it shows |
|------|---------------|
| Active Ads on Out-of-Stock SKUs | Ad spend on OOS variants - pause candidates |
| Revenue at Risk from active incidents | Live revenue impact when a monitoring incident is open |
| Catalogue Drift vs Amazon | Adobe Commerce SKUs active on Amazon but OOS on the main store |
| Email Revenue Share | Email-attributed revenue as a share of Adobe Commerce total |

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="OAuth authentication fails">
    Check that the integration was created with the correct resource permissions (Orders, Products, Inventory, Customers, Store - all read-only) and that it was **activated** in Adobe Commerce Admin (an integration that is only saved but never activated has no valid Access Token or Access Token Secret yet). Verify all four values (Consumer Key, Consumer Secret, Access Token, Access Token Secret) were copied correctly - each is generated once at activation and cannot be recovered afterward, only regenerated. Also verify the Store URL does not have a trailing slash and matches the admin-panel domain exactly. If authentication still fails after re-checking all of this, deactivate and reactivate the integration in Adobe Commerce Admin to generate a fresh set of four credentials and reconnect with the new values.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="B2B cards show no data">
    B2B-specific cards (B2B Revenue Share, B2B AOV Delta, B2B Account Silence) require Adobe Commerce B2B edition and at least one active company account. If your installation is the standard Adobe Commerce edition without the B2B module, these cards will remain empty.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Revenue in Vortex IQ differs from Adobe Commerce reports">
    Adobe Commerce reports typically use the order created date in the configured locale timezone. Vortex IQ uses UTC. Boundary-day differences are expected, particularly for stores in UTC-offset timezones. For 30-day windows these differences average out. Also check whether your Adobe Commerce report is showing net revenue (post-credit memo) rather than gross - Vortex IQ Total Revenue is gross of credit memos.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Multi-website setup - only one website's data is showing">
    The Vortex IQ Adobe Commerce connector reads all websites within a single Adobe Commerce installation using the Admin API. Ensure the integration token has access to Global scope (not restricted to a specific website). For separate Adobe Commerce installations, each requires its own connector connection.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
