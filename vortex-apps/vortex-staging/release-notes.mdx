The following are the product fixes and releases implemented since Vortex Staging (Shopify) went live. For BigCommerce, see [StagingPro Product Fixes/Releases](/vortex-apps/staging-pro/product-fixes-releases).

**Release Notes May 2026**

- Installing Vortex Staging now follows the same setup flow as StagingPro and Vortex Backup, for a consistent experience across every Vortex App.

**Release Notes April 2026**

Vortex Staging launches inside the unified Vortex Apps toolkit

- Vortex Staging launched for Shopify merchants with a home, bulk migration, selective migration, themes, history and settings screens matching StagingPro's BigCommerce experience.

**Release Notes February 2026**

- One-click replica creation: spin up a staging copy of a Shopify store in a single click, instead of a manual multi-step setup.
- Vortex Staging can now sync automatically with a connected StagingPro (BigCommerce) parent environment.
