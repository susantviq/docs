The CloudHub sign-in page is the front door to your multichannel commerce console. It is a separate authentication surface from the main Vortex IQ login at `app.vortexiq.ai/login`, because CloudHub predates the AI OS unification and still ships its own credentialed login at the `247cloudhub.co.uk` domain.

This page explains exactly what the **Hey Glad to see you!** screen does, the credentials you need, the **Remember Me** behaviour, and what to do if you forget your password.

## What the login page shows

The CloudHub login page is a two-column layout. The left rail carries the brand message:

> Hey Glad to see you!
> To connect to your Multichannel Ecommerce Platform, please login.

Underneath that strapline sits a stylised office desk image and the **247Commerce** logo lockup with the standard footer:

> 2011 to 2026 247 Commerce Limited. All Rights Reserved.
> 247 Cloudhub is a registered trademark of 247Commerce.
> Privacy Policy . Terms of Use

The right rail is the actual sign-in form. The CloudHub cloud-and-247 logo sits at the top, centred, with the wordmark **CloudHub** beside it. Below the logo there are two input fields and a **Login** button.

## Before you begin

You need:

1. A CloudHub account. CloudHub accounts are provisioned by 247Commerce or Vortex IQ during onboarding. Self-service signup is not available; new accounts go through your account manager.
2. The work email tied to your account.
3. The password you set during the initial invite. If you have lost the password, use the [Forgot your password?](#forgot-your-password) flow described below.
4. A modern browser. Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari, and Brave all work. Cookies must be enabled for `247cloudhub.co.uk`.

## How to sign in

1. In the **Enter your email** field, type the email address tied to your CloudHub account. The field has an envelope icon on the right.
2. In the **Please enter your password** field, type your password. The field has a padlock icon on the right.
3. Decide whether to leave **Remember Me** ticked. It is on by default. With Remember Me on, your session persists across browser restarts for several weeks. With it off, you are signed out the moment the browser closes.
4. Click the blue **Login** button on the right of the Remember Me row.

If the credentials are valid, you land on the [CloudHub dashboard](/vortex-apps/cloudhub/dashboard) immediately. If they are wrong, the form shows an inline error. The error is generic and does not disclose whether the email exists in our system.

## Forgot your password

Below the **Login** button, two small text links sit on the right:

- **Forgot your password?** in blue.
- **Need an account?** with a **Contact Us** link in blue.

Click **Forgot your password?** to start the password reset flow:

1. The page asks for the email address tied to your CloudHub account.
2. CloudHub sends a password reset email to that inbox.
3. Open the email and click the reset link. It expires after a short window (typically one hour) for security.
4. Set a new password on the reset screen.
5. Sign in with the new password.

If the email is not on file, the system does not tell you. The reset confirmation message is the same whether the email exists or not. This is a deliberate security choice that prevents account enumeration.

## Need a new CloudHub account

CloudHub accounts are not self-service. To request one, click **Contact Us** under **Need an account?** and the link routes to the 247Commerce contact form. Your account manager provisions the VendorDB code, the marketplace connections, and the user invite within a few business days.

## What you see after login

The first thing you see is the CloudHub dashboard. The top navigation bar carries the CloudHub logo, a **Services** link, three quick-link shortcuts (Add a Product, Scan Pack Ship, Inventory Hub), and the user avatar with a 247 badge. The dashboard itself shows the Sales Report chart, Lifetime Sales / Average Per Order / Lifetime Order Count / Units Ordered cards, the Total Revenue / Units Ordered / Avg Order Value strip, and the Fast Selling Lines top ten table. Full walkthrough on the [CloudHub dashboard page](/vortex-apps/cloudhub/dashboard).

## Session behaviour

| Behaviour | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Successful login with Remember Me ticked | Session persists across browser restarts. Closing the tab keeps you signed in. |
| Successful login with Remember Me unticked | Session ends when the last CloudHub tab closes. |
| Idle for an extended period | Session expires server-side. The next click prompts a re-login. |
| Login from a second browser | Both sessions remain valid. CloudHub does not enforce single-session per user by default. |
| Password change | All sessions are invalidated. You must sign in again everywhere. |

## Common questions

**Can I sign in to CloudHub with my Vortex IQ Google account?**
Not yet. CloudHub authentication is currently email and password only at the `247cloudhub.co.uk` domain. SSO unification with the rest of Vortex IQ is on the roadmap.

**Why is my account locked after a few failed attempts?**
CloudHub rate-limits failed login attempts to protect against credential stuffing. After several wrong-password attempts, the account is temporarily locked. Wait fifteen minutes and try again, or use **Forgot your password?** to reset.

**I have several VendorDBs. Do I sign in with the VendorDB code?**
No. You sign in with your email and password as a user. Once authenticated, you switch between the VendorDBs you have access to from the avatar menu, see [Switch VendorDB](/vortex-apps/cloudhub/account/switch-vendordb). The VendorDB is not part of the login credentials.

**The footer copyright says 247 Commerce Limited but the docs say Vortex IQ. Which is the publisher?**
CloudHub is operated by 247 Commerce, a Vortex IQ group company. Vortex IQ is the AI OS umbrella that contains CloudHub alongside the other Vortex Apps. The 247 Commerce footer reflects the legal ownership of the CloudHub trademark; the product surface is part of the wider Vortex IQ AI OS.

**Does Remember Me store my password?**
No. Remember Me stores a long-lived session cookie that the server can validate. The password itself is never stored client-side. The cookie can be revoked by changing your password.
