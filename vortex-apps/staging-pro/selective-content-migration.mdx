Selective Content Migration is the StagingPro screen for surgical updates: copy a single product, a specific promotion, a category, a theme, a custom page, or a page template's Page Builder content from one environment to another without overwriting everything else.

Use it as a follow-up to a Bulk migration, or as the primary tool when you only need to sync a small slice of the catalogue.

## When Selective is the right tool

| Scenario | Selective vs Bulk |
| --- | --- |
| Update one product description across environments | Selective |
| Move a few promotions to UAT for testing | Selective |
| Sync three categories that were edited yesterday | Selective |
| Copy a custom page from Staging to UAT | Selective |
| Refresh the entire catalogue | [Bulk](/vortex-apps/staging-pro/bulk-content-migration) |

Selective is non-destructive on items you do not select. **Selective runs always update**: a picked item that already exists on the destination is brought into sync with the source rather than duplicated — there is no strategy choice on this screen.

## The interface

The screen shows six pick tabs: **Products, Categories, Custom Pages, Promotions, Themes, and Page Templates**.

<Frame>
  ![Selective Migration](/images/SelectiveMigration.png)
</Frame>

For each tab:

- Pick **source store and storefront**, then **destination store and storefront**. The storefront (channel) mapping can be reviewed and edited from any tab via **Edit mapping**; Default↔Default is pre-mapped, and mappings are remembered per store pair in your browser.
- Search and tick the items you want to copy. Your staged picks accumulate across pages, and the **Start Selective Migration (N)** button shows the total.
- **Dry Run — Forecast & ETA** prices exactly the run you have staged (your picks plus your product-option choices) before you commit.

<Note>
  Switching to another pick tab, or changing the source storefront, deliberately **abandons all staged picks** — each run is staged from one tab at a time. The category tree, page list, and channel map are cached for 60 seconds, so an item created moments ago can take up to a minute to appear in the browse (products are always live).
</Note>

## Advanced Filters (Products and Categories)

The **Advanced Filters** button (next to the search box) opens a panel with:

- A **category checkbox tree** — checking a category selects its whole subtree; a "Select all" row sits at the top. With a single source storefront selected, only that storefront's tree is shown.
- Product filters: **Visibility**, **Availability**, **Featured**, and **Modified on or after** (a date floor).
- **Storefront Assignment → "Not on any storefront"** (shown when the source is All Storefronts): because BigCommerce has no native filter for this, the browse switches to a **batch scan** — a progress line ("Checked 100 of 115 products — 1 not on any storefront") with a **Scan next 100** button. Matches accumulate and can be ticked and migrated like any other pick.

The button shows a badge counting active filters; **Reset** clears them.

## Products

Picking products and pressing **Start** (or **Dry Run**) opens the **Selective Product migration options** popup, where every sub-component is ticked by default and the engine honours your ticks exactly:

Include Images · Videos · Variants · Variant Metafields (needs Variants) · Custom Fields · Bulk Pricing Rules · Metafields · Reviews · Related Products · Channel Assignments · Modifiers · Complex Rules (needs Modifiers) · Product Locale (multi-storefront destinations only) · Custom Template Associations.

**Include Brand** and **Include Categories** are locked on: product-to-brand and product-to-category links can't exist without them. Only the brands and categories your picked products actually reference migrate (plus category ancestors, so the tree stays intact).

Behaviours worth knowing:

- **Related products link, they don't import.** Related products that already exist on the destination are connected by SKU; missing ones are neither migrated nor associated (the skipped link is listed with a reason). The product's "Automatically show related products" setting is copied as-is. A selective run never widens beyond your picks.
- **Storefront assignments mirror the source, within the mapping.** Each picked product's placements on mapped storefronts are made to match the source — missing placements added, extra ones removed (removals appear in the Deleted column). Storefronts outside the mapping are never touched. A product unassigned everywhere on the source ends up unassigned on the destination.
- With **Include Channel Assignments unticked**, the migration makes no placements: newly created products land on the default storefront (BigCommerce's own behaviour), and existing destination products keep their current assignments.
- **Product-list modifier options** are linked by SKU to products already on the destination; unmappable choices are dropped with a warning naming the choice and the fix, and a dependent complex rule becomes a reasoned skip rather than a failure.
- The **Breakdown by Phase** on the run detail counts only your picked products' components — never store-wide totals.

## Categories

The Categories tab renders the tree **hierarchically** (parent-first, with indentation). Selection is hierarchy-aware: picking a child auto-picks its ancestor chain (even across pages); unpicking a parent unpicks its descendants; unpicking a child keeps its ancestors. Under All Storefronts, a **Channels** column shows which storefront tree(s) each category belongs to. Category metafields and template associations ride along automatically. Existing destination categories are matched (case-insensitively) and updated rather than failing as duplicates.

Category picks migrate the categories themselves. To bring across the products inside them, either use the Products tab with the category-tree filter, or run a Bulk migration.

## Custom Pages

The Custom Pages tab lists pages hierarchically, like Categories; picking a child page auto-picks its parents. Search matches the **page name** only. A pages pick migrates the page records **and the Page Builder designs on those exact pages** — nothing else:

<Note>
  **Header, footer, and page-type layouts are never touched by a Custom Pages migration.** Those are channel-wide *global widgets*, and they travel only via the **Page Templates** tab with the **Include global widgets** option. A matched destination page is updated in place; a new page is created.
</Note>

## Promotions

Picked promotions migrate as complete units (conditions, actions, schedule, coupon codes). Customer groups ride along automatically because promotion targeting references them. Product, category, and brand references are re-pointed at the matching destination items; a promotion whose reference cannot be mapped is **skipped with a reason rather than migrated with broadened scope** — a discount is never silently widened. Storefront-scoped promotions land on the mapped storefronts.

> **Note that promotions created via the BigCommerce API cannot be edited after creation.** This is a BigCommerce constraint. Workaround: delete the promotion in the source, recreate it correctly using the standard editor, then migrate.

## Themes

Pick one or more themes and choose, per theme, which destination storefront(s) to set it live on in the **Activate on** column (a storefront can have only one active theme, so two picked themes can't target the same storefront). Activation is optional — leave it empty to upload without activating.

Theme migration guarantees:

- The theme is only touched when you migrate it — product, page, or settings runs never change themes.
- Theme files travel byte-for-byte; only the theme's settings file is adjusted so your live design settings (fonts, colours, logo) carry over instead of the vendor's demo defaults, with links re-pointed at the destination.
- **Nothing on the destination is ever deleted or overwritten**: every migration uploads a brand-new copy (auto-renamed if the name exists), and old copies remain as an instant rollback path. StagingPro never deletes a theme, under any setting.
- Activation is verified by reading back which theme is actually live, and other storefronts are provably restored to their original themes. If a safety rule refuses a switch, the run tells you the one manual step (Storefront → My Themes → apply the copy).

<Note>
  BigCommerce caps a store at roughly **20 themes**, and each migration consumes a slot. StagingPro checks the cap up front and warns as you approach it; clean up superseded copies manually under Storefront → My Themes.
</Note>

## Page Templates

The Page Templates tab picks **template files** (for example `pages/home`) and migrates their Page Builder **template-level content**. Templates with no Page Builder content are hidden from the list. Tick **Include global widgets** per template to also carry the channel-wide global widget content (header/footer) — this chip is the *only* way global widgets migrate. The run detail shows a **Page templates** section recording what was picked and whether global content was included and migrated.
