**Settings** holds your plan and the app's connections.

## Your plan

See your plan, this month's usage and what is included. See [Plans and limits](/vortex-apps/seo-geo-agent/plans-and-limits).

## Browser extension

This shows whether the VortexIQ extension is ready in this Chrome. The extension asks ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini your test questions, and runs content research.

- It runs in your own Chrome, with your own sign-ins, and never signs in for you.
- Chrome shows a bar saying **VortexIQ Browser Agent started debugging this browser** while it works. This is expected, because the extension uses it to read the answers.
- Connecting is per browser. Another browser on your account does not count.

## Google Analytics

Connect Google Analytics to see AI visits, purchases and revenue on **Overview**. Sign in with Google, then choose your property.

Without it, the rest of the app still works, including AI orders, which come from your Shopify orders.

## Your VortexIQ account

If your account needs attention, a bubble appears at the bottom right of **Overview**. Click it to resend the email that finishes setting up your account.
