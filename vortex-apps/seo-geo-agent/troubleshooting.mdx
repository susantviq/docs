## Extension and tests

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The app says the extension isn't installed">
    The extension is optional, and tests run on our servers without it. To use it, install it in this Chrome, then reload the page. If you just updated it, reload too.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The extension needs permission">
    In **Run a test**, press **Allow in the extension** and accept on the page that opens. The card updates by itself.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An assistant was skipped">
    You probably aren't signed in to it in this Chrome. Sign in, then run the test again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Stop didn't work straight away">
    A run stops after the question in progress. Anything it didn't reach is recorded as skipped.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Content and publishing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't generate topics">
    Check your plan: Free has no content plans, and paid plans have a monthly limit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A rewrite hasn't come back">
    Rewrites usually take 2 to 3 minutes. After 15 minutes, the article's page offers **Try again**. Your draft is untouched.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Publishing is blocked by checks">
    Fix what the page lists, or press **Publish anyway** at the top of the page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An article has no View live link">
    A scheduled or draft article has no page customers can reach. Use **Open in admin** instead.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Store content

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="We could not open this just now">
    Your store did not let the app read that item. Nothing has been changed. Try again in a minute, and email support@vortexiq.ai if it keeps happening.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The Store list shows only published posts">
    The app could not fully connect to your store, so it is reading what is public. Drafts and editing may not work. Email support@vortexiq.ai.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I undo image compression?">
    No. **Update image** replaces the original in Shopify, and the app doesn't keep a copy.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Numbers

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Agent-ready says 0%">
    A product counts when it is active and published to your online store. Check **Sales channels** on a product. If **Online Store** is on and it still counts as not reachable, contact support.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="AI orders looks low">
    It is an estimate from what Shopify records about each order, over the last 60 days. Many assistant visits show only in Google Analytics.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="AI visits shows a dash">
    Connect Google Analytics in **Settings**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Still stuck?

Email support@vortexiq.ai with your store's name and what you were doing.
