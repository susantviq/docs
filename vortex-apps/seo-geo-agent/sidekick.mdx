You can ask Sidekick about your AI visibility from anywhere in your Shopify admin. It uses the app's data to answer.

## Questions to try

- "How visible is my store in AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini?"
- "What stops AI shopping agents from selling my products?"
- "Which websites do AI assistants recommend instead of my store?"

## Actions Sidekick can start

| Ask for | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **An AI citation test** | The app opens with your questions filled in. You review them and press **Run test**. Nothing runs until you do. |
| **An AI visibility plan** | The app runs its analyst on crawler access, structured data, competitors and content gaps. |

<Note>
  Citation answers come from tests you have run. If you haven't run one, start in [AI visibility](/vortex-apps/seo-geo-agent/ai-visibility).
</Note>
