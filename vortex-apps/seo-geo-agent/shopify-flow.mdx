The app adds three actions to Shopify Flow, on every plan. None of them changes your products.

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Run store health check** | Checks your products, collections, pages and articles. The score updates in the app when it finishes. |
| **Sync store content** | Refreshes the app's copy of your posts, pages and products. |
| **Add AI visibility question** | Adds a question to your list. It is asked the next time you run a test in the app. |

## Add an action to a workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a workflow">
    Open the Shopify Flow app and create a workflow. Choose a trigger, such as **Scheduled time**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the action">
    Add an action and search for its name. For **Add AI visibility question**, enter the question.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Turn the workflow on. A draft workflow doesn't run.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- **Timing:** scheduled workflows can start a few minutes after their set time.
- **Health check:** Flow shows success when the check starts. Look for **Refreshed from Flow** in **Store health** to confirm it finished.
- **Questions:** a question already on your list isn't added twice. Flow shows a message if your list is full.
- **Tests:** a Flow action adds questions but doesn't run a test. Run tests from the app.
