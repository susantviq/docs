This page takes you from installing the app to being ready for your first test.

## What you need

- A Shopify store, and permission to install apps on it.
- Any browser the Shopify admin supports. Chrome on a computer is only needed for the optional VortexIQ extension.
- A Google Analytics property, if you want to see AI visits. This is optional.

## Set up the app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install from Shopify">
    Install the app and approve the permissions Shopify asks for. The app opens in your Shopify admin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go through the welcome steps">
    The welcome steps introduce your sales, AI testing, content planning, store fixes and Sidekick. They show once, until you finish them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the VortexIQ extension (optional)">
    On the **Add the VortexIQ extension (optional)** step, add the extension to Chrome, or skip it. Without it, tests and research run on our servers. With it, they can also run in your own signed-in assistants, for more accurate results. The app detects it, then asks you to allow this app in the extension. You accept once, on the extension's own page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Google Analytics">
    On the **Sign in with Google Analytics** step, sign in and choose your property. You can skip this and connect later in [Settings](/vortex-apps/seo-geo-agent/settings).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The extension runs in desktop Chrome, so add it from a computer. Everything else works on any device.
</Note>

## The app's pages

| Page | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Your headline numbers and AI referrals |
| **AI visibility** | Buyer questions and test results |
| **Content plans** | Research, topics and writing |
| **Needs approval** | Reviewing written articles |
| **Publish** | Publishing and scheduling |
| **Store content** | Editing content, store health and product images |
| **Settings** | Your plan, the extension and Google Analytics |

## What the permissions are for

| Permission | Why the app needs it |
| --- | --- |
| Orders (read) | Estimating which orders came from AI assistants |
| Products | Reading your catalog, and saving description, SEO and image changes you make |
| Content | Reading and publishing blog posts and pages |
| Files | Banner images and image uploads |
| Online store navigation | Redirects, so old links keep working when an address changes |
